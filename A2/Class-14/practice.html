Simple Past vs. Present Perfect

Learn when to use general life experience (Present Perfect) vs. specific past times (Simple Past).

💡 Grammar Breakdown: Past Tense vs. Present Perfect

1. Present Perfect (Unspecified Time / Experience): Have you ever + past participle?
"Have you ever eaten sushi?"
"Yes, I have." / "No, I haven't."
Signal words: ever, never, already, yet
2. Simple Past (Definite / Specific Past Time): Subject + past tense verb
"I ate sushi last week."
"We cooked hamburgers yesterday."
Signal words: last week, last night, yesterday, in 2023
