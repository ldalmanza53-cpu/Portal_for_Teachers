💡 Grammar Key: Converting Personal Experiences to Questions

1. General Life Experience (Present Perfect): Statement: "I've eaten sushi." / "I've never tried Thai."
Question: Have you ever eaten sushi? / Have you ever tried Thai?
2. Past Feeling / Opinion (Simple Past): Statement: "I liked it." / "They loved it."
Question: Did you like it? / Did they love it?
