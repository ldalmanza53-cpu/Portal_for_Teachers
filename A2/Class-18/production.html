Grammar & Language Scaffolding Cheat Sheet

1. Comparing Homes & Places (Comparatives)

• Short adjectives (+er + than): small → smaller than, big → bigger than, quiet → quieter than

• Long adjectives (more + adj + than): modern → more modern than, comfortable → more comfortable than

• Irregular comparatives: good → better than, bad → worse than

2. Recounting Moving Day (Past Simple)

• Regular verbs (+ed): unpack → unpacked, invite → invited, paint → painted, cook → cooked

• Irregular verbs: buy → bought, move → moved, go → went, make → made, meet → met

3. Explaining Recipes (Sequencers & Imperatives)

• Time Sequencers: First, Next, Then, After that, Finally

• Imperative Cooking Verbs: chop, boil, fry, bake, mix, slice, stir, serve
