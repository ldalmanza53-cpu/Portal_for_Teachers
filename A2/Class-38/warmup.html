First Conditional (A2 Level) Cheat Sheet

Formula:
If + Present Simple, WILL / WON'T + Verb

Used to talk about real and possible future situations and their probable results.

Example Chains:
  • If it rains tomorrow, I will stay home.
  • If I stay home, I will watch a movie.
  • If I watch a movie, I will make popcorn!
