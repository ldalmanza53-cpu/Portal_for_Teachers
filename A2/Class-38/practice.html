First Conditional Formula & Rules

Structure Formula:

IF + Simple Present , WILL / WON'T + Base Verb

Example: If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.

⚠️ Watch Out Spanish Speakers! (Fossilized Errors)

  • Incorrect: If it will rain, I will take an umbrella.
  • Correct: If it rains, I will take an umbrella. (No "will" in the IF-clause!)
  • Incorrect: I will to call you later.
  • Correct: I will call you later. (Base verb after modal "will", no "to").

Unless = If ... Not

"Unless you study, you won't pass." = "If you don't study, you won't pass."
