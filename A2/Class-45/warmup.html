📚 First Conditional & Workplace Grammar Reference

🎯 Positive Consequences

  • "If you learn web coding, you will get a job in tech."
  • "If she practices speaking, she will pass the interview."
  • "If they manage time well, they will finish early."

⚠️ Risks & Negative Outcomes

  • "If he doesn't arrive on time, he will lose his job."
  • "If a guide lacks teamwork, the tour will be messy."
  • "If you ignore clients, you won't make sales."

💡 Grammar Tip: First Conditional

Use If + Present Simple for the action, and Will + Verb for the future consequence!

✅ "If you study (present), you will succeed (future)."
❌ "If you will study, you succeed."
