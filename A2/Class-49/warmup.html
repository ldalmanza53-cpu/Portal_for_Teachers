📚 Fact Language Pillars

Facts are objective truths supported by evidence. In English, we use the Present Simple tense, specific measurement verbs, and precise location prepositions.

🔬 Scientific Truths (Present Simple)

  • "Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius."
  • "The Earth revolves around the Sun."
  • "Plants need sunlight and water to grow."

🌍 Geography & Location

  • "The Amazon River is located in South America."
  • "Tokyo is the capital city of Japan."
  • "Mount Everest is the highest mountain."

📏 Measurements & Data

  • "A blue whale weighs up to 180 tons."
  • "The human body consists of 206 bones."
  • "Cheetahs can run up to 120 km/h."

⚠️ Fact vs. Opinion

  • Fact: "Paris is in France." (Objective)
  • Opinion: "Paris is beautiful." (Subjective)
  • Fact Marker: "Data shows that..." / "In fact,..."
