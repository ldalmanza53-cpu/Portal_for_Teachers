Grammar Cheat Sheet: Orders & Requests

CEFR A2 Reference Guide for Direct Orders, Reported Orders & Polite Requests

Imperatives & Reported Orders
  • Direct Command:
    "Clean your room right now!"
  • Reported Order (tell/order to):
    "My mom told me to clean my room."
  • Negative Reported Order:
    "My boss ordered us not to be late."
Formal vs. Informal Requests
  • Formal: Could you please + verb?
    "Could you please print these files?"
  • Formal: Would you mind + verb-ing?
    "Would you mind holding the door?"
  • Informal: Can you / Could you + verb?
    "Can you pass the salt, please?"

Response Rules Matrix

Accepting:

• Formal: "Certainly, right away!" / "Of course, happy to assist."

• Informal: "Sure thing!" / "Yeah, no problem!"

Declining (With Reason):

• Formal: "I do apologize, but I am currently busy."

• Informal: "Sorry, I can't right now, I'm studying."
