SB-210 Unit 11 Grammar Rule 1

Tyler & Harry: Roommate Reported Requests

When reporting requests or imperatives, use ask, tell, or say followed by an infinitive (to + verb).

Tab Score 0 / 8
Critical Grammar Rule: Negative Reported Requests

In negative requests, the word "not" comes BEFORE "to"!
Example: Direct: "Don't leave your shoes." $\rightarrow$ Reported: "Tyler told him NOT TO leave his shoes." (NOT: told him to not leave)

SB-210 Unit 11 Grammar Rule 2

Barbecue Party Excuses: Reported Statements

When reporting statements in the past, backshift main verbs and modal verbs. Change pronouns as required to maintain accurate context.
