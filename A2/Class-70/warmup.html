1. Reported Statements Formula Clause Backshift
Subject + Reporting Verb + (that) + Backshifted Clause

Used to report facts, opinions, or announcements. The verb tense steps back into the past when the reporting verb is in the past tense.

Verbs: stated, pointed out, explained, claimed, admitted.
Example: "Revenue is higher." ➔ She stated that revenue was higher.
2. Reported Requests & Commands Formula Infinitive Structure
Subject + Reporting Verb + Object + (not) to + Base Verb

Used to report instructions, advice, or demands. Do NOT use "that clause" for standard request reporting!

Verbs: asked, instructed, requested, advised, urged.
Example: "Send the report." ➔ He asked us to send the report.
