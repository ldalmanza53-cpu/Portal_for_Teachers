Grammar Quick Bank

Structure: wish + subject + past simple Expressing a wish about present states that are untrue.
I wish I had... I wish I lived... I wish I knew...
Structure: wish + subject + could + verb Wishes about present ability or permission to do something.
I wish I could travel... I wish I could play... I wish I could fly...
Structure: wish + subject + were / was Use "were" or "was" for unreal present situations.
I wish I were taller... I wish I were on vacation... I wish it were sunny...

10-Minute Wish Activity Guide

01
Setup & Roles (1 Minute) Choose who is Role A (Wish Collector) and Role B (Wish Crafter). Start the 10-minute session timer.
02
Round 1 — Crafter B Speaks (3.5 Minutes) Collector A draws a prompt card. Crafter B states their current reality and expresses 2-3 wishes using "I wish I had...", "I wish I lived...", or "I wish I could...". Record a 1-minute audio challenge.
03
Round 2 — Crafter A Speaks (3.5 Minutes) Switch roles! Role A becomes the Wish Crafter and records their present wish story.
04
Peer Check & Badge (2 Minutes) Open the Peer Checklist, evaluate the target past simple grammar structures, and generate your Wish Master Badge!
