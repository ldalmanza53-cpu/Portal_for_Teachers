Grammar Tip: Politeness & Softening Words ("Softeners")

When talking about weaknesses or negative traits in English, use softeners like "a bit", "sometimes", or "a little" to sound polite and self-aware!

Example: Instead of saying "I am impatient", say "Sometimes I am a bit impatient."
