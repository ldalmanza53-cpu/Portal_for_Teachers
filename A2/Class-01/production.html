Host Helper Cheat Sheet

A2 Support

Ask your guest 2 follow-up questions in the Simple Past tense:

Where... "Where were you when that happened?"
Who... "Who was with you?"
Did you... "Did you enjoy it? Why?"
How... "How did you feel?"

Use these sentence openers to begin your 1-minute story:

Time opener: "When I was 7 years old..."
Memory: "I clearly remember when..."
Habit: "My family used to..."
Event: "One day, we went to..."

Past Tense Verbs Reference

Standard +ed: play → played, walk → walked
Ends in -e (+d): live → lived, like → liked
Consonant + y (→ -ied): study → studied, cry → cried
Double last consonant: stop → stopped, plan → planned

10-Minute Activity Guide

01
Setup & Roles (1 Minute) Decide who is Student A (Host) and Student B (Guest). Set your role in the top header.
02
Round 1 — Guest B Speaks (3.5 Minutes) Host picks a memory card and reads it aloud (or uses TTS). Guest B thinks for 30s and speaks for 1 minute in Simple Past. Host asks 2 follow-up questions.
03
Round 2 — Switch Roles (3.5 Minutes) Swap roles. Guest A picks a new card, records their story, and answers the host's questions.
04
Peer Review Check (1.5 Minutes) Open the Host Checklist. Highlight 2 verbs used correctly and 1 verb to improve, then publish your episode badge!
