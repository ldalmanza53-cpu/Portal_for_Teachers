A2 Past Simple Grammar Quick Reference

Regular Verbs (+ed)

Add -ed or -d to standard action verbs for early life memories:

  • live ➔ lived
  • play ➔ played
  • watch ➔ watched
  • walk ➔ walked
  • study ➔ studied

Essential Irregular Verbs

Common irregular verbs used when talking about childhood:

  • be ➔ was / were
  • go ➔ went
  • have ➔ had
  • grow up ➔ grew up
  • make ➔ made

Past Time Expressions

  • • When I was a child / kid...
  • • In primary school...
  • • When I was [7] years old...
  • • Back then, / In those days...
  • • Many years ago...

Host Question Formulas

Use Did for actions or Was/Were for states:

Where did you live?

What did you play?

Was it fun?

Were you happy?
