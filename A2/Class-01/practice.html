📖 Past Tense Grammar Rules

Compare how we form questions and answers with the verb BE (was/were) versus other verbs (did + base verb / irregular forms).

1

Past of BE: Was / Were

Information Questions:

Where were you born?

↳ I was born in Argentina.

Yes/No Questions & Short Answers:

Were you born in Buenos Aires?

↳ Yes, I was.

↳ No, I wasn't. I was born in Córdoba.

2

Simple Past: Did + Base Verb

Information Questions:

When did you move to Los Angeles?

↳ I moved here 10 years ago. I didn't speak English.

Yes/No Questions & Short Answers:

Did you take English classes in Argentina?

↳ Yes, I did. I took classes for a year.

↳ No, I didn't. My aunt taught me at home.

Irregular Verbs Cheat Sheet (Used in this lesson)

begin → began
think → thought
grow → grew
meet → met
move → moved
have → had
teach → taught
speak → spoke
take → took
be → was / were
