Interactive practice module for English learners (A2/B1)

📌 Quick Rule: Use the Simple Past when you specify when an event ended (e.g., last night, yesterday, in December). Use the Present Perfect for life experiences up to now without a specific finished time (e.g., Have you ever...?, I have never...).
