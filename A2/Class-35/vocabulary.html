gather

verb
gather verb
BACK FACE

Meaning

To come together in a group with family or friends.

Forms / Variations

Base: gather | Present: gathers | Past: gathered | Continuous: gathering

Common Collocations

gather around, gather for dinner, gather with relatives

A2 Example Sentences

  • Our family gathers every November for a big Thanksgiving meal.
  • We gather around the table to share what we are thankful for.
⚠️️ Language Note: In English, people "gather" together. Do not confuse it with "collect" objects from the floor.
