Grammar Spotlight: Evaluations and Comparisons

🎯 1. Evaluations

With Adjectives

✔ Our apartment isn't big enough for our family.

✔ This apartment is too hot.

Structure: not + adj + ENOUGH | TOO + adj
With Nouns

✔ There aren't enough windows.

✔ The neighbors make too much noise.

Structure: ENOUGH + count/noncount noun | TOO MUCH/MANY + noun

⚖️ 2. Comparisons

With Adjectives

✔ The building isn't as quiet as our old one.

✔ The location is just as convenient as the old one.

Structure: (not) AS + adj + AS
With Nouns

✔ We don't have as many bedrooms as we used to.

✔ We don't have as much privacy as we had.

Structure: AS MANY + count plural + AS | AS MUCH + noncount + AS
