Simple Present Passive without "by"

We use the Simple Present Passive to talk about general facts, international trade, agricultural products, languages, and industries around the world.

Grammar Formula
Active Voice (People/Farmers):
People + speak + Spanish in Colombia.
Passive Voice (Fact Focus):
Spanish + is spoken in Colombia.

Formula: Subject + AM / IS / ARE + Past Participle

Choosing "IS" vs "ARE"
IS + Past Participle

Use with singular or uncountable nouns:

  • Rice is grown
  • Tea is exported
  • French is spoken
ARE + Past Participle

Use with plural nouns:

  • Crops are grown
  • Cars are made
  • Handicrafts are sold
Why omit "by people"? In general facts, the agent (who does the action) is obvious or general, so we omit "by".
