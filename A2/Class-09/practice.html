Rule 1

Expressions of Quantity

Count vs. Noncount

Use different quantifiers depending on whether the noun is countable (plural -s) or noncount (uncountable).

🚗 Count Nouns Plural Nouns (-s)
  • too many / fewer / too few + Plural Noun
  • aren't enough + Plural Noun

✔ There are too many cars downtown.

✔ We need fewer accidents and more buses.

✔ There aren't enough parking spaces.

💧 Noncount Nouns Singular / Mass Nouns
  • too much / less / too little + Noncount Noun
  • isn't enough + Noncount Noun

✔ There is too much traffic and noise.

✔ We need less pollution in the city center.

✔ There isn't enough parking near the park.

Rule 2

Polite Indirect Questions

Statement Word Order

Turn direct information questions into polite requests by adding a starter phrase and changing the sentence to statement word order (Subject + Verb).

Common Starter Phrases
Can you tell me...?
Could you tell me...?
Do you know...?
Word Order Changes
Direct: Where is the post office?
✔ Can you tell me where the post office is?
✖ Can you tell me where is the post office?
Direct: What time does it open?
✔ Do you know what time it opens?
✖ Do you know what time does it open?
