A2 Grammar Rules & Drills

Master how to express excess problems (too, too much, too many) and solutions (should, need to).

1

TOO + Adjective

Use too before adjectives to describe a problem.

• The bus is too crowded.

• Tickets are too expensive.

• The train is too late.

2

TOO MUCH + Uncountable

Use too much with singular/uncountable nouns.

• There is too much traffic.

• There is too much noise.

• It costs too much money.

3

TOO MANY + Countable

Use too many with plural countable nouns.

• There are too many cars.

• There are too many trucks.

• There are too many people.

4

SHOULD / NEED TO

Follow with a Base Verb to give solutions.

• We should build cycle paths.

• The city needs to add buses.

• People should walk more.
