Grammar Rule

Past Tense: "Be" with Born & Verbs with Died

  • 📌 Use a form of be with born: I was born here. (NOT: I born here.)
  • 📌 Don't use a form of be with the verb die: He died last year. (NOT: He was died last year.)

Grammar Rule

"Used to" for Past Habits & States

Rule 1: Base Form in Questions & Negatives

Use base form use to (no 'd') after did or didn't:

✔ Did you use to play sports?

✖ Did you used to play sports?

✔ I didn't use to like bananas.

✖ I didn't used to like bananas.

Rule 2: Don't Combine "Never" with Double Negatives

Use never used to directly without didn't:

✔ I never used to wear sunglasses.

✖ I never didn't use to wear sunglasses.

Rule 1: Born vs Be
✅ I was born in Colombia.
❌ I born in Colombia.
Tap for explanation
Explanation

"Born" is an adjective/participle in English that requires the auxiliary verb was/were.

Rule 2: Died vs Was Died
✅ My granddad died in 2010.
❌ My granddad was died in 2010.
Tap for explanation
Explanation

"Die" is an intransitive verb. Use simple past died without "was" or "were".

Rule 3: Did ... Use To
✅ Did you use to play sports?
❌ Did you used to play sports?
Tap for explanation
Explanation

The auxiliary verb Did already carries past tense, so use base form use to.

Rule 4: Never Used To
✅ I never used to wear hats.
❌ I never didn't use to wear...
Tap for explanation
Explanation

Do not create double negatives. Use never used to directly with the main verb.
