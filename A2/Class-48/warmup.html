Landmark Fact Verification Grammar Matrix

4 Key Scaffolding Pillars
1. Confirming True Facts

Is [Landmark] in [Country]?

"Yes, it is! The Colosseum is located in Rome, Italy."

2. Correcting False Facts

No, it isn't! It is in...

"Is the Eiffel Tower in Italy? No, it isn't! It is in France."

3. Asking Locations

Where is [Landmark] located?

"Where is Machu Picchu located? It is in Peru."

4. Describing Famous Features

It is famous for [noun/-ing]

"The Taj Mahal is famous for its white marble dome."
