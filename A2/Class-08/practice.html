Forming Polite Indirect Questions

VRB-71 Rule

When asking for information politely in English, start with "Could you tell me...?" or "Do you know...?". The rest of the sentence uses statement word order (Subject + Verb).

1. Drop 'Does' / Add '-s'

Remove does and add -s / -es to the verb.

✔ ...what time the tour ends?

✖ ...what time does the tour end?

2. Drop 'Do' (Plural)

Remove do and keep the base verb unchanged.

✔ ...where the totem poles come from?

✖ ...where do the totem poles come from?

3. Move 'Is / Are' to End

Place the verb is / are after the subject noun phrase.

✔ ...where the Empress Hotel is?

✖ ...where is the Empress Hotel?
