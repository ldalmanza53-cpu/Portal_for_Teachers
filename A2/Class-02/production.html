A2 Grammar: Past Habits vs. Current Routines

Past Habits (used to)

Use used to + verb (infinitive) for past repeated actions that are no longer true today.

(+) I used to play video games.

(-) I didn't use to exercise.

(?) Did you use to eat fast food?

Present Simple & Adverbs

Use Present Simple with frequency adverbs for current routines.

• I always go to bed early.

• I usually cook healthy food.

• I rarely drink soda nowadays.

Contrast Connectors

Connect your past and current habits smoothly:

  • • ...but now, I...
  • • ...however, nowadays I...
  • • ...whereas today, I...
  • • ...in contrast, now I...

Host Question Formulas

Ask your guest about their transformation:

What did you use to do?

How did your habits change?

What do you usually do now?
