Grammar Quick Bank

Structure: subject + used to + base verb "I used to play football every day."
Negative: subject + didn't use to + base verb "I didn't use to drink coffee."
In the past, I used to... 5 years ago... When I was younger...
Adverbs of Frequency (before verb): always (100%) • usually (80%) • often (60%) • sometimes (40%) • rarely (10%) • never (0%)
I usually... I always prefer to... These days, I...
Contrast Connectors: Use to connect past habit with present routine.
, but now...
, however, today...
, instead, now...

10-Minute Activity Guide

01
Setup & Roles (1 Minute) Select who is Role A (Host - Journalist) and Role B (Guest - Storyteller). Start the 10-Minute master timer.
02
Round 1 — Guest B Speaks (3.5 Minutes) Host picks a card from the deck, reads it aloud or clicks TTS. Guest B uses "used to", "but now", and frequency adverbs to record a 1-minute podcast story. Host asks 2 follow-up questions.
03
Round 2 — Guest A Speaks (3.5 Minutes) Swap roles! Guest A selects a new prompt card and records their habit evolution story.
04
Peer Review & Badge (2 Minutes) Open the Peer Checklist, check off target grammar structures used, and generate your Podcaster Master Badge!
