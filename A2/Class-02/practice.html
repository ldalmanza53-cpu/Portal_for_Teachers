Definition

What does "Used to" mean?

Used to refers to something that you regularly did in the past, but don't do anymore. It contrasts a past routine or state with your current situation.

+

Affirmative (+)

Subject + used to + Verb

"I used to play baseball."

"She used to collect comic books."

-

Negative (-)

didn't use to + Verb

"I didn't use to collect anything."

"I never used to play sports."

?

Questions (?)

Did + Subject + use to + Verb?

"Did you use to play volleyball?"

"What did you use to collect?"

⚠️
Important Spelling Rule: Drop the "d" after Did / Didn't!

In negative sentences and questions, use didn't use to or Did you use to (NOT didn't used to or Did you used to).

⚡ Past Habit vs. Present Situation

Past Habit (Used to):

"I didn't use to collect anything..."

Present Habit (Now):

"...but now I collect old records."

Past Habit (Used to):

"I never used to play sports..."

Present Habit (Now):

"...but now I play tennis."
