THEN
Grammar Core

Comparing Past Habits (used to) & Present Habits (Present Simple)

Use "used to + base verb" for actions that were repeated habits in the past but are no longer true today. Combine it with Present Simple to highlight how your life has changed!

1. Past Habits (THEN)

Repeated actions in the past that stopped

used to + verb
Positive Form
Subject + used to + base verb

"She used to watch cartoons every morning."

Negative Form
Subject + didn't use to + base verb

"I didn't use to like broccoli as a kid."

Question Form
Did + Subject + use to + base verb?

"What did you use to do in your free time?"

2. Current Habits (NOW)

Regular routines & habits in the present

Present Simple
Common Connectors & Adverbs
now usually these days today every day
Full Comparison Examples
5 years ago vs. Today

"I used to walk to work, but now I take the subway."

Childhood vs. Adult Routine

"He used to sleep late, but now he usually wakes up at 6 AM."

Watch Out! Common A2 English Mistake

Don't forget: In negative sentences or questions with did / didn't, drop the 'd' from 'used to'!

I didn't used to like tea. I didn't use to like tea. ✓
