A2 Practice: Speculating with Modals & Adverbs

💡 Expressing Probability Cheat Sheet:
• Adverbs: Maybe it means... / Perhaps it means...
• Modals: It could mean... / It may mean... / It might mean...

1. A: I just had a job interview. The interviewer didn't offer to shake my hand.

B: Maybe it means

Show Sample Answer
e.g., ...he was in a hurry, or he was worried about germs.

2. A: I sent my friend three text messages, but she didn't respond.

B: It could mean

Show Sample Answer
e.g., ...she left her phone at home, or she is in a busy meeting.

3. A: My boss was late for our meeting. And she didn't make eye contact at first.

B: It may mean

Show Sample Answer
e.g., ...she was feeling stressed about a project.

4. A: I just got this new coat, and I really like it. But sometimes I see people whispering.

B: Perhaps it means

Show Sample Answer
e.g., ...they admire your style, or you forgot to take off the price tag!
