How to Explain Gestures & Body Language

In English, we explain what actions mean using these standard patterns:
• "When you [action], it means [meaning]."
• "[Action] shows that [clause]."
• "People use [gesture] to [express emotion/intent]."

Pattern 1: When + Action
When you [gesture], it means...

"When you wave, it means hello."

Pattern 2: Gesture + Shows
[Gesture] shows that...

"Shrugging shows that you don't know."

Pattern 3: Purpose
People use [gesture] to...

"People nod to express agreement."

💡 A2 Speaking Tips

  • 1. State the action clearly: "When you bow in Japan..."
  • 2. Explain the meaning using means or shows: "...it shows respect."
  • 3. Pause after punctuation marks for natural rhythm.
