Evaluations & Comparisons

Express opinions about apartments using too..., isn't... enough, and as... as.

💡 Grammar Spotlight: Giving Opinions & Comparisons

1. Excess (Negative Opinion): is too + adjective
"The apartment is too small for three people."
2. Insufficiency (Not Satisfied): isn't + adjective + enough
"The apartment isn't close enough to school."
3. Comparison of Equality / Difference: (not) as + adjective + as
"The old apartment isn't as big as Hudson Street."
Adjective Word Bank
big bright close expensive far small (used in #1)
