A2 Grammar Scaffolding Cheat Sheet

1. Short Adjectives (1 syllable or ending in -y) → Add -er + than

cheap → cheaper than | fast → faster than | easy → easier than

"Option A is cheaper than Option B, but Option B is faster."
2. Long Adjectives (2+ syllables) → Use more + adjective + than

expensive → more expensive than | comfortable → more comfortable than | practical → more practical than

"The hotel is more comfortable than the cabin."
3. Irregular & Equality Forms
  • Good → better than
  • Bad → worse than
  • As... as → "Option A is as good as Option B."
  • Not as... as → "Option A is not as expensive as Option B."
4. Key Evaluation Expressions

• "It is too expensive for my budget."
• "Option A is better for students because..."
• "I think Option B is the most practical choice."
