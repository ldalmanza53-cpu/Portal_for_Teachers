Reporting Commands (Told)

Direct Imperatives & Orders

Use tell + object + to / not to + verb base when reporting direct instructions or orders.

POSITIVE COMMAND FORMULA:
Subject + told + [person] + to + Verb base
NEGATIVE COMMAND FORMULA:
Subject + told + [person] + not to + Verb base
Examples:

• Direct: "Don't arrive late!"
• Reported: He told us not to arrive late.

Reporting Polite Requests (Asked)

Polite Questions & "Please"

Use ask + object + to / not to + verb base when reporting polite requests (e.g. "Please...", "Can you...", "Could you...").

POLITE REQUEST FORMULA:
Subject + asked + [person] + to + Verb base
Note: Remove words like "Please", "Can you", or "Could you" in reported speech.
Example:

• Direct: "Could you help me with the cake, please?"
• Reported: She asked me to help her with the cake.

Pronoun & Possessive Shifts

How personal pronouns change from Direct to Reported Speech

Crucial Rule
Direct me
him / her
Direct my

Direct your
my / his / her
Common Mistakes to Avoid!
  • Wrong Word Order: "He told me to not come."
    ✓ Correct: "He told me not to come."
  • Missing Object: "She said to bring snacks."
    ✓ Correct: "She told me / asked me to bring..."
  • Keeping 'Please' or 'Can you': "He asked me can you buy..."
    ✓ Correct: "He asked me to buy..."
