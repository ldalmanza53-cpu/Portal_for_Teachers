How to Express Problems & Give Advice

In English, when someone shares a problem, we use specific modal verbs and phrases to offer suggestions. Master these 5 target patterns below!

Pattern 1

You should...

+ Base Verb

"You should see a doctor."

Pattern 2

Why don't you...

+ Base Verb ?

"Why don't you take a nap?"

Pattern 3

How about...

+ Verb-ING ?

"How about calling a taxi?"

Pattern 4

You could...

+ Base Verb

"You could borrow my laptop."

Pattern 5

It's a good idea to...

+ Base Verb

"It's a good idea to rest."
