Modals for Necessity & Suggestion

Express rules & obligations (must, need to, have to) vs. advice & recommendations (should, ought to, 'd better).

💡 Grammar Key: Necessity vs. Suggestions

1. Describing Necessity (Rules & Obligations): • must: Formal rules or law ("You must carry ID.")
• have to / need to: Practical requirements ("You need to check out by noon.")
• don't have to: Absence of obligation / optional ("You don't have to change money.")
2. Giving Suggestions (Advice & Warnings): • should / ought to: General recommendations ("You should try local food.")
• 'd better: Strong advice with potential warning ("You'd better make reservations.")
• shouldn't: Advisable NOT to do ("You shouldn't pack too much.")
