Grammar Reference: Obligations & Suggestions

1. Expressing Obligations (Strict Rules & Laws)

• must + verb: Strong rule or personal obligation. "You must show your visa at customs."

• have to + verb: External rule or law. "You have to wear a seatbelt during takeoff."

• mustn't + verb: Prohibition (forbidden!). "You mustn't leave your luggage unattended."

• don't have to + verb: Absence of obligation (optional). "You don't have to pay for breakfast; it's included."

2. Giving Suggestions & Recommendations (Helpful Advice)

• should + verb: Good advice. "You should arrive 3 hours before an international flight."

• shouldn't + verb: Bad idea. "You shouldn't carry large amounts of cash."

• It's a good idea to + verb: Polite recommendation. "It's a good idea to download offline maps."

• Why don't you + verb: Friendly invitation/suggestion. "Why don't you buy a daily metro pass?"

3. Key Clarifying Questions for Travel

• "Do I have to print my ticket, or can I use my phone?"

• "Is it obligatory to show passport photos?"

• "What should I do if my flight is delayed?"
