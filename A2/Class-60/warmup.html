🪧 Common Signs & Modal Grammar Guide

Use modal verbs to explain what signs mean: must / have to (obligations), mustn't / can't (prohibitions), and can / allowed to (permission).

📵

No Mobile Phones

  • MUSTN'T "You mustn't use your phone here."
  • CAN'T "You can't make phone calls in this room."
  • NOT ALLOWED "Phones are not allowed inside."
🤫

Quiet Zone

  • MUST "You must keep quiet in the library."
  • HAVE TO "You have to turn off your ringtone."
  • REQUIRED "Silence is required in this area."
⚠️

Caution: Wet Floor

  • HAVE TO "You have to walk slowly and carefully."
  • MUSTN'T "You mustn't run on the wet floor."
  • CAN "You can slip if you run."
🅿️

Free Parking

  • CAN "You can park your car here."
  • ALLOWED "Visitors are allowed to park for 2 hours."
  • DON'T HAVE TO "You don't have to pay for parking."
