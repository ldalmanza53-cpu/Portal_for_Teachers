Concrete Plans

Definite

Use when you have a ticket, appointment, time, or decision made before now.

Structure 1: be going to + verb

"I am going to visit my grandmother tomorrow."

Structure 2: Present Continuous (+ time word)

"I am flying to Paris on Monday. (I have a ticket!)"

🤔
50% Uncertain / Idea

Possible Plans

Uncertain

Use when you are not sure, thinking, or deciding right now without fixed arrangements.

Structure 1: Maybe / Perhaps + will

"Maybe I will go to the park later."

Structure 2: I might / I want to + verb

"I might watch a movie tonight." / "I want to learn French."
