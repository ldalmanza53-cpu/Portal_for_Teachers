Grammar & Language Reference: Possible vs. Concrete Plans

1. Possible Plans (Flexible & Not Confirmed)

• might + verb: "I might travel to Italy this summer."

• thinking of + verb-ing: "I am thinking of buying a new bike."

• maybe + will + verb: "Maybe I will visit my grandparents this weekend."

• hope to + verb: "I hope to pass my driving test next month."

2. Concrete Plans (Fixed Time, Date, or Place)

• Present Continuous (am/is/are + verb-ing): "I am flying to London tomorrow at 9 AM."

• be going to + verb (strong intention): "We are going to move into our new apartment on Friday."

• Fixed Time Expressions: "at 5:00 PM", "on Tuesday morning", "already booked", "signed the contract"

3. Clarifying Questions to Ask Your Partner

• "Have you already bought the ticket?"

• "Is the date fixed, or can you change it?"

• "Are you sure, or is it just an idea for now?"
