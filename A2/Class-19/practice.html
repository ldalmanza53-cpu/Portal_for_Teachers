Unit Focus • Vacation & Weekend Plans

Future with be going to and will

Express decided plans with be going to vs. undecided / possible plans with will.

💡 Grammar Key: Decided Plans vs. Possible Plans

1. Decided Plans (be going to + verb): Use when a decision has already been made.
"I've decided on one thing – I'm going to take a bike tour."
"We're going to go surfing every day."
2. Possible / Undecided Plans (will + verb): Use before making a final decision (often with I guess, I think, maybe, probably).
"I guess I'll stay home."
"Maybe I'll take a course."
"I probably won't go
