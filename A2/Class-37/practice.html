Time Contrasts: Past, Present, and Future

Learn to contrast the past (used to / simple past), present (simple / continuous), and future (will / going to / might).

💡 Grammar Guide: Time Contrasts

1. Past: Expresses past habits or historical facts.
• A few years ago, there were just houses.
• We used to go to a burger place.
• In the past, kids used to hang out.
2. Present: Describes current situations or ongoing trends.
• These days, they're building apartments.
• Today, people order food online.
• Nowadays, kids meet online.
3. Future: Expresses predictions or future possibilities.
• Soon, there will be cities.
• Restaurants might not exist.
• We are going to have virtual friends.
