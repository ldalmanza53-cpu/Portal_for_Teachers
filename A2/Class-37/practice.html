Contraste de Tiempos Verbales: Pasado, Presente y Futuro

Aprende a diferenciar el uso del pasado (used to / simple past), presente (simple / continuous) y futuro (will / going to / might).

💡 Guía Gramatical: Time Contrasts

1. Past (Pasado): Expresa hábitos pasados o hechos históricos.
• A few years ago, there were just houses.
• We used to go to a burger place.
• In the past, kids used to hang out.
2. Present (Presente): Describe situaciones actuales o tendencias continuas.
• These days, they're building apartments.
• Today, people order food online.
• Nowadays, kids meet online.
3. Future (Futuro): Predicciones o posibilidades futuras.
• Soon, there will be cities.
• Restaurants might not exist.
• We are going to have virtual friends.
