Grammar Rule: -ED vs. -ING Adjectives

How Do You Feel About Entertainment?

Use -ED adjectives (excited, bored, interested, amazed) to describe how A PERSON FEELS.
Use -ING adjectives (exciting, boring, interesting, amazing) to describe THE MEDIA / THING that causes the feeling!

💡

Expressing Opinions

"In my opinion,..." / "I think that..." / "For me,..."

❤️

Describing Personal Reaction

"It makes me feel..." / "I felt amazed by..." / "I was bored during..."

🌟

Recommending

"I recommend watching..." / "You shouldn't miss..."

💡 Fluent Review Speaking Tips

  • 1. Always state your opinion clearly at the beginning using "In my opinion..." or "I think...".
  • 2. Match -ed for your feelings ("I was amazed") and -ing for the media ("It was amazing").
  • 3. Finish with a clear recommendation for your audience!
