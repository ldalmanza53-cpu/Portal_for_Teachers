💡 Essential Grammar: Imperative Verbs

How do we give instructions in English?

Use the Base Verb directly (e.g., *Open, Press, Mix*) without "you". To tell someone NOT to do something, add Don't before the verb (e.g., *Don't touch, Don't forget*).
