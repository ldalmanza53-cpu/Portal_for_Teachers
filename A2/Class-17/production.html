Imperatives & Directives Grammar Cheat Sheet

1. Positive Imperative Action Verbs (Base Form)

Do not use "You". Start directly with the base verb!

Press the button
Turn the dial
Insert the plug
Walk straight
Fold the paper
Open settings
Pull the lever
Check the light
Connect cable
2. Negative Imperatives & Warnings ("Don't...")
Don't touch the red wire.
Don't press hard on the screen.
Make sure not to turn off power.
Be careful not to spill water.
3. Time Sequencers & Location Prepositions

• First, locate the power button on the back.

• Next, turn left at the traffic light.

• Then, place the battery inside the slot.

• Finally, wait for 10 seconds.
