🍳 Kitchen Action Verbs Breakdown

  • Pound: Flatten meat using a mallet or hammer.
  • Sprinkle: Scatter small particles like salt, pepper, or herbs.
  • Spread: Cover a surface evenly with butter, cheese, or mustard.
  • Roll: Turn meat or dough into a cylinder shape.
  • Beat: Mix eggs rapidly with a fork or whisk.
  • Bake: Cook food inside an oven using dry heat.
