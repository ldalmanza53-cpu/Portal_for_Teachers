How Parents Give Orders & Rules

Learn how to express direct commands ("Clean your room!") and report them to others using tell someone to or ask someone not to.

Affirmative Order

Direct Imperative

"Clean your room!"

Reported Speech

My mom told me to clean my room.

Grammar Rule:

tell + person + to + infinitive verb

Negative Order

Direct Imperative

"Don't use your phone!"

Reported Speech

My dad told me not to use my phone.

Grammar Rule:

tell / ask + person + NOT + to + verb

Rules & Expectation

Direct Rule

"Be home by 9:00 PM."

Describing Rules

I am supposed to be home by 9:00 PM.

Grammar Rule:

be + supposed to + verb

Common Household Orders Practice List

10 Key Expressions
"Finish your homework before video games!"

→ Mom told me to finish my homework before playing video games.

"Don't leave your wet towel on the bedroom floor!"

→ Dad told me not to leave my wet towel on the floor.

"Lower the volume on the television!"

→ My parents asked me to turn down the television volume.

"Don't stay out past your curfew on Sunday!"

→ My father told me not to stay out past my curfew.

"Put your dirty clothes in the laundry hamper."

→ She asked me to put my clothes in the hamper.
