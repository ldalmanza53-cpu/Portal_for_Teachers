A2 Grammar: Parent Orders & Reported Commands

1. Direct Parent Imperatives

Parents use direct base verbs or "Don't":

• Positive: "Clean your room!" / "Do your homework!"

• Negative: "Don't stay up late!" / "Don't play games!"

2. Reported Commands Formula

Reporting orders using tell / order + person + to / not to + verb:

• My mom tells me to clean my room.

• My dad orders me not to play games late.

• My parents ask me to eat vegetables.

3. Expressing Obligation & Rules

How teenagers express house rules and consequences:

• have to: I have to turn off my phone at 10 PM.

• must: I must obey my parents' house rules.

• can't: I can't go out with friends until I finish.

Pro Tips for Radio Guests

Express feelings naturally on the radio show:

  • • Combine reported orders with your feelings (e.g. "It's annoying, but I obey!").
  • • Contrast mom's orders vs dad's orders.
