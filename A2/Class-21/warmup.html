Concrete Plans

Definite

Use when you have a booking, ticket, or fixed schedule made before speaking.

be going to + verb

"I am going to visit Paris on Monday."

Present Continuous (+ time)

"I am flying to Tokyo tomorrow morning."

🤔
50% Uncertain

Possible Plans

Ideas

Use when you are not sure yet, thinking, or making ideas without tickets.

I might + verb

"I might visit the Eiffel Tower."

Maybe + will / want to

"Maybe I will try local food tonight."

💡
Tips & Rules

Travel Advice

Recommendations

Use to give tips, suggestions, or reminders to fellow travelers.

You should / shouldn't + verb

"You should carry cash."

Imperatives (Commands / Tips)

"Wear comfy shoes! / Don't forget your passport!"
