A2 Grammar: Concrete Plans, Possible Plans & Travel Advice

Concrete Plans (Fixed Dates)

Use Present Continuous or be going to for arrangements with fixed times or booked tickets.

• I am flying to Rome on June 15th.

• We are staying at the Grand Palace Hotel.

• She is taking the train at 9:00 AM.

Possible Plans (Uncertain)

Use might + verb or thinking of + verb-ing for possibilities without fixed bookings.

• We might visit the museum on Tuesday.

• I am thinking of renting a kayak.

• Maybe we will try local seafood.

Travel Advice & Rules

Give advice and requirements to travellers:

  • • You should... (Friendly advice)
  • • You must... (Strong necessity/rule)
  • • You have to... (Important requirement)
  • • You shouldn't... (Negative advice)

Consultation Questions

Useful formulas for your travel dialogue:

Consultant: "When are you travelling?"

Consultant: "What advice do you need?"

Client: "What should I pack for this trip?"
