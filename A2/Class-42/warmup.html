💼 Job Suitability & Reason Justifier

Learn to explain why you would be good or bad at different jobs using traits and reasons!

📚 Job Justification Reference

🌟 Why I'd Be GOOD at a Job

  • "I'd be a good tour guide because I'm friendly."
  • "I'd make a great teacher as I'm very patient."
  • "I'd be good at programming since I like math."
  • "I'm good at cooking, so I'd be a great chef."

🚫 Why I'd Be BAD at a Job

  • "I'd be a bad accountant because I hate math."
  • "I wouldn't be a good nurse as blood scares me."
  • "I'd be terrible at sales since I am shy."
  • "I'm bad at waking up early, so no baker job!"

🎭 Useful Personality Traits

  • Patient (Teacher / Nurse)
  • Organized (Manager / Accountant)
  • Creative (Chef / Artist / Designer)
  • Punctual (Pilot / Driver)

💡 Grammar Tip: Good at + -ING

Always use a verb + -ing or a noun after "good at" or "bad at"!

✅ "I'm good at listening to people."
✅ "I'm bad at drawing."
❌ "I'm good at listen."
