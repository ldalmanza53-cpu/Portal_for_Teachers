3rd Person Grammar Setup

Select pronouns and view how verbs change for 3rd person singular!

💡 Grammar Rule: 3rd Person Singular Verb + 'S'
  • I live $\rightarrow$ He/She lives
  • I like $\rightarrow$ He/She likes
  • I have $\rightarrow$ He/She has
  • I am $\rightarrow$ He/She is
