For Yourself 🥤

"Can I have...?"

Asking to receive something politely.

"Can I have a glass of water, please?"
"Could I have the check, please?"
"May I have a menu, please?"
💡 Formula: Can / Could / May I have + [Noun] + please?
Asking Action 🤝

"Could you please...?"

Asking someone to perform an action.

"Can you close the door, please?"
"Could you please help me?"
"Could you repeat that, please?"
✨ Formula: Can / Could you + [Base Verb] + please?
Accepting 👍

Saying YES

Polite answers when agreeing to help.

"Sure! Here you go."
"Of course! No problem."
"Certainly! Right away."
🌟 Tip: Always smile and say "Here you go!" when handing an item.
Declining 🙏

Saying NO Politely

How to refuse gently without being rude.

"I'm sorry, I can't right now."
"Sorry, I don't have one."
"I'd love to, but I'm busy."
🔑 Key Softener: Always start with "Sorry" or "I'm sorry" to be polite!
