Phrasal Verbs

Two-part verbs consist of a action verb + particle (preposition or adverb). Match them with appropriate household items.

Separable Phrasal Verbs Rule

With Nouns: The object can come before or after the particle.

  • "Pick up the trash." (Valid)
  • "Pick the trash up." (Valid)

With Pronouns (it/them): The pronoun MUST go between the verb and particle!

  • "Pick it up." (Correct!)
  • "Pick up it." (INCORRECT!)

Polite Request Formulas

Formula 1: Gerund (-ing)
Would you mind + verb-ING ...?

e.g., "Would you mind picking up the trash?"

Formula 2: Base Verb
Could you (please) + BASE verb ...?

e.g., "Could you please take off your shoes?"

Shared Living Co-Op Context & Role Instructions

🏡 The Shared Living Scenario

Welcome to the Shared Space Co-Op! Living together in a apartment requires clear, polite communication to manage daily chores without causing tension. You will practice asking roommates to take care of items using two-part phrasal verbs.

🎭 Role Responsibilities

Role A: Request Maker

Identifies household clutter or tasks and makes polite requests using *Would you mind...* or *Could you please...*.

Role B: Co-Tenant

Responds enthusiastically and politely using *Not at all*, *Sure*, *I'd be happy to*, or *No problem!*.

Role C: Harmony Auditor

Monitors timer, checks particle placement rules (nouns vs pronouns), and evaluates the exchange on the rubric.

⏱️ 10-Minute Roadmap

  1. Phase 1 (Min 0-3): Complete Part A Matrix matching and construct 3 custom co-op items.
  2. Phase 2 (Min 3-7): Draft requests in Part B Validator and practice roleplaying 3-4 scenarios.
  3. Phase 3 (Min 7-10): Spin scenarios in Audio Studio, record live dialogue, and complete Role C Peer Rubric.
