Grammar Reference: Making & Responding to Requests

CEFR A2 Guide for Formal, Informal, and Permission Requests

Informal Requests (Friends & Family)
  • Can you + base verb?
    "Can you pass the salad, please?"
  • Could you + base verb?
    "Could you lend me your charger?"
  • Do me a favor and + verb?
    "Do me a favor and hold this."
Formal Requests (Hotel, Work, Strangers)
  • Could you please + base verb?
    "Could you please print 10 copies?"
  • Would you mind + VERB-ING?
    "Would you mind bringing extra towels?"
  • I was wondering if you could...
    "I was wondering if you could help me."

Adding Reasons & Polite Responses

Always Add a Reason:

• Use because, so that, or since:

"...because my hands are full."

"...because my phone battery is dead."

Accepting / Declining:

• Accept: "Sure, no problem!" / "Certainly!"

• Decline politely: "I'd love to help, but..." / "I do apologize, but..."
