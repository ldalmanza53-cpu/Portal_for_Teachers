🎯 Context

Welcome to the "Then & Now" Studio! You are hosting or guesting on a lifestyle documentary project exploring how life, daily habits, interests, and technology have changed from the past to today.

👥 Roles

Role A (Studio Host): Draw comparison cards and ask your guest to contrast their past vs. present life.

Role B (Lifestyle Guest): Answer by explicitly contrasting past habits with present reality using past and present verbs.

⏱️ 10-Minute Timetable

  1. 0:00–2:00: Review contrast structures (*In the past I..., but now I...*).
  2. 2:00–5:00: Round 1 (Role A interviews Role B using Cards 1–10).
  3. 5:00–8:00: Round 2 (Swap roles; Role B interviews Role A using Cards 11–20).
  4. 8:00–10:00: Share 1 surprising then-vs-now contrast about your partner with the class.
