How to Make & Respond to Suggestions

Learn the 3 main formulas to propose ideas, plus polite ways to accept or decline! Click any speaker icon to listen.

Formula 1 👉

Let's + Base Verb

Use base verb (no "to", no "-ing"). Simple & direct!

"Let's eat pizza!"
"Let's watch a movie."
"Let's play soccer!"
⚠️ Common mistake: Don't say Let's to go or Let's going. Say Let's go!
Formula 2 ❓

Why don't we + Base Verb?

Friendly question format. Use base verb!

"Why don't we go to the park?"
"Why don't we drink ice tea?"
"Why don't we study together?"
💡 Tip: Always end with a question mark (?) when writing this suggestion!
Formula 3 🍦

How about + Noun / V-ing?

Use a thing (noun) OR verb with -ing!

"How about ice cream?" 🍦
"How about swimming?" 🏊
"How about going home?" 🏠
🔑 Grammar note: Add -ing after "How about" for actions: swimming, playing, eating.

💬 How to Respond to Suggestions

👍 Accepting Suggestions (Say YES)
"Great idea!"
"Sounds good!"
"Sure! I'd love to!"
👎 Declining & Countering (Polite NO + New Idea)
"Sorry, I'm tired."
"I can't. I have homework."
"No thanks. How about pizza instead?"
