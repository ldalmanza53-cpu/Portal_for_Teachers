A2 Grammar: Making & Responding to Suggestions

Making Suggestions Formulas

Use these common structures depending on verb form:

• Why don't we + base verb?
e.g. Why don't we go to the museum?

• How about / What about + verb-ing?
e.g. How about going to the beach?

• Let's + base verb.
e.g. Let's cook pasta tonight.

• We could + base verb.
e.g. We could take the early train.

Accepting Suggestions

Enthusiastic responses when agreeing:

• That sounds like a great / fantastic idea!

• That works really well for me.

• I'd love to! What time shall we meet?

• Perfect! Let me book the tickets.

Declining & Counter-Proposals

Polite ways to decline and offer alternative ideas:

• I'd love to, but I'm busy / short on money.

• That sounds nice, but it's a bit too far.

• How about doing it tomorrow instead?

• Why don't we try another place instead?

Pro Tips for A2 Speakers

Keep conversations natural and friendly:

  • • Always give a polite reason when declining.
  • • Use rising tone for questions (e.g. Shall we?).
  • • Follow up declining with a counter-suggestion.
