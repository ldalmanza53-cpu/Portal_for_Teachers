Direct vs. Reported Speech

In English, when we tell another person what someone said earlier, we use Reported Speech. At A2 level, we move tenses one step into the past (backshifting) and choose between said and told me.

1 Core Rule: "Said" vs "Told"

Reporting Verbs

SAID (No Direct Person Listener)

"She said that she was happy."

Pattern: Subject + SAID + (that) + clause

TOLD ME (Requires Object Listener)

"She told me that she was happy."

Pattern: Subject + TOLD + Person (me/him/her/us) + (that) + clause

2 Tense Backshifting at A2

Present → Past
Direct Speech Tense Reported Tense Example Change
Present Simple Past Simple am/is → was, like → liked
Present Continuous Past Continuous is working → was working
Modal: CAN COULD can help → could help
Future: WILL WOULD will call → would call
