Grammar Reference: Tense Backshift Matrix (VRB-116)

Master how present tense verbs shift into past forms in reported statements.

Modals (Can) can → could

Modal can shifts to past modal could.

Direct: "They can change plans."
Reported: Tim said they could change plans.
Present Continuous 'm/is/are → was/were

Be verb shifts to was or were + verb-ing.

Direct: "I'm salsa dancing."
Reported: Steve said he was salsa dancing.
Future (Will) 'll/will → would

Future will / 'll shifts to conditional would.

Direct: "I'll make it up to you."
Reported: Steve told Tim he would make it up to him.
Present Simple (Be) 're/are → were

Plural are / 're shifts to past plural were.

Direct: "We're on a call."
Reported: Jessica said they were on a call.
Negative Modals can't → couldn't

Negative modal can't shifts to couldn't.

Direct: "John can't come."
Reported: Jessica said John couldn't come.
Opinion Verbs think → thought

Opinion verb think shifts to past thought.

Direct: "I think it's awful."
Reported: Tina said she thought it was awful.

Key Grammar Rules to Remember

1. Said vs. Told

Use said when you don't mention the listener ("Tim said he was busy"). Use told when you name the listener ("Steve told Tim he would call").

2. Optional "That"

The word that is optional in spoken reported speech (e.g. "Steve said he was..." and "Steve said that he was..." are both 100% correct).
