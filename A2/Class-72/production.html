🎯 Context

Welcome to "Global Vision Think Tank"! You are strategy consultants briefing your team on recent statements made by world leaders, scientists, and experts, and brainstorming what you would do in those hypothetical situations.

👥 Roles

Role A (Senior Strategy Director): Reads the expert statement and asks what your partner would do in that situation.

Role B (Innovation Consultant): Reports the expert's statement first, then provides a hypothetical solution (*He said that... If I were..., I would...*).
