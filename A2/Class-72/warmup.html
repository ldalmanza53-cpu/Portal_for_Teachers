Imaginary Situations & Reported Statements

At A2 level, we learn to describe imaginary situations using conditionals (If I won the lottery, I would buy a house) and report statements that others made earlier (She told me that she was happy). When we combine them, we report people's dreams and hypothetical wishes!

1 Imaginary Situations

If + Past → Would

Use If + Past Simple for the imaginary condition, and would + verb for the result.

"If I had a magic key, I would open every door."

Condition: Past (had) | Result: would + open

2 Reported Statements

Backshifting

When reporting what someone said in the past, shift present tenses to past tenses.

Direct: "I am happy." → Reported: She said that she was happy.

is/am → was | can → could | will → would

3 SAID vs. TOLD ME

Object Rules

Said does not take a person listener object. Told MUST take a listener object (me, him, her, us).

✔ She SAID that she liked travel.
✔ She TOLD ME that she liked travel.

✘ Wrong: She told that... / She said me that...
