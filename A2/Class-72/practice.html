Grammar & Interrogation Cheat Sheet (T-113)

Essential formulas for past simple questions and reported speech lie detection.

1. Deductions Formula He/She said that

When reporting why you think someone is lying, shift their direct words to past tenses.

Direct quote: "The bathroom is too small!"
Reported: He said (that) the bathroom was too small.
2. Cross-Examination Past Simple Qs

Use WH- Question words + did + subject + base verb or was/were.

• What hotel were you in?
• What was wrong with the bathroom?
• What did the manager do?
3. Common Backshifts Present → Past

Essential verb shifts when reporting suspect statements.

is / am → was
are → were
don't / doesn't → didn't
can't → couldn't

How to Play "Who is Lying?" in Class

Step 1: Select 1 Truth

Three contestants choose one true statement belonging to only one of them. The other two bluff!

Step 2: Cross-Examine

Classmates ask specific questions about details (places, times, actions) to spot contradictions.

Step 3: State Deductions

Classmates report who is lying with grammatical justifications: "He said that..."
