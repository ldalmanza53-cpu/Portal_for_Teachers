Discussing City Problems Politely

When speaking with city officials, use polite openers and indirect question word order. Instead of demanding action directly, ask politely using structures like "Excuse me, could you tell me...?" or "I would like to report..."

Gold Rule: Indirect Formal Question Word Order

Notice how the subject comes BEFORE the verb in formal indirect questions!

1. Polite Opener "Could you tell me..."
2. Question Word "...where..."
3. Subject "...the recycling bin..."
4. Verb (At End) "...is?"
❌ Direct/Informal: "Where is the recycling bin?"
✅ Polite/Formal: "Excuse me, could you tell me where the recycling bin is?"
