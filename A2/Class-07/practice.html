Noncount nouns (traffic, noise, parking, pollution) usan too much, less, isn't enough, too little.

📌 Regla rápida: Las preguntas indirectas son solicitudes educadas. Usa el orden de oración afirmativa después del conector: "Can you tell me where the post office is?" (No: where is the post office).
1. A: Excuse me. Can you tell me where the post office is?
B: Yes, of course. The post office is on the next corner.
2. A: And could you ?
B: You can find a really good restaurant on Central Avenue.
3. A: OK. Do you ?
B: Yes. The restaurant is called Giorgio's.
4. A: Thanks. Can you ?
B: Yes. They serve Italian food.
5. A: Oh, good! Do you ?
B: It opens at 5:00. Tell them Joe sent you!
