💡 Quantifier Rules Quick Recap:
• Countable Nouns (apples, cars, people): too many, a few, fewer, enough
• Uncountable Nouns (water, traffic, money, noise): too much, a little, less, enough

Exercise 1: At the Supermarket

A: Why is there traffic in the store today?
B: Because there are people buying groceries for the holiday. We only need apples and milk.

Exercise 2: Preparing Dinner

A: Is there salt in the soup?
B: No, it needs more salt, but don't add !
💡 Indirect Questions Word Order Rule:
Direct Question: Where is the bus stop?
Indirect Question: Do you know where the bus stop is? (Subject + Verb order, no invert!)

Question 1

Direct: "Where is the nearest bank?"

You: Could you tell me

Question 2

Direct: "What time does the supermarket open?"

You: Do you know

Question 3

Direct: "How much does a train ticket cost?"

You: Can you tell me

Scenario: Asking for Information in a City

Complete the polite conversation combining quantifiers and indirect questions.

Tourist: Excuse me!
Local: Yes! It's only 5 minutes away. But don't go now, there are people waiting for tickets.
Tourist: Oh I see. Also,
Local: Sure! There's one around the corner. They have great coffee and nice cakes!
1. Expressions of Quantity Matrix
Category Countable Nouns (cars, books) Uncountable Nouns (water, money)
Excess / High Quantity Too many Too much
Sufficient Quantity Enough Enough
Small Quantity A few A little
Comparative Decrease Fewer Less
2. Indirect Questions Structure

Common Introductory Phrases:

  • Do you know...?
  • Could / Can you tell me...?
  • I wonder...
❌ Wrong: Do you know where is the post office?
✓ Correct: Do you know where the post office is?
