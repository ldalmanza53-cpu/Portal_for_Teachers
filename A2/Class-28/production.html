Grammar Reference: Describing Gadget Purpose

CEFR A2 Grammar Formulas & Target Structures

used for + -ING

Use prepositions (for) followed by gerunds (-ing):

"An air fryer is used for cooking crispy food without oil."

used to + BASE VERB

Use infinitives (to) followed by base verbs:

"An electric kettle is used to boil water rapidly."

allows / helps us to

Explain user benefit & capability:

"Noise-canceling headphones allow us to study in quiet."

Common ESL Errors to Avoid:

❌ Incorrect:

• "A vacuum is used for clean floors." (Missing -ing)

• "A watch is used to tracking steps." (Wrong combination)

✅ Correct:

• "A vacuum is used for cleaning floors."

• "A watch is used to track steps."
