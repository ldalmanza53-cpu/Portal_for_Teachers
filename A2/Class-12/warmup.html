Evaluation Cheat Sheet

  • Short Adjectives: Add -er + than
    quiet → quieter than, cheap → cheaper than
  • Long Adjectives: more / less + adjective + than
    more convenient than, less isolating than
  • Equal Comparison: as + adjective + as
    isn't as quiet as, as fast as
  • Irregulars:
    good → better, bad → worse, little → less
Step 2: Grammar Core

Expressing Present Wishes (`I wish` & `If only`)

When we evaluate our current situation and want it to be different, we use I wish or If only + Past Simple / Could. Notice that we use past tense to describe a present regret or desire!

1

I wish + Past Simple

For present situations you want to change

Current Reality: My commute is long.
"I wish my commute was/were shorter."

💡 Formal Note: In formal English, were is often used for all subjects (I wish it were warmer).

2

I wish + could + Verb

For present abilities or possibilities

Current Reality: I can't afford a new home.
"I wish I could afford a new home."

💡 Replaces can't or inability with could + base verb.

3

If only + Past Simple / Could

Stronger emotional emphasis

Current Reality: The street is noisy.
"If only this street were quieter!"

💡 Expresses stronger emotion or urgent regret than I wish.
