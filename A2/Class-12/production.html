10-Minute Consultation Guide

01
Setup & Role Switch (1 Minute) Select Consultant (Role A) and Client (Role B). Press Play on the Master Session Timer.
02
Consultation Round 1 (3.5 Minutes) Consultant A draws a prompt card and asks evaluation questions ("What is wrong with your current situation?"). Client B evaluates ("It is too small"), compares Option A vs Option B ("Option A is cheaper than B"), and expresses present wishes ("I wish I had...").
03
Consultation Round 2 (3.5 Minutes) Switch roles! Role A becomes the Client with a new prompt card and records a 60-second wish response.
04
Evaluation & Certification (2 Minutes) Open the Peer Checklist, mark language criteria, and award your Certified Life Upgrade Badge!
