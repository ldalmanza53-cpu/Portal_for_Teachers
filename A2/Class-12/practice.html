Grammar Reference

Evaluations, Comparisons & Wish Structures

🎯 1. Evaluations

In evaluations, enough goes after adjectives and before nouns.

Adjective + Enough

✔ This house isn't bright enough.

❌ NOT: isn't enough bright

Enough + Noun

✔ It doesn't have enough light.

❌ NOT: light enough

Formula: not + adj + ENOUGH | ENOUGH + noun

⚖️ 2. Comparisons with as...as

Use (not) as... as or just as... as to compare two items.

With Adjectives

✔ My new apartment isn't as large as my old one.

With Nouns & Equality

✔ This apartment has just as much privacy as my old one.

Formula: (not) AS + adj + AS | AS MUCH/MANY + noun + AS

💫 3. Wish (Could / Would / Were)

Express desires to change a current situation using modal past forms or subjunctive were.

Ability (Could)

✔ I can't move, but I wish I could.

Willingness / Action (Would)

✔ My landlord won't call, but I wish he would.

State of Being (Were)

✔ My house isn't nice. I wish it were more attractive.

Tip: Use were / weren't for all subjects in formal English wishes!
