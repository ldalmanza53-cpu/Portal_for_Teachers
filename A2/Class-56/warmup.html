📚 A2 Definition Formulas & Rules

Learn how to define people, things, and places using the 3 key relative pronouns: WHO, WHICH / THAT, and WHERE.

People 👤

Use "WHO"

A [person] is someone who [action/job].

Use who when defining jobs, professions, or types of people.

Things & Objects 📦

Use "WHICH" or "THAT"

A [thing] is a tool which/that [function].

Use which or that when defining objects, tools, or instruments.

Places & Shops 📍

Use "WHERE"

A [place] is a location where [activity happens].

Use where when defining locations, buildings, or shops.
