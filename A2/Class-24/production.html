Grammar Cheat Sheet: Politeness & Registers

CEFR A2 Reference Guide for Asking and Responding to Favors

Formal Register (Strangers, Managers, Officials)
  • Could you please + base verb?
    "Could you please call a taxi?"
  • Would you mind + verb-ing?
    "Would you mind holding the door?"
  • I was wondering if you could...
    "I was wondering if you could help me."
Informal Register (Friends, Family, Roommates)
  • Can you + base verb?
    "Can you pass the salt?"
  • Could you + base verb?
    "Could you lend me a charger?"
  • Do me a favor and + base verb
    "Do me a favor and feed the cat."

Response Rules Matrix

Accepting:

• Formal: "Certainly, I'd be happy to." / "Of course."

• Informal: "Sure thing!" / "Yeah, no problem!"

Declining (Always include reason!):

• Formal: "I am so sorry, but I am currently busy."

• Informal: "Sorry, I can't right now, I'm studying."
