How to Describe Imaginary Situations

In English, we use imaginary conditionals to talk about dreams, unfulfilled wishes, or hypothetical events in the present or future:
• "If + Past Simple" sets the imaginary condition.
• ", I WOULD / COULD + Base Verb" expresses the imaginary consequence.
• "If I had a superpower, I would fly across the world."

Formula 1: Standard Order
If + Past Simple, I would + Verb

"If I lived in space, I would see stars."

Formula 2: Ability (Could)
If + Past Simple, I could + Verb

"If I found a magic box, I could open it."

Formula 3: Reverse Order
I would + Verb IF + Past Simple

"I would travel if I won money."

💡 Grammar & Pronunciation Tips

  • 1. Remember the comma pause: Pause briefly after the if clause!
  • 2. Use would for certainty of imaginary consequence, and could for potential ability.
  • 3. Notice that "If I were..." is frequently used instead of "If I was..." in formal imaginary English.
  • 4. Set speech rate to 0.75x or 0.90x to repeat each line with clear stress!
