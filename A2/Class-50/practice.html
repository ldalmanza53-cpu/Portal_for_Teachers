Past Continuous vs. Simple Past

Interactive practice on actions in progress and interrupting events

Grammar Focus

Past Continuous (was / were + -ing) Use for an action in progress in the past.
Simple Past (verb + -ed / irregular) Use for an action that interrupts it.
I was having lunch when I spilled coffee on my clothes.
I was driving to the airport, but I got a flat tire.
While I was shopping one day, a celebrity walked into the store.
