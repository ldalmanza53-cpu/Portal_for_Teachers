📚 Personal Storytelling Pillars

A great personal story has four main parts: an Introduction, Time Connectors, Past Actions, and a Feeling/Reflection.

👋 1. Starting Your Story

  • "I want to tell you about a special day."
  • "Two years ago, something funny happened to me."
  • "Last summer, I had an unforgettable experience."

⏳ 2. Sequence Connectors

  • "First, I packed my bags and left home."
  • "Then, suddenly, the lights went off."
  • "After that, we decided to go outside."

🏃 3. Key Past Actions (Past Simple)

  • "I visited my grandparents in the countryside."
  • "I lost my keys, but my friend helped me."
  • "We tried a traditional dish for the first time."

🌟 4. Ending & Feelings

  • "In the end, everything was okay."
  • "I felt extremely happy and proud."
  • "I learned an important lesson that day."
