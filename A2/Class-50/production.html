Welcome to the "My Story, My Journey" Podcast Studio! Interview your partner about their life experiences, childhood memories, achievements, and fun personal anecdotes.

👥 Roles

Role A (Podcast Host): Draw story cards and ask your guest to share personal anecdotes and details.

Role B (Guest Speaker): Share stories about yourself using past tenses, time markers, and sequence words.
