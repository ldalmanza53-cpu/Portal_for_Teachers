Grammar Reference: Travel Plans, Requests & Apologies

CEFR A2 Guide for Vacation Roleplay

Vacation & Travel Plans
  • be going to + verb (Intentions):
    "I am going to visit Rome next month."
  • Present Continuous (Fixed Bookings):
    "We are flying to Paris on Friday at 9 AM."
  • might + verb (Open Plans):
    "We might rent a car at the airport."
Polite Travel Requests
  • Could you please + verb?
    "Could you please check my booking?"
  • Would you mind + VERB-ING?
    "Would you mind changing my seat?"
  • Can you + verb? (Informal):
    "Can you send me the itinerary?"

Apologies, Excuses & Smooth Responses

Apologies & Excuses:

• "I'm so sorry for arriving late — my taxi was stuck in heavy traffic."

• "I apologize for forgetting my ticket voucher — I was super busy."

Forgiving Responses:

• "Don't worry about it at all!"

• "That's totally fine, no problem!"

• "I understand completely."
