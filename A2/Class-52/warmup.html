Grammar Core Comparison

Telling Past Stories vs. Sharing Recent Activities

To master A2 English communication, you must know when to use Past Simple for sequenced finished stories versus Present Perfect for recent life updates.

1. Telling Stories

Past Simple + Time Connectors

Verb-ed / V2

Use for finished past actions at a specific time. Stories use Time Connectors to show the sequence of events.

Essential Story Connectors:
First Next / Then Suddenly After that In the end

"First, I lost my keys yesterday."

"Suddenly, a small cat jumped out!"

"In the end, we arrived safely two days ago."

2. Recent Life Activities

Present Perfect + Signal Words

Have/Has + V3

Use for recent events connected to NOW without specifying a finished past time. Uses Signal Words.

Essential Signal Words:
Just (short time ago) Already (earlier) Yet (negative/q's) Recently / Lately

"I have just completed my English exam."

"She has already cleaned her apartment."

"We haven't ordered our dinner yet."

Grammar Anchor Rule Reference Table

Feature Telling Stories (Past Simple) Recent Activities (Present Perfect)
Time Reference Specific finished time (yesterday, last week, in 2021) Unspecified recent time or result connected to present
Key Markers First, then, suddenly, after that, 2 days ago Just, already, yet, recently, lately, ever, never
Verb Formula Subject + Verb-ed (V2) Subject + have/has + V3
Common Mistake ❌ "I have seen him yesterday." ❌ "I didn't finish yet." (Better: I haven't finished yet)
