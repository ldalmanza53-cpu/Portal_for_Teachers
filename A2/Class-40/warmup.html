⏪ Past Tense

Past Simple (Completed actions)

Signals: yesterday, last year, 2 days ago

  • "I visited my grandparents yesterday."
  • "She bought a new car last month."

▶️ Present Tense

Present Simple & Continuous

Signals: every day, right now, currently

  • "I study English every morning."
  • "We are working on a project now."

⏩ Future Tense

Be going to & Will (Plans & Predictions)

Signals: tomorrow, next week, in 2030

  • "I am going to travel next week."
  • "It will rain tomorrow afternoon."
