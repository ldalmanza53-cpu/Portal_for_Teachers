You and your partner are recording an episode of "The Life Time Capsule" podcast, exploring how people change over time.

🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Assign Roles: Student A is the Podcast Host and Student B is the Guest. Switch roles after 5 minutes.
  2. Draw a Timeline Card: The Host reads the question aloud and starts the timer.
  3. Answer Across 3 Time Frames: The Guest must answer using the Past, the Present, and the Future.
  4. Ask a Follow-Up Question: The Host asks one extra question (e.g., "Why did you change?" or "When are you going to start?").
