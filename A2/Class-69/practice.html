Core Grammar Principle

Understanding Tense Backshift in Reported Speech

When reporting what someone said in the past, the main verb usually moves one tense back into the past (Tense Backshift). We also change pronouns (I $\rightarrow$ he/she, we $\rightarrow$ they) to match the speaker context.

1

Tense Backshift Map

Main verbs move one step back in time:

  • Present Simple $\rightarrow$ Past Simple
  • Present Cont. $\rightarrow$ Past Cont.
  • Past / Pres. Perf. $\rightarrow$ Past Perfect
  • Will / Can / May $\rightarrow$ Would/Could/Might
2

SAID vs. TOLD

Use TOLD when mentioning the listener object. Use SAID without an object.

✔ She told her (that) she had plans.

✔ She said (that) she had plans.

✘ She told that she had plans. (Incorrect)

3

Pronouns & "That"

Pronouns shift to match context (I $\rightarrow$ he/she, my $\rightarrow$ his/her, we $\rightarrow$ they). The word "that" is optional!

• Mason: "My in-laws..."

• Mason said his in-laws...

* Both "said that he..." and "said he..." are 100% correct.
