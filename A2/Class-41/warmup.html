💬 Opinion Exchange: Likes, Skills & Reactions

Practice expressing preferences, abilities, and agreeing or disagreeing naturally

📚 Expression & Reaction Reference

❤️ Likes & Dislikes

  • "I love cooking Italian food."
  • "I like playing video games."
  • "I don't like waking up early."
  • "I can't stand rainy days."

🎨 Skills & Abilities

  • "I can play the guitar quite well."
  • "I am good at drawing portraits."
  • "I can't swim very fast."
  • "I'm not very good at singing."

🤝 How to Agree

  • Positive Likes: "Me too!" / "So do I!"
  • Negative Likes: "Me neither." / "Neither do I."
  • Positive Can: "I can too!" / "So can I!"
  • Negative Can't: "Neither can I."

✋ How to Disagree

  • Positive Statement: "Really? I don't."
  • Negative Statement: "Oh, I do!"
  • Positive Can: "Oh, I can't."
  • Negative Can't: "Really? I can!"
