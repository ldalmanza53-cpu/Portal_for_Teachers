Imperatives and Infinitives for Suggestions

Aprende a dar recomendaciones y consejos de uso tecnológico con Imperativos + Infinitivos.

💡 Guía Gramatical: Expresiones de Sugerencia

1. Sugerencias Afirmativas (+ Infinitivo): • Make sure to charge your phone.
• Be sure to update the app.
• Remember to back up your files.
• Don't forget to reset your passwords.
2. Sugerencias Negativas (+ NOT + Infinitivo): • Be sure not to get your phone wet.
• Try not to use public Wi-Fi networks.
• Try not to use your birthday as a code.
Frases de Sugerencia Disponibles
Make sure to . . . Be sure not to . . . Try to . . . Try not to . . . Remember to . . . Don't forget to . . .
