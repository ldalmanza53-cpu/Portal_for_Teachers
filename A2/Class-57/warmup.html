How to Discuss Books, Movies, TV & Games

Use Present Simple to describe plot summaries ("The book is about...", "The main character lives in...").
Use Past Simple to describe your viewing or reading experience ("I watched it yesterday", "I read three chapters").

💡 Fluent Media Critic Tips

  • 1. Identify the genre first: "This sci-fi movie / fantasy novel is..."
  • 2. Use Present Simple for plots: "The main character travels to Mars."
  • 3. Use Past Simple for personal time: "I watched it last night."
  • 4. End with a star rating or recommendation: "I give it 4 out of 5 stars!"
