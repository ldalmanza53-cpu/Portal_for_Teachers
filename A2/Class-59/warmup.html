How to Speculate on Body Language & Gestures

When we are not 100% sure what someone feels, we use speculation structures.
Learn key A2 formulas: "It looks like...", "Maybe they are...", "This gesture probably means...", and "He / She might feel..."

Visual Impression
It looks like...

"It looks like she is tired."

Possibility
Maybe they are...

"Maybe he is impatient."

Meaning & Symbol
This gesture probably means...

"...that you don't know the answer."

Modal Verb
He / She might feel...

"She might feel stressed."

💡 A2 Interpreter Tips

  • 1. Describe the physical gesture first: "He was tapping his foot..."
  • 2. Use might or maybe for uncertainty: "She might be nervous."
  • 3. Use It looks like + clause: "It looks like they agree."
