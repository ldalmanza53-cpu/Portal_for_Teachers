💡 Grammar Rules (Degree of Certainty):
• 100% Certain: must / definitely ➔ "That must mean 'no'." / "That definitely means 'no'."
• 50-80% Likely: probably ➔ "It probably means she is confused."
• 30-50% Possible: might / may / could / maybe / perhaps ➔ "It might mean 'call me'."
