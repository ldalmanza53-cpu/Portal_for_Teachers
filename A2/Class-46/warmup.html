📚 Expressions for Places, Inventions & Art

🏛️ Remarkable Places

  • "Machu Picchu is located in Peru."
  • "The Colosseum was built in ancient Rome."
  • "It is a breathtaking and famous landmark."

💡 Great Inventions

  • "The telephone was invented by Bell."
  • "The printing press changed human history."
  • "Smartphones help us communicate instantly."

🎨 Iconic Masterpieces

  • "The Mona Lisa was painted by Da Vinci."
  • "David was sculpted by Michelangelo."
  • "Starry Night is a beautiful painting."

💡 Grammar Tip: Simple Passive

Use is/are + past participle for present facts, and was/were + past participle for historical actions!

✅ "The Eiffel Tower was built in 1889."
✅ "The museum is visited by millions every year."
