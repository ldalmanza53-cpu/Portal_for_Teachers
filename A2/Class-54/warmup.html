Past Stories vs. Recent Activities

Use Past Simple (visited, climbed, bought) for finished trips at specific times (last year, in 2022). Use Present Perfect (have seen, have just arrived) for recent life experiences without a specific time!

💡 A2 Speaking Tips

  • 1. Emphasize finished time phrases like "In 2022" or "Last summer" before using Past Simple verbs.

  • 3. Use sequence words like "First," "Then," and "Finally" to structure your story clearly.
