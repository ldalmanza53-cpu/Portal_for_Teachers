Imperatives and Infinitives for Suggestions

Aprende a dar consejos e instrucciones tecnológicas usando imperativos y frases con infinitivo.

💡 Cómo dar sugerencias e instrucciones

1. Imperativos (Afirmativo / Negativo) Usa la forma base del verbo o Don't + verbo:
• Be sure to turn it on.
• Don't forget to charge the battery.
• Make sure to back up your files.
2. Infinitivos (to + verbo base) Combina expresiones clave con to + verbo:
• Try to keep it dry.
• Remember to log out.
• Try not to drop it.
