Should Have + Past Participle

Past Criticism & Advice

Use should have + past participle to express advice or criticism about what was the right/good thing to do in a past situation (which didn't happen).

FORMULA:
Subject + should have + Past Participle (V3)
Contraction: should've / Negative: should not have (shouldn't have)
Textbook Example:

"The guest should have apologized and gone home."

If I Were..., I Would Have...

Hypothetical Past Reactions

Use If I were [person], I would have + past participle to imagine yourself in someone else's situation in the past and state your reaction.

FORMULA:
If I were [the host/guest], I would have + V3
Note: Always use If I were (subjunctive) instead of "If I was" in formal grammar.
Textbook Example:

"If I were the host, I would have invited them inside anyway."
