Cooking Verbs & Sequencers Cheat Sheet

1. Imperative Cooking Verbs (Actions)
chop (cut into pieces)
slice (cut thin layers)
peel (remove skin)
boil (cook in water)
fry (cook in hot oil)
bake (cook in oven)
whisk (beat rapidly)
stir (mix around)
pour (transfer liquid)
sprinkle (scatter lightly)
grate (shred cheese)
simmer (gentle boil)
2. Recipe Sequence Adverbs
First, Step 1
Next, Step 2 / 3
Then, Middle steps
After that, Later steps
Meanwhile, Parallel
Finally, Last step
3. Apprentice Clarifying Questions

• "How long do I cook/bake it?" → "Cook it for 10 minutes."

• "How much salt/sugar should I add?" → "Add a pinch of salt."

• "What heat should I use?" → "Use medium heat."
