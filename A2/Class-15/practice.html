🧀 Textbook Example: Macaroni & Cheese

  1. First, boil the macaroni in a large pot.
  2. Then, melt the butter in a saucepan and stir in the flour.
  3. Next, slowly add the milk and cook until the sauce is thick.
  4. After that, add the grated cheddar cheese and stir until smooth.
  5. Finally, mix the drained macaroni with the cheese sauce and serve hot.
