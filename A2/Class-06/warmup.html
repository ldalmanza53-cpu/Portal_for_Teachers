📖 Formal City Questions Grammar Rules

Master how to ask polite questions in English cities with simple A1 rules.

1

Polite Starters

Always start with a softening phrase so you don't sound ordering or rude to strangers.

• "Excuse me, could you tell me..."

• "Could you please tell me..."

• "Do you know..."

2

Word Order Swap

In formal questions, the subject comes BEFORE the verb (it becomes statement order!).

...where is the station?

...where the station IS.

3

Yes / No Questions

For questions that start with "Is there...?" or "Does...?", add IF in formal style!

Direct: "Is there a bank near here?"

Formal: "...if there is a bank near here?"
