💡 Levels of Certainty:
• Slight possibility: might, may, maybe, perhaps
• Possibility: could, probably
• Strong possibility / Certainty: must, definitely
1. Perhaps it means she doesn't agree with you.

a. (maybe)

b. (might)

c. (may)

2. That gesture could mean, "Come here."

a. (probably)

3. That almost definitely means he doesn't understand you.

a. (must)
