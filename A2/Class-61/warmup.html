1. Personalities, Strengths & Weaknesses

Focus on adverbs of degree (*overly, exceptionally, notoriously*) and descriptive adjectives.

"The director was excessively stubborn, but his strength lay in strategic foresight."
"The engineering team was notoriously passive when challenging orders."
reckless visionary overly cautious rigid

2. Environmental Problems & Impacts

Focus on precise ecological terminology and compounding consequences.

"Chemical toxicity poses an irreparable threat to marine biodiversity."
"Mangrove deforestation removed natural storm barriers, triggering severe erosion."
effluent salinization microplastics bioaccumulation

3. Past Regrets & Hypothetical Situations

Focus on 3rd Conditionals, *Wish / If only + Past Perfect*, and *Should have + V3*.

"If the team had installed backup valves, the spill would not have occurred."
"Management should have heeded early warnings rather than slashing safety."
If only we had... Had they audited... would have been averted

4. Explanations, Reasons & Judgements

Focus on formal connectors (*due to, on account of, owing to*) and past modal speculation (*must have*).

"The unit ruptured on account of metal fatigue and inadequate testing."
"Delaying public evacuation was grossly negligent and completely unjustified."
due to must have known highly ill-advised owing to
