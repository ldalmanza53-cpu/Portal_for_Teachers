Core Grammar Concept

The Verb "To Be" for People

We use am / is / are to ask and talk about someone's name, country of origin, age, job, feelings, and state.

1. Present Simple Forms

Affirmative & Contractions
Subject Full Form Contraction Example
I am I'm I am a teacher.
You / We / They are You're / We're / They're They are from Canada.
He / She / It is He's / She's / It's She is 25 years old.

2. Question Formulas

A. Wh- Questions (Asking Information)
Wh- Word + am / is / are + Subject + Rest?

Example: "Where is she from?" / "What is his job?"

B. Yes / No Questions (Confirmation)
Am / Is / Are + Subject + Complement?

Example: "Is he a doctor?" $\rightarrow$ "Yes, he is." / "No, he isn't."

CEFR A1 Common Beginner Pitfalls
Age Trap ("Have" vs "Be")

He has 25 years old.

He IS 25 years old.

In English, we use to be for age, never have!

Articles with Jobs

She is doctor.

She is A doctor.

Always put a / an before singular professions.

His vs Her Possessives

His = 👨 Male | Her = 👩 Female

What is his name? (John)

What is her name? (Maria)
