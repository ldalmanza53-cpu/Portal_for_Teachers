Asking & Understanding Questions About People (A1 Speaking Activity)

⏱️ Time: 10 Mins 👥 Pair Interview 🆔 Personal Questions
⏱️ Speed-Friending Clock
10:00
Phase 1: Setup & Question Review (2 Mins)
🎭 Activity Roles
Role A (The Interviewer):
  • Asks personal questions to gather student details (*"What is your name?", "Where are you from?"*).
  • Listens and clicks each field on screen as you verify the answer!
  • Asks for spelling if needed (*"Can you spell that?"*).
📋 10-Minute Activity Steps
  1. 0–2 Mins: Pair up, review interview questions, and select a profile card.
  2. 2–5 Mins: Round 1 — Interview student B and check off their profile details.
  3. 5–8 Mins: Swap Roles! Pick a new profile card and repeat the interview.
  4. 8–10 Mins: Class Introductions — Introduce your partner to the class!
