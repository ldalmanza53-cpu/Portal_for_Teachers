Possessive Adjectives

Learn how to talk about names and people using my, your, his, and her!

1st Person (Me)

my

Talking about yourself 🙋

"What's your name?"

➔ "My name's Carlos."

2nd Person (You)

your

Asking the person in front of you 🫵

➔ "What's your name?"

Asking someone directly.

3rd Person (Male ♂️)

his

Talking about a boy or man 👦

"What's his name?"

➔ "His name's Ethan."

3rd Person (Female ♀️)

her

Talking about a girl or woman 👧

"What's her name?"

➔ "Her name's Isabella."

💡

Grammar Note: Short Forms (Contractions)

What's = What is   |   name's = name is
