📚 Personal Pronouns vs. Possessive Adjectives

Click any card or sentence to hear the clear English pronunciation!

🙋‍♂️

I ➔ MY

First Person (Self)
I am a student. Pronoun
My name is Alex. Possessive
👉

YOU ➔ YOUR

Second Person (Listener)
You are in my class. Pronoun
What is your name? Possessive
👨

HE ➔ HIS

Third Person (Male)
He is from Japan. Pronoun
His name is Marco. Possessive
👩

SHE ➔ HER

Third Person (Female)
She is a teacher. Pronoun
Her name is Sarah. Possessive
