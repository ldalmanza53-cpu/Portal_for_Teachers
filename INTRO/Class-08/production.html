🧳 Lost & Found Desk

Describing Personal Belongings & Possessives (A1 Speaking Activity)

⏱️ Time: 10 Mins 👥 Pair Roleplay 🎒 Belongings & Possessives
⏱️ Activity Clock
10:00
Phase 1: Setup & Target Language Review (2 Mins)
🎭 Activity Roles
Role A (Lost & Found Agent):
  • Asks questions about the lost bag and contents (*"What bag do you have?", "What is inside?"*).
  • Checks off each belonging on screen as Role B confirms them.
  • Hands over the bag when verified (*"Is this your bag?"*).
📋 10-Minute Activity Steps
  1. 0–2 Mins: Pair up, review target questions/answers, and select a belonging card.
  2. 2–5 Mins: Round 1 — Agent interviews Owner to identify their lost bag & belongings.
  3. 5–8 Mins: Swap Roles! Pick a new prompt card and repeat the conversation.
  4. 8–10 Mins: Class Check — Present a classmate's belongings to the class!
