Talking About Belongings with 'To Be'

Learn how to identify personal items, ask about ownership using "Is this your...?" / "Are these your...?", and describe objects with colors, sizes, and possessives!

1. Singular Items (This / It is)

Use This is / It is for single belongings like phones, watches, bags, and umbrellas.

This is my phone It is blue Is this your bag?

2. Plural Items (These / They are)

Use These are / They are for plural items like keys, glasses, shoes, and headphones.

These are my keys They are new Are these your glasses?
