☕ The Welcome Café

Speed Greetings & Short Introductions (A1 Level)

⏱️ Time: 10 Mins 👥 Pair Activity 🌐 Context: Language Exchange Café
⏱️ Session Timer
10:00
Phase 1: Prep & Card Selection (2 Mins)
🎭 Interactive Roles
Role A (The Welcomer / Host):
  • Greet Role B warmly (*"Good morning! Welcome!"*).
  • Introduce yourself (*"My name is..."*).
  • Ask Role B their name, origin, and job.
  • Say a friendly goodbye (*"Great to meet you!"*).
📋 Activity Schedule
  1. 0–2 Mins: Select a profile card & practice key letter/word sounds.
  2. 2–5 Mins: Role A interviews Role B at the Welcome Café.
  3. 5–8 Mins: Swap roles, pick a new card, repeat chat.
  4. 8–10 Mins: Class Showcase! Introduce your partner.
