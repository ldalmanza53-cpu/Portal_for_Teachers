📖 Verb "TO BE" Question Formula

Move AM / IS / ARE to the front of the sentence to ask a question!

💡 Formula: [Am / Is / Are] + [Subject] + [Rest of Sentence]?
Verb: AM 🙋‍♂️

Am I... ?

Subject: I (First Person)

Verb: IS 👨‍👩‍👧

Is He / She / It... ?

Subjects: He, She, It, Carlos, Sarah

Question Is he a doctor?
🔊
Affirmative (+) Yes, he is.
✅
Negative (-) No, he isn't.
❌
Verb: ARE 👥

Are You / We / They... ?

Subjects: You, We, They, Students

Question Are you a student?
🔊
Affirmative (+) Yes, I am.
✅
Negative (-) No, I'm not.
