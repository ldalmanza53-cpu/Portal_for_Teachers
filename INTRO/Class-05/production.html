🎒 Language School Lost & Found Office

Identifying Personal & Classroom Objects (A1 Speaking Activity)

⏱️ Time: 10 Mins 👥 Pair Roleplay 🔤 Grammar: A / An / Plurals
⏱️ Session Timer
10:00
Phase 1: Setup & Target Language Review (2 Mins)
🎭 Activity Roles
Role A (Lost & Found Clerk):
  • Greet the student (*"Hello! How can I help you today?"*).
  • Hold up items one by one (*"What is this?"* / *"What are these?"*).
  • Verify ownership (*"Is this your notebook?"*).
  • Click on items to mark them Claimed as they are correctly identified.
📋 Activity Breakdown
  1. 0–2 Mins: Pair up, choose roles, and practice scaffolded questions.
  2. 2–5 Mins: Round 1 — Clerk verifies Student B's lost items.
  3. 5–8 Mins: Swap Roles! Pick a new Student card & repeat dialogue.
  4. 8–10 Mins: Class Inventory Check — Report returned items to class!
🗣️ Helpful Language (Click to Listen) Practice Pronunciation
1. Clerk Questions
What is this? 🔊
What are these? 🔊
Is this your...? 🔊
Are these your...? 🔊
2. Student Identification
This is a [object]. 🔊
This is an [eraser/apple]. 🔊
These are my [plural]. 🔊
Yes, that's mine! 🔊
3. Grammar Helper
"a" + consonant sound: a pen, a chair, a ruler
"an" + vowel sound: an eraser, an umbrella
Plurals (no a/an): glasses, scissors, keys
