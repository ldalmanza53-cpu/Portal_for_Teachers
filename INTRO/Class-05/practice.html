Demonstratives & Plural -s Sounds

Singular (This / It's)

One Item

Demonstrative Statement

This is a laptop.

Question: What's this?

What's = What is

Answer: It's a flash drive.

It's = It is

Plural (These / They're)

Multiple Items

Demonstrative Statement

These are laptops.

Question: What are these?

Answer: They're flash drives.

They're = They are

Plural -s Pronunciation Rules

Click any word to hear how the ending "-s" or "-es" is pronounced in English!

3 Ending Sounds
/s/ Sound Voiceless ending

Unvoiced sound like a snake hissing /s/.

/z/ Sound Voiced ending

Vocal cords vibrate like a bee buzzing /z/.

/ɪz/ Sound Extra Syllable

Adds an extra syllable sound /ɪz/ after sibilants.
