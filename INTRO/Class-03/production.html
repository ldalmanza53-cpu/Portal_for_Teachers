📝 Community Club Registration Fair

Giving Contact Information & Registering Members (A1 Level)

⏱️ Time: 10 Mins 👥 Pair Dialogue 📋 Target: Phone, Email & Address
⏱️ Session Timer
10:00
Phase 1: Prep & Symbol Review (2 Mins)
🎭 Activity Roles
Role A (Registration Officer):
  • Greet the member and ask which club they want to join.
  • Ask for their phone number, email address, and home street address.
  • Confirm spelling of email symbols (`@`, `.`, `-`, `_`).
  • Ask for their preferred contact method (WhatsApp, Email, SMS).
📋 10-Minute Schedule
  1. 0–2 Mins: Choose a card & review email symbol pronunciation.
  2. 2–5 Mins: Dialogue 1 — Officer registers Member.
  3. 5–8 Mins: Swap roles, choose a new card & repeat.
  4. 8–10 Mins: Class Information Check — Verify contact cards!
