📐 The Workspace Setup

Describing Object Locations with Prepositions of Place (A1 Activity)

⏱️ Time: 10 Mins 👥 Pair Roleplay 📍 Prepositions of Place
⏱️ Session Timer
10:00
Phase 1: Setup & Preposition Review (2 Mins)
🎭 Activity Roles
Role A (The Organizer):
  • Holds the workspace plan for the chosen card.
  • Answers Role B's questions using prepositions (*"The laptop charger is **under** the notebook."*).
  • Checks off each item on screen as Role B identifies its spot!
📋 10-Minute Activity Steps
  1. 0–2 Mins: Pair up, review prepositions, and pick a workspace card.
  2. 2–5 Mins: Round 1 — Role B asks where items go; Role A describes locations.
  3. 5–8 Mins: Swap Roles! Pick a new workspace prompt and repeat.
  4. 8–10 Mins: Class Location Check — Share one object location with the class!
