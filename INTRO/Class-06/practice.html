Grammar Focus & Visual Rules

Grammar & Preposition Master Guide

Review singular vs. plural rules, demonstratives, and prepositions of place.

1

Singular Nouns (One Object)

It / This
Yes/No Question:

"Is this your wallet?"

✓ Short Answer: "Yes, it is." / "No, it's not."

Where Question:

"Where is my cell phone?"

✓ Statement: "It's in the box."

2+

Plural Nouns (Multiple / Two Parts)

They / These
Yes/No Question:

"Are these your keys / glasses?"

✓ Short Answer: "Yes, they are." / "No, they're not."

Where Question:

"Where are my headphones?"

✓ Statement: "They're on the desk."
