❓ Question Formula: Singular (Where IS...?)
Where is the + [Singular Object]?

• Where is the phone? $\rightarrow$ It is on the desk.
• Is the phone under the desk? $\rightarrow$ No, it isn't. It's on the desk.
❓ Question Formula: Plural (Where ARE...?)
Where are the + [Plural Objects]?

• Where are the keys? $\rightarrow$ They are in the box.
• Are the keys next to the box? $\rightarrow$ No, they aren't. They're in the box.
