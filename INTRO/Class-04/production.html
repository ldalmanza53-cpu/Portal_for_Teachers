Phase 1: Warm-up & Setup (2 Mins)
🎭 Activity Roles
Role A (Check-In Desk Clerk):
  • Greet the attendee and check their record.
  • Ask verification questions (*"Are you [Name]?"*, *"Are you from [Country]?"*).
  • Identify errors in your system record and click [Fix Record] when corrected.
  • Confirm: *"Okay, everything is correct now!"*
📋 Activity Breakdown
  1. 0–2 Mins: Pair up & review language scaffolding phrases.
  2. 2–5 Mins: Verification Round 1 (Clerk A checks Attendee B).
  3. 5–8 Mins: Swap Roles! Pick a new Attendee card & repeat.
  4. 8–10 Mins: Class Verification Check — Report attendee identity.
