Grammar Focus 14: Negative Statements & Questions

Learn how to form negative sentences using not and answer Yes/No questions using short answers with the verb be.

Negative Statements with be

I'm not from Rio.

We're not from China.
You're not early.

Contraction Rule: We're = We are

Yes/No Questions & Answers

Click row for Q&A

Are you from São Paulo?

Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Am I early?

Yes, you are. / No, you're not.

Is she from the U.S.?

Yes, she is. / No, she's not.

Is he from Mexico?

Yes, he is. / No, he's not.

Is it French?

Yes, it is. / No, it's not.

Are you from South Korea?

Yes, we are. / No, we're not.

Are they in Egypt?

Yes, they are. / No, they're not.

Notice: Short positive answers do not use contractions (*Yes, I am*, NOT *Yes, I'm*).
