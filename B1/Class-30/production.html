B1 Eco Solutions Toolkit

1. Describing Problems

• One major ecological issue is plastic accumulation.

• Deforestation leads to severe loss of biodiversity.

• Excessive emissions cause global temperatures to rise.

2. Giving Solutions (Modals)

• Governments must enforce renewable energy laws.

• Citizens ought to reduce household waste.

• Cities should invest in electric public transit.

• Companies need to transition to green packaging.

3. Conditionals for Action

• If we ban diesel cars, air quality will improve.

• If cities built more parks, urban heat would decrease.

Sample Dialogue Model

Specialist: "Single-use plastic is destroying marine ecosystems. Governments must enforce bans, and if we adopt reusable containers, ocean waste will drop."

Officer: "Excellent proposal! How should local stores encourage customers to bring their own bags?"
