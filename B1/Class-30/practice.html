Grammar Plus 10: Complete Reference Guide

In English B1 writing and speech, prepositions like by, as a result of, because of, through, and due to introduce the cause behind passive state changes.

Preposition Function Example Sentence
by Direct agent or cause The environment is being harmed by climate changes.
due to Reason / Attribute Our soil is being contaminated due to pesticide use.
through Means or process Infectious diseases have been spread through waste dumping.
because of Reason for effect Air quality has been affected because of deforestation.
as a result of Direct consequence Smog pollution has been reduced as a result of hybrid cars.
