• The firm launched the app in 2022.

• While engineers were testing, a crash occurred.

2. Evidence Predictions (Be Going To)

• Automation is bound to replace manual filing.

• Hybrid work is likely to remain popular.

• By 2030, scientists will have developed cleaner batteries.
