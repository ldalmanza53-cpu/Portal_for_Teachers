Preposition & Tense Reference Guide

Compare commonly confused past prepositions and future time markers with clear formulas and audio examples.

Future Points vs Periods

By vs. Within

By = deadline ("not later than a point in time").
Within = duration ("some time during a period").

✓ "By 2050, we will have eliminated starvation."
✓ "Within the next five years, people will have invented apps."
❌ Common Traps to Avoid:
• NOT: Within 2050 (2050 is a specific point, use by)
• NOT: By the next five years (five years is a period, use within)
Past Duration vs Start Point

Since vs. For

Since + specific starting point in time.
For + duration or total length of time.

✓ "The UN has been in existence since 1945."
✓ "The UN has been in existence for about 70 years."
Quick Test: 1980 is a starting point (use since). 34 years is a total duration (use for).
Specific Periods of Time

In vs. During

Use both in and during to refer to events taking place within a specific period, decade, or era.

✓ "Rock 'n' roll became popular in/during the 1950s."
✓ "The Beatles were popular in the 1960s."
Time Span & Elapsed Time

From ... To ... & Ago

From [Start] To [End] shows beginning and end points.
[Time] + Ago shows time back from present moment.

✓ "World War II lasted from 1939 to 1945."
✓ "I listened to their album a few days ago."
