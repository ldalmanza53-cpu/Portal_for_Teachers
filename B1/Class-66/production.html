B1 Social Opinion Toolkit

1. Social Stance Markers

• In my opinion, public health is vital.

• From my point of view, housing is a right.

• I personally feel that youth need centers.

✗ Never say "According to me". Use "In my view".

2. Agreement Syntax

• I agree with your point on transit.

• I completely agree that rent limits help.

✗ Avoid "I am agree". Say "I agree".

3. Respectful Disagreement

• I see your point, but taxes affect workers.

• I'm afraid I disagree because costs rise.

• That might be true, however elders need buses.

4. Social Causal Linkers

• Poverty increases due to high housing costs.

• Free sports lead to lower youth crime rates.

✗ Do not use "because" and "so" together.
