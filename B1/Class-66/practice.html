📜 1. Passive Modals Formula & Rules

Rules & Obligations

Use passive modals when you want to focus on the rule or action, rather than who performs it.

Standard Modals:
Modal + BE + Past Participle (V3)

• Cell phones should be turned off during exams.
• Food must not be eaten in the library.

"Ought To" Passive:
ought to + BE + Past Participle (V3)

• Students ought to be allowed to express opinions.
• Homework ought to be submitted on time.

Negative Passive Modals:
shouldn't + BE + V3 / ought not to + BE + V3

• Students shouldn't be required to wear uniforms.
• Rules ought not to be ignored.

🏷️ 2. Tag Question Polarity & Auxiliary Matrix

Tag Flip

Match the tag question auxiliary verb to the tense of the main clause, and flip the polarity (+ ➔ - or - ➔ +).

Present Simple (be):

Food is expensive, isn't it? / Supermarkets aren't cheap, are they?

Present Simple (action verbs):

Prices rise rapidly, don't they? / Stores don't lower prices, do they?

Present Continuous (is/are -ing):

Families are cutting back, aren't they?

Present Perfect (have/has V3):

Costs haven't stopped rising, have they?

⚠️ B1 Level Common Grammar Pitfalls & Traps

Pitfall #1: Confusing "Have" with "Are" in Continuous Tense
❌ Families are cutting back, haven't they?
✔️ Families are cutting back, aren't they?
Pitfall #2: Double Negative Tag Questions
❌ Stores don't lower prices, don't they?
✔️ Stores don't lower prices, do they?
Pitfall #3: Missing "BE" in Passive Modals
❌ Cell phones should banned in class.
✔️ Cell phones should be banned in class.
