B1 City Rules & Policy Toolkit

1. Stance & Opinion Markers

• In my view, downtown speed limits should be lower.

• From my point of view, public transit must be free.

• I personally feel that e-scooters are dangerous.

✗ Never say "According to me". Use "In my view".

2. Modals for City Rules

• Heavy trucks should be restricted at night.

• Cyclists should be allowed to use bus lanes.

• Pet owners should be required to keep leashes.

✗ Never say "should to be". Modals take bare verbs.

3. Polite Disagreement

• I see your point, but parking bans hurt local shops.

• I'm afraid I disagree because elders need cars.

• That might be true, however noise permits help.

4. Urban Impact Linkers

• Pollution decreases due to fewer diesel buses.

• Lower speed limits lead to safer pedestrian crossings.

✗ Do not combine "because" and "so" together.
