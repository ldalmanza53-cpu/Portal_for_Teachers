📖 B1 Grammar Matrix: Passive Modals for Rules & Regulations

Master modal strength variations, permission vs. obligation verbs, and avoid typical B1 level syntax traps.

⚡ 1. Strength of Feeling Scale (Modals of Regulation)

🔴 Absolute Obligation & Prohibition Strict Law
must be / mustn't be / have to be
  • Cyclists must be required to wear helmets by law.
  • Drivers mustn't be allowed to park in designated bike lanes.
  • Speed limits have to be obeyed in school zones.
🔵 Strong Recommendation / Policy Advisory
should be / ought to be / shouldn't be
  • Drivers should be required to yield to pedestrians.
  • Cyclists ought to be encouraged to use reflective gear.
  • Honking shouldn't be permitted near hospital zones.

⚖️ 2. Verb Nuance: Permissions vs Obligations

🟢 Allowed / Permitted (Permissions)

Use when giving or denying permission to perform an action.

✔️ Drivers shouldn't be permitted to exceed 30 km/h in urban zones.
🟣 Required / Obligated (Duties)

Use when making an action mandatory or standard duty.

✔️ Drivers should be required to signal before turning.

⚠️ Common B1 Grammar Traps & Pitfalls

Trap #1: Omitting "BE"
❌ Drivers should required to signal.
✔️ Drivers should be required to signal.
Trap #2: Double "TO" with Ought To
❌ Cyclists ought to be to wear helmets.
✔️ Cyclists ought to be required to wear helmets.
Trap #3: Active vs Passive Confusion
❌ Bike lanes shouldn't park in.
✔️ Drivers shouldn't be allowed to park in bike lanes.
