B1 Learning Methods Toolkit

1. Stating Learning Habits

• I learn best when I test myself directly.

• When learning a skill, I tend to break it down into sub-tasks.

• I find active recall more effective than passive reading.

2. Giving Recommendations

• To break through a plateau, you ought to change your routine.

• Learners should focus on deliberate practice micro-goals.

• It is essential to seek immediate feedback.

3. Cause, Purpose & Conditionals

• In order to retain vocabulary, spaced repetition is key.

• If you practice 20 minutes daily, your muscle memory will improve rapidly.

Sample Dialogue Model

Strategist: "I tend to forget new vocabulary quickly. To fix this, I ought to use spaced repetition flashcards instead of cramming before exams."

Coach: "That's a great strategy! If you practice active recall every morning, how much will your retention increase?"
