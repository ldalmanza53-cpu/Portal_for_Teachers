Describing Methods with "By + Gerund"

Use by + verb-ing to explain how to accomplish a goal or learn something effectively.

Question: How do you learn new words?
Answer: You can learn by writing them down.

B1 Grammar Rules: "By + Gerund"

1. Explaining "How" (Preposition + Gerund)

PREPOSITION
  • Structure: BY + verb-ING.
    "You learn idioms by watching American sitcoms."
  • Incorrect: by to watch or by watch.
    Always use the -ing gerund form directly after 'by'.

2. Expressing Goals & Methods

PATTERNS
  • Pattern A: The best way to [goal] is by [gerund].
    "The best way to practice is by sending text messages."
  • Pattern B: You can [goal] by [gerund].
    "You can improve your accent by singing along to songs."
