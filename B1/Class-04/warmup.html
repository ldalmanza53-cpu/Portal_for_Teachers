Permanent

Grammar Spotlight: -ed vs -ing Adjectives

1. Adjectives with -ed

Describes how a person feels inside.

"I am bored because the class is long."
2. Adjectives with -ing

Describes the thing or cause that produces the feeling.

"The movie is boring, so I am bored."
