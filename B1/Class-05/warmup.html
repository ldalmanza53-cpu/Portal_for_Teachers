Expressing Pros

Advantages

  • "On the plus side, working as a [Job] offers..."

Expressing Cons

Drawbacks
  • "A significant drawback to working as a..."
  • "On the downside, [Job]s often suffer from..."
  • "One main disadvantage of this role is..."

Contrast Connectors

Linking Words
  • However: "High salary. However, hours are long."
  • Whereas: "Nurses help people, whereas devs code."
  • Although: "Although stressful, it pays well."
