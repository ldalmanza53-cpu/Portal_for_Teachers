B1 Enviro Language Toolkit

1. Present Passive Voice (Impact Focus)

• Millions of tons of waste are dumped into oceans.

• Rainforests are being cleared for commercial agriculture.

• Toxic gases are released by industrial facilities.

• Groundwater reserves are being depleted by over-farming.

2. Cause & Effect Connectors

• Air pollution is caused by / stems from vehicle exhausts.

• Agricultural runoff leads to / results in dead zones.

• Habitat fragmentation accelerates species extinction.

3. Impact & Severity Verbs

• Extreme heatwaves threaten local food security.

• Chemical spills contaminate freshwater drinking supplies.

• Coral bleaching destroys essential marine shelters.

Sample Dialogue Model

Researcher: "Rainforests are being cleared for cattle ranching. Losing trees leads to severe loss of carbon sinks and accelerates climate change."

Inspector: "I see. How severely does this habitat loss threaten local wildlife species?"
