B1 Grammar Structures

Causative & Passive Service Grammar

Learn how native speakers express that someone else is performing a task for them using HAVE / GET.

The Causative Formula

Standard / Formal
Subject + HAVE / GET + Object + Past Participle (V3) + by Agent + at Location
HAVE vs GET: "Have" is neutral/formal. "Get" is common in everyday spoken English.
Agent (by): Optional, specifies who performs the service (e.g., by a mechanic).
Location (at/in): Specifies where the service takes place (e.g., at the salon).

Active vs. Causative Comparison

Active Sentence (I do it myself)

"I washed my car at home."

Causative Sentence (A service performed for me)

"I had my car washed by a car cleaner at the garage."
