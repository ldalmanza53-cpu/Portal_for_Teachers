The 4 Levels of Request Formality

Choosing the right level of formality depends on who you are speaking to and how big the favor is (imposition).

Level 1

Informal

Friends, close family, small favors, relaxed settings.

Formulas:

• Can you + [verb]?

• Will you + [verb]?

• Pass me the... / Give me...

"Can you pass the salt?"
Level 2

Semi-Formal

Coworkers, acquaintances, everyday polite requests.

Formulas:

• Could you + [verb]?

• Would you + [verb]?

• Is it OK if I + [verb]?

"Could you send me that document when you get a chance?"
Level 3

Formal

Bosses, teachers, strangers, medium-to-large favors.

Formulas:

• Would you mind + [verb]-ing?

• Would it be possible to + [verb]?

• Do you mind if I + [verb]?

"Would you mind checking my work when you have a moment?"
Level 4

Highly Formal

VIPs, executives, major favors, delicate situations.

Formulas:

• I was wondering if you could...

• I would be extremely grateful if...

• Would you be so kind as to...

"I was wondering if you could grant me a brief extension on the project deadline."
⚠️

Grammar Spotlight: The "Would You Mind...?" Response Trap

"Would you mind...?" literally asks: "Does this bother or inconvenience you?"

✅ AGREEING to do the favor: Say "NO"

To AGREE, answer in the negative because you do NOT mind helping!

• "No, not at all!"

• "Not at all, I'd be happy to."

• "Of course not, here you go."

❌ REFUSING to do the favor: Say "YES" + Reason

Saying "Yes" means "Yes, I DO mind!" (It bothers me). It sounds rude unless softened with an apology.

• "Actually, I'm a bit busy right now..."

• "I'm afraid I do, I'm expecting a call."

• "Yes, sure!" ❌ (Grammatically contradictory!)
