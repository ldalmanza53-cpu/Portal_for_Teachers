¡Atención Hispanohablantes! Spanish vs. English Traps
1. Answering "Would you mind...?"

In Spanish "¿Te importaría...?" you answer "Sí" to accept. In English, "mind" means "object to". So to ACCEPT, say: "No, not at all." or "No, I don't mind."

2. Past Simple in "if" Clauses

Patterns like "Would you mind if I..." or "Would it be all right if I..." require PAST SIMPLE (unreal hypothetical): "borrowed", "used", "stayed".

3. Gerund vs Base Verb

Notice: "Would you mind helping..." uses a gerund (-ing). But "Could you help..." and "I was wondering if you could help..." use base verbs.
