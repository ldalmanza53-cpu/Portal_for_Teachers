📚 Formality Register Reference Matrix

Master the structure, target rules, and softening modifiers across workplace registers.

🔍
Tier 1: Informal Peers / Friends
  • Can you + V1...? "Can you grab me a coffee on your way back?"
  • Do you mind + V-ing...? "Do you mind holding the elevator for a second?"
  • Could you give me a hand with...? "Could you give me a hand with this laptop cable?"
Tier 2: Neutral / Polite Colleagues / Team Leads
  • Could you please + V1...? "Could you please share the Q3 presentation slides?"
  • I was wondering if you could + V1... "I was wondering if you could grant a short extension."
  • Would it be possible to + V1...? "Would it be possible to reschedule our 2 PM check-in?"
Tier 3: High Formal Executives / VIP Clients
  • I would be extremely grateful if you could... "I would be extremely grateful if you could approve the budget."
  • Would you be so kind as to + V1...? "Would you be so kind as to provide a recommendation letter?"
  • May I respectfully request that... "May I respectfully request a brief review of this proposal?"
Softening Modifiers Diplomatic Shield
  • possibly / by any chance "Could you possibly check this formula by any chance?"
  • when you have a moment / quick favor "Do you mind taking a quick look when you have a moment?"
  • at your earliest convenience "Please forward the executed contract at your earliest convenience."
