B1-B2 Challenge & Resilience Toolkit

1. Framing the Challenge

• We faced a daunting obstacle. (✗ no 'faced to')

• The team was confronted with systemic friction.

• She dealt with the crisis effectively. (✗ no 'dealed')

2. Explaining Causes & Origins

• The issue stems from poor communication.

• The bottleneck was brought about by staff shortages.

• This friction resulted in project delays.

3. Describing Adaptation

• We had to pivot our strategy completely.

• They managed to tackle the issue head-on.

• I worked hard to overcome the barrier. (✗ no 'from')

4. Key Challenge Terminology

• Bottleneck / Hurdle: An obstacle causing friction.

• Daunting / Formidable: Intimidatingly difficult.

• Mitigate: Make less severe or reduce risk.
