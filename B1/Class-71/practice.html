⚖️ 1. Complex Noun Header Preposition Rules

Group A: Nouns taking OF Preposition OF

Nouns that express parts, outcomes, or intrinsic characteristics require the preposition OF + Gerund / Noun:

• The most challenging part OF running a marathon...
• One rewards OF learning a new language...
• The most dangerous part OF mountain climbing...
• The easiest part OF starting a project...

❌ Incorrect: "The most challenging part about running a marathon..."

Group B: "Thing" taking ABOUT Preposition ABOUT

When using superlative adjective modifiers with the noun header thing, you MUST use the preposition ABOUT + Gerund / Noun:

• The most surprising thing ABOUT moving abroad...
• The most exciting thing ABOUT skydiving...
• The scariest thing ABOUT public speaking...
• The funniest thing ABOUT learning to cook...

❌ Incorrect: "The scariest thing of public speaking..."

🧩 2. Complete Sentence Predicate Architecture

Complex Noun Phrase Subject
"The scariest thing about public speaking"
Linking Verb
WAS / IS
Gerund Predicate Phrase
"stepping onto the stage."

💡 Key takeaway: The predicate following was should begin with a Gerund (`verb-ing`), NOT an infinitive (`to step`) or a base verb (`step`).

⚠️ 3. B1 Level Pitfall Spotlight (Click cards to flip)

Trap 1: Preposition Error

Using "about" with noun header "part".

❌ The best part about the trip...
Click to reveal correction
✓ Correct Structure
✓ The best part OF the trip...

Nouns like part, rewards, aspects, difficulties strictly require OF.

Formula: PART + OF
Trap 2: "Thing" Preposition

Using "of" with noun header "thing".

❌ The scariest thing of skydiving...
Click to reveal correction
✓ Correct Structure
✓ The scariest thing ABOUT skydiving...

Superlative noun header thing (scariest thing, best thing) requires ABOUT.

Formula: THING + ABOUT
Trap 3: Infinitive Predicate

Using infinitive after verb "was".

❌ ...was to step onto stage.
Click to reveal correction
✓ Correct Structure
✓ ...was stepping onto the stage.

In VRB-114 complex noun phrases, the predicate verb following was MUST be a gerund (`-ing`).

Formula: WAS + VERB-ING
Trap 4: Base Verb Preposition

Using base verb after preposition of.

❌ Rewards of learn a language...
Click to reveal correction
✓ Correct Structure
✓ Rewards of learning a language...

Any verb following a preposition (of, about) must take the -ing gerund form.

Formula: PREPOSITION + GERUND
