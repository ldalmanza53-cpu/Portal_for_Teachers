B1-B2 Opinion & Reason Toolkit

1. Accurate Stance Markers

• In my view, this policy needs revision.

• From my perspective, remote work builds trust.

• To my mind, the pricing model is too high.

✗ Avoid "In my point of view" or "In my opinion, I think".

2. Clause vs Preposition Linkers

• because + clause: "...because it saves time."

• because of + noun: "...because of time constraints."

• given that + clause: "Given that market costs rose..."

3. Noun Frame Justification

• The main reason why [X] is that [Y].

e.g., "The main reason why sales dropped is that prices increased."

✗ Never say "The reason why... is because".

4. Citing External Sources

• According to recent market research...

✗ Never use "According to me" for your own opinion.
