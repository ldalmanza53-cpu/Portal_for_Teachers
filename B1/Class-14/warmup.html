Compelling past stories rely on contrasting what was expected against what actually happened. Combine Past Perfect (background setup), Past Continuous (ongoing context), and Past Simple (main event) with precise contrast linkers.

Contrast Connectors Blueprint & Common Traps

Group 1

Concession Connectors

Show unexpected outcomes despite prior facts or expectations.

Although / Even though + Subject + Verb
Despite / In spite of + Noun / Verb-ING

Common A2-B1 Trap:

❌ Despite of the rain... (NEVER put 'of' after Despite!)

❌ Although the rain... (Needs a full clause: 'Although it rained...')

Group 2

Parallel Contrast

Compare two simultaneous past situations or differing habits.

While / Whereas + Subject + Verb
Ideal for Past Continuous comparisons between two people or places.

Example:

"While my sister was relaxed, I was sweating profusely."

Group 3

Transition Adverbs

Connect two independent sentences with a punctuated shift.

Sentence 1. However, / In contrast, Sentence 2.
Sentence 1; on the other hand, Sentence 2.

Punctuation Rule:

Always place a comma immediately after However, In contrast, or On the other hand,!
