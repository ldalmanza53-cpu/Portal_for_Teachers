B1 Narrative Tenses & Stative Verb Matrix

Compare grammar rules, formulas, time conjunction triggers, and stative verb restrictions.

PAST SIMPLE V2 / -ed

Completed Main Actions

Recounts completed actions that occurred at a specific moment or in a finished time frame.

Trigger Signal Words:

when, yesterday, suddenly, in 2023, then, as soon as

"The fire alarm rang while we were in the lobby."
PAST CONTINUOUS was/were + V-ing

Ongoing Background Action

Sets the scene or describes an action in progress when another shorter event interrupted it.

Trigger Signal Words:

while, as, at 9 PM, simultaneously, during

"While I was presenting the report, the screen went black."
PAST PERFECT had + V3 (participle)

Prior Action in Past

Shows an action completed *before* another past moment or past simple action.

Trigger Signal Words:

by the time, already, before, after, had realized

"By the time we reached gate 4, the plane had taken off."
USED TO + BASE used to + verb

Past Habits & States

Contrasts past repeated routines or states that are no longer true in the present.

Trigger Signal Words:

used to, former, but now, previously, no longer

"I used to commute by subway, but now I work remotely."

Crucial B1 Rule: Stative Verbs In Continuous Tenses

Verbs describing states, feelings, or mental processes (know, understand, believe, realize, belong, need, want) are NEVER used in Past Continuous!

I was knowing the answer
✓ I knew the answer
