CD 2:2 Grammar Focus

Passive Voice with Cause Prepositions

When we describe environmental problems, we often focus on the action happening to the environment (Passive Voice) and connect it to its cause using prepositions.

1

Present Continuous Passive

is / are being + Past Participle

Used for environmental damage that is currently happening right now.

Our water supply is being contaminated

by toxic chemicals.

Our city streets are being damaged

as a result of heavy traffic.

The roads aren't being repaired

due to a lack of funding.

2

Present Perfect Passive

has / have been + Past Participle

Used for events that started in the past or have completed with present results.

Our community center has been closed

because of high costs.

Many public parks have been lost

through overbuilding.

Underground water has been contaminated

because of livestock farming.

Cause Prepositions Breakdown

by

Direct agent or cause performing the action.

e.g. caused by carbon dioxide

as a result of

Direct consequence of a major process.

e.g. accelerated as a result of deforestation

due to

Reason or origin for the state or problem.

e.g. threatened due to water pollution

because of

Reason followed by a noun or noun phrase.

e.g. polluted because of industrial waste

through

Means or method by which something happens.

e.g. depleted through rapid urbanization
