Aspect & Job Comparison Matrix

Aspect Linkers

in terms of + [Noun Phrase]

"In terms of schedule flexibility, software development is superior to nursing."

• when it comes to + [Noun]: "When it comes to salary, finance wins."

• as far as [Noun] is concerned: "As far as stress is concerned, accounting is calmer."

Modified Comparatives

far / much + [Comparative Adj] + than

"Investment banking is far better paid than high school teaching."

• slightly less + [Adj] + than: "Architecture is slightly less rigid than civil engineering."

• much more demanding than: "Emergency medicine is much more demanding."

Equative Formations

as + [Adjective] + as

"A senior designer position is as fulfilling as a non-profit role."

• not as [Adj] as: "On-site nursing is not as flexible as remote writing."

Pros & Cons Linkers

While [Job A] offers [Pro], [Job B] entails [Con].

"While sales management offers high bonuses, it entails high targets."

• the advantage of [X] far outweighs: "The stability far outweighs the long hours."
