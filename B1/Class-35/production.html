B1 Learning Methods Toolkit

1. Explaining Cognitive Mechanisms

• Active recall forces your brain to retrieve information.

• Spaced repetition interrupts the forgetting curve.

• Interleaving improves strategy selection during tests.

2. Comparing Method Efficiency

• While re-reading is comfortable, self-testing is much more effective.

• Flashcards offer quick recall, whereas mind maps build big-picture context.

3. Recommendations & Conditionals

• Students must use active testing before major exams.

• You ought to break down complex skills into sub-tasks.

• If you use spaced reviews, your long-term memory will improve.

Sample Dialogue Model

Consultant: "Active recall forces your brain to retrieve knowledge without looking at notes. While re-reading feels easier, active recall builds stronger neural pathways."

Auditor: "That sounds effective! How often should a student schedule these active testing sessions?"
