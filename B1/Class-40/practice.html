Grammar Rules: Expressions for Suggestions


Expression Required Verb Form Example Sentence
Have you thought about...? Gerund (verb-ing) Have you thought about using an app?
What about...? Gerund (verb-ing) What about taking a walk during lunch?
Maybe you could... Base Verb Maybe you could talk to your manager.
Why don't you...? Base Verb Why don't you turn off notifications?
It might be a good idea to... Base Verb (with to) It might be a good idea to read earlier.
One thing you could do is... Base Verb / Infinitive One thing you could do is schedule study time.
