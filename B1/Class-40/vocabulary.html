B1 Language Focus

Stress Expressions Usage

  • • stressed out = Adjective describing a state of severe worry or anxiety.
  • • under pressure = Prepositional phrase: under strain/demands from work or authority.
  • • my stomach was in knots = Idiom for physical tightness caused by nerves.
  • • blow off steam = Metaphorical idiom (like a steam engine) for releasing tension.
