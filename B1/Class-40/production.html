B1 Suggestions Toolkit

1. Formulating Suggestions

• Why don't we try organizing a weekly standup?

• How about introducing a digital suggestion box?

• What if we reduced meeting lengths by half?

2. Modals for Ideas & Proposals

• We could consider switching to paperless billing.

• I suggest that we should hire a local consultant.

• It might be a good idea to offer flexible hours.

3. Evaluating & Responding

• That sounds like a practical choice because it saves money.

• I see your point, but we ought to consider the timeline.

• This suggestion would allow us to improve team trust.

Sample Dialogue Model

Lead: "Why don't we try using an online project board? How about eliminating daily status emails? This would allow the team to focus on deep work."

Evaluator: "That sounds effective! How will you ensure everyone updates the board regularly?"
