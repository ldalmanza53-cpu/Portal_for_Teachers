B1 Improvement Toolkit

1. Feelings & Initial Expectations

• I was expecting the service to be much better.

• I felt disappointed / frustrated when...

• I was under the impression that it would be included.

• I was hoping for a smooth experience, but...

2. Stating Problems & Complaints

• The main problem was that...

• I would like to complain about the organization.

• There was a serious issue with the schedule.

• It failed to live up to its promise because...

3. Discussing Improvements

• The process needs to be improved.

• What needs to be done is retraining the staff.

• They should focus on improving communication.

• Management ought to upgrade the facilities.

Sample Dialogue Model

Client: "I was expecting a quiet resort, but I felt extremely frustrated when construction started at 6:00 AM."

Auditor: "I completely understand your disappointment. The booking policy needs to be improved, and guests ought to be warned before arriving."
