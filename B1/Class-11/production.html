📚 Direct vs Indirect Request Reference Matrix

Compare direct imperatives with polite indirect structures across airport hospitality situations.

🔍
Direct Requests (Imperatives) Too Abrupt
  • Give me the Wi-Fi code. Imperative format sounds like an order to staff.
  • I want a quiet desk now. Direct statement of desire lacks politeness buffer.
  • Move your bag. Direct command to fellow passenger causes friction.
Indirect Requests (Polite B1) Recommended
  • Could you please tell me the Wi-Fi code? Modals (`Could / Would`) soften the request structure.
  • I was wondering if there is a quiet desk available? Past continuous introductory phrase adds distance.
  • Would you mind moving your bag slightly? Requires gerund verb form (`moving`).
Politeness Softeners Diplomatic Buffer
  • possibly / by any chance "Could you possibly check if flight BA-102 is delayed?"
  • when you have a moment / if time permits "Would you mind bringing an extra pillow when you have a moment?"
  • at your earliest convenience "Please confirm my gate change at your earliest convenience."
