Connector Syntax Cheat Sheet

Use this reference to select the right connector depending on whether a full clause or a noun phrase follows.


Connector Grammar Pattern Example Sentence
`for` + Noun Phrase / Gerund (`-ing`) Known for introducing new products.
`because` / `since` + Clause (`Subject + Verb`) Popular because customers know what to expect.
`because of` / `due to` + Noun Phrase Difficult to get into due to the crowds.
`The reason (that/why)` + Clause + `is (that)` The reason people go there is to be seen.
