Deep Past

Past Perfect

Marks an event completed BEFORE another past event or timestamp.

Formula: had + Past Participle (V3)

Example: "The train had already left when I arrived."

Background Context

Past Continuous

Sets the scene or describes an action IN PROGRESS in the background.

Formula: was / were + Verb-ING

Example: "Heavy rain was pouring outside..."

Main Interruption

Past Simple

Marks completed main events, sudden interruptions, or narrative steps.

Formula: Regular Verb-ed / Irregular V2

Example: "Suddenly, the lights went out!"
