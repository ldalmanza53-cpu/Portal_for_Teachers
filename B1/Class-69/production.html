B1-B2 Future Trajectory Toolkit

1. Future Perfect Accomplishments

• will have + V3: "By 2030, I will have launched my firm."

• "By December, we will have completed the trial."

✓ Completed prior to a future deadline.

2. Future Continuous Trajectory

• will be + V-ing: "In 5 years, I will be managing a team."

• "This time next year, we will be studying in Tokyo."

✓ In progress at a specific future moment.

3. Time Clauses & Expressions

• "By the time I turn 30..." (Present Simple!)

• "In three years' time..." (Apostrophe required!)

• "Within the next decade..."

✗ Never say "by the time I will arrive".

4. Trajectory Vocabulary

• Roadmap: Strategic plan outlining future steps.

• Horizon: Future point where long-term goals lie.

• Aspiration: A strong ambition or desired milestone.
