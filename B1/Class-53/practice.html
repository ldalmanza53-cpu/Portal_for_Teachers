Infinitive vs Direct Clause
With "In order to"

"In order to make money, you have to charge enough money to cover your costs."

Directly expresses goal using base verb make.

Shortened "To" (Direct Infinitive)

"To make money, you have to charge enough money to cover your costs."

Natural spoken English often drops in order at the start!

Pitfall 1: "For + Infinitive"

❌ For to open a café, get money...
✅ In order to open a café... / To open...

Pitfall 2: "Because of + S + V"

❌ We opened because of we love coffee...
✅ because we love coffee...

Pitfall 3: Missing 'For'

❌ In order the café to be legal...
✅ In order for the café to be legal...
