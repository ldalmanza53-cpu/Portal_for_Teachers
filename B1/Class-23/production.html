B1 Everyday Object Language Toolkit

1. Describing Current States (Adjective)

Formula: [Object] + is + [Adjective]

is broken (laptop, toy)
is cracked (screen, mug)
is jammed (zipper, drawer)
is leaking (tap, pipe)
is frayed (cable, wire)
is wobbly (chair leg, table)

2. Stating Noun Defects

Formula: There is a [Defect] on/in the [Object]

"There is a crack on my phone screen."
"There is a leak under the kitchen sink."
"There is a tear along the jacket seam."

3. Recurring Malfunctions

Formula: [Object] + keeps + [Verb-ing]

"The Wi-Fi router keeps disconnecting."
"The kitchen faucet keeps dripping."
"The bedroom door hinge keeps squeaking."

4. Expressing Repair Needs

Formula A: needs to be + [Past Participle]
Formula B: needs + [Verb-ing]

"The cable needs to be replaced."
"The chair screw needs tightening."
"The knife blade needs sharpening."

5. Sample Roleplay Dialogue

Customer: "Hello! My charging cable is frayed and there is a tear in the coating. It keeps disconnecting."

Technician: "I see. How long has it been disconnecting? Does the wire get warm?"

Customer: "It gets hot when touched. It needs to be replaced."

Technician: "Understood! I will issue a repair dispatch voucher now."
