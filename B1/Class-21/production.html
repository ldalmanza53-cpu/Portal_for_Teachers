B1 Cultural Toolkit

1. Previous Knowledge & Assumptions

• I used to think that...

• I was under the impression that...

• Before moving away, I assumed that...

• My understanding was that people...

2. Reality Validation & Contrast

• In reality, however...

• It turns out that...

• Contrary to popular belief...

• Unlike what I originally thought, ...

3. Cultural Norms & Expectations

• You are expected to + [verb]

• You are (not) supposed to + [verb]

• It is considered polite/impolite to + [verb]

• It's standard practice to + [verb]

Sample Dialogue Model

Expat: "Before I moved, I was under the impression that tipping 20% was mandatory in every restaurant."

Inspector: "That's a common belief! In reality, in many European countries, you are only expected to leave small change if the service was great."
