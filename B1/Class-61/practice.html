🎬 2. Defining vs. Non-Defining Relative Clauses

Feature Defining Relative Clause Non-Defining Relative Clause
Information Type Essential information required to specify who or what. Extra / Parenthetical information. The sentence stands on its own without it.
Punctuation NO COMMAS ❌ COMMAS REQUIRED ( , ... , ) ✔️
Allowed Pronouns who / that / which who / which ONLY (NEVER use 'that')
Interchange SB-217 Example A screenwriter is a talented person who/that develops a script. Movie directors, who "make or break" a film, are respected.

⚠️ B1 Trap #1: Comma + 'That' Illegal Combination

In non-defining relative clauses (separated by commas), you can never use the relative pronoun 'that'. You must use who for people or which for things.

❌ "Casting directors, that choose actors, are vital."
✔️ "Casting directors, who choose actors, are vital."

⚠️ B1 Trap #2: Missing 'BE' Auxiliary in Process Passive

A common mistake when forming process passives with modals is forgetting the base verb be before the past participle.

❌ "First, a business plan has to written."
✔️ "First, a business plan has to be written."
