B1 Language Scaffolding Toolkit

Modals of Advice

Use to give recommendations, warnings, or personal suggestions.

• "You should definitely bring cash."
• "You ought to greet the host first."
• "You had better not be late for meetings."
Cultural Expectations & Customs
be expected / supposed to + verb: Social rules.
"You are expected to remove your shoes at the entrance."
It's custom / polite to + verb: Common etiquette.
"It's considered rude to leave food on your plate."
Permission & Taboos
• "You are allowed to ask for the bill."
• "It is strictly forbidden to take photos."
• "You must not interrupt senior officers."
Making Cultural Comparisons
• "Unlike back home, people here tip generously."
• "In contrast to my city, transport is silent."

Activity Guide & Context (10 Minutes)

Welcome to CultureWise Protocol! In this 10-minute B1 pair speaking activity, you will roleplay as an Expat Cultural Advisor and a Relocating Client to prepare for moving abroad.

Phase 1 (0:00 - 1:30): Intake & Prompt Selection

Click 'Draw Random Pair Prompts' to assign scenarios to Student A and Student B.

Phase 2 (1:30 - 5:00): Round 1 Briefing

Student A acts as Cultural Advisor and briefs Client B. Student B audits grammar and asks follow-up probes.

Phase 3 (5:00 - 8:30): Round 2 Role Swap

Swap roles! Student B acts as Cultural Advisor and briefs Client A.

Phase 4 (8:30 - 10:00): Cultural Clearance

Review scores, generate the Cultural Clearance Certificate, and share top advice!
