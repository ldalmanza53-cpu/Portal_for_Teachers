B1-B2 Knowledge Validation Toolkit

1. Past Deductions & Speculation

• They must have left early (high certainty).

• He couldn't / can't have accessed the vault.

✗ Never use "mustn't have" for logical deduction.

2. Stance Markers & Judgements

• From my perspective, the policy failed.

• In my view, this decision was prudent.

✗ Avoid "According to me" or "In my point of view".

3. Clause vs. Preposition Reasons

• given that + clause: "...given that sales dropped."

• owing to + noun: "...owing to high churn."

4. Noun Frame Justification

• The main reason why [X] is that [Y].

✗ Avoid "The reason why... is because".
