⚖️ Comparative Grammar: "Should Have" vs "Would Have"

Should (not) have + V3 Judgment / Criticism

Expresses what was expected or morally correct. Used to criticize oneself or others for a past mistake or omitted duty.

• "Sue should have put it on her calendar."

• "He shouldn't have gone over the speed limit."

Would (not) have + V3 Hypothetical Advice

Expresses personal reaction in a hypothetical scenario. Means: "If I had been in that person's shoes, I would have done this..."

• "I wouldn't have lent her money."

• "I would have cooked something simple."

⚠️ 3 Common Past Modal Traps To Avoid

1. Phonetic Fallacy ("of")

❌ "I should of called."

✔️ "I should have called."

2. Incorrect Verb Form

❌ "He must have forgot."

✔️ "He must have forgotten." (Needs V3)

3. Unnecessary "to"

❌ "She could to have left."

✔️ "She could have left."
