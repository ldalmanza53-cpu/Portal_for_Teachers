Advanced Discourse Functions

Target Language Matrix (B2–C1)

Explore high-level expressions for explaining, speculating with precision, and rendering formal judgements.

1. Explanations & Reasons

due to / on account of + Noun Phrase

"The loss occurred due to poor internal oversight."

owing to the fact that...

"Owing to the fact that servers crashed, logs were lost."

the underlying rationale is...

"The underlying rationale was to mitigate financial risk."

in light of recent evidence...

"In light of recent evidence, we must reconsider."

2. Speculations (Certainty)

must have + Past Participle (90%+ Certain)

"The engineer must have discovered an ethical flaw."

might well be / could conceivably (50% Certain)

"They could conceivably be concealing a breach."

presumably / bound to have...

"Management is bound to have known the consequences."

there is every likelihood that...

"There is every likelihood that data was leaked."

3. Judgements & Evaluations

highly ill-advised / completely unjustified

"It was highly ill-advised to suppress the audit."

in hindsight, it represented a...

"In hindsight, it represented a critical oversight."

fully justifiable given the context

"The executive action was fully justifiable."

amounted to serious negligence

"Their failure to report amounted to negligence."
