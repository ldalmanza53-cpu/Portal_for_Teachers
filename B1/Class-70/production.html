B1-B2 Resilience Toolkit

1. Accomplishments (Present Perfect)

• have managed to + V: "I have managed to secure funding."

• succeeded in + V-ing: "She has succeeded in launching her clinic."

✓ Use Present Perfect when time is unstated and relevant now.

2. Sustained Effort (Present Perfect Cont.)

• FOR + duration: "...working for five months."

• SINCE + starting point: "...striving since last year."

✓ Focuses on ongoing labor through frustration.

3. Concessive Linkers (Frustration vs Reward)

• Despite + Gerund/Noun: "Despite facing delays..."

• In spite of + Gerund/Noun: "In spite of the grueling pace..."

• Although + Subject + Verb: "Although it was hard..."

4. Critical Diagnostic Traps

✗ "succeeded to do" → ✓ succeeded in doing

✗ "Although... but..." → ✓ Use only one!

✗ "Despite of..." → ✓ Despite (no 'of')

✗ "a rewarded job" → ✓ a rewarding job
