Textbook Model Conversation (Ex 3A) 4 Speakers (A, B, C, D)
A: What job would you like to have?
B: I'd like to be a flight attendant.
C: What would be the good points?
B: Well, traveling around the world would be exciting.
D: Would there be any bad points?
B: Oh, sure. I'd dislike packing and unpacking all the time.
Notice gerund (-ing) forms for job duties!

Grammar & Language Reference

Gerund Patterns & Job Comparisons

Master B1 structures: Gerunds after verbs and prepositions, plus job comparisons (better hours/pay, not as... as, would rather).

¡Atención Hispanohablantes! Spanish vs. English Traps
  • Preposición + Gerundio: En español dices "bueno para trabajar" (infinitivo). En inglés DEBES usar gerundio tras preposición: "good at working with people", "interested in traveling".
  • Dislike + Gerundio: En español dices "No me gustaría empacar". En inglés usas: "I'd dislike packing" o "I wouldn't enjoy packing".
  • Estructura "Not as... as": Para comparar empleos dices "It's not as well-paid as..." (No es tan bien pagado como...).
  • Would rather: Decir "I'd rather have..." (Preferiría tener) va seguido de verbo en forma base (sin 'to').
