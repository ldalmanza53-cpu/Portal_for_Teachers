B1 Core Target Structures Overview

Master the three key grammar pillars of this unit: relative clauses for people, It + when clauses for situations, and aspect prepositional frames for job comparisons.

👤 Target 1: People

Relative Clauses (who / that)

Formula:
Subject + Verb + people / colleagues + who / that + Verb
Expressing Likes:

"I really like colleagues who are supportive and punctual."

Expressing Dislikes:

"I can't stand people who always interrupt others."

⏳ Target 2: Situations

"It + when" Clauses

Formula:
Subject + Verb + IT + WHEN + Situation Clause
Expressing Likes:

"I love it when managers acknowledge good work."

Expressing Dislikes:

"It bothers me when meetings start late without notice."

💼 Target 3: Job Aspects

Aspect Frames & Contrast Connectors

Key Prepositions & Linkers:
In terms of... / When it comes to... / As far as... is concerned
Aspect Comparison:

"In terms of salary, finance pays well; whereas teaching offers better hours."

Security vs Flexibility:

"When it comes to security, healthcare is reliable; however, tech has more flexibility."
