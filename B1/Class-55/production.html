B1-B2 Deductions & Speculation Toolkit

1. Strong Positive Deduction

• They must have left early; the office is dark.

• The file must have been deleted by an admin.

2. Strong Negative Deduction (Impossibility)

• He can't have taken the money; he was absent.

• They couldn't have breached the safe without keys.

✗ Avoid "mustn't have" for negative certainty.

3. Speculation & Uncertainty

• The shipment might have been delayed in customs.

• They could have missed the connecting flight.

4. Offering Explanations

• This explains why client churn spiked in Q2.

• The drop occurred as a result of technical bugs.

• Considering that log files were wiped, we suspect a breach.
