Speculation & Evidence Matrix

Master the 4 core pillars of past deductions and forensic explanations: High Positive Certainty, Impossibility, Moderate Possibility, and Evidence Linkers.

1. High Positive Certainty
must have + V3

Strong logically necessary past conclusion based on solid evidence.

2. Impossibility / Negative
can't / couldn't have + V3

Logically impossible past action. Never use 'must not have' for impossibility!

3. Low / Moderate Possibility
might / may / could have + V3

Plausible past scenario when evidence is incomplete or uncertain.

4. Evidence Anchors
Judging by..., Given that...

Formal introductory clauses connecting observable data to deductions.
