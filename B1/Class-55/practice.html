⚠️ Common B1 Trap #1: Avoid "mustn't have" for deduction

Use "couldn't have" or "must not have" instead!

❌ Incorrect: "He mustn't have heard the news." (In standard American/B1 English, mustn't have is rarely used for logical impossibility).
✅ Correct: "He couldn't have heard the news" or "He must not have heard the news."

⚠️ Common B1 Trap #2: Forgetting Past Participle (V3)

Always use Past Participle after HAVE!

❌ Incorrect: "She must have get fired" or "He might have work late."
✅ Correct: "She must have gotten fired" / "He might have worked late."
