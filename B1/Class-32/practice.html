Grammar Rule Key

Expressing Preferences in B1 English

Use would rather + base verb (no "to") or would prefer + infinitive (to + verb).

Rather: I'd rather take an art course.
Prefer: I'd prefer to take an art course.
Compare: ...rather [verb] THAN [verb]

B1 Preference Rules Summary & Reference

1. Would Rather (Bare Infinitive)

NO "TO"
  • Positive: Subject + would rather + base verb.
    "I'd rather get a new job."
  • Negative: Subject + would rather not + base verb.
    "I'd rather not go to Australia."
  • Comparison: ...rather [verb A] THAN [verb B].
    "I'd rather take a class than hire a tutor."

2. Would Prefer (Full Infinitive)

REQUIRES "TO"
  • Positive: Subject + would prefer + to + verb.
    "I'd prefer to take an art course."
  • Negative: Subject + would prefer not to + verb.
    "I'd prefer not to study in either place."
  • Comparison: ...prefer [to verb A] RATHER THAN [verb B].
    "I'd prefer to study online rather than go to campus."
