B1 Major Preferences Toolkit

1. Stating Preferences

• I'd prefer to major in Computer Science.

• I'd rather study Marketing than Finance.

• I find Graphic Design more appealing than Literature.

• I lean towards Environmental Policy.

2. Comparing Pros & Cons

• The main advantage of Engineering is job security.

• The biggest downside of Fine Arts is income variability.

• On the one hand, X pays well; on the other hand, Y offers better work-life balance.

3. Contrast & Comparison

• Business is versatile, whereas Philosophy is theoretical.

• Nursing involves higher stress compared to Health Admin.

Sample Dialogue Model

Student: "I'd rather major in Data Science than General IT. The main benefit is higher starting salaries, although the math workload is much heavier."

Advisor: "That makes sense. Would you prioritize a flexible remote job over creative design work?"
