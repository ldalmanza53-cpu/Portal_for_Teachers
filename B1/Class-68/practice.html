📖 B1 Grammar Reference Matrix: Past Accomplishments

Master the difference between Present Perfect (unspecified lifetime accomplishments) and Simple Past (specific finished time periods), and avoid common B1 syntax traps.

⚖️ 1. Present Perfect vs Simple Past for Accomplishments

🟣 Present Perfect (Lifetime Accomplishments) Unspecified Time
have / haven't + managed to + base verb
have / haven't + BEEN + able to + base verb

Focuses on results achieved up to the present moment in life. Time is not specified.

  • I have managed to save money for college.
  • She has been able to help many students.
🟡 Simple Past (Finished Time / Specific Details) Specific Finished Time
was / wasn't + able to + base verb
managed to + base verb (with past time expression)

Focuses on an action completed at a specific past time (e.g., last year, in 2021, when I was in school).

  • I was able to get my degree in 2022.
  • I managed to buy a car last month.

⚠️ Common B1 Grammar Traps & Pitfalls

Trap #1: Omitting "BEEN"
❌ I have able to get a job.
✔️ I have been able to get a job.
Without 'been', you must use simple past: "I was able to get a job."
Trap #2: Past Tense after "TO"
❌ I've managed to learned Spanish.
✔️ I've managed to learn Spanish.
'to' must be followed by base infinitive verb.
Trap #3: Mixing Tenses with Specific Time
❌ I have been able to pass last year.
✔️ I was able to pass last year.
Specific past time expressions require Simple Past.
