B1-B2 Endeavor Language Toolkit

1. Unspecified Achievements

• I have managed to reach my target sales.

• We have succeeded in passing the audit.

• She has overcome major structural obstacles.

✗ Avoid "succeeded to pass". Say "succeeded in passing".

2. Sustained Striving

• I have been working on this for 3 years.

• They have been striving to expand since 2024.

3. Frustration vs Reward

• Despite facing bottlenecks, we persevered.

• Although the process was grueling, it was rewarding.

✗ Never combine "Although" and "but" together.

4. Key Endeavor Vocabulary

• Milestone: A significant stage or event in development.

• Bottleneck / Setback: A problem that delays progress.

• Rewarding: Providing satisfaction and self-fulfillment.
