B1 Language Toolkit Scaffolds

Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

Use Present Perfect for life experiences; Past Simple for specific times in the past.

• "I have lived in three foreign cities."
• "When I was in Spain in 2022, I ate dinner late."
Habits & Cultural Adaptation
Used to + verb: Past habit no longer true.
"Back home, we used to drink tea at 5 PM."
Get used to + -ing: Process of adaptation.
"It took me weeks to get used to driving on the left."
Making Cultural Contrasts
• "...is much more formal than back home."
• "Unlike in my country, people here rarely tip."
• "...is far less crowded during weekends."
Cultural Advice Modals
• "You should definitely try the local bakery."
• "You must make sure to remove your shoes."
• "You shouldn't speak loudly on the train."

Activity Instructions (10 Minutes)

Welcome to GlobalXchange HQ! In this 10-minute B1 pair activity, you will roleplay as an Expat Ambassador and a Cultural Officer to audit real or imagined experiences living abroad.

Phase 1 (0:00 - 1:30): Boot & Prompt Drawing

Draw two outcome prompts from the prompt bank and assign one to Student A and one to Student B.

Phase 2 (1:30 - 5:00): Round 1 Interview

Student A speaks as Expat Ambassador. Student B audits, checks B1 grammar, and asks probe questions.

Phase 3 (5:00 - 8:30): Round 2 Role Swap

Swap roles! Student B speaks; Student A audits and takes evaluation notes.

Phase 4 (8:30 - 10:00): Passport Issuance

Review final scores, click "Generate Passport Stamp", and share top takeaway with class!
