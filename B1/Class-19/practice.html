Unit 4 B1 Grammar Practice • Past Continuous, Simple Past & Past Perfect

Unit 4 B1 Grammar Reference

Use these tense combinations to make your story sound vivid and accurate!

1. Past Continuous vs. Simple Past

Use Past Continuous for background events in progress, and Simple Past for interrupting actions.

"I was walking on the beach when I saw a bottle."

2. Past Perfect

Use Past Perfect (had + past participle) to show an action happened before another past action.

"Inside there was a note that someone had written 50 years earlier."

3. Narrative Time Linkers

Connect your ideas smoothly with time expressions:

Suddenly As soon as After that In the end
