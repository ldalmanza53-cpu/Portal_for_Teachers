Time Tenses & Prepositions Matrix

Reference guide for expressing past, present, and future time in English B1.

Prepositions of Time

In, Since, & For

IN + Specific Era / Decade / Year "In the 1960s, people listened to record players."
SINCE + Past Starting Point (with Present Perfect) "Since the Middle Ages, people have lived in towns."
FOR + Period / Duration of Time "For many years, people didn't spend time on phones."
Tense Formulas

Past, Present, & Future Verbs

PAST SIMPLE (-ed / irregular) "People listened to music on record players."
PRESENT SIMPLE / CONTINUOUS (nowadays, now) "Nowadays, people mostly use MP3 players."
FUTURE SIMPLE (will + verb) "In the future, people will listen through brain chips."
