Traditional Methods vs. Modern Shifts Matrix

Compare past habits (used to + V1, would + V1) against modern shifts using contrast linkers (whereas, while, unlike) and modern passive replacements (has been replaced by, have streamlined).

1. Past Habits vs States
used to + V1 (Action/State) | would + V1 (Action only)

'Used to' works for past states & actions; 'would' only for repeated past actions.

2. Direct Contrast Linkers
whereas / while + Clause | unlike + Noun

Directly juxtaposes traditional methods with modern alternatives.

3. Modern Replacement Passive
has/have been replaced by + [New Tech]

Highlights technological obsolescence and structural method shifts.

4. Accustomed Habit Nuance
be / get used to + V-ing

Expresses familiarity with modern practices (e.g. "is used to collaborating").
