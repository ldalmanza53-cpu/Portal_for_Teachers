Textbook Exercise B

When can you omit who / that?

Click the who/that button in each sentence to strike it through if it is OPTIONAL (Object Relative Clause). Leave it unchanged if it is REQUIRED (Subject Relative Clause).

Grammar Golden Rule:
  • Optional (Omit): Followed by Subject + Verb (e.g. who I met)
  • Required (Keep): Followed directly by a Verb (e.g. who is messy)
