Toggle the order of clauses to see how punctuation changes automatically!

1

Present Habits & Routines

Present Simple + Present Simple

"When I feel tired, I drink green tea."

Describes things that are generally or habitually true.

2

Interrupted Past Actions

Past Continuous + When + Past Simple

"I was studying when the phone rang."

A longer action was interrupted by a shorter event.

3

Future Conditions

When + Present Simple $\rightarrow$ Future Simple (Will)

"When I arrive home, I will call you."

Never use 'will' inside the when-clause itself!
