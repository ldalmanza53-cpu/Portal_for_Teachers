B1 Language Toolkit

Favors, Voicemails & Past Storytelling

1. Asking for Favors Politely

Polite Formulas
  • Could you possibly + base verb...?
  • Would you mind + verb-ING...?
  • I was wondering if you could + base verb...?
  • Do you think you could + base verb...?

2. Leaving Voicemail Messages

Message Structure
Greeting: "Hi [Name], this is [Name] calling..."
Reason: "I'm calling because something happened..."
Closing: "Please call me back as soon as you get this."

3. Past Storytelling Tenses

Grammar Focus

Combine tenses to justify why you need the favor:

  • Past Continuous: Background action ("While I was walking...")
  • Past Simple: Main interrupting event ("...the door slammed shut.")
  • Past Perfect: Earlier action ("I realized I had forgotten my keys.")
