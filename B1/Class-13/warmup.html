📊 Registers of Politeness for Asking Favors Click any card to hear the audio!
Informal (Friends / Peers)

Can you + [verb]?

"Can you pass me that pencil?"

Polite (Co-workers / Classmates)

Could you + [verb]?

"Could you lend me a dollar for coffee?"

Very Formal (Bosses / Strangers)

Would you mind + [verb-ing]?

"Would you mind opening the window?"

💡 Structure Rule 1: Could you / Can you / Do you think you could + BASE VERB (e.g., help, pass, give)
💡 Structure Rule 2: Would you mind + GERUND (-ING) (e.g., closing, helping, waiting)

🚨 The "Would You Mind...?" Trap!

When someone asks: "Would you mind opening the window?" (Literally: Does it bother you?)

  • ✔️ To AGREE (Yes, I will help): Say "No", "Not at all!", or "Sure, no problem!"
  • ❌ Do NOT say "Yes": Saying "Yes" means "Yes, I mind! Don't do it!"
