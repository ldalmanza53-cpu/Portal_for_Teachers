Pragmatic Favor Matrix & Phrase Bank

Explore the 5 essential building blocks of B1 favor diplomacy. Click any pronunciation button to hear authentic stress patterns and softening intonation.

1. Pre-Favor Signals

Check Availability

Always warn the listener before asking for a favor to soften the social impact.

  • "Could I ask a quick favor?"
    Informal / Peer
  • "Do you have two minutes?"
    Neutral / Work
  • "I hate to ask, but..."
    Empathy Warning

2. Informal Peer Frames

Direct & Friendly

Use with desk neighbors, peers, and close team colleagues.

  • "Can you do me a quick favor?"
    Can you + V1
  • "Could you give me a hand with this?"
    Give a hand with + Noun/V-ing

3. Neutral & Workplace Frames

Standard B1

Ideal for daily team interactions and routine workplace requests.

  • "I was wondering if you could take a look?"
    I was wondering if + S + could + V1
  • "Would you mind reviewing this?"
    Would you mind + V-ing

4. High-Stakes Formal

Executives / Directors

Use when requesting letters of recommendation or high-value favors.

  • "I would be immensely grateful if you could..."
    Immensely grateful + if + could
  • "Would you be so kind as to grant..."
    Would you be so kind as to + V1

5. Softening Modifiers

Burden Reducers

Minimize perceived effort to increase likelihood of a positive response.

  • "by any chance / possibly"
    "Could you possibly check this?"
  • "if it's not too much trouble"
    Sentence Suffix Softener

6. Granting & Declining

Role B Responses

Accept with enthusiasm or decline diplomatically without causing offense.

  • "I'd be glad to help out!"
    Granting Enthusiastically
  • "I'm afraid I'm tied up right now, but..."
    Diplomatic Decline + Alternative
