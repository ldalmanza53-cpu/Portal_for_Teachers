Life Events & Tense Sequencing Matrix

Master the 4 fundamental milestone grammar pillars: Anchored Past Events, Unanchored Experiences & Ongoing States, Prerequisite Achievements, and Simultaneous Stages.

1. Anchored Past
Past Simple + in [Year] / at age X / [Time] ago

Completed events at a specific past point or period.

2. Unanchored / Ongoing
Pres. Perf + since / for / ever / so far

Life experience or continuous state reaching into now.

3. Prerequisite Milestone
Past Perfect + by the time / before

Action completed BEFORE another past event.

4. Simultaneous Stage
Past Continuous + while / as

Background continuous action during another event.
