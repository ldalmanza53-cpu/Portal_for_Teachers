B1 Milestone Language Toolkit

1. Age Markers & Definite Past

• She graduated at the age of twenty-two.

• He bought his house in his late twenties.

• I left home five years ago.

2. Cumulative Experience & Duration

• I have lived in three cities so far.

• She has worked here since 2021.

• They have known each other for ten years.

3. Trajectory Phrasal Verbs

• I grew up in Spain before moving to France.

• Last year, she embarked on a new business venture.

• He hopes to settle down in the countryside.

Sample Dialogue Model

Strategist: "I grew up in Toronto, but at age eighteen I moved to Vancouver. I have lived here for six years so far, and last year I embarked on my own consulting career."

Auditor: "Clear trajectory! What major milestones have you achieved since you moved to Vancouver?"
