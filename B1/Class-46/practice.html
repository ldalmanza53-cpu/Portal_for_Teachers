1. Future Perfect (Completed Events)

Use will have + past participle for actions completed by a specific future point.
• "In five years, I will have gotten a master's degree."
• "Will you have moved to a new home?"

2. Future Continuous (Ongoing Actions/States)

Use will be + verb-ing for actions in progress at a future point.
• "I think I will be living in a new city."
• "She will be working at a software company."
