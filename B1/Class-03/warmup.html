⚡ The Preference Intensity Spectrum

When expressing likes and dislikes, remember: verbs of preference are followed by Gerunds (-ing)!

❤️

Love / Adore

+ Verb-ing

"I love hanging out with funny people."

🙂

Like / Enjoy

+ Verb-ing

"I enjoy going on outdoor hikes."

😐

Don't Mind

+ Verb-ing / If...

"I don't mind staying up late."

🙁

Dislike

+ Verb-ing / When...

"I dislike talking about work."

🚫

Can't Stand / Hate

+ Verb-ing / People who...

"I can't stand people who lie."

📐 Ideal Friend Grammar Formula

Pattern 1: Relative Clause + Adjective

My ideal friend is someone who is [adjective].

e.g., "...someone who is reliable and honest."

Pattern 2: Relative Clause + Action

An ideal friend always [verb-s] when...

e.g., "...always listens when I have a problem."

Pattern 3: Shared Preference

I value friends who love [verb-ing].

e.g., "...who love trying new restaurants."
