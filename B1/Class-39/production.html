B1 Causative & Suggestions Toolkit

1. Causatives (Things to Have/Get Done)

• I need to have my air conditioner serviced.

• We are going to get the roof repaired next week.

• She had her passport renewed yesterday.

2. Making Suggestions & Proposals

• Why don't we get a certified technician to inspect it?

• How about having the catering arranged early?

• We could consider getting three different quotes.

3. Advice & Necessity Modals

• You must have safety checks done annually.

• Homeowners ought to get licenses verified first.

Sample Dialogue Model

Client: "I need to get my car brakes inspected before my road trip next week."

Consultant: "Why don't you have them checked at a specialized garage? How about booking an appointment today to avoid delays?"
