Grammar Topic 1: Causative Passive

Get or Have Something Done

Sentences with get / have + object + past participle are passive. They express getting a service performed by someone else.

Common Mistake Alert

DON'T use any form of 'be' before the past participle!

✓ Where can I have my watch fixed?
✗ Where can I have my watch be fixed?

Grammar Topic 2: Making Suggestions

Verb Patterns for Giving Advice

Different suggestion phrases require specific verb forms (Base Verb, Gerund -ing, or Infinitive to + verb).

Base Verb Why don't you...
Maybe you could...
Gerund (-ing) What about...
Have you thought about...
Infinitive (to + verb) It might be a good idea...
One option is...

Unit 11 Grammar Reference Guide

Quick reference for Causative Passive and Suggestion Verb Patterns.

Causative Passive Structure

Subject + get/have + Object + Past Participle (V3)

  • Active: I cut my hair. (I did it myself)
  • Passive Causative: I get my hair cut. (A hairdresser does it)
  • Important: Never insert 'be' before V3!

Suggestion Patterns

Why don't you / Maybe you could + Base Verb
What about / Have you thought about + Gerund (-ing)
It might be a good idea / One option is + to + Verb
