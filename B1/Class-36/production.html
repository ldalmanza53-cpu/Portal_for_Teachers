B1 Eco Strategy Toolkit

1. Describing Problems

• One major crisis is plastic accumulation.

• Deforestation leads to severe loss of biodiversity.

• Burning fossil fuels causes global temperatures to rise.

2. Giving Solutions (Modals & Conditionals)

• Governments must enforce packaging bans.

• Cities should invest in electric transit.

• If we ban single-use items, ocean waste will drop.

3. Expressing Personal Preferences

• I'd rather carry reusable containers than pay packaging taxes.

• I'd prefer to invest in solar panels.

• I find electric bicycles more appealing than driving.

• I lean towards local organic food markets.

Sample Dialogue Model

Advocate: "Urban air pollution is caused by vehicle emissions. While cities should expand subways, I'd rather ride an electric bicycle daily because it reduces my individual carbon footprint immediately."

Auditor: "That's a practical choice! What obstacle might stop you from cycling in winter?"
