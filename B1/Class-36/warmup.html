Module 2: Trade-off Dynamics

Preference & Career Choice Comparison Lab

Construct precise comparison sentences for sustainable career choices and eco-habits. Master key grammar patterns: prefer [Gerund] to [Gerund], would rather [Base Verb] than [Base Verb], and leaning towards [Noun/Gerund].

Pattern 1: Prefer
prefer + V-ing + TO + V-ing
"I prefer taking public transit to driving a gas SUV."
Pattern 2: Would Rather
would rather + V1 + THAN + V1
"I would rather study renewable energy than work in oil."

leaning towards + Noun/Gerund
