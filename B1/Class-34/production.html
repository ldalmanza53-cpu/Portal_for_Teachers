B1 Preferences & Strategy Toolkit

1. Expressing Preferences

• I'd prefer to study with active recall.

• I'd rather use flashcards than highlight textbooks.

• I find self-paced courses more appealing than lectures.

• I lean towards project-based learning.

2. Comparing Pros & Cons

• The main advantage of spaced repetition is retention.

• The biggest downside of cramming is rapid forgetting.

• While online courses offer freedom, classroom learning provides structure.

3. Validating Habits & Giving Advice

• I learn best when I test my memory directly.

• To break a study plateau, you ought to change your routine.

• Learners should focus on immediate feedback loops.

Sample Dialogue Model
