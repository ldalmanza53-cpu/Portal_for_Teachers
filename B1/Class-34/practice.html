Would Rather vs. Would Prefer & By + Gerund

Key Rule: would rather takes bare verb (e.g., rather study). would prefer takes infinitives (e.g., prefer to study). In negative forms, place not after rather or before to.
Key Rule: Combine ideas using by + verb-ing. For negatives without comparison, use by not + verb-ing. For negative comparisons, use not by ... but by ....

Quick Grammar Reference Guide


1. Expressing Preferences

• Would rather (Bare Infinitive):
"I'd rather take classes at night."
"I'd rather not take up either." (Negative)

• Would prefer (Full Infinitive):
"I'd prefer to become a teacher."
"I'd prefer not to take any classes." (Negative)

2. Methods & Advice (By + Gerund)

• Standard Method:
"One way to build vocabulary is by writing down new words."

• Negative Advice (Without Comparison):
"Improve your grammar by not repeating common mistakes."

• Negative Comparison:
"You become fluent not by translating everything, but by trying to think in English."
