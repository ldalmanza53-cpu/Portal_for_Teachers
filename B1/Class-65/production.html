B1 Opinion & Discussion Toolkit

1. Giving Your Opinion

• In my opinion, remote work is great.

• From my point of view, cities need parks.

• I personally believe that education is key.

✗ Avoid "According to me". Say "In my opinion".

2. Agreeing

• I completely agree with you.

• That's a very good point.

• You're absolutely right.

✗ Never say "I am agree". Say "I agree".

3. Disagreeing Politely

• I see your point, but it is expensive.

• I'm afraid I disagree because we need time.

• That might be true, however there are risks.

4. Reasons & Examples

• ...because of / due to heavy traffic.

• The main reason is that...

• For instance / For example, my city built a subway.
