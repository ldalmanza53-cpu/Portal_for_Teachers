🕵️ Laboratorio de Deducciones de Misterio (Sección 7)

Usa modales de deducción pasada para explicar eventos misteriosos: must have (99% seguro), might / could have (posible), y couldn't have (imposible).

👰‍♀️💐

1. La Novia que no llegó

The bride didn't show up for her wedding. She sent flowers to the groom with a note: "Thank you."

Explicaciones posibles (Deducciones):

• Must have: She must have changed her mind at the last minute.

• Might have: She might have fallen in love with someone else.

• Couldn't have: She couldn't have wanted to marry him.

🧳🚕

2. Regreso desde el Aeropuerto

A man arrived at the airport with a suitcase, saw his brother, grabbed a cab, and went back home.

Explicaciones posibles (Deducciones):

• Must have: He must have forgotten his passport at home.

• Might have: He might have had an argument with his brother earlier.

☀️🌊

3. El Hombre Empapado

It was a hot, sunny day. A man arrived home. He was soaking wet.

Explicaciones posibles (Deducciones):

• Must have: He must have fallen into a swimming pool.

• Could have: Kids could have sprayed him with a garden hose.

📖🛒

4. Comprador de Revistas (7B)

A man went around town and bought all the copies of the latest issue of a specific magazine.

Ejemplo del libro:

• Might have: "Well, the magazine might have had an article about him."

• Must have: "He must have wanted to hide embarrassing news."

📚 Guía Comparativa de Modales Pasados B1

Aprende a diferenciar el uso de cada modal pasado según el propósito comunicativo:

Modal Pasado Uso Principal Traducción Aproximada Ejemplo en Inglés
Should have + V3 Crítica / Consejo no seguido / Regreso al pasado Debió haber / Deberías haber You should have made an excuse.
Shouldn't have + V3 Reproche / Acción inadecuada en el pasado No debió haber... She shouldn't have asked for money.
Would have + V3 Reacción hipotética ("¿Qué habrías hecho tú?") Yo habría / Él habría... I would have told him to wash dishes.
Must have + V3 Deducción de alta certeza (99% seguro) Debe haber (pasado)... / Seguro que... He must have forgotten his wallet.
Might / Could have + V3 Deducción posible (50% probabilidad) Podría haber / Tal vez... The magazine might have had an article.
Couldn't have + V3 Deducción de imposibilidad absoluta Imposible que haya... She couldn't have known about it.

⚠️ Errores Típicos B1 (¡Evítalos!)

  • ❌ Incorrecto: "I should of gone."
    ✔️ Correcto: "I should have gone." (Nunca uses "of" por confusión fonética con 've).
  • ❌ Incorrecto: "He must to have seen."
    ✔️ Correcto: "He must have seen." (Los modales no llevan "to").
  • ❌ Incorrecto: "They should have went."
    ✔️ Correcto: "They should have gone." (Exige Participio Pasado V3, no Pasado Simple).
