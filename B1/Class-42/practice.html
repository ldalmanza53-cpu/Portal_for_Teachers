Past Time Expressions Guide


Expression Time Focus Example Sentence
ago Specific past distance from now The games started 3,000 years ago.
in Specific year or century Held in 1896 / in the 13th century.
during Throughout a period/decade Spread during the 18th century.
from... to... Definite start and end points From 1960 to 1970.
for Total duration of an event The Beatles were together for 10 years.
since Start point continuing into present Women have competed since 1900.
