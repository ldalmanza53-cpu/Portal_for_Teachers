B1 Past Time Expressions Toolkit

1. Definite Past Markers (Past Simple)

• The company opened in 2019.

• They graduated three years ago.

• We completed the audit last Thursday.

2. Simultaneous Events (Past Continuous)

• While engineers were testing, a blackout occurred.

• The alarm rang as we were leaving the building.

3. Prior Sequence (Past Perfect)

• By the time the meeting started, he had arrived.

• We reviewed the contract prior to signing.

• As soon as the team had finished, they celebrated.

Sample Dialogue Model

Officer: "In 2023, our firm launched a new service. Prior to the launch, we had been testing the system for five months. While developers were fixing bugs, marketing launched campaigns."

Auditor: "Clear timeline! What had been completed by the time the official launch date arrived?"
