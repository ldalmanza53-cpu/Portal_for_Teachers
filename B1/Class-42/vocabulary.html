B1 Language Focus

Talking About Historic Events

  • • Past Simple with dates: Use specific dates or years (e.g., In 2003, scientists completed...).
  • • Present Perfect for duration: Use since or for when an event started in the past and continues (e.g., Since the early 1980s, HIV has infected...).
  • • Passive Voice: Often used when focusing on the victim or result (e.g., John Lennon was killed...).
