🔑 B1 Grammar Golden Rule for Indirect Questions:

When asking indirect questions, the second half uses statement word order (Subject + Verb), NOT question word order. Do not use auxiliary do/does/did in the embedded clause!

❌ "Do you know where is the room?" ✅ "Do you know where the room is?"
Direct Directives / Orders Too Blunt
  • Where is the main Keynote Hall? Direct question; sounds demanding in professional settings.
  • Give me the presentation slides. Imperative command without politeness buffer.
  • Is Dr. Aris in his office? Direct Yes/No question; lacks diplomatic softening.
Indirect Questions (B1 Target) Recommended
  • Could you tell me where the main Keynote Hall is? Indirect Wh- question with Subject + Verb order.
  • I was wondering if you could email me the slides? Past continuous introductory clause adds polite distance.
  • Do you know whether Dr. Aris is available? Uses 'if / whether' for embedded Yes/No queries.
Politeness Softeners Diplomatic Buffer
  • possibly / by any chance "Could you possibly check if the workshop room is open?"
  • when you have a moment "Would you mind bringing an extra chair when you have a moment?"
  • at your earliest convenience "I would be grateful if you could confirm my slot at your earliest convenience."
