Page 24 Comprehensive Reference Guide

Master requests using modals, if-clauses, gerunds, and indirect message patterns with proper word order and negative infinitives.

1. Simple Past After "If" in Polite Requests
Would you mind + if + Subject + Simple Past Verb...?
Would it be all right + if + Subject + Simple Past Verb...?

Correct: "Would you mind if I used your car?"

Correct: "Would it be all right if I borrowed your laptop?"

❌ Incorrect: "Would you mind if I using..." OR "Would it be all right if I use..."
2. Gerund vs. "If" Clause with "Would you mind"
Direct action by listener: Would you mind + Verb-ing...?
Action by speaker: Would you mind if I + Simple Past...?

Asking listener to do it: "Would you mind giving me a ride to class?"

Asking permission for yourself: "Would you mind if I got a ride with you?"

3. Placement of "NOT" in Negative Infinitives
Tell / Ask + person + NOT + to + base verb

Direct: "Don't forget to write."

Indirect: "Could you tell Susie not to forget to write?"

❌ NEVER place "not" inside the infinitive ("to not forget"). "NOT" comes BEFORE "to"!
4. Indirect Wh- & Yes/No Message Patterns
Yes/No: Ask + person + IF / WHETHER + Subject + Verb
Wh- : Ask + person + WH-WORD + Subject + Verb

Yes/No: "Could you ask Susie if she's busy this weekend?"

Wh- Question: "Can you ask Susie what she is doing Saturday?"

⚠️ No inversion in indirect Wh- questions! (Subject + Verb)
¡Atención Hispanohablantes! Common Spanish Speaker Traps
1. Negative Infinitive Order

In Spanish we say "dile que no olvide" or "no olvidar". In English indirect commands, place NOT before TO: "not to forget" (NEVER *"to not forget"*).

2. Past Tense After "If"

Translating "¿Te importaría si uso...?" leads to using simple present *"if I use"*. In polite English conditional requests with *Would you mind if...*, use simple past: "if I used".

3. No Auxiliary Inversion

In Spanish indirect questions keep verb inversion. In English, statement word order is mandatory: "what she is doing" (NOT *"what is she doing"*).
