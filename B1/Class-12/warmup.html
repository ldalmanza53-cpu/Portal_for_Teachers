Quick Reference Rules

Direct Question vs Indirect Request:

❌ Where IS the bank? (Inverted)

✅ Could you tell me where the bank IS? (Subject + Verb)

Would you mind... Rule:

❌ Would you mind to open the door?

✅ Would you mind OPENING the door? (+ Gerund -ING)
