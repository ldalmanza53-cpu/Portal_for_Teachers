📚 Grammar Master Reference: Relative Clauses

Understanding the difference between Defining and Non-Defining relative clauses is essential for B1 fluency and formal writing.

⚖️ Defining vs. Non-Defining Comparison Matrix

Feature Defining Relative Clauses Non-Defining Relative Clauses
Information Type Essential to identify who/what we are talking about. Extra / Additional information. Sentence makes sense without it.
Comma Usage NO COMMAS ❌ COMMAS REQUIRED ( , ... , ) ✔️
Can use 'THAT'? YES (who / which / that) NO ❌ (Never use 'that' after a comma)
Example Sentence An art editor who works in publishing creates design layouts. A game programmer, who needs math skills, writes video game code.

⚠️ B1 Trap #1: The 'That' Comma Violation

In non-defining clauses (extra info with commas), you cannot replace who or which with that.

❌ "A photo editor, that selects images, works hard."
✔️ "A photo editor, who selects images, works hard."

⚠️ B1 Trap #2: Missing the Closing Comma

Non-defining clauses in the middle of a sentence must be enclosed by two commas (before and after the relative clause).

❌ "Extras, who are hired for crowd scenes usually don't have lines."
✔️ "Extras, who are hired for crowd scenes, usually don't have lines."
