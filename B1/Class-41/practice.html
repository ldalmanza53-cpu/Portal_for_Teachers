Predicting the Future with Tenses

Master how to talk about future events, ongoing future activities, and actions completed by a specific future deadline.

Structure 1

Future with will

Predict future events or general situations.

Subject + will / won't + Base Verb
"We will spend vacations in space."
"We won't have colonies on Mars."
Common Time Indicators: In the future, Soon, Sometime soon.
Structure 2

Future Continuous

Predict ongoing actions happening in the future.

Subject + will be + Verb-ing
"Human beings will be living on another planet."
"We won't be living here."
Common Time Indicators: In 50 years, In 20 years, At this time next year.
Structure 3

Future Perfect

Predict actions completed by a certain time.

Subject + will have + Past Participle (V3)
"Within 50 years, we will have set up a research center on Mars."
"By 2050, a company will have built a resort on the moon."
Key Time Markers: By 2050, By the end of this century, Within 50 years, In less than 20 years.
