B1-B2 Debate & Stance Toolkit

1. Formal Stance Markers

• In my view, pay transparency is necessary.

• From my perspective, remote work builds trust.

✗ Never say "According to me" or "In my point of view".

2. Balancing Pros & Cons

• While proponents argue that AI boosts speed, critics maintain that it introduces bias.

• Despite the benefits of X, Y remains a concern.

3. Recommendation Syntax

• I recommend adopting a trial phase.

• I suggest that management review policies.

✗ Never say "I recommend to adopt".

4. Debate Vocabulary

• Trade-off: Compromise between two choices.

• Precedent: An earlier decision setting a rule.

• Consensus: General agreement among a group.
