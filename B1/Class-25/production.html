B1 Problem Toolkit Reference

1. Describing Defects (Adjective vs. Noun)

Adjective: [Object] is + [Adjective]
Noun: There is a + [Noun] in/on the [Object]

is cracked / a crack
is torn / a tear
is jammed / a jam
is leaking / a leak
is frayed / a fray
is clogged / a clog

2. Recurring Issues (Keeps + -ing)

Formula: [Object] + keeps + [Verb-ing]

"The air conditioner keeps blowing hot air."
"My headphone audio keeps cutting out."
"The bathroom tap keeps dripping water."

3. Refusal Malfunctions (Won't + Verb)

Formula: [Object] + won't + [Base Verb]

"The hotel room door keycard won't unlock."
"The washing machine drum won't drain water."
"The smart TV remote won't change channels."

4. Action & Solutions Formula

Formula A: needs to be + [Past Participle]
Formula B: needs + [Verb-ing]

"The cracked screen needs to be replaced."
"The loose chair leg screw needs tightening."
"The clogged shower drain needs clearing."

5. Sample Quality Audit Dialogue

Customer: "Hello Inspector! I have a problem with my suit luggage. The main zipper is jammed and there is a tear in the fabric seam. It keeps catching and won't zip closed."

Inspector: "I see. How long has the zipper been catching? Is the slider bent or broken?"

Customer: "The metal teeth are bent. It needs to be replaced immediately."

Inspector: "Understood! I am logging this defect and issuing your Problem Audit Certificate."
