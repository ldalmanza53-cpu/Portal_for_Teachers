Past Memories & Specific Events

Past Time Clause Formulas

Until / By the time + Simple Past ➔ Main Clause (Past Perfect) "Until I went to college, I had never cooked a real meal."
Before + Simple Past ➔ Main Clause (Past Perfect / Simple Past) "Before I graduated high school, I had never worked."
After / Once / As soon as / The moment + Simple Past ➔ Simple Past "As soon as I got my first paycheck, I started to be more responsible."
Present General Truths & Advice

Present Time Clause Formulas

Once / As soon as + Present Simple ➔ Present Simple / Modal "Once you move in with roommates, you have to learn to work together."
The moment + Present Simple ➔ Main Present Result "The moment you get a credit card, budget control becomes vital."
Comma Rule Warning ⚠️ When the time clause comes first, use a comma. When it comes second, no comma is needed! (e.g. "You have to learn to work together once you move in with roommates.")
