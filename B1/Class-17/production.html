B1 Past Tenses Toolkit

Quick Grammar Reference Sheet

1. Past Simple

Completed Events

Use for completed actions that happened at a specific time in the past.

Form: Subject + Past Verb (-ed / irregular)
Ex: "I lost my keys while I was walking."

2. Past Continuous

Background Actions

Use for actions in progress at a specific moment in the past.

Form: was / were + Verb-ing
Ex: "While I was taking a photo, it started rain."

3. Past Perfect

Earlier Past

Use for an action that happened BEFORE another action in the past.

Form: had + Past Participle
Ex: "I realized I had left my phone at home."

4. Used to

Past Habits

Use for past habits or states that are no longer true today.

Form: used to + Base Verb
Ex: "I used to go to that beach every summer."

Useful Inspector Audit Starters

  • "Where were you when this happened?"
  • "What were you doing right before that?"
  • "Had you ever experienced something like this before?"
  • "What did you use to do in that situation?"
