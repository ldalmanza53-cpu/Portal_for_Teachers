📊 Active vs. Passive Past Modals Syntax Matrix

Understand how the active sentence transforms into the passive voice by shifting focus from the active agent to the receiver of the action.

Grammar Voice Focus / Subject Modal Structure Past Participle Agent Example Sentence
Active Voice Ancient peoples (Agente) must have used (V3) stone circles (Object) "Ancient peoples must have used stone circles."
Passive Voice Stone circles (Objeto) must have BEEN used (V3) by ancient peoples "Stone circles must have been used by ancient peoples."

⚠️ 3 Frequent B1 Passive Traps To Avoid

1. Omitting "BEEN"

❌ "The Mayans could have killed by disease."

✔️ "The Mayans could have been killed by disease."

Without "been", the sentence remains active and means the Mayans killed the disease!

2. Word Order Errors

❌ "Trees must been have destroyed."

✔️ "Trees must have been destroyed."

"Been" always follows "have", never before it.

3. Forgetting "BY"

❌ "The stones were carved ancient engineers."

✔️ "The stones were carved by ancient engineers."

Use "by" to introduce the active agent performing the action.
