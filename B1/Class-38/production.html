B1 Setting Suggestions Toolkit

1. Making Suggestions

• Why don't we try establishing quiet hours?

• How about adding ergonomic chairs?

• I suggest that we should create a shared calendar.

2. Modals for Ideas & Proposals

• We could consider hiring a local guide.

• It might be a good idea to install smart lighting.

• You ought to research accommodation options in advance.

3. Adapting & Justifying

• For a corporate workplace setting, I recommend quiet pods.

• This suggestion would allow us to save energy.

• The main benefit of this option is its low cost and high feasibility.

Sample Dialogue Model

Consultant: "For an open-office setting, why don't we establish quiet zones? We could also encourage employees to use noise-canceling headsets. This would significantly improve concentration."

Auditor: "That's a practical suggestion! What is the estimated cost of implementing quiet zones?"
