🎤 2. Tabla de Verbos Musicales de la Unidad (Infinitivo vs Participio Pasado)

Verbo Base (Infinitivo) Participio Pasado (V3) Tipo de Verbo Ejemplo en Pasivo
compose (componer) composed Regular (+d) The song is composed by the songwriter.

add (agregar/añadir) added Regular (+ed) The song is added to playlists.
choose (elegir) chosen Irregular The track is chosen for airplay.
play (reproducir/sonar) played Regular (+ed) The song is played on the radio by the DJ.

🔗 Conectores de Secuencia (Sequence Adverbs)

Para guiar al oyente a través del proceso cronológico de una producción, iniciamos cada oración con un conector:

First, the song is composed... (Paso 1)
Next, the song is recorded... (Paso 2)
Then, the vocals are mixed... (Paso 3)
After that, the recording is taken... (Paso 4)
Finally, the song is played on the radio... (Paso 6)

⚠️ Errores Frecuentes de Nivel B1 (Grammar Traps)

1. Olvidar el auxiliar 'is / are':
❌ "The song recorded by the studio."
✔️ "The song is recorded by the studio."
2. Confundir el participio de 'choose':
❌ "The song is choosed by the DJ."
✔️ "The song is chosen by the DJ."
3. Error de pluralidad:
❌ "The vocals and instruments is mixed."
✔️ "The vocals and instruments are mixed."
