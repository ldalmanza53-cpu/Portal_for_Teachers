B1 Level • Past Continuous vs. Simple Past & Past Perfect

💡 Quick Grammar Reference

  • Past Continuous vs. Simple Past: Non-action or state verbs (e.g., want, need, know, have) are rarely used in past continuous.
  • Past Perfect: Shows that one past action happened before another past action (PAST PERFECT → SIMPLE PAST → NOW).
