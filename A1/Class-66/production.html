Validate Plans & Send Text Messages (10 Min)

Real-World Scenario

Checking & Confirming Plans via Text

You made plans with a friend or colleague earlier this week. Before going out, send a quick text message to check details (activity, day, time, or place). Your partner reads the message, checks their details, and replies to confirm or update!

Role A

The Plan Sender / Checker

Sends a text to review details: "Hi! Are we still meeting for...?"

Role B

The Plan Receiver / Confirmator

Checks details, validates or corrects: "Yes, that's right!" or "Actually..."

Language Reference Cheat-Sheet

Checking / Validating:
  • Are we still meeting for [Activity]?
  • Just checking: Are you going to [Place] on [Day]?
  • Do you remember our plan for [Day] at [Time]?
Confirming Details:
  • Yes, that's right! See you then!
  • Everything is ready! I am excited.
  • Perfect! See you at [Place] at [Time].
Correcting / Updating:
  • Actually, can we meet at [Time] instead?
  • Oh! The time is [Time], not [Old Time].
  • Sorry, the location changed to [Place].

How to Play (10 Minutes)

1
Setup & Pick Prompt (2 Mins)

2
Text Exchange & Validation (6 Mins)

Role A sends a text checking details. Role B reads, verifies on their calendar, and replies with a confirmation or correction. Swap roles and repeat!
