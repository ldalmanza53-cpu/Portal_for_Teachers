Present Continuous

UNIT 10 PART 1

Present: Action happening right now.
Future: Scheduled plans with a future time expression (e.g., tonight, on Friday, this weekend).

Form:

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

Examples:

Present: "Who is talking at the door?"
Future: "We are watching a movie tonight."

Future with Be Going To

FUTURE PLANS

Use be going to + base verb to talk about future intentions, plans, or predictions based on evidence.

Form:

Subject + am/is/are + going to + base verb

Examples:

"I'm going to take a French course."
"Is Sam going to work this summer?"

Indirect Messages

UNIT 10 PART 2

Tell + that: Statements ("tell Haru that...").
Ask + to: Requests ("ask Caitlin to...").
Ask + NOT to: Negative requests ("ask Alex not to bring...").

Negative Request Form:

Ask + person + NOT + TO + base verb

Examples:

"Please ask Alex not to bring extra luggage."
