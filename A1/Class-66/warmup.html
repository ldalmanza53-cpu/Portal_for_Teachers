📌 Prepositions of Time Quick Guide:

  • AT: Specific times (at 8:15 PM), night (at night), exact moments.
  • ON: Days of the week (on Friday), specific dates, day parts (on Sunday afternoon).
  • IN: Parts of day (in the morning/afternoon), future durations (in 10 minutes), months/years.
