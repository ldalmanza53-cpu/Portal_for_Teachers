💚 Rules for AGREEING
1. Agreeing with Positive Statements (+)
Statement: "I like pizza."
Response: "Me too!" or "So do I!"
2. Agreeing with Negative Statements (-)
Statement: "I don't like rainy weather."
Response: "Me neither!"
❤️ Rules for DISAGREEING
1. Disagreeing with Positive Statements (+)
Statement: "I love action movies."
Response: "Not me!" / "I don't. I prefer comedies."
2. Disagreeing with Negative Statements (-)
Statement: "I don't like coffee."
Response: "I do! I love coffee."
