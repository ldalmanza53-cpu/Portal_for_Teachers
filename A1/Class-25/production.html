🏥 Real Context & Scenario

Members at a Community Wellness Center are attending their annual habit & health review. Wellness Advisors ask questions about daily, weekly, and leisure habits to validate member records, record exact frequency counts, and recommend suitable healthy group activities.

Core Learning Objectives
  • Use adverbs of frequency (*always, usually, often, sometimes, hardly ever, never*).
  • Express frequency time frames (*every day, once a week, twice a month*).
  • Validate and confirm information (*"So you [always/sometimes]..., right?"*).
👥 Student Roles
Role A: Wellness Advisor / Surveyor Interviewer

Asks frequency questions (e.g., "How often do you drink water?") and validates the answer back (e.g., "So you drink 8 glasses every day, right?").

Role B: Community Member Respondent

Answers questions using frequency adverbs and time expressions (e.g., "I usually cook dinner at home, but I eat out once a week.").
