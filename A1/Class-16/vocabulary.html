would like

Forms: phrase / modal expression

A polite way to say you want something or want to do something.

Common Collocations:

would like to go, would like a drink

Example Sentences:
  • I would like to invite you to my birthday party.
  • Would you like to drink some tea with us?
💡 Common Learner Note:

Spanish speakers often say "I want" directly. Using "would like" is much more polite when inviting someone or offering something.
