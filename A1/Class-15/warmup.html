📐 Invitation Rules & Sentence Builder
Learn key formulas for inviting, accepting, and declining politely.
Function Formula / Pattern Examples
Inviting Would you like to + [verb] + on [day]?
Do you want to + [verb] + this [time]?		 • Would you like to go to the cinema on Saturday?
• Do you want to drink coffee at 4:00 PM?
Accepting Yes, I'd love to!
Sure, that sounds great!		 • Yes, I'd love to! Saturday works for me.
• Sure, that sounds like fun!
Declining Politely I'm sorry, I can't. I have to [reason].
I'd love to, but I'm busy.		 • I'm sorry, I can't. I have to study for an exam.
• I'd love to, but I have to work on Sunday.
