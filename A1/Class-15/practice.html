Grammar Rule Helper: How Much vs. How Old

Section 9 (VRB-74/75)
💵 Price Question ("How much")
  • Singular: "How much is this necklace?" → "It is $10."
  • Plural: "How much are these books?" → "They are $2 each."
🎂 Age Question ("How old")
  • Singular: "How old is it?" → "It is 20 years old."
  • Plural: "How old are they?" → "They are 10 years old."
