Would you like to...? & Verb + to + Verb

Learn how to invite a friend, say yes enthusiastically, or say no politely with a reason. Click any speaker icon to listen and repeat!

1. Invite & Say Yes

Q: Would you like to go out on Friday?

✓ Yes, I would.

✓ Yes, I'd love to. Thanks!

Contraction: I'd = I would
2. Say No Politely (Excuses)

Q: Would you like to go to a concert?

✗ I'd like to, but I have to work late.

✗ I'd like to, but I need to save money.

✗ I'd like to, but I want to visit my parents.

Pattern: have to / need to / want to + base verb
3. Ask Follow-Up Details
📅 When is it? On Sunday
📍 Where is it? At City Stadium
⏰ What time does it start? At 7:00 PM
🏁 When does it end? At 9:30 PM
