The Weekend Hangout Planner

Students talk to their classmates to make weekend plans! They practice asking friends out to various fun activities, checking availability, accepting invitations warmly ("Yes, I'd love to!"), or declining politely with a simple reason ("Sorry, I can't. I'm busy.").

10 Minutes Total
Pair Practice (A1 Level)
20 Outcome Prompts
✉️

Role A: Host / Inviter

Makes invitations with activity, day & time

Inviter
  • Select Prompt: Choose an invitation card from the 20 task prompts.
  • Invite Clearly: Provide event details (Activity + Day + Time).
  • React: Say "Great! See you then!" or "Oh, no problem! Maybe next time."

Target Sentence Starters:

• "Would you like to go to the [cinema] on [Saturday at 7:00 PM]?"

• "Do you want to eat [pizza] with me [tonight at 7:30 PM]?"

• "Great! See you on [Saturday]!"

🎟️

Role B: Guest / Invitee

Responds by accepting or politely declining

Invitee
  • Listen: Listen to your partner's invitation details carefully.
  • Accept: Say "Yes, I'd love to!" or "Sure, that sounds great!"
  • Decline Politely: Say "I'm sorry, I can't" + simple reason (busy, tired, study).

Target Sentence Starters:

• "Yes, I'd love to! That sounds awesome."

• "Sure! What time should we meet?"

• "I'm sorry, I can't. I'm busy with family / I have to study."

Teacher 10-Minute Activity Breakdown

Phase 1: Setup 2 Mins

Review target structures: "Would you like to...?" / "Yes, I'd love to!" / "Sorry, I can't. I'm busy."

Phase 2: Round 1 3 Mins

Role A invites Role B using outcome prompts. Role B accepts or declines. Record events on planner!

Phase 3: Swap Roles 3 Mins

Timer triggers SWAP ROLES alert! Role B becomes Inviter, Role A responds with new cards.

Phase 4: Wrap-Up 2 Mins

Quick class poll: "Who is going to the cinema?", "Why couldn't [Name] go to the concert?"
