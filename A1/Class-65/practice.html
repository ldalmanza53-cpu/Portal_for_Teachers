Indirect Statements: TELL

TELL + THAT

Use TELL when passing along information, news, or statements from another person. The connector word is THAT.

Statement Formula:
[Polite Opener] + TELL + [Person] + THAT + [Statement Clause]
"Please tell Haru that the barbecue is on Saturday."
"Could you tell Mia that the dance performance is tonight?"

Indirect Requests: ASK

ASK + TO

Use ASK when asking someone to do an action or request. The connector word is an infinitive TO + base verb.

Request Formula:
[Polite Opener] + ASK + [Person] + TO + [Base Verb Action]
"Could you ask Caitlin to call me at 4:00?"
"Would you ask Maika to meet me at the stadium?"
