Real-World Communication Scenario

"Sorry I missed your call!"

You tried calling a friend or coworker, but they didn't pick up. You need to send a short, concise text message stating Who you are, Why you called, and What action is needed!

A

Role A: The Caller & Sender

Initiates call, receives no answer, sends text

  • Step 1: Call Student B. Hear the "Ring Ring... No Answer!" sound.
  • Step 2: Compose a 2–3 sentence text message including:
    • • Who: "Hi [Name], it's [Your Name]."
    • • Why: "I am running late / I am at the store..."
    • • Action: "Please call me back / Text me..."
  • Step 3: Send the text and wait for Role B's response.
Target Goal: Clear 3-part message
B

Role B: The Receiver & Replier

Reads text, extracts info, sends confirmation

  • Step 1: Receive the text alert on the phone simulator.
  • Step 2: Click the Text-To-Speech (TTS) icon to listen to your partner's message aloud.
  • Step 3: Choose a Quick-Reply or type a brief confirmation (e.g., "Got it! See you at 5:00.").
Target Goal: Accurate comprehension & fast confirmation

10-Minute Classroom Flow

0 - 2 Minutes

Stage 1: Prompt Setup

Pairs pick an Outcome Prompt Card and review A1 vocabulary scaffolding.

2 - 8 Minutes

Stage 2: Text Exchange

Role A simulates missed call and texts. Role B reads, listens, and replies. Swap roles!

8 - 10 Minutes

Stage 3: Wrap-Up Sharing

Pairs read their text conversation aloud or present their solution to the teacher.
