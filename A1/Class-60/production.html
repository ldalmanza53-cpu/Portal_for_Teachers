🏔️ Campus Travel & Outdoor Club Desk

Weekend Trip Planning • Distances, Temperatures & Measurements

📍 Real Context & Scenario

Students visit the Campus Travel & Outdoor Club Desk to plan a weekend trip to a nearby national park and mountain town. They ask questions about travel distances (*"How far...?"*), expected weather temperatures (*"How cold/hot...?"*), and trip measurements (*bag weight limits, mountain heights, water bottle sizes*).

📋 Instructions for Students:
1 Work in Pairs: Decide who is Student A (Traveler) and Student B (Travel Advisor).
2 Phase 1 (4 Mins): Student A asks travel questions. Student B answers with numbers and units.
3 Phase 2 (4 Mins): Role Swap! Student B asks questions; Student A answers.
🧳 Role A (Traveler) Asks about distance, weather, and packing limits.
🏕️ Role B (Travel Advisor) Answers using kilometers, °C, kilograms, meters, and liters.

🗺️ Distances & Locations:
• "How far is the [place] from [place]?"
• "It is [number] kilometers away."
• "It is [number] meters from here."
• "It takes [number] minutes by bus/taxi."
🌡️ Weather & Temperatures:
• "What is the temperature today?"
• "It is [number] degrees Celsius."
• "It is minus [number] degrees outside."
• "Is it too hot/cold for a hike?"
⚖️ Weights, Heights & Volumes:
• "How heavy can my backpack be?"
• "The limit is [number] kilograms."
• "How tall is the mountain? (___ meters high)"
• "Bring at least [number] liters of water."
