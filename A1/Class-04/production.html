STUDENT FAIR REGISTRATION DESK

A1 ESL Speaking Activity • Personal Information & Spelling

Focus: Asking & Giving Info Level: A1 Beginner
🎓 Real Context

Students visit the registration desk at an International Student Exchange Fair to receive their event entry badges. The registration clerk asks for personal details to complete the registration form, while the visiting student introduces themselves and provides accurate information.

👥 Student Roles
  • Role A (Registration Clerk): Asks questions to complete the registration form (Name, Age, Country, Phone, Email).
  • Role B (Visiting Student): Introduces self and provides personal details using the identity card.
  • Role C (Clerk Assistant): Asks for letter-by-letter spelling, double-checks phone/email numbers.
⏱️ 10-Minute Schedule
  • 0:00-1:00: Setup & Role Cards
  • 1:00-5:00: Round 1 Registration Interview
  • 5:00-9:00: Round 2 Role Swap & New Identity
  • 9:00-10:00: Class Wrap-up & Badge Presentation
