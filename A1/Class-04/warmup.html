Grammar Rules

Wh- Question Matrix

Notice how Wh- questions for personal info are structured:

1. Asking for Name / Phone Number
What is ('s) your name / phone number?
2. Asking for Origin / Country
Where are you from?
3. Asking for Age
How old are you?
