Grammar Cheat Sheet: Existential Structures

Affirmative (+)
  • Singular: There is a park near here.
  • Plural: There are two supermarkets.
  • Uncountable: There is lots of traffic.
Negative (-)
  • Singular: There isn't a train station.
  • Plural: There aren't any big cafes.
  • Zero Count: There is no cinema nearby.
Questions (?)
  • Singular: Is there a pharmacy here?
  • Plural: Are there any bus stops?
  • Answers: Yes, there is. / No, there aren't.

Prepositions of Place

next to The gym is next to the park.
opposite The school is opposite the bank.
near / close to There is a cafe near my house.
behind The bakery is behind the church.
