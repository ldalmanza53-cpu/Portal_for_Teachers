Activity Context & Rules

The Ultimate Travel & Food Showdown

🎬 The Scenario: You and your partner are famous travel vlogging partners! You have 10 minutes to plan your next episode. You must agree on 1 Destination and 1 Local Dish to feature on camera.

⏱️ 10-Minute Activity Breakdown:

  1. Preparation (2 mins): Read your prompt and review your partner role.
  2. Compare Places (3 mins): Describe and compare the 2 cities using simple comparatives (e.g. hotter, quieter, bigger).
  3. Agree / Disagree Food (3 mins): Debate the 2 food dishes (e.g. spicy, sweet, tasty) until you reach a compromise.
  4. Wrap-Up (2 mins): Announce your final decision to the vlog camera / class!
A1 Speaking Tip: Don't forget to use words like "I agree!", "I prefer...", or "Me too!"

Vlogger Partner Roles

Choose or assign one role to each student in your pair!

A

The Energetic Explorer

Vlogger A
  • Loves big, warm, vibrant, and fast-paced cities.
  • Prefers bold, spicy, exotic, and flavorful dishes.
  • Goal: Convince partner to pick the exciting city & bold dish!
B

The Relaxed Traveler

Vlogger B
  • Prefers cool, quiet, scenic, and peaceful places.
  • Enjoys simple, traditional, sweet, or comforting foods.
  • Goal: Convince partner to pick the relaxing spot & sweet/comfort dish!
