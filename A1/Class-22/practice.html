9. Real-Life Situation Roleplay Cards

Situation 1

Phone Call with Mom

You are at home. Mom calls and asks what your siblings are doing. Describe 2 actions happening right now.

Sample Answer:

"Hi Mom! My brother is studying in his room and my sister is watching TV."

Situation 2

Family Picnic Report

You are at a sunny park picnic. Look around and describe what your relatives are doing.

Sample Answer:

"My uncle is grilling burgers, and my cousins are playing soccer."

Situation 3

Babysitter Log

You are babysitting your younger cousins. Send a text message to their parents updating them.

Sample Answer:

"Don't worry! The kids are eating dinner and reading storybooks."

Situation 4

Video Call with Best Friend

Ask your classmate 3 questions about what their family members are doing right now.

Sample Answer:

"Is your father working today? What is your mother cooking?"

Present Continuous Formula & Rules

Affirmative Formula:

Subject + am / is / are + Verb-ing

Example: My brother is studying English.

Common Spanish Speaker Pitfalls:

  • Don't forget "be"! Incorrect: *My mom cooking. Correct: My mom IS cooking.
  • Drop Silent -E: make → making (NOT makeing), dance → dancing.
  • Double Consonant (CVC): run → running, swim → swimming.
