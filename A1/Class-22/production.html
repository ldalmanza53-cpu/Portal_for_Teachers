🥳 Real Context & Scenario

Students attend a Welcome Party for New Coworkers and Neighbors. During a casual speed-networking activity, participants show a digital family photo or tree on their phones to explain their family structure and connections to a new colleague.

Target Grammar & Focus
Possessive 's + Family Relationships Vocabulary

Examples: "This is my sister's husband." • "Her brother's daughter is her niece." • "Who is he?"

🤝 Student Roles
Role A: New Coworker / Neighbor A Presenter

Shares details about their family members and explains relationships using possessive 's and family terms (e.g., "This is my brother's wife").

Role B: New Coworker / Neighbor B Interviewer

Asks clarifying questions, confirms who is who in the photos, and validates relationships (e.g., "So Carlos is your nephew?").

⏱️ 10-Minute Step-by-Step Lesson Timeline

SWAP ROLES!

08:00 - 10:00 Step 3
Class Validation

✨ Possessive 's Rule
Rule: Person + 's + Relationship
  • "My sister's husband"
  • "My mother's father"
  • "My brother's daughter"
👥 Relationship Words
  • Parents = Mother & Father
  • Siblings = Brothers & Sisters
  • Niece = Sister/Brother's daughter
  • Nephew = Sister/Brother's son
  • Cousin = Aunt/Uncle's child
❓ Question Frames
  • "Who is he / she?"
  • "Are you married or single?"
  • "Is he your brother or cousin?"
  • "What is your sister's name?"
✅ Relationship Validation
  • "So, [Name] is your [relation], right?"
  • "Which means he/she is your..."
  • "Ah, I see! You have a big family."
