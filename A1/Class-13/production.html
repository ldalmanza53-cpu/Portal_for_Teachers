The Neighborhood Flea Market

Welcome to the weekend flea market! Students pair up to buy and sell secondhand items, gifts, clothes, and vintage collectibles. Practice asking "How much is this?", sharing prices, and expressing simple opinions using A1 adjectives like cheap, expensive, cool, useful, and beautiful.

Total Time: 10 Minutes
Target: Pair Work (A1 Beginner)
Goal: 20 Outcome Prompts Completed
🛒

Role A: The Shopper

Looking for bargains & gifts

Buyer
  • Goal: Point to items and ask for prices.
  • Express Opinion: Say if the item is cheap, expensive, cool, or useful.
  • Decide: Decide whether to buy or leave the item.

Key Dialogue Prompts:

• "How much is this [item]?" / "How much are these...?"

• "I think $25 is too expensive!"

• "Great! $5 is very cheap. I will buy it!"

🏪

Role B: The Market Seller

Selling market goods

Seller
  • Goal: State the exact price when asked.
  • Defend Value: Describe why the item is special or useful.
  • Close Sale: Thank the shopper or offer another item.

Key Dialogue Prompts:

• "Hello! This [item] costs $[price]."

• "It is very soft / old / unique / clean!"

• "Thank you! Have a nice day at the market!"

Teacher 10-Minute Breakdown

Step 1: Setup 2 Mins

Project key target phrases on screen. Model one quick interaction with a volunteer student (e.g., asking for the price of a jacket).

Step 2: Pair Practice 6 Mins

Students use the Outcome Prompts deck. Minute 3: Audio alert triggers mandatory role swap (A becomes B, B becomes A).

Step 3: Wrap-Up Poll 2 Mins

Class feedback: Ask "Who found something cheap?", "What was the most expensive item?", "Who bought the vintage jacket?".

Asking & Discussing Prices

Singular Question: "How much is this [jacket / hat / ring]?"
Plural Question: "How much are these [boots / sunglasses]?"
Seller Answer: "It costs $25." / "They cost $30."
Expressing Opinion: "I think $25 is too expensive!"
Buying Decision: "That is a great price! I will take it."

Essential A1 Opinion Adjectives

Expensive

Beautiful
Very pretty

Useful
