Singular vs. Plural Price Expressions

Learn how to ask about prices using is this for 1 item and are these for 2+ items or paired items!

SINGULAR (1 Item) ⏰ 🧥 💡

Question Formula:

How much IS THIS [item]?

Answer Formula:

IT'S $[price].

"How much is this clock?" → "It's $12.00."
PLURAL (2+ / Pairs) 🕶️ 🎧 🥾

Question Formula:

How much ARE THESE [items]?

Answer Formula:

THEY'RE $[price].

"How much are these sunglasses?" → "They're $8.00."
