Grammar Quick Rules

1. Be going to + Base Verb

Used for fixed future plans and intentions.

I am going to drive across the country.

2. Verb + Infinitive

Pair main verbs like plan, want, hope with to + verb.

I plan to stay here.

I don't plan to be here long.

3. Polite Questions with Would Like
