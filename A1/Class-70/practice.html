Story Grammar Hints

1. School completion & Goals: Use regular past finished for ending school and wanted for past desires.

2. Search Actions: Irregular knew describes mental state. Phrasal verb looked for expresses looking online.

3. Infinitive of Purpose ("To save"): Notice sentence 4 starts with "To save money...". After preposition "To" expressing reason, use base verb save!

4. Final Sequence: Irregular bought (ticket) -> regular registered -> regular started.
