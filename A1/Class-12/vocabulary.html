Target Language Focus: How much is / How much are

1. Singular Items (1 Thing)

Use is this / It's for 1 item (ring, MP3 player, shirt, belt, lamp):

❓ "How much is this ring?"
💬 "It's twelve dollars."

2. Plural Items (2+ Things)

Use are these / They're for plural/pair items (earrings, socks, flip-flops):

❓ "How much are these earrings?"
💬 "They're twenty dollars."

Useful Yard Sale Bargaining Expressions

Expressing Price Concern: "Oh, that's pretty expensive."
Suggesting Lower Price: "Well, how about thirty dollars?"
Making Decision: "OK. I'll take it." / "I'll think about it."
