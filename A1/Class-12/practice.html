🎧 Listening: "Wow! It's expensive!"

Listen to two friends shopping. Listen carefully to discover the color, price, and if they buy each item!

1. Tablet

Listen to Alex & Maya discuss the tablet.

Alex: Look at this tablet! What color is it?

Maya: It's black. Wow, it's $399!

Alex: Ouch! That's too expensive. Let's not buy it.

2. Headphones

Listen to Alex & Maya discuss the headphones.

Alex: How about these headphones?

Maya: These red ones? They're $45!

Alex: That's a great price! Let's get them!

3. Sunglasses

Listen to Alex & Maya discuss sunglasses.

Maya: Look at those brown sunglasses!

Alex: Wow, they are $150.

Maya: $150? No way, that's expensive.

4. T-shirt

Listen to Alex & Maya discuss the T-shirt.

Alex: Check out this blue T-shirt!

Maya: It's only $18! Very nice.

Alex: Perfect! Let's buy it right now.
