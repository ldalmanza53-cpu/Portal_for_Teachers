Real Context & Scenario

At The City Market Snack Stand

Students enter a bustling city food market! One student plays a customer with a specific budget who wants to order snacks and drinks. The other plays the market vendor who handles prices, calculates totals, and answers questions about preferences and menu options.

Role A: Customer
  • Ask about menu item prices.
  • Express food & drink preferences.
  • Compare prices (cheap vs. expensive).
  • Order items and check cash change.
Role B: Market Vendor
  • Greet customer and state prices clearly.
  • Suggest items based on customer preference.
  • Calculate total order costs.
  • Confirm cash handed and give change.
