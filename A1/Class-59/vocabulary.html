Base Form

BIG

Click card to reveal Comparative, Superlative & Rules
Base: Big
Comparative: Bigger
Superlative: The Biggest

Meaning: Large in size or amount.

Common Collocations: Big city, big difference, big ocean.

Example Sentences:

"Tokyo is bigger than London."

"Jupiter is the biggest planet in our solar system."

💡
Common Mistake Note: Do not say "more big". Short 1-syllable adjectives take -er (bigger) and double the last consonant.
