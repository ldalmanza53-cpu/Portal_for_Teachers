Grammar Rule Guide

Module 1: Comparative Adjective Builder

Learn how to change short adjectives into comparative forms (adjective + -er + than). Pay attention to double consonants!

Standard Rule: + -er than

Add -er than to normal adjectives.

cold → colder than | long → longer than

Double Consonant Rule

Double the last letter if standard 1-vowel pattern.

big → bigger than | hot → hotter than
