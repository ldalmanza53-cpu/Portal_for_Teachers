✏️ Step 2: Sentence & Preposition Builder

Remember: Use AT + exact time (e.g., at 7:00 AM) and IN THE + part of day (e.g., in the morning).

Exact Time: AT
Build: "I wake up at 7:00 AM."
Part of Day: IN THE
Build: "I watch TV in the evening."
