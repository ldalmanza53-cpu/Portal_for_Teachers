📌 Adverb Position Rules

• Before main verbs: I always jog in the morning.

• After "don't / doesn't": I don't usually have breakfast.

• After verb "to be": She is rarely late for work.

⏱️ Time Expressions Position

• Placed at the end of the sentence:

twice a week • every evening • once a year • very often

• Example: I lift weights about three times a month.
