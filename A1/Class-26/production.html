🌍 Real-World Context: The Flatmate Compatibility Interview

Pair Work

You and your partner are young professionals/university students living in a busy city. A sunny, affordable two-bedroom apartment has just become available, and you are considering signing a 12-month lease together.

Before signing the contract, you need to have an honest 10-minute lifestyle interview. Sharing a bathroom, kitchen, and quiet study/work hours only works if your daily routines are compatible!

Role A • Current Tenant Leads Mins 1–5

The Leaseholder (Host)

  • You already hold the lease and work/study from home three days a week.
  • You need a reliable flatmate who respects quiet hours after 10:30 PM and shares kitchen chores fairly.
  • Your Goal: Ask 5–6 outcome prompts from Tab 2, ask follow-up questions ("Why?", "How often?"), and note potential conflicts.
Useful Starter Lines:

"To make sure we get along, walk me through your typical weekday..."

"How often do you tend to stay up late during the week?"

Role B • Applicant Leads Mins 5–9

The Prospective Flatmate

  • You really want this apartment because it is close to your university/office, and you want to make a great impression.
  • Answer honestly about your morning, cooking, social, and weekend habits—while showing where you can be flexible.
  • Your Goal: Give detailed B1 answers (using frequency adverbs & used to / tend to), then switch roles at 5:00 to interview Role A!
Useful Starter Lines:

"On a regular weekday, I usually wake up around..."

"I used to be messy in college, but nowadays I always..."

1. B1 Habit Structures

tend to + infinitive🔊

"I tend to wake up early even on weekends."

used to + infinitive (past routine)🔊

"I used to stay up until 2 AM, but now I sleep by 11."

be used to + -ing (accustomed to)🔊

"I am used to sharing a kitchen with roommates."

2. B1 Frequency Expressions

on a regular basis / every other day🔊

"On a regular basis, I meal prep on Sunday afternoons."

every once in a while / from time to time🔊

"Every once in a while, I order takeout instead of cooking."

seldom / rarely / hardly ever🔊

"I seldom watch TV during the workweek."
