When rewriting negative advice (shouldn't), place not directly before the infinitive verb: It's important NOT to drink milk. (Do not say: It's important to don't drink milk).

Unit 12 Grammar Focus Reference

Complete overview of modals for requests/suggestions & infinitives with adjectives/nouns.

📌 Adjective + Infinitive; Noun + Infinitive

Use It's + adjective/noun phrase + to + verb to give advice or make recommendations.

Adjective: It's important to get some rest.

Adjective: It's sometimes helpful to drink garlic tea.

Noun Phrase: It's a good idea to take vitamin C.

Negative Statement: Put not before to.
✓ With a cold, it's important not to exercise too hard.
✗ it's important to don't exercise

🛍️ Modals: Requests & Suggestions

Use modal verbs at the pharmacy to ask for products or give suggestions.

Making Requests (Asking):

• Can / May I help you?

• Can I have a bag of cough drops?

• Could I have something for a cough?

• May I have a bottle of pain medicine?

Giving Suggestions (Offering):

• What do you suggest / have for a backache?

• You could try this new cream.

• You should get a heating pad.

• Why don't you try these pills?
