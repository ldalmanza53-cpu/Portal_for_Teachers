Prepositions of Time - Grammar Helper

AT (Specific times, noon/night)

Use AT for exact clock hours, noon, and night.

Examples: at 7:00 AM, at 8:30 PM, at noon, at night

IN (Parts of the day)

Use IN for general periods of the day.

Examples: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening

ON (Days and weekends)

Use ON for days of the week, dates, and weekend days.

Examples: on weekdays, on Saturday mornings, on weekends

BEFORE / AFTER / FROM... TO

BEFORE / AFTER: Sequence of actions (e.g., before work, after dinner).

FROM... TO...: Time ranges (e.g., from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM).
