Grammar Rulebook

Future Intentions: Verb + Infinitive

To express future plans, hopes, and expectations, pair a main verb with an infinitive (to + base verb).

Affirmative Statements

Subject + Main Verb + Infinitive (to + base verb)

I plan to travel abroad.

I would love to buy a sports car.

Negative Statements

Don't / Doesn't + Main Verb + Infinitive OR Verb + not + to-infinitive

I don't plan to live with my parents.

I hope not to become famous.

Future Questions

Do + Subject + Verb + Infinitive? / What + would you like + to-infinitive?

Do you plan to study English?

What would you like to learn?
