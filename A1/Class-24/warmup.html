A1 Essential Rules

Prepositions of Time, Location & Detail

Learn when to use IN, ON, AT, and WITH when describing your activities.

1

Time Prepositions

  • AT + exact clock time / night "at 8:00 AM", "at 5:30 PM", "at night"
  • IN + parts of the day / months "in the morning", "in the evening"
  • ON + specific days / weekends "on Mondays", "on weekends"
2

Location Prepositions

  • AT + specific places / institutions "at home", "at school", "at work", "at the gym"
  • IN + enclosed areas / parks "in the park", "in the garden", "in the kitchen"
3

Companions & Frequency

  • WITH + people / friends "with my family", "with my best friend"
  • ADVERBS (Before verb) "I always cook", "I usually run"
