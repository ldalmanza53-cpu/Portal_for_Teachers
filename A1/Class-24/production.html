🏡 Real Context & Scenario

Students attend a Community Lifestyle Exchange Fair at their local community center. Members interview each other about their daily routines and hobbies to find out exactly where, when, with whom, for how long, and with what items they do their activities.

Target Grammar & Focus
Adding Rich Details to Basic Verbs (Where, When, Who, How long, Tools)

Example: "I study English at the library on Tuesdays with my classmate for one hour using my laptop."

👥 Student Roles
Role A: Lifestyle Interviewer Interviewer

Asks specific detail questions using WH-words (e.g., "Where do you study?", "What time do you start?", "Who do you go with?", "What tools do you bring?").

Role B: Community Member Respondent

Describes their daily and leisure activities with rich details including exact locations, times, duration, companions, and equipment.

Complete Detailed Sentence:

💡
Teacher Tip: Encourage students to answer with complete sentences containing at least 3 detail dimensions (e.g. Where + When + Companion).
❓ WH-Detail Questions
Where? → Location
"Where do you play tennis?"
When / What time? → Schedule
"What time do you start?"
Who with? → Company
"Who do you go with?"
⏱️ Duration & Equipment
How long? → Duration
"How long does it take?"
👉 "For 30 minutes / for 2 hours."
What items/tools? → Equipment
"What do you bring/use?"
👉 "Using my laptop / notebook."
📍 Preposition Guide
  • Locations: in my room, at the park, at school
  • Days & Times: on Saturdays, at 8:00 AM
  • Companions: with my brother, with my classmate
💬 Summary Validation

Summarize Details:
"So, you [activity] at [place] on [time] with [person] for [duration], correct?"

Confirm Answer:
"Yes, that's right!" / "No, I do it for two hours."
