🎪 Real Context & Scenario

The neighborhood is organizing a grand Community Family Reunion Feast. To reserve seating, order food, and issue entry badges, event coordinators at the registration desk must register every family and record exact quantities (adults, children, pets, chairs, and special meals).

Target Grammar & Vocabulary
Cardinal Numbers + Quantity Expressions (How many, a lot of, some, no, there are)

Examples: "How many chairs do you need?" • "We have 4 adults and some pets." • "So you have 6 people in total, correct?"

👥 Student Roles & Interaction
Role A: Reunion Coordinator Interviewer

Asks questions about family size, pets, chairs, and meals. Records the numbers and validates them by repeating the totals back to the guest.

Role B: Family Guest Respondent

Answers questions about their family members, numbers, pets, and event preferences using cardinal numbers and quantity terms.

🔢 Cardinal Numbers
  • 1 - one / 2 - two / 3 - three
  • 4 - four / 5 - five / 6 - six
  • 7 - seven / 8 - eight / 9 - nine

📊 Quantity Words
  • How many...? (Questions)
  • A lot of... (Large quantity)
  • Some... (Medium quantity)
  • No / Not any... (Zero quantity)
❓ Asking Questions
  • "How many people live in your house?"
  • "How many children do you have?"
  • "Do you have any pets?"
  • "How many chairs do you need?"
✅ Validation Phrases
  • "So, you have [number] people..."
  • "...is that correct?"
  • "In total, you need [number] chairs."
  • "Thank you! Your registration is complete."
