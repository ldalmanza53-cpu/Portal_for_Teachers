📖 A1 Quick Grammar Reference

1. Asking Questions: "How many...?"

Use How many + [plural noun] + are there / do you have?

  • "How many brothers do you have?"
  • "How many people are there in your family?"

2. "There is" vs. "There are"

There is = 1 singular item | There are = 2 or more (plural)

  • "There is one dog in my house." (1 item)
  • "There are five people in my family." (5 items)

3. Expressing Possession: "have" / "has"

I / You / We / They $\rightarrow$ have | He / She / It $\rightarrow$ has

  • "I have two sisters."
  • "He has three cousins."
