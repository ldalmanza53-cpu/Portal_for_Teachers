Story Grammar Explanations

1. Completing Milestones & Wants: Use regular past finished for finishing high school and wanted for past desires.

2. Mental States & Searches: Irregular knew describes mental state. Phrasal verb looked for expresses searching online.

3. Infinitive of Purpose ("To save"): Sentence 4 starts with "To save money...". When expressing intention or reason, use To + base form (save).

4. Narrative Action Sequence: Irregular bought (ticket) → regular registered → regular started taking classes.
