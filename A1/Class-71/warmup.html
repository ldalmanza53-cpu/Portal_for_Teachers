Describing Changes (Past → Now)

Use Present Perfect ("I've moved to a new city"), Simple Past ("I got a new job"), Present ("I'm not in school anymore"), or Comparatives ("My life is busier now").

Describing Future Plans (Now → Ahead)

Use Verb + Infinitive: hope to, plan to, want to, would like to ('d love to) + base verb ("I hope to travel abroad") or be going to ("I'm going to learn Italian").
