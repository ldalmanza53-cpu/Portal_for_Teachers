INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY CENTER

A1 ESL Speaking Activity • Welcome Social Meet-and-Greet

Focus: Validate & Complement Level: A1 Beginner
🌐 Real Context

You are attending a Welcome Social at a local International Community Center. Participants mingle in groups of 3 to greet each other, validate details they heard about someone, complement the information with positive comments, and practice friendly farewells.

👥 Student Roles
  • Role A (Host): Greets visitor, introduces self and Role B, validates known facts (*"You are Sarah, right?"*).
  • Role B (Resident): Welcomes newcomer, complements facts (*"Rome is beautiful!"*), shares background.
  • Role C (Newcomer): Responds to greetings, confirms information, shares hobbies, practices polite farewells.
