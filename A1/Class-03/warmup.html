A1 Grammar Reference & Matrix

1st Person vs. 3rd Person Matrix

Master the switch between talking about yourself ($I / My$) and introducing someone else ($He / His$ or $She / Her$).

Person Subject Pronoun Possessive Adjective Example Sentence
Self (1st Person) I My "I am Marco. My name is Marco."
Other Male (3rd Person) He His "This is Marco. He is from Italy. His name is Marco."
Other Female (3rd Person) She Her "This is Sarah. She is a teacher. Her name is Sarah."
