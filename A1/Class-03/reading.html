Level B1–C1 • Topic 3 (10 Interactive Exercises)

🎯 Contrastive Tonic Stress & Heteronym Speed Lab

In English, shifting pitch prominence (tonic stress) onto a single word transforms the entire pragmatic subtext—just like in "I didn't say he took the keys" or "She didn't buy the red car."

💡 Ayuda ES: En los ejercicios 1–6, haz clic sobre la palabra exacta de la oración que debe llevar el acento tónico para expresar el subtexto indicado. En los 7–10, elige la transcripción fonética (IPA) y significado correcto.
Level B1–C1 • Topic 4 (10 Interactive Exercises)

⚡ Noun–Verb Homograph Stress-Shift & Spoken Reductions Lab

Two-syllable homographs typically take 1st-syllable stress as Nouns (RE-cord) and 2nd-syllable stress as Verbs (re-CORD). In Exercises 7–10, decode rapid American connected speech reductions.

💡 Ayuda ES: Los sustantivos (Nouns) suelen acentuarse en la 1.ª sílaba (RE-cord) y los verbos (Verbs) en la 2.ª sílaba (re-CORD).
