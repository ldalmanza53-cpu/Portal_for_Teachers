The Preference Scale (Love to Hate)

In English, we use different verbs to show how much we like or dislike something. Click any phrase to hear its pronunciation!

❤️ Love

I love...

Very strong positive feeling (+100%)

"I love pizza!" 🍕
👍 Like

I like...

Positive feeling (+50%)

"I like football." ⚽
😐 Don't mind

I don't mind...

Neutral feeling (0%)

"I don't mind cooking." 🍳
👎 Don't like

I don't like...

Negative feeling (-50%)

"I don't like rain." 🌧️
💔 Hate

I hate...

Very strong negative (-100%)

"I hate spiders!" 🕷️

Grammar Rule for A1 Learners

Rule 1: Use with Nouns

Subject + Verb + Noun

Example: "I like cats." / "She loves ice cream."

Rule 2: Use with Action Verbs (-ing)

Subject + Verb + Verb-ing

Example: "I like swimming." / "I hate waking up early."
