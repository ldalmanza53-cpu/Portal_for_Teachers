A1 Beginner Speaking Activity

International Student Speed Friending

Students attend a fast-paced "Speed Friending Event" to meet classmates! They practice asking and answering questions about food, sports, weather, entertainment, and hobbies using target structures: "I love...", "I like...", "I don't like...", "I hate...", "Do you like...?" and reacting with "Me too!" or "Not me!".

10 Minutes Total
Pair Work (A1 Level)
20 Task Prompts
🎤

Role A: Friend A (Interviewer)

Initiates questions & discovers likes

Interviewer
  • Ask Questions: Pick a card and ask "Do you like [topic]?" or "What is your favorite...?"
  • React: Agree or disagree using "Me too!", "Not me!", or "Really?"
  • Share: State your own likes and dislikes clearly.

Role A Target Sentence Frames:

• "Do you like [cooking / cats / rainy days]?"

• "What is your favorite [food / sport / season]?"

• "Me too! I love [topic]!" / "Oh, not me! I hate it."

😊

Role B: Friend B (Respondent)

Shares preferences & gives reasons

Respondent
  • Express Opinions: Answer using "I love...", "I like...", "I don't like...", "I hate...".
  • Add Details: Give a simple reason ("because it is fun / warm / nice").
  • Ask Back: Ask "How about you?" to keep the conversation flowing.

Role B Target Sentence Frames:

• "Yes, I love [dogs] because they are friendly!"

• "No, I don't like [early mornings]."

• "I prefer [coffee] more than [tea]. How about you?"

Teacher 10-Minute Activity Flow

Step 1: Setup 2 Mins

Review core expressions: love, like, don't like, hate, prefer. Model one dialogue: "Do you like pizza?" -> "Yes, I love it!"

Step 2: Pair Practice 6 Mins

Pairs complete outcome prompts. At the 3-minute mark, the role swap alert triggers automatically so partners switch roles.

Step 3: Wrap-Up Poll 2 Mins

Class check-in: Ask "Who found a shared hobby?", "Who hates waking up early?", "Who loves rainy days?"

Agreeing with Preferences

Same Opinion (Positive):

A: "I love dogs!" → B: "Me too!"

Same Opinion (Negative):

A: "I don't like rain." → B: "Me neither!"

Disagreeing / Different Opinions

Different Opinion (Positive):

A: "I like waking up early." → B: "Oh, I don't! I hate early mornings."

Different Opinion (Negative):

A: "I don't like video games." → B: "Really? I love them!"
