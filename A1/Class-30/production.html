Community Travel Agency: Vacation Memory Verification

Imagine you work at a local community travel agency. Before publishing the community newsletter, review staff must interview travelers to verify facts and details from their recent vacations, confirming correct information and correcting mistakes using Simple Past tense.

🔍

Role A: Travel Fact-Checker

Reviewer / Interviewer
  • ✓ Ask verification questions using Did you...? / Was it...? / Were you...?
  • ✓ Check draft notes on partner's transport, weather, accommodation, and food.
  • ✓ Record 3 confirmed facts in the Partner Verification Sheet.
💡 Target Phrase: "Did you travel by plane?" / "Was the weather sunny?"
🧳

Role B: Traveler / Vacationer

Interviewee / Witness
  • ✓ Confirm or correct facts using short answers and past tense statements.
  • ✓ Answer with: "Yes, I did!" or "No, I didn't. I stayed in an apartment."
  • ✓ Provide accurate details to update the agency's records.
💡 Target Phrase: "No, I didn't go by plane, I went by bus!"

❓ Verification Questions
  • Did + Subject + Base Verb...?

    "Did you travel by plane?" / "Did you eat seafood?"

  • Was + Subject (singular)...?

    "Was the weather sunny?" / "Was the trip cheap?"

  • Were + Subject (plural)...?

    "Were you happy?" / "Were they friendly?"

✅ Confirmations (Short Answers)
  • Yes, I did.

    "Yes, I did. I stayed in a hotel."

  • Yes, it was.

    "Yes, it was very warm and sunny."

  • Yes, we were.

    "Yes, we were happy with the hotel."

❌ Corrections Formula
  • No, I didn't + base verb...

    "No, I didn't travel by plane."

  • + I + Past Verb (Correction)

    "...I traveled by bus."

  • Complete Formula: "No, I didn't [X]. I [Y]."
