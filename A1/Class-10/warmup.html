📐 Singular vs. Plural Price Rules
Learn how English changes between singular items (one) and plural items (two or more / pair).
Item Type Question Structure Answer Structure Examples
Singular
(1 item)		 How much is this [item]? It's $[Price]. • Coffee ($3.50)
• T-Shirt ($15.00)
• Umbrella ($12.00)
Plural
(2+ items or pairs)		 How much are these [items]? They're $[Price]. • Shoes ($45.00)
• Bananas ($2.50)
• Sunglasses ($20.00)
