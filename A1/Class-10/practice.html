Grammar Focus Reference

Study rules for singular/plural demonstratives, one/ones pronouns, and price reactions.

1. Demonstratives & Pronoun Substitution

Singular Nouns (backpack, cap):

• Question: How much is this / that backpack?

• Clarification: Which one? The orange one.

• Answer: It's $36.99.

Plural Nouns (boots, jeans, sunglasses):

• Question: How much are these / those boots?

• Clarification: Which ones? The brown ones.

• Answer: They're $95.50.

2. Price Evaluation Expressions

That's cheap! $ (Low price)
That's reasonable. $$ (Fair price)
That's OK / not bad. $$ (Acceptable price)
Wow! That's expensive! $$$ (High price)
