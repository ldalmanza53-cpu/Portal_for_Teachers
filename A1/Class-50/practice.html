Grammar Reference: Adjective / Noun + Infinitive

Adjective + Infinitive

Structure: It's + [Adjective] + [to + verb]

✓ It's important to get some rest.

✓ It's helpful to drink garlic tea.

✓ It's necessary to see a doctor.

Noun Phrase + Infinitive

Structure: It's + [Noun Phrase] + [to + verb]

✓ It's a good idea to take vitamin C.

✓ It's a mistake to ignore a high fever.

⚠️ Common Grammar Pitfalls to Avoid:

  • Do NOT omit "to": Say "It's important to rest" (NOT "It's important rest").
  • Do NOT use "-ing": Say "It's a good idea to drink water" (NOT "It's a good idea drinking water").
