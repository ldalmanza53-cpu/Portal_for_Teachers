CEFR A1 Core Grammar

Personal Information Grammar Rules

Master Yes/No questions, short negative answers, affirmative corrections, and the strict A vs. AN article rule.

1. Asking Yes/No Questions with "Be"

Invert the verb Be (Is / Are) and the subject pronoun (he / she / it / you / they).

Country & Age "Is he from Spain?"
"Is she 24 years old?"
Occupations (With Article!) "Is he a chef?"
"Is she an artist?"

2. The Indefinite Article Rule: A vs. AN

Singular occupations always require an indefinite article. Use A before consonant sounds and AN before vowel sounds (A, E, I, O, U).

A + Consonant Sound
  • a chef
  • a teacher
  • a doctor / pilot
AN + Vowel Sound (A, E, I, O, U)
  • an artist (Vowel A)
  • an engineer (Vowel E)
  • an architect / accountant

3. Correcting False Statements

Combine the short negative answer with the true affirmative statement.

Q: "Is Mario from Italy?"
Negative: "No, he isn't."
Correction: "He is from Spain."
Q: "Is Sofia a doctor?"
Negative: "No, she isn't."
Correction: "She is an artist."
