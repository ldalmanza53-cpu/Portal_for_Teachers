A1 Grammar Cheat Sheet

Confirming & Correcting with "To Be"

Learn how to ask Yes/No questions and make corrections!

1. Asking Confirmation Questions

Put Is at the beginning of the question:

Country:
"Is he/she from [Country]?"
Job:
"Is he/she a [Job]?"
Age:
"Is he/she [Age] years old?"

2. Confirming (When True)

"Yes, he / she is."

3. Correcting (When False)

Short negative answer + full true statement:

"No, he/she isn't. He/She is [Correct Fact]."

Example: "No, he isn't. He is from Spain."
