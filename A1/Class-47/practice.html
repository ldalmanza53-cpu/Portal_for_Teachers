Modal Verbs: Can vs. Should

1. CAN / CAN'T (Possibility / Ability)

Used to talk about options, choices, or things that are possible to do.

• "What can I do in Mexico City?"

• "You can walk around the historic center."

• "You can't miss the street food." (Strongly recommended / must-see)

2. SHOULD / SHOULDN'T (Advice / Recommendation)

Used to give advice, suggestions, or express what is a good/bad idea.

• "What should I see there?"

• "You should visit the National Museum."

• "You shouldn't miss the Diego Rivera murals."

B: No, you definitely use the subway. It's fast and not too expensive.

4. Shopping in Istanbul

A: Where I get some nice jewelry in Istanbul?

B: You miss the Grand Bazaar. It's the best place for bargains.

5. Eiffel Tower View

A: What I see from the Eiffel Tower?

B: You see all of Paris, but in bad weather, you see anything.
