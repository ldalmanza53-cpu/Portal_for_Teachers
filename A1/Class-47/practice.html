    Grammar Reference: Modal Verbs

    CAN (Possibility / Ability)

    Expresses possibilities or options available to a traveler.

    "What can I do in Mexico City?"
    "You can walk around the historic center."
    CAN'T (Impossibility)

    Expresses impossibility or something that isn't allowed/feasible.

    "In bad weather, you can't see anything."
    "You can't visit on Mondays (it's closed)."
    SHOULD (Advice / Recommendation)

    Used to give strong advice, opinions, or good ideas.

    "What should I see there?"
    "You should visit the museum."
    SHOULDN'T (Negative Advice)

    Used to advise someone NOT to skip or miss something important.

    "You shouldn't miss the street food!"
    "You shouldn't skip the old town."
