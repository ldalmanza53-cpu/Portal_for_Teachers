💡 Possibility vs. Suggestion Formula
1. Possibility (Option):
You can + base verb
Example: "You can visit the museum." (It is an available option)
2. Suggestion (Good Idea):
You should + base verb
Example: "You should try the local street food!" (It is recommended)
3. Asking for Ideas:
What can I do in [City]? / Where should I go?
Example: "What can I do in Bogotá?"
4. Direct Imperative:
[Base Verb] + [Place]!
Example: "Don't miss the local market!"
