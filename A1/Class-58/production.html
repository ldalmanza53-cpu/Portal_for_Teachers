🍔 The Campus Diner

Choosing & Ordering Lunch • Agreeing & Disagreeing Practice

📍 Context & Learning Objectives

Two university friends meet at a busy campus diner for lunch between classes. They look at the menu together to compare food likes and dislikes (agreeing and disagreeing), then practice ordering food and drinks at the counter.

📋 Student Instructions:
1 Work in Pairs: Decide who is Student A and Student B.
2 Phase 1 (4 Mins): Student A states preferences & orders. Student B agrees/disagrees & acts as server.
3 Phase 2 (4 Mins): Swap roles! Student B states preferences & orders.
👤 Role A (Friend / Customer) Shares likes/dislikes & places lunch orders.
🧑‍🍳 Role B (Friend / Server) Agrees/disagrees & confirms the order.
