Grammar Rulebook VRB-108 Key Notes

📚 Would and Will: Ordering Food & Polite Requests

Learn the difference between informal speech and standard restaurant English etiquette.

👨‍🍳 Server Questions (Would) Would you like...?

Servers use "Would you like...?" to make offers politely. Avoid asking direct "Do you want...?" questions in service settings.

❌ Direct / Informal:
"What do you want to eat?"
✅ Polite / Server English:
"What would you like to eat?"
❌ Direct / Informal:
"Do you want salad or soup with that?"
✅ Polite / Server English:
"Would you like salad or soup with that?"
👤 Customer Ordering (Will / Would) I'll have... / I'd like...

When ordering, native speakers use "I'll have..." (instant decision) or "I'd like..." (polite request).

❌ Blunt / Demanding:
"I want the steak."
✅ Polite Customer Speech:
"I'll have the steak, please."
❌ Direct:
"Give me coffee."
✅ Polite Customer Speech:
"I'd like a coffee, please."

⚡ Contractions Breakdown Cheat Sheet

I'll = I will
Decision at the moment of ordering
I'd = I would
Expressing polite desire
