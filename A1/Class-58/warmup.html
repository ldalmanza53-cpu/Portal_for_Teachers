Negotiation & Ordering Phrases
Practice listening and repeating key expressions for agreeing, politely disagreeing, and placing orders.

Suggesting & Agreeing

"Why don't we share the garlic bread?"
Use for friendly starters suggestions
"How about we order the Caesar salad?"
Making a polite food proposal
"I completely agree! That sounds delicious."
Strong enthusiastic agreement
"Good idea! I'd love to try that."
Friendly agreement

Politely Disagreeing

"I'm not sure about that. I don't really like seafood."
Softening a disagreement with personal preference
"I'd prefer steak instead of chicken."
Offering an alternative choice
"That's a nice thought, but I'm allergic to nuts."
Stating dietary restrictions gently
"Could we get something else instead?"
Redirecting choices smoothly

Ordering Food & Speaking to Server

"Excuse me, we are ready to order."
Calling the waiter
"I would like the grilled salmon with vegetables, please."
Ordering main course
"Could I have a fresh lemonade, please?"
Ordering drinks
"We'll take the chocolate cake to share."
Ordering shared dessert
