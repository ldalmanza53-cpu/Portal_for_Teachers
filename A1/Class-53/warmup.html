Grammar Cheat Sheet (A1)

1. "HAVE A / HAS A" + Health NOUN

Use with specific physical sickness nouns:

  • I / You / We / They have a headache / cold / fever.
  • He / She / Mark has a stomachache / toothache.

2. "FEEL / FEELS" + Symptom ADJECTIVE

Use with descriptive feelings or state adjectives:

  • I / You / We / They feel tired / dizzy / sick.
  • He / She / Sara feels dizzy / sick / exhausted.
