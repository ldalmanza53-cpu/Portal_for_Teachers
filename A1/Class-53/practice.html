Grammar Guide: Explained Solution

1. Polite Requests

Use May or Could before the subject to politely ask for items or permission:

  • May I help you? / Could I help you?
  • May I have something for...?
  • Could I have something else?

2. Giving Suggestions and Advice

Notice how the structure changes depending on the verb used:

  • should + base verb: "You should drink water."
  • suggest + noun: "I suggest an old-fashioned bowl of soup."
  • try + to + infinitive: "try to get some rest."
  • try + noun (gentle imperative): "try these other pills."
