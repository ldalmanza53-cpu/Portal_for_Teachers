1. At work (En el trabajo)

A: Here's the perfect cold remedy: garlic juice, onions, and carrots. You should drink a cup every two hours.

B: But I don't like carrots.

A: Well, then I an old-fashioned bowl of chicken soup! And to get some rest, too.

2. At a drugstore (En la farmacia)

A: I help you?

B: Yes. I have something for a cold? It's a bad one.

A: Yes. I have these pills. They're a little strong. Just don't drive after you take them.

B: Hmm. I drive to work. I have something else?

A: Well, these other pills then. They won't make you sleepy.

GUION PERSONALIZADO DE ROLEPLAY

Guía Gramatical: Solución Explicada

1. Peticiones Educadas (Requests)

Usa May o Could precedidos por el sujeto para pedir productos o permiso de forma amable:

  • May I help you? / Could I help you? (¿Puedo ayudarle?)
  • May I have something for...? (¿Podría darme algo para...?)
  • Could I have something else? (¿Podría darme otra cosa?)

2. Dar Sugerencias y Consejos

Diferencia la estructura según el verbo utilizado:

  • should + verbo base: "You should drink water."
  • suggest + sustantivo: "I suggest an old-fashioned bowl of soup."
  • try + to + infinitivo: "try to get some rest."
  • try + sustantivo (imperativo suave): "try these other pills."
