Connecting Ideas with Conjunctions

1. AND (Similarity / Addition)

Connects two similar or positive ideas into a single sentence.

"Los Angeles is a big city, and the weather is nice."

2. BUT (Contrast in one sentence)

Connects two contrasting ideas in a single sentence using a comma.

"Boston is a big city, but it's not too big."

3. THOUGH (Contrast at sentence end)

Placed at the end of the second sentence, preceded by a comma.

"It's a big city. It's not too big, though."

4. HOWEVER (Formal Contrast)

Used between two separate sentences or at the end of the second sentence.

"It's a big city. It's not too big, however."
