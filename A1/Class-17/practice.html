Sentence Transformation

1. Original: "His age is about 32."

Rewrite using age range: "He is in his ____."

2. Original: "She is 1.78 meters tall."

Rewrite in spoken metric height: "She's 1 ____ 78."

3. Original: "Does he use corrective eye glasses?"

Rewrite with alternative eyewear: "No, he wears ____."

4. Original: "Describe her physical appearance."

Rewrite as a question: "What ____ she ____ like?"

5. Original: "His hair is not long at all."

Rewrite using 'pretty': "His hair is pretty ____."

💡 Grammar Cheat Sheet: Describing People

1. Asking Questions

  • General appearance: What does s/he look like?
  • Height: How tall is s/he?
  • Hair: How long is her hair? / What color is his hair?
  • Age: How old is s/he?

2. Saying Heights

U.S. System: five (foot) ten / 5'10" / five foot ten inches (tall)

Metric System: 1 meter 78 / one meter seventy-eight tall / 178 cm

3. Age Ranges & Common Errors

✔️ She's about 32. / She's in her thirties. / He's in his twenties.

❌ Spanish Speaker Error: DON'T say "He HAS 20 years". Use "BE": "He IS 20 years old."

❌ DON'T say "How is he like?". Say "WHAT DOES HE LOOK LIKE?".
