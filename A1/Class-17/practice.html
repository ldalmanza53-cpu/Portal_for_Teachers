Subject vs. Object Pronouns

Object pronouns receive the action or follow prepositions (like, stand, in).

Subject Object Target Context in Movies Audio
I / You me / you People ("They make me laugh")
He him Male actor (Leonardo DiCaprio)
She her Female actress (Keira Knightley)
It it Singular Movie / Genre (Sci-Fi, 3-D)
They them Plural Movies (Horror films, Rom-Coms)
Pro Tip: Singular vs Plural

Use it for single items or non-countable genres (science fiction, 3-D). Use them for plural genres ending in -s (horror films, comedies, thrillers).

Giving Reasons & Expressing Opinions

Asking "Why?" and building full justification structures.

1. Asking Questions Why? / Why not?

Positive statement: "I love horror movies." → "Really? Why?"

Negative statement: "I don't like thrillers." → "Why not?"

2. Answering with "Because..." Explaining reason

• "Because I don't like to feel scared."

• "Because they make me laugh."

• "Because special effects are better on a big screen."

3. Opinion Adjectives to Remember Vocabulary

Dull (Boring) Scary (Frightening) Hilarious (Very funny) Exciting (Full of action) Special (Unique/great)
