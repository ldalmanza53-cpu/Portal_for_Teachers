The Event Planning Survey

ESL A1 Speaking Activity • Likes, Dislikes & Reasons ("because...")

Level: A1 (Beginner) Duration: 10 Mins Pair Survey
10:00
Step 1: Setup & Target Language Teacher reviews grammar and introduces scenario (0 - 2 mins)

🌍 Real Context & Scenario

Students are helping organize an upcoming class event (a weekend picnic and party). To choose the best foods, drinks, and activities, they survey each other about their preferences and the reasons behind them using "because...".

Role A: Event Organizer / Survey Leader
Asks partner about their likes and dislikes, prompts them with "Why do you like / dislike it?", and notes down their answers.
Role B: Survey Participant
States their preference clearly and gives a simple reason using "because it is...".

⏱️ 10-Minute Teacher Breakdown

  • 1
    Setup & Review (2 Mins)

    Review core sentence frame on board: I like/love/dislike/hate [X] because it is [adjective]. Ask: Why?

  • 2
    Pair Practice (6 Mins)

    Students work in pairs. Student A surveys Student B using task prompts. At 3 minutes, signal to SWAP ROLES.

  • 3
    Quick Class Summary (2 Mins)

    Ask 2–3 pairs: What is one thing your partner likes and why?

🛠️ Survey Reason Builder

💬 Dialogue Exchange Preview

"Do you like pizza? Why?"
"I love pizza because it is delicious!"

❓ Survey Questions

  • Do you like [X]?
  • Why do you like / dislike it?
  • What is your favorite...?

❤️ Expressing Preferences

  • I love [X] because...
  • I like [X] because...
  • I don't like [X] because...
  • I hate [X] because...

✨ Reason Adjectives

  • delicious / sweet / healthy
  • fun / exciting / relaxing
  • cold / warm / noisy / quiet
  • expensive / cheap / hard / easy
