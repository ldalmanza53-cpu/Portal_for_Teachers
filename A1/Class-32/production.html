Activity Overview & Role Play Rules

Real Context: International Student Club Welcome Party mingler in triads.

Role A The Connector

Welcomes & Introduces

Welcomes new members to the club. Introduces self first, then introduces friend (Role B) to newcomer (Role C).

🎯 Practice: Prompts 1–10 (Self & Others)
Role B The Friend

Responds & Shares Origin

Stands with Role A. Responds politely when introduced to Role C and shares basic origin or student status details.

🎯 Practice: Prompts 11–15 (Responding)
Role C The Newcomer

Joins & Asks Questions

Joins the group conversation, introduces self warmly, and asks simple follow-up questions to keep speaking.

🎯 Practice: Prompts 16–20 (Questions)
