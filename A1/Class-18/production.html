Real Context & Scenario

You are new members at a local Community Center attending a morning coffee break. You want to make new friends! Talk about your jobs, daily habits, what your family/friends do, entertainment you like, and invite each other to a weekend activity.

Target Competencies (Performance Check)

✓ Personal details & work (Self & Third Persons)
✓ Daily routines & habits
✓ Entertainment likes & dislikes
✓ Making, accepting & declining invitations

Roles & Interaction

Role A: Community Member A First Leader

Asks about Member B's work, habits, family/friends, and entertainment preferences. Invites Member B to a weekend activity.

Role B: Community Member B Respondent & Second Leader

Answers Member A's questions clearly. Accepts or declines the invitation with a reason. Swaps roles at 5 minutes!

Step 3: Check-Up

Teacher asks 2–3 pairs to report on their partner's habits, job, or weekend plans.

Work & Daily Routines

Where do you work?

I work in a hospital / school / office.

What time do you start work?

I start work at 8:00 AM every morning.

Third Person (He / She):

My brother is a teacher. He works in a high school and starts at 7:30 AM.

🎬

Entertainment Likes & Dislikes

Expressing Likes:

I love watching action movies because they are exciting.

Expressing Dislikes:

I don't like horror movies because they are scary.

Asking Preference:

Do you like listening to pop music or rock music?

💌

Making & Responding to Invitations

Making an Invitation:

Would you like to go to the cinema with me this Saturday at 6:00 PM?

Accepting:

Yes, I'd love to! That sounds great!

Declining with Reason:

Sorry, I can't. I have to work / study on Saturday.

🔔
