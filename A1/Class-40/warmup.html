Topic: Verb Choice (be vs. have got)
Needs Repair ❌

Incorrect A1 Sentence:

"She is got long curly brown hair."

Quick Doctor Hints

1. Use BE (is / are) for: Height, build & age: "He is tall and slim."
2. Use HAVE GOT (has got) for: Hair, eyes & facial features: "She has got blue eyes."
3. Use WEAR (wears) for: Glasses & clothes: "He wears glasses."
Module 4: Reference Guide

A1 Physical Appearance Cheat Sheet

Master the three core verbs and English adjective word order.

1. Verb: TO BE

Height • Build • General Look

  • He is tall and athletic.
  • She is short and slim.
  • My grandfather is bald.

2. Verb: HAVE GOT

Hair • Eyes • Beard / Freckles

  • She has got long wavy brown hair.
  • He has got green eyes and freckles.
  • He has got a beard and a mustache.

3. Verb: WEAR

Glasses • Accessories • Clothes

  • She wears reading glasses.
  • He wears cool sunglasses.
  • She wears a blue shirt.

Golden Rule: Hair Adjective Order

1. Length
long / short
2. Style
wavy / curly / straight
3. Color
blonde / brown / black
4. Noun
hair
