Community Event Review Club

Welcome to the weekly podcast review recording session! Members interview each other about recent activities, movies, dining, sports, or trips. Your goal is to describe what happened in the simple past tense and give your honest opinions with adjectives.

A

Role A: Review Host

Interviewer & Moderator

Asks Questions
  • Ask your partner about past events: "What did you do last weekend?"
  • Prompt for opinions: "How was it? Did you like it?"
  • Record partner's review details in the Podcast Review Log.
B

Role B: Guest Reviewer

Storyteller & Critic

Shares Experience
  • Describe what you did using past verbs: "Last Saturday, I went to a new restaurant."
  • Express your opinion clearly: "In my opinion, the food was delicious!"
  • Give a star rating (1 to 5 stars) and say why you liked or disliked it.
