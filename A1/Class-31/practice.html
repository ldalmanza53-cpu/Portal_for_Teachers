Past Tense Verbs (Action Verbs)

• Statements: I went to London. / I saw Tower Bridge.

• Questions: What did you see? / Did you go in?

• Negative: No, I didn't. / I didn't have time.

Past Tense of Verb "BE"

• Statements: It was really interesting.

• Questions: Was the Queen there?

• Negative: No, she wasn't.
