Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix

1st Person vs 3rd Person Singular
1st & 2nd Person (I / You / We / They)
  • Do you work in an office?
  • What time do you wake up?
  • Do they drink coffee?

Statement answer: I work in an office. (No -s on verb)

3rd Person Singular (He / She / Daniela)
  • Does she work in an office?
  • What time does Kenji wake up?
  • Does he drink coffee?

Statement answer: She works in an office. (+s on verb)
