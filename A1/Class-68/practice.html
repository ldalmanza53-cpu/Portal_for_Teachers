Grammar Guide: Expressing Changes Over Time

Notice the different tenses and structures used in the textbook exercise to talk about personal transformation:

Structure 1

Present Perfect

For actions completed in an unspecified time that affect the present.

• I've changed my hairstyle.
• I've joined a gym.
• I've made new friends.
Structure 2

Simple Past

For completed past events at a distinct or implied moment in life.

• I got a pet.
• I moved into my own apartment.
• I got married.
Structure 3

Comparatives & Adverbs

Using comparative adjectives or adverbs of manner to show change.

• I dress differently now.
• I'm more outgoing than before.
• My life is easier now.
Structure 4

Negatives & "Anymore"

Expressing that a past status or condition is no longer true today.

• I'm not in high school anymore.
• I don't live with my parents anymore.
