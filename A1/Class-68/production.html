A1 Speaking & Listening Activity

☕ The Life Update: Before & Now

Reunite with an old friend at a coffee shop and share how your home, job, habits, and hobbies have changed!

⏱️ 10-Minute Activity Timer
Stage 1: Prep
10:00
Phase 1: Preparation & Prompt Card Review (2 Mins)
👥 Context & Roles

Coffee Shop Catch-Up: You haven't seen your friend in 5 years! Compare your past life with your life today.

🔍 Role A: Old Friend
Asks questions about the past vs. present (*"Where do you live now?"*, *"Do you still...?"*).
💬 Role B: Storyteller
Describes their life changes (*"In the past, I lived in... but now I live in..."*).
🔄 Active Change Visualizer
⏪ Before (Past)
Lived in a small, quiet village.
⏩ Now (Present)
Lives in a huge, crowded city.
📝 Class Presentation Summary Builder
In the past, my partner lived in a small village, but now they live in a big city!
