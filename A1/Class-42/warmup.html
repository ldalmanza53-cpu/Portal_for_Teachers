Grammar Guide: General vs. Specific Past

General Past

Describes broad experiences, habits, or facts in the past without an exact clock time or precise date.

Signal Words:

  • in the past
  • when I was young / a child
  • before / long ago
  • lived in / studied at
  • many times / often

Example:

"I traveled to France when I was young."

Specific Past

Describes a single finished action that happened at an exact, verifiable time and place.

Signal Words:

  • yesterday at 4 PM
  • last night at 8:00
  • two hours ago
  • on Monday morning
  • at school / at the park

Example:

"I arrived in Paris yesterday at 4 PM."

💡 Detective's Rule: If you can point to a clock or a calendar for the exact moment, it's Specific! If it describes a long phase or broad life experience, it's General.
