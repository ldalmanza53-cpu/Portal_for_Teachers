Rule

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect vs. Simple Past

PRESENT PERFECT Indefinite time in the past

Question: "Have you ever eaten Cuban food?"

Answers: "Yes, I have. I've had it many times." / "No, I haven't."

SIMPLE PAST Specific event or finished time

Specific Event: "I ate a lot of Cuban food when I lived in Miami."

Finished Time: "I saw a big alligator at the park last week."

Rule

Grammar Focus: For vs. Since

FOR + Duration Length / Period of time

Examples: for two weeks, for a few months, for several years, for a long time, for six months.

"I've lived here for three years."

SINCE + Starting Point Specific moment in past

Examples: since 6:45, since last weekend, since 2009, since elementary school, since Monday.

"I've worked at the hotel since last year."
