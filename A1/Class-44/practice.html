📘 Present Perfect Grammar Focus

When to use "Have you ever...?"

Use Present Perfect with ever to ask about experiences in someone's entire life up to now (indefinite time).

Have + Subject + ever + Past Participle?

"Have you ever locked your keys in the car?"

Short Answers

When answering "Have you ever...?", respond with short answers or follow up with Simple Past for specific details:

  • ✅ Yes, I have. / Yes, I have done that.
  • ❌ No, I haven't. / No, I've never done that.
  • 💬 "Yes, I have. I locked them inside last year!"
