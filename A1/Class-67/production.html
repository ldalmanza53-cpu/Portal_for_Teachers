👥 Context & Roles

Real Context: You call a clinic, store, or friend, but nobody answers! Leave a brief 3-part message after the beep (*Who*, *Why*, *Contact Info/Action*).

📞 Role A: The Caller
Dials the number, waits for the beep, and speaks a clear voice message using the prompt.
📝 Role B: The Voicemail Host
Plays the voicemail greeting, listens, and records key details on the note slip.
