Physical Appearance Grammar Rules

Category Verb Correct Example ❌ Common Mistake
Height, Age & Build am / is / are She is tall and slim. She has got tall.
Hair, Eyes & Facial Features have got / has got He has got curly brown hair. He is curly brown hair.
Accessories & Clothes wear / wears They wear round glasses. They have got glasses eyes.

Pro Tip for A1 Learners:

Use is/are with adjectives (tall, short, slim, young, old). Use has got with nouns (hair, eyes, beard, mustache).
