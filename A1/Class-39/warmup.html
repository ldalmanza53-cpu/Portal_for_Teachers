ESL A1 Appearance Grammar Chart

1. Verb BE

Height, Build & Age

Use is / are + adjective:

  • He is tall / short.
  • She is slim / young.
  • They are elderly.
2. HAVE GOT

Hair, Eyes & Beard

Use has got / have got + noun:

  • She has got blue eyes.
  • He has got curly hair.
  • He has got a white beard.
3. Verb WEAR

Glasses, Hats & Clothes

Use wears / is wearing:

  • He wears black glasses.
  • She wears a red cap.
  • She wears gold earrings.

Adjective Order Tip:

Describe hair in this order: Length + Style + Color + hair → "long wavy brown hair" or "short curly black hair".
