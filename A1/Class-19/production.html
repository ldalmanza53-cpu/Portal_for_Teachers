Showing Family Photos at the Café

ESL A1 Speaking Activity • Family Members & Current Activities (Present Continuous)

Level: A1 (Beginner) Duration: 10 Mins Pair Practice
10:00
Step 1: Setup & Target Language Warm-Up Teacher reviews family vocabulary and Present Continuous structure (0 - 2 mins)

☕ Real Context & Scenario

Two classmates/friends meet at a café during lunch. They open their phone photo galleries and show pictures of their family members, asking and telling each other who everyone is and what they are doing right now or these days.

Role A: Photo Owner (Presenter)
Shows photos/describes family members, states their relationships, and explains what they are doing right now using the Present Continuous ("is working", "are cooking").
Role B: Curious Friend
Reacts to the descriptions, asks questions about Role A's family members and their current activities, and shares quick updates about their own family.

⏱️ 10-Minute Teacher Breakdown

  • 1
    Setup & Warm-Up (2 Mins)

    Review target language: Family words (mother, brother...) + Present Continuous ("My sister is studying right now").

  • 2
    Pair Practice (6 Mins)

    Students work in pairs using prompt cards or mobile photos. At 3 minutes, signal to SWAP ROLES.

  • 3
    Wrap-Up Check-in (2 Mins)

    Ask 2–3 pairs: What is Student B's brother doing right now? Is Student A's mother working today?

💡 How to Use the Gallery Tool

In pairs, Student A can tap a photo on this virtual phone to simulate showing a photo at the café. Practice asking and answering:

  • Question: "Who is this?" / "What is your father doing?"
  • Answer: "This is my father. He is working at his office today."
  • Follow-up: "Is he busy right now?"

👥 Family Vocabulary

  • mother / father / parents
  • brother / sister / siblings
  • grandmother / grandfather
  • aunt / uncle / cousin

⚡ Present Continuous Rules

  • I am + [verb]-ing
  • He / She / My brother is + [verb]-ing
  • They / My parents are + [verb]-ing
  • Is your [brother] working today?

🎯 Action Verbs (-ing)

  • working / studying / reading
  • cooking / eating / drinking
  • sleeping / relaxing / watching TV
  • shopping / driving / traveling
