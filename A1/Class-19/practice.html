Grammar Focus: Present Continuous

Expressing actions happening right now or around these days (Be + Verb-ing).

Yes/No Questions & Short Answers

Are you living at home now?

Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Is your sister working in another city?

Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

Are your parents studying English this year?

Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

Wh- Questions & Continuous Answers

Where are you working now?

I'm not working. I need a job.

What is your brother doing?

He's traveling in Thailand.

What are your friends doing these days?

They're studying for their exams.

Spelling Rules Reference

1. Standard Rule (+ing)

work → working

eat → eating

study → studying

2. Drop Silent -e (+ing)

live → living

have → having

3. Double Consonant (CVC)

swim → swimming

run → running
