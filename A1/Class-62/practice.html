Comparisons with Adjectives

Formulate comparative questions for 2 items (Which [noun] is [comparative], [A] or [B]?) and superlative questions for 3+ items (Which [noun] is [superlative]: [A], [B], or [C]?).

2 Items = Comparative (-er / more)
3+ Items = Superlative (the -est / the most)
