Collocation Rules & Usage Guide

Collocations are word partnerships that naturally go together in English. Learn the general pattern for each verb to build your vocabulary faster!

DO Duties & Chores

Use DO for work, duties, studies, chores, and non-specific tasks where no physical object is created.

• do my homework
• do chores
• do the laundry / the dishes

GO Movement & -ING

Use GO when moving from one place to another, or with sports and hobbies ending in -ing.

• go dancing / shopping
• go online
• go swimming / skiing

HAVE Experiences & Fun

Use HAVE for events you experience, meals/drinks, and quality time.

• have a party
• have a lot of fun / a good time
• have lunch / a break

MAKE Creating & Producing

Use MAKE when you create or manufacture something from scratch, make sounds/calls, or prepare food/beds.

• make a phone call
• make the bed
• make a video / dinner

TAKE Time Off & Trips

Use TAKE for taking time away from work, taking transportation, trips, or taking a short rest.

• take a day off
• take a bike ride / a trip
• take a shower / a break

Pro Memory Trick!

Notice the difference:

  • DO homework = compulsory work.
  • MAKE dinner = creating food.
  • HAVE fun = enjoying the experience.
  • TAKE a trip = traveling somewhere.
  • GO shopping = movement activity.
