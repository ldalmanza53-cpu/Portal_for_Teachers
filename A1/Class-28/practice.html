💡 Grammar Cheat Sheet: Simple Past Tense

1. Affirmative Regular Verbs (+ed)

  • Standard: work → worked, visit → visited
  • Ending in -e: live → lived, like → liked
  • Consonant + y: study → studied
  • Short vowel + consonant: stop → stopped

2. Irregular Verbs (Special Forms)

  • go → went
  • eat → ate
  • buy → bought
  • see → saw
  • drink → drank

3. Negatives & Questions

Negative: Subject + didn't + BASE VERB

Example: She didn't go to school yesterday.

Question: Did + Subject + BASE VERB ...?

Example: Did you study last night?

🚨 Avoid Spanish Speaker Fossilized Errors!

  • ❌ I didn't went to work.
    ✅ I didn't go to work. (Use base verb after didn't)
  • ❌ Did you played soccer?
    ✅ Did you play soccer? (Use base verb after Did)
  • ❌ I buyed a car last week.
    ✅ I bought a car last week. (Buy is irregular)
