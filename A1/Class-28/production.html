✈️ The Memory & Vacation Exchange

A1 Speaking Module • Focus: Simple Past Tense (Regular & Common Irregular Verbs) & Past Time Markers

🌍 Real Context & Scenario

Students are attending a Memory & Vacation Exchange at a local community cultural center. After returning from holidays, weekend trips, or special celebrations, members interview each other to share memorable stories and learn about past experiences.

🎭 Roles & Objectives

  • Role A (Interviewer): Asks simple past questions using question frames like "Where did you go?", "What did you do?", "Who were you with?", and "Did you like it?".
  • Role B (Storyteller): Describes past experiences using Simple Past tense verbs (regular and irregular) and past time markers (yesterday, last weekend, two days ago).

⏱️ 10-Minute Lesson Timeline

Setup & Board Reference: Review Simple Past forms (went, ate, visited, saw, bought) and past time markers (yesterday, last weekend, ago).

5:00 - 8:00
