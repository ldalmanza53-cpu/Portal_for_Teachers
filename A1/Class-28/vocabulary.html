/mjuːˈziː.əm/
Category: Weekend Place Noun
Definition

A building where interesting, artistic, or historical objects are shown to the public.

Context Sentence

"Did you go to the museum on Saturday?" — "Yes, I visited an art exhibition."

A1 Simple Past & Usage Rule

Use 'to the' with museum: 'go to the museum'. In Simple Past: 'went to the museum'.
