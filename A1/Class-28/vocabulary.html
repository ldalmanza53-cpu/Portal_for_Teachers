visited

Base: visit | Past Simple: visited

To go to see a person or a place for a short time in the past.

Common Collocations:

visited my family, visited a museum

Example Sentences:
  • I visited my grandparents last weekend.
  • We visited a famous beach yesterday.
💡 Common Learner Note:

Spanish speakers often say "I did visit" in positive sentences. In English, simply say "I visited" directly without adding "did"!
