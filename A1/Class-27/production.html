💻 The Virtual Family Reunion

A1 Speaking Module • Focus: Habitual vs. Current Family Activities & Leisure Habits

🌍 Real Context & Scenario

Students are participating in a Virtual Family Reunion over video call. Relatives who live in different cities are catching up on their lives. They discuss their regular weekly routines and leisure hobbies, as well as new or temporary activities happening right now.

🎭 Roles & Objectives

  • Role A (Host Relative): Asks structured questions about the partner's and their family's regular habits ("What do you do in your free time?") and current updates ("What is your sister doing these days?").
  • Role B (Visiting Relative): Answers using Simple Present for habitual routines and Present Continuous for current/temporary activities for themselves and family members.

Language Reference Sheet

🔄 Habitual Actions (Simple Present)

Use for regular routines and leisure hobbies.

• I / They + verb (e.g., I play tennis on Saturdays)

• He / She + verb-s (e.g., My sister cooks dinner every night)

• Time Markers: usually, on weekends, every day, in my free time

📍 Current Actions (Present Continuous)

Use for temporary updates or things happening right now.

• I am + verb-ing (e.g., I am learning French this month)

• He/She is + verb-ing (e.g., My brother is studying right now)

• Time Markers: right now, these days, this week, this month

❓ Family Catch-Up Questions

• "What do you usually do in your free time?"

• "What is your sister doing these days?"

• "Is anyone in your family traveling right now?"

• "How do you and your family spend weekends?"
