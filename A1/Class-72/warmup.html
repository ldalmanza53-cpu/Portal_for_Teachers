ESL Lesson Plan & Worksheets (CEFR A1)

Discussing Plans, Invitations & Life Changes

Time: 50–60 mins
Level: Beginner A1

⏰ 50-Minute Lesson Outline

1. Warm-Up & Review (10 mins):

Elicit past vs. present sentences on board. Write "In the past I lived in... Now I live in...".

2. Invitations Drill (10 mins):

Model "Would you like to...?" with accept/decline responses. Focus on polite excuses ("because I have to...").

3. Future Goals Setup (15 mins):

Introduce "going to", "want to", and "plan to". Students fill out their 3 vision board goal cards.

4. Pairwork Speaking Roulette (15 mins):

Pair students. Use Section 4 roulette cards for 60-second rapid-fire practice.
