Grammar Reference Guide

Simple Present Wh- Questions

Use do with I, you, we, they. Use does with he, she, it.

Wh- Question Structure Matrix

Click speaker icons to hear pronunciation
Wh- Word Auxiliary Subject Main Verb Example Answer Audio
What do you do? I'm a full-time student.
Where do you go to school? I go to the Brooklyn School of Music.
How do you like your classes? I like them a lot!
What does Tanya do? She's a teacher. She teaches art.
Where does Ryan work? He works for a tech company in San Francisco.
What do Bruce and Ivy do? They work at an Italian restaurant.
+s

Most Verbs

Add -s to the base form for he, she, it.

  • work → works
  • design → designs
  • cook → cooks
+es

Ending in -ch, -sh, -s, -x

Add -es for soft sibilant endings.

  • teach → teaches
  • manage → manages
  • do → does
-ies / Irregular

Consonant + Y & Irregular

Change y → ies or memorize irregulars.

  • study → studies
  • have → has
  • go → goes
