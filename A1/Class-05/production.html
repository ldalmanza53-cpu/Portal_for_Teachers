🌆 Real Context

Students attend a vibrant Community Career Day Fair. Working professionals are visiting the classroom to showcase their jobs. Students mingle in triads to interview different professionals, ask about their workplaces, schedules, uniforms, and daily tasks, and decide which job sounds most interesting!

ROLE A

The Professional

Uses the assigned Job Profile Card to answer questions clearly. Describes their title, workplace, uniform, and routine.

ROLE B

The Job Seeker

Interviews the professional to discover what the job involves, asks about daily duties, and decides if it is an exciting role.

ROLE C

The Career Advisor

Asks detailed follow-up questions about start times, weekend work, uniforms, and job difficulty to advise the job seeker.

Grammar Patterns to Remember

  • Preposition 'in': I work in a hospital / in an office.
  • Preposition 'at': I start work at 8:00 AM.
  • Articles 'a' vs 'an': I am a doctor. / I am an auto mechanic.
  • Present Simple Question: Do you wear a uniform? / Where do you work?

How to Run this Activity

  1. Divide students into small groups of 3 (Role A, B, C).
  2. Assign Role A a profile from the generator card on screen.
  3. Press Start Clock to begin the 10-minute master timer.
  4. In Round 1 (4 mins), Role B & Role C interview Role A using the outcome prompts.
  5. When 5 minutes pass, the timer will chime! Click Rotate Roles to change jobs and switch responsibilities.
  6. In Round 2 (4 mins), repeat with new roles!
