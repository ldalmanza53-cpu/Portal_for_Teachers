Regular Verbs (Ends in -ed)

Irregular Verbs (Word changes!)

Remember: After "didn't", we use the BASE verb (e.g., I didn't go NOT I didn't went)!

1. Regular Verbs (+ed)

Add -ed to base verb:

  • clean $\rightarrow$ cleaned
  • watch $\rightarrow$ watched
  • cook $\rightarrow$ cooked

2. Common Irregular Verbs

Verbs that change spelling:

  • go $\rightarrow$ went
  • eat $\rightarrow$ ate
  • buy $\rightarrow$ bought

3. Negative Form (didn't)

Subject + didn't + Base Verb:

✓ I didn't go to school.

✗ I didn't went to school.
