Future Expressions • ESL Grammar Focus

Making Future Plans & Invitations with Be Going To

Use be going to + base verb to discuss prior decisions, future intentions, and scheduled activities. Combine it with invitation formulas like Would you like to...? or Do you want to...? to make plans with others!

Statements (Affirmative & Negative)

Formulas

Affirmative Formula:

Subject + am / is / are + going to + Base Verb

"I am going to work late tonight."

Negative Formula:

Subject + am not / is not / are not + going to + Base Verb

"I am not going to stay home on Saturday."

Question Structures

Asking Plans

Yes / No Questions:

Am / Is / Are + Subject + going to + Base Verb?

"Are you going to stay in town next weekend?"

Wh- Questions:

Wh- Word + am / is / are + Subject + going to + Base Verb?

"What are you going to do tonight?"

Politeness & Invitation Pragmatics

How to extend invitations and accept or decline politely using future reasons.

Making an Invitation

  • "Would you like to go out tonight?"
  • "Do you want to see a movie?"

Accepting

  • "We'd love to come!"
  • "Sure! Let's try to go on Saturday."

Declining + Future Reason

  • "Sorry, I can't. I'm going to work late."
  • "I'm going to stay at the office till 7:00."
