A1 Speaking & Listening Activity

Making Weekend Plans & Arrangements

Two friends want to hang out together this week, but both have busy personal schedules with work, classes, or chores. They must talk to each other, check availability, negotiate a time, and agree on a shared plan!

A

Role A: The Initiator

Proposes the initial activity and time

  • Look at your card for the target Activity, Day, and Time.
  • Call/Text Student B and make a suggestion (e.g., "Do you want to play tennis on Saturday at 10 AM?").
  • Listen to B's conflict, check your schedule, and negotiate an alternate time!
B

Role B: The Responder

Handles conflicts & offers counter-suggestions

  • Listen to Student A's invitation politely.
  • Politely decline the first offer using your schedule constraint (e.g., "Sorry, I can't. I'm busy...").
  • Propose an alternative time/day when you are free (e.g., "How about Sunday at 2 PM?").

10-Minute Activity Guide

0 - 2 Minutes

Phase 1: Setup & Review

Select a prompt card, read your role constraint, and review target phrases in the Phrasebook.

2 - 8 Minutes

Phase 2: Pair Dialogue

Student A invites Student B. Student B declines with reason and counter-offers until both agree on a slot.

8 - 10 Minutes

Phase 3: Class Announcement

Pairs share their final plan: "We are going to [Activity] on [Day] at [Time]!"
