Hair Style & Color

🕒 Recent Events Cards (Simple Past)

Use these action verbs with recent time markers: yesterday, this morning, 10 minutes ago, just now.

Lost an item
"He lost his black wallet 10 minutes ago."
Arrived at the party
"She arrived at the reunion this morning."
Bought something
"He bought a new hat yesterday."
Dropped / Found
"She dropped her keys just now."
