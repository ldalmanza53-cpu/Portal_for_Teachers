Module 2: Grammar Practice

Comparative Form Builder

Learn how to transform adjectives into comparative structures: -er than vs. more ... than.

1

Short Adjectives (1 syllable or ends in -y)

Add -er than (e.g. quiet → quieter than, clean → cleaner than).

Special case (-y): noisy → noisier than | Double consonant: big → bigger than.

2

Long Adjectives (2+ syllables)

Use more + adjective + than (e.g. expensive → more expensive than, modern → more modern than).

Example: "Hotel A is more expensive than Hotel B."

Comparative Adjectives Cheat Sheet

A1 English Grammar Rules

1. Short Words (1 Syllable) → +er than
  • clean → cleaner than
  • old → older than
  • cheap → cheaper than
  • small → smaller than
2. Words ending in -y → -ier than
  • noisy → noisier than
  • dirty → dirtier than
3. Long Words (2+ Syllables) → more + word + than
  • expensive → more expensive than
  • modern → more modern than
  • beautiful → more beautiful than
