Critical Distinction Rule

"How High" vs. "How Tall"

🏔️ How High

Used for Natural Geographic Features, elevations, mountains, cliffs, and waterfalls.

• "How high is Mount Everest?" (8,848 meters)
• "How high is Niagara Falls?" (52 meters)
🏙️ How Tall

Used for Man-Made Structures (towers, skyscrapers, statues, buildings) and living entities (people, trees).

• "How tall is the Tokyo Skytree?" (634 meters)
• "How tall is the Burj Khalifa?" (828 meters)
2 Items

Comparative Adjectives

Compares EXACTLY TWO items using -er + than or more ... than.

Formula: Subject + verb + adj-er/more adj + than + Object
Which Q: "Which desert is drier, the Sahara or the Atacama?"
Answer: "The Atacama is drier than the Sahara."
3+ Items

Superlative Adjectives

Compares THREE OR MORE items using the + -est or the most.

Formula: Subject + verb + the + adj-est/most adj (+ in the world)
Which Q: "Which island is the largest, Greenland, New Guinea, or Honshu?"
Answer: "Greenland is the largest island in the world."
