📍 Real Context & Scenario

Two students meet at the Campus International Lounge during a Study Abroad Information Fair. They talk about their hometowns, vacation spots, and potential exchange cities, practicing place-describing adjectives (*quiet, noisy, modern, historic, big, small*) and comparative forms (*"Tokyo is bigger than Kyoto"*).

3 Phase 2 (4 Mins): Role Swap! Student B describes/compares places; Student A responds.

🏙️ Useful A1 Adjectives for Places:
• Big / Small
• Quiet / Noisy
• Clean / Crowded
• Modern / Historic
• Cheap / Expensive
• Hot / Cold / Sunny
⚖️ Comparative Grammar Rules:
• Short adjectives (+er than): bigger, quieter, cleaner, colder
• Long adjectives (more ... than): more expensive, more modern, more beautiful
• Irregulars: good ➔ better than, bad ➔ worse than
• Practice frame: "[Place A] is [comparative] than [Place B]."
🗣️ Sentence Starters:
• "My hometown is [adjective], [adjective], and [adjective]."
• "I think [City A] is much more exciting than [City B]."
