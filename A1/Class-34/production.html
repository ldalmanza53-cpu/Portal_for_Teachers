Municipal Tourism Board Scenario

The City Map Verification Desk

Welcome to the official Municipal Tourism Bureau! The city is releasing a new official tourism map tomorrow. Before printing, Map Inspectors and Local Guides must pair up to audit draft notes, verifying exact quantities of places and validating their precise relative locations.

A

Role A: Map Inspector

Auditor / Questioner

Asks & Audits

You hold the draft audit checklist. Ask your partner targeted questions to double-check quantities, presence, and precise locations on the town draft.

Target Question Stems:

  • "How many [places] are there near...?"
  • "Is there a [place] on/near...?"
  • "Is the [place] next to / opposite [place]?"
B

Role B: Local Guide

Local Expert / Respondent

Confirms & Corrects

You know the city thoroughly. Answer the Inspector's inquiries. Confirm accurate details or correct errors with relative position details.

Target Response Stems:

  • "There is one..." / "There are two..."
  • "Yes, it is next to / opposite..."
  • "No, there aren't any, but there is..."

Quantities & Existence

There is vs There are

Singular (1 place):

"There is one bakery near the park."

"Is there a hospital on Main Street?"

Plural (2+ places):

"There are three cafes near the station."

"How many parks are there in the city?"

Negative (0 places):

"There aren't any hotels near the river."

Prepositions of Place

Relative spatial relationships on maps

Next to

Side by side

"The bank is next to the cafe."

Opposite

Across the street

"The park is opposite the school."

Between

Middle of two items

"Between bank & library."

Behind

At the back of

"Behind city hall."
