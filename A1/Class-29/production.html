Travel & Adventure Club Exchange

Interview your partner about past vacations, outdoor adventures, and memorable trips using Simple Past verbs and time markers.

🌍 Real Context: Club Meetup

Welcome to the Travel & Adventure Club Meetup! Members pair up to interview each other about past vacations, hikes, beach trips, and outdoor journeys. Your goal is to gather exciting details to post on the club's Adventure Wall!

🎭 Roles & Responsibilities

  • Role A (Travel Reporter): Asks simple questions about past adventures ("Where did you go?", "What did you see?", "How was the weather?").
  • Role B (Adventurer): Shares past travel memories using Simple Past tense verbs (went, hiked, swam, ate, stayed) and time expressions (last summer, two weeks ago).
