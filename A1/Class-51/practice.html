🛍️ Modal Requests (Asking for Help / Items)

Use Can, Could, or May + subject + base verb to request something politely:

• Can / May I help you?

• Can I have a bag of cough drops?

• Could I have something for a cough?

• May I have a bottle of pain medicine?

💡 Making Suggestions & Advice

Use could, should, or Why don't you...? when recommending solutions:

• What do you suggest / have for a backache?

• You could try this new cream. (Soft suggestion)

• You should get a heating pad. (Strong advice)

• Why don't you try these pills?

💡 Key Usage Distinction:

  • May is used for asking permission / making requests ("May I have...?"), NOT for giving suggestions to someone else (don't say "You may try..." when advising).
  • Use could or should when giving advice or suggesting options to others.
