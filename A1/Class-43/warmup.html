Pro Tip for A1 Grammar

If a sentence tells you EXACTLY WHEN (e.g. yesterday, last night, in 2021), it is SPECIFIC. If it only says before or in the past, it is GENERAL!

Grammar Reference Cheat Sheet

General Past Experience

Use when talking about an event that happened, but NOT WHEN it happened.

Time Markers: before, in the past, a long time ago, once

Example: "She visited her grandparents before."

Specific Past Event

Use when pinpointing the EXACT TIME, DATE, OR DAY of the event.

Time Markers: yesterday, last Friday, two days ago, in 2021, at 8:00 AM

Example: "I saw that movie last night."
