Neighborhood Block Party: Family Activities Exchange

ESL A1 Speaking Activity • Discussing Family Activities & Routines

Level: A1 (Beginner) Duration: 10 Mins Pair Survey
10:00
Step 1: Setup & Target Language Warm-Up Teacher reviews family structures and action verbs (0 - 2 mins)

🎈 Real Context & Scenario

Neighbors are attending a planning meeting for an upcoming Neighborhood Block Party. They chat with each other to discover what activities their families enjoy doing together (on weekends, evenings, and holidays) so they can organize fun family games and food stalls for the event.

Role A: Neighborhood Host / Committee Member
Asks neighbor about their family structure and what activities they do together (e.g., "What does your family do on weekends?", "Do you cook together?").
Role B: Resident / Neighbor
Shares details about family activities using simple present verbs and frequency phrases (e.g., "My family cooks dinner together every Sunday") and asks back.

⏱️ 10-Minute Teacher Breakdown

  • 1
    Setup & Board Review (2 Mins)

    Model basic Q&A: "What does your family do on Saturdays?" ➔ "My family goes to the park together."

  • 2
    Pair Exchange & Role Swap (6 Mins)

    Pairs talk using prompt cards. Student A surveys Student B. At 3 minutes, signal to SWAP ROLES.

  • 3
    Block Party Sharing Wrap-Up (2 Mins)

    Ask 2–3 pairs: "What family activity did you discover for the block party?"

❓ Asking About Family Activities

  • What does your family do on weekends?
  • Do you and your family cook together?
  • What is your favorite activity together?

🗣️ Describing Shared Routines

  • My family goes to the park together.
  • We watch movies on Friday nights.
  • My parents and I clean the house on Saturdays.

⏰ Time & Frequency Expresssions

  • every Saturday / every Sunday
  • on weekends / in the evening
  • always / usually / sometimes / together
