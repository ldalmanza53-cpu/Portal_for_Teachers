📊 Adverbs & Expressions of Frequency

How often do you do your favorite activities?

100%
ALWAYS
"I always drink coffee in the morning." (7 days a week)
80%
USUALLY
"I usually watch TV after dinner." (5–6 days a week)
50%
SOMETIMES
"I sometimes go swimming on Saturdays." (2–3 days a week)
0%
NEVER
"I never wake up late on Mondays." (0 days)

🗓️ Useful Time Expressions:

  • Every day: "I read books every day."
  • Once a week: 1 time per week.
  • Twice a week: 2 times per week.
  • On weekends: Saturdays & Sundays.
