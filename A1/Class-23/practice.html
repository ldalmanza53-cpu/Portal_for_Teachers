📊 Frequency Scale & Expressions

Always (100%)

An activity happens every single day or 100% of the time.

WEEKLY FREQUENCY SIMULATOR (7 DAYS A WEEK)
Example Sentence
I always exercise before class.

⏱️ Time Expressions of Frequency

  • 🔹 every day (100%) 🔊
  • 🔹 once a week (1x/7 days) 🔊
  • 🔹 twice a month (2x/month) 🔊
  • 🔹 three times a year 🔊
📌 Important Rule: Adverbs of frequency usually go before the main verb (e.g., "I often watch TV").
*Note: Usually and sometimes can also begin a sentence (e.g., "Usually I exercise" or "Sometimes I watch TV").
