🎪 Real Context & Scenario

Students attend a Community Hobby & Sports Club Signup Event. To find a compatible "Hobby Buddy" or match with a local club, participants interview each other about what they like to do in their free time and how frequently they practice those activities.

Target Grammar & Focus
Action Verbs (play, go, do, watch, listen) + Frequency Adverbs/Expressions

Examples: "I always play tennis on Saturdays." • "How often do you go swimming?" • "I do yoga twice a week."

🤝 Student Roles
Role A: Club Ambassador / Matchmaker Interviewer

Asks about leisure activities, favorite sports, and frequency of practice (e.g., "What is your favorite leisure activity?", "How often do you practice sports?").

Role B: New Community Member Applicant

Shares favorite leisure activities and states frequency using adverbs (always, usually, sometimes, never) and phrases (every day, twice a week, on weekends).
