Grammar Focus Reference

Study rules for Simple Present Wh- Questions, irregular verbs, and time prepositions.

1. Simple Present Wh- Questions & Statements

Statements (He/She/It ends in -s):

• Verbs with he/she/it end in -s: He/She walks to school. (vs. I/You/We/They walk to school.)

• Irregular 3rd person forms: have → has, go → goes, do → does.

Wh- Questions (Do vs. Does):

• Use does with he/she/it and do with all others.

• ⚠️ Don't add -s to the verb in questions: Where does she live? (NOT: Where does she lives?)

2. Time Expressions Guide

in vs. at vs. on:

• Use in with parts of the day: in the morning / afternoon / evening.

• Use at with night & clock times: at night, at 7:00.

• Exception: on Friday night (specific day).

• Use on with days: on weekdays, on Mondays, on weekends.

Duration & Time Markers:

• Use until for end points: works until 3:30.

• Use early / late for relative time: gets up early, works late.
