Grammar Focus

Comparative Adjectives

Use -er than for short adjectives (cheaper than) and more ... than for long adjectives (more expensive than).

Complete the Comparative Sentences

1. Silk is (expensive) than cotton.
2. Plastic earrings are (cheap) than gold earrings.
3. Wool socks are (warm) than cotton socks.
4. Leather boots are (durable) than rubber shoes.

Shopping Dialogue Practice

Customer: Excuse me, what is this shirt made of?

Clerk: It's made of 100% cotton! It's very soft.

Customer: How much is it?

Clerk: It's $25. The silk one is more expensive.
