B1 Grammar Formulae & Structures

History & Grammar Matrix Blueprint

Deconstruct complex B1 grammar patterns used by historians and social scientists: Used to vs Present habits, Historical Passives, and Cause/Effect Connectors.

🔄 Comparison Pattern

Used To / Would vs. Present Habits

Express contrast between historical states/actions and present situations.

[Past] Subject + used to + Verb
whereas / while
[Present] Subject + Present Simple
"Humans used to rely on horses for transport, whereas today vehicles are used."
🏛️ Passive Voice Pattern

Historical Passive Voice

Shift focus to historical milestones or animals when the agent is obvious or general.

Subject + was / were + Past Participle
(+ by Agent)
"Cats were revered in ancient Egypt and were buried with honors."
🔗 Reasoning Pattern

Cause, Purpose & Effect Connectors

Link historical drivers with social consequences and environmental outcomes.

Action + in order to / so that + Purpose
As a result of / Due to + Noun Phrase
"Animals were trained in order to protect livestock; as a result, food security improved."

Topic: Domestication
A2: "People domesticated dogs a long time ago because they wanted help for hunting."
B1+: "Dogs were domesticated thousands of years ago in order to assist early humans with hunting."
Topic: Transport
A2: "In the past, people used horses to travel. Now they use cars."
B1+: "While horses used to be relied upon for transport, motor vehicles are predominantly used today."
Topic: Urbanization & Pets
A2: "Cities grew bigger, so pets became friends for families."
B1+: "As a result of rapid urbanization, pets became key emotional support companions."
Topic: Modern Conservation
A2: "We should protect animals because they are dying."
B1+: "Modern society must preserve natural habitats so that endangered species survive."
