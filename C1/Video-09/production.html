Grammar & Zooarchaeology Language Matrix

Used To / Would, Passive Voice & Present Perfect

Master essential B1 grammar structures to describe human-animal history and evolutionary shifts!

1. Used to / Would (Past Habits & Roles)
Used to + Base Verb (Past State/Habit):

"Dogs used to guard hunting camps."

Would + Base Verb (Repeated Action):

"Horses would carry riders across royal empires."

2. Passive Voice (Was/Were + V3)
Domestication & Historic Usage:

"Cattle were domesticated 10,000 years ago."

Present Passive (Is/Are + V3):

"Camels are still used for desert transport."

3. Present Perfect (Has/Have + V3)
Unfinished Evolution / Modern Shift:

"Human-animal bonds have transformed deeply."

Recent Conservation Efforts:

"Sanctuaries have been created to protect species."
