Agency Context & Roles

Talent Agency

"Global Talent & Impression Agency"

You are team members at an executive consulting agency. Your goal is to evaluate good and bad first impressions in professional and social settings, express strong B1 opinions, and recommend recovery strategies for candidates!

Select active role to view checklist & starters:

Role A: Candidate / Talent Specialist

Presents first impression strategies, describes non-verbal cues, and states opinions.

  • "In my view, maintaining firm eye contact creates immediate trust."
  • "I believe that dressing appropriately shows respect for the evaluator."
  • "If someone makes a bad impression, they should apologize politely."

Role B: Talent Evaluator & Recruiter

Challenges opinions, evaluates impression scenarios, and asks probing follow-up questions.

  • "Do you really think a weak handshake ruins a candidate's chance?"
  • "From your perspective, how can someone recover after arriving late?"
  • "I agree up to a point, but technical skill matters more in my opinion."

Role C: Peer Language & Impression Auditor

Monitors opinion signals, tracks target B1 vocabulary usage, and logs audit conclusions.

  • "Great use of 'From my perspective...' to express your view!"
  • "You used the target word 'slouching' accurately."
  • "Let's insert that opinion formula into our Talent Audit Scratchpad!"
