Syntax Blueprint

B1 Presentation Grammar Matrix

Master Modals of Advice & Obligation, Signposting Connectors, and First/Second Conditionals for compelling pitches.

1 Modals of Advice & Obligation
Subject + [should / must / ought to / don't have to] + Base Verb
  • Should: Friendly recommendation ("You should maintain eye contact.")
  • Must / Need to: Essential obligation ("You must avoid slide clutter.")
  • Ought to: Formal advice ("You ought to practice your timing.")
2 Signposting Connectors
[Signpost Transition], + Main Clause
  • Start: "To begin with, let's look at..."
  • Shift: "Moving on to our next point..."
  • Contrast: "In contrast, traditional methods..."
  • Finish: "To summarize / In conclusion..."
3 Conditionals for Pitching
1st: If + Present, Will + Verb
2nd: If + Past, Would + Verb
  • 1st Conditional (Real): "If you engage them early, they will stay interested."
  • 2nd Conditional (Hypothetical): "If I were you, I would use a strong opening hook."
