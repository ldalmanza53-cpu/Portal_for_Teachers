Presentation Mastery Language Matrix

Modals of Advice, Signposting & Impact Connectors

Master essential B1 grammar structures to deliver structured public speaking feedback!

1. Modals of Advice & Obligation
Recommendation (Should / Ought to):

"A speaker should maintain steady eye contact."

Strong Advice (Must / Make sure to):

"You must avoid reading directly from slides."

2. Signposting Transitions
Starting & Moving to Points:

"To begin with... / Turning now to..."

Illustrating & Summarizing:

"This illustrates that... / In conclusion..."

3. Audience Impact Expressions
By + Gerund (Method → Outcome):

"By pausing, the speaker creates suspense."

Ensuring Comprehension:

"...which ensures the audience stays engaged."
