Grammar & Syntax Blueprints

B1 Role Model Grammar Matrix

Master Relative Clauses, Present Perfect of Ongoing Impact, and Modals of Admiration to write moving descriptions of inspirational figures.

1 Relative Clauses (who / whose)
Defining: Person + [who] + Action
Non-Defining: Person, [whose + noun], + Action
  • Who: "A mentor is someone who guides others."
  • Whose (Possessive): "My teacher, whose advice changed my career, was humble."
2 Present Perfect for Ongoing Impact
Subject + [has/have] + Past Participle + Impact
  • Has demonstrated: "She has demonstrated integrity."
  • Have inspired: "They have inspired thousands of youth."
3 Modals of Admiration & Deduction
Subject + [must be / should empower / ought to lead]
  • Must be (Deduction): "She must be extremely resilient."
  • Should (Expectation): "A role model should lead by example."
