Role Model & Leadership Language Matrix

Relative Clauses, Trait Vocabulary & Admiration Connectors

Master essential B1 grammar structures to describe inspirational figures and societal impact!

1. Relative Clauses for Impact
People (who / whose):

"A role model is someone who inspires others..."

"...leaders whose integrity is unquestioned."

Contexts (where / in which):

"In a society where challenges abound..."

2. Trait Vocabulary (Nouns vs. Adjectives)
Abstract Noun → Adjective:

Inspiration → Inspiring / Inspired

Courage → Courageous | Resilience → Resilient

Authenticity & Empathy:

"Demonstrates empathy by actively listening..."

3. Expressing Admiration & Influence
Looking Up To & Setting Examples:

"I look up to X because they overcome..."

Legacy & Impact:

"X sets a positive example by demonstrating..."
