Grammar & Music Industry Strategy Matrix

Modals of Success, Present Perfect & Relative Clauses

Master essential B1 grammar structures to articulate music industry growth strategies!

1. Modals of Advice & Obligation
Must / Should / Ought to:

"To succeed, a band must release singles regularly."

Have to / Need to:

"Musicians need to practice setlists prior to major tours."

2. Present Perfect for Ongoing Milestones
Have + Past Participle (Ongoing Success):

"Top acts have accumulated millions of streams."

Have consistently + Past Participle:

"They have consistently engaged fans on social media."

3. Relative Clauses for Band Identity
Who / Whose / That / Which:

"A group whose members write authentic lyrics will win."

Defining Specific Characteristics:

"They focus on tracks that connect emotionally with youth."
