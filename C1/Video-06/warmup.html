Grammar & Structure

Strategy Matrix & Sentence Builder

Master B1 syntax blueprints: Clauses of Purpose, Modals of Recommendation, and Industry Collocations.

1

Clauses of Purpose

Expressing intentions and band objectives.

• in order to + infinitive
"rehearse daily in order to build stage presence"
• so as to + infinitive
"engage on social media so as to retain fans"
• so that + subject + modal
"tour regional clubs so that they can grow"
2

Recommendation Modals

Giving strategic advice & setting rules.

• must / have to (Strong/Essential)
"Bands must preserve creative synergy."
• should / ought to (Strategic Advice)
"Artists ought to pitch playlist curators."
• need to (Practical Requirement)
"You need to schedule sound checks early."
3

Music Collocations

Natural word pairings in the music business.

build + a fanbase
refine + stage presence
stream + digital content
organize + regional tours
sign + a record deal
maintain + group synergy
