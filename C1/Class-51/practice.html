Grammar Plus (Unit 7)

Advanced Whenever & Wherever Rules

Master adverbial clause reduction (Ellipsis of subject + be), special idiom meanings ("no matter when/where"), and focus adverb restrictions!

1

Adverbial Clause Reduction (Ellipsis)

Omitting subject + be

If whenever, wherever, when, or where are followed by subject + be + adjective / past participle, the subject and be are often deleted in formal English.

• Pet owners must take pets to the vet whenever taking them is advisable.
• Laws should be enforced where they are applicable.
2

Special Meanings & Idioms

"No matter when/where" & "Uncertainty"

• Meaning 1: "No matter when/where" (e.g. Mine doesn't like it whenever I give him a bath!)
• Meaning 2: "Although I don't know when/where" (e.g. We'll get together on his birthday, whenever that is!)

3

Focus Adverbs Constraint (even, just, right, only)

Important exception rule!

Whenever and wherever are rarely used following the focus adverbs even, just, right, and only. Use when or where instead!

✔ My cats show affection even when I'm in a bad mood.
✖ (NOT: even whenever)
✔ Elephants survive only where protected from illegal hunting.
✖ (NOT: only wherever)
