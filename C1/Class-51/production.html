Behavior & Contrast Language Engine
TYPICAL HABITS Expressions:

• "As a rule, [subject] usually / generally tends to..."
• "More often than not, [subject] will normally..."

CONTRAST & EXCEPTIONS Connectives:

• "...however, with the exception of [scenario], when they..."
• "...whereas [person B] acts completely differently unless..."
• "...apart from times when stress hits, in which case..."
