Impact & Values Structural Matrix

Upgrade simple A2 descriptions into articulate B1+ statements using relative clauses of attribution, value evaluation formulas, and result/legacy connectors.

1

Relative Clause of Attribution

[Figure] + "is a leader who/whose" + [Key Action or Attribute] + "in order to transform society."

Directly attributes actions or possessions to an influential person.

"Malala is an activist whose courage inspired millions of students worldwide."

2

Value Evaluation Formula

"What stood out most about" + [Person] + "was their" + [Value Term] + ", which exemplified" + [Principle].

Provides a clear, thoughtful moral assessment of a figure's character.

"What stood out most about my mentor was her empathy, which exemplified true leadership."

3

Legacy & Result Marker

"As a result of their" + [Value/Perseverance] + ", they left a lasting legacy by" + [Outcome].

Links individual effort to enduring historical or social outcomes.

"As a result of his relentless dedication, he left a lasting legacy by establishing public clinics."

🚀A2 Simplistic Statements vs B1+ Impact Upgrades

Examine how basic sentences are upgraded into rich, precise B1 descriptions of figures and values.

A2 SIMPLISTIC FORM Basic Description

"Nelson Mandela was a good man. He went to prison and made peace."

CEFR B1+ IMPACT UPGRADE Relative Clause + Value

"Nelson Mandela was a resilient leader who dedicated his life to justice, proving that unwavering resilience can transform a nation."

A2 SIMPLISTIC FORM Basic Description

"My high school teacher helps everyone a lot and she is very nice."

CEFR B1+ IMPACT UPGRADE Relative Clause 'Whose' + Result

"My teacher is a mentor whose empathy and patience encouraged us, and as a result of her support, we succeeded."

A2 SIMPLISTIC FORM Basic Description

"Marie Curie worked very hard in science and won two Nobel Prizes."

CEFR B1+ IMPACT UPGRADE Value Evaluation + Legacy

"Marie Curie exemplified the value of relentless dedication through breakthroughs that left a lasting legacy in modern medicine."

A2 SIMPLISTIC FORM Basic Description

"An activist spoke in our town yesterday about clean water."

CEFR B1+ IMPACT UPGRADE Value Stance Marker

"What stood out most about the community hero was her courage when speaking up for marginalized families."
