📐 B1 Contrast Connectors & Trend Structures
1. Contrast Structure
[Trend A / Present] + whereas / while + [Trend B / Past]

Example: "Many people work from home nowadays, whereas in the past everyone commuted to an office."

2. Prepositional Contrast
In contrast to / Compared with + [Noun Phrase], [Subject + Verb]

Example: "Compared with traditional fast fashion, second-hand clothing is growing rapidly."

Connector Function Example Usage
Whereas / While Direct contrast "Young people prefer streaming, whereas older adults watch TV."
In contrast to Prepositional contrast "In contrast to 10 years ago, cash is rarely used."
On the other hand Transition sentence "Cities are loud. On the other hand, remote towns offer peace."
Tend to / Shift toward Describing trends "There is a clear shift toward plant-based diets."

📚 B1 Modern Lifestyle Vocabulary Vault
Digital Detox
Taking a temporary break from electronic devices.
"More people do a weekend digital detox to reduce stress."
Gig Economy
Temporary, flexible, or freelance jobs instead of full-time employment.
"In contrast to permanent contracts, gig jobs offer flexibility."
Slow Living
A lifestyle emphasizing a slower pace of life and mindfulness.
"While hustle culture was popular, slow living is now trending."
Thrift Culture
Buying second-hand clothes or items to save money and stay sustainable.
"Thrift shopping has become cooler than buying fast fashion."
Micro-Housing
Compact, ultra-efficient small apartment living in big cities.
"Compared with large suburban houses, micro-flats are cheaper."
Plant-Based Diet
Eating food mostly derived from plants rather than animals.
"There is a massive shift toward plant-based eating."
