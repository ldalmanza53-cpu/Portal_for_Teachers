Section 7 (Grammar Plus) • Page 145

Optional and Required Relative Pronouns with Prepositions & Possession

When a relative pronoun is the object of a preposition, special relative pronouns are mandatory:

Preposition + WHOM (People)

When the relative pronoun is the complement or object of a preposition referring to people, whom is required (not who).

"No one can live on that land except people to whom special permits have been given."
Preposition + WHICH (Things)

When a preposition precedes the relative pronoun referring to objects or concepts, which is required (not that).

"Principles against which my generation has rebelled."
WHOSE (Possession)

Whose indicates possession and can represent people, animals, or objects. It is always required.

"Fitness classes whose purpose is to provide safe exercise..."

Complete with whom, which, or whose
