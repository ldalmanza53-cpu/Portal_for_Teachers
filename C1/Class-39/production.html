Step 2 — Complement & Upgrade Engine

Basic B1 ➡️ Upgraded B1+ Opinion Transformer

Compare standard B1 opinion phrases with more precise, nuanced B1+ complements!

1. Expressing Strong Belief UPGRADE
BASIC B1:

"I think remote work is much better for everyone."

UPGRADED B1+:

"I am firmly convinced that remote work significantly enhances work-life flexibility."

2. Sharing a Personal Angle UPGRADE
BASIC B1:

"In my opinion, living in a small apartment is hard."

UPGRADED B1+:

"From my perspective, micro-living requires significant discipline and spatial compromise."

3. Expressing Polite Doubt / Concern UPGRADE
BASIC B1:

"I don't agree with quitting social media completely."

UPGRADED B1+:

"I hold serious reservations about total digital detachment, as it may cause social isolation."

4. Conceding & Balancing Points UPGRADE
BASIC B1:

"I agree vegan food is healthy, but it is expensive."

UPGRADED B1+:

"While I recognize the health advantages of plant-based diets, I contend that costs remain high."
