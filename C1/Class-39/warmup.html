3 Essential B1+ Sentence Formulas

Formula A: Direct Stance
[B1+ Starter] + [Clause]

Example: "From my perspective, remote work vastly improves family balance."

Formula B: Concession (While/Although)
[While/Although ...] + [Main Stance]

Example: "While digital detox is difficult initially, it produces mental clarity."

Formula C: Cause & Consequence
[Stance] + [Consequently / Leading to]

Example: "Minimalism reduces clutter, consequently lowering anxiety."
