Formula 1

Compound Adjectives

[Adverb/Noun/Adj] + [-] + [Participle/Adj] → Compound Attribute

Hyphenate compound words when placed directly before a noun to express complex character traits concisely.

Ex: "A forward-thinking leader..."
Formula 2

Relative Attribution

[Changemaker] + [,] + [who / whose + clause] + [,] + [Main Verb]

Use non-defining relative clauses to seamlessly weave achievements or background info without restarting sentences.

Ex: "Dr. Patel, whose vision saved thousands, spoke..."
Formula 3

Passive Voice for Impact

[Beneficiary / Cause] + [was / has been + Past Participle] + [by / for]

Shift the focus onto the results and communities transformed, elevating the objective tone of your writing.

Ex: "The neighborhood was transformed by youth energy."
