Phrasal Verb Meanings

check on: visit or contact to see if someone is all right.
look after: take care of someone or something.
live up to: fulfill or achieve expected standards/responsibilities.
take after: resemble an older relative in behavior or character.
face up to: confront and accept a difficult situation/problem.
get through: survive or successfully endure a tough situation.

Possessive Rule Highlight: As with other superlative adjectives, the article "the" is NOT used when the noun is preceded by a possessive!
Example: "Venezuela's best-known poet will be reading one of his works..." (NOT "Venezuela's the best-known")
