Language Validation Toolkit & Matrix

Structures for Describing People Who Make a Difference

Validate baseline B1 sentence structures and pair character traits with concrete achievements!

1. Defining Identity & Role
"Subject + IS A PERSON WHO + [Verb Phrase]"
  • • "She is an inspiring leader who devoted her life to childhood literacy."
  • • "He is a visionary innovator who created affordable water filters."
2. Expressing Traits & Reputation
"IS WIDELY KNOWN FOR BEING + [Adjective/Noun]"
  • • "They are widely known for being selfless and persistent in adversity."
  • • "She is recognized for her immense empathy and initiative."
3. Highlighting Remarkable Impact
"WHAT MAKES [Person] REMARKABLE IS THAT..."
  • • "What makes him remarkable is that he never gave up despite obstacles."
  • • "What sets her apart is her ability to motivate others."
