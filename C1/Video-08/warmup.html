Language Formula Blueprints

Blueprint 1

Past Simple vs. Present Perfect

Finished Historical Time (Past Simple): In 1920, brands printed flyers.
Past Action to Present Result (Present Perfect): In recent years, algorithms have transformed advertising.
Blueprint 2

Historical Passive Voice

Past Passive (was / were + V3): Billboards were erected along railways.
Present Passive (is / are + V3): Customized ads are generated by AI.
Blueprint 3

Time & Sequential Connectors

Key Connectors:

During (In the middle of)
