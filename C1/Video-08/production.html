Grammar & Historical Language Matrix

Past Passive, Present Perfect & Historical Connectors

Master essential B1 grammar structures to describe marketing milestones and brand transformation!

1. Past Passive Voice
Was / Were + Past Participle:

"The first TV commercial was broadcast in 1941."

Plural Form:

"Memorable slogans were introduced to build brand loyalty."

2. Present Perfect (Has / Have + V3)
Unfinished Evolution to Present:

"Digital marketing has transformed consumer engagement."

Recent Innovations:

"Social media platforms have replaced mass broadcasting."

3. Historical Connectors
With the advent of / Prior to:

"With the advent of radio, audio ads reached millions."

Sequential Evolution:

"Prior to algorithmic targeting, marketing was generic."
