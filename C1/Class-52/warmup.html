CEFR B1 Grammar Architecture

Modals of Possibility & Expressing Suitability

Learn how to elevate simple statements into nuanced CEFR B1 career pitches using possibility structures (could, might, offer the possibility to, enable us to) and personal suitability markers (I am particularly drawn to, My skills align with).

1. Expressing Environmental Possibilities

CEFR B1 Focus: Speculating on impact and potential

// Pattern A: [Job Role] + could / might / may + [Base Verb]

// Pattern B: [Career] + offers the possibility to / enables us to + [Verb]

✓ "Urban forestry could reduce city heat islands significantly."

✓ "Renewable energy engineering enables us to transition away from fossil fuels."

2. Expressing Personal Fit & Suitability

CEFR B1 Focus: Personal stance & skill matching

// Preference: I am particularly drawn to + [Noun / -ing Phrase]

// Alignment: My skills in [Skill] align with + [Job Domain]

✓ "I am particularly drawn to marine biology because I love ocean conservation."

✓ "My skills in data analysis align with climate modeling."
