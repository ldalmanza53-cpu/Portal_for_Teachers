Language Framework

Possibility Modals & Personal Idea Expressions

Master how to discuss potential career paths and express your personal eco-innovations!

1

Modals of Possibility

Expressing Potential Job Responsibilities
🌿 Could / Could potentially:

"An urban ecologist could potentially design rooftop gardens to reduce heat in cities."

⚡ Might / Might be able to:

"A carbon auditor might help large corporations measure and reduce emissions."

🌊 May / May work toward:

"Marine conservationists may establish protected zones for endangered coral reefs."

2

Stating Personal Ideas & Stance

Introducing Green Career Proposals
💡 In my view / From my perspective:

"In my view, a promising career path is vertical farming because it saves land."

🗣️ If you ask me / I would propose:

"If you ask me, I would propose that cities hire rewilding specialists for parks."

🌟 I strongly believe that:

"I strongly believe that green careers will become the most essential jobs of this century."
