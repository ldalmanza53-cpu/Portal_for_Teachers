Grammar Plus • Page T-145 Sec 7

Placement of Direct and Indirect Objects

Pattern A (Preposition Clause)

Direct Object + to/for + Indirect Object

"The boss owes a month's salary to him."

"The hotel chef made an omelet for my mother."

Pattern B (Direct Transfer)

Indirect Object + Direct Object

"The boss owes him a month's salary."

"The clerk handed Eleanor a pen."

⚠️ Mandatory Pronoun Rule

When BOTH objects are pronouns, ONLY Pattern A is possible:

✅ "The boss owes it to him."

❌ "The boss owes him it." (INVALID)

Dual-Object Verbs Reference:

Verbs taking 'TO': bring, hand, pay, serve, give, owe, promise, throw, sell, hand.
Verbs taking 'FOR': order, make, buy, find.
