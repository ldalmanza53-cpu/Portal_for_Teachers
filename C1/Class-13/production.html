Summit Context & Roles

Podcast Studio

"Future Soundwaves Summit"

You are co-hosts on a global youth tech podcast. Your mission is to analyze groundbreaking scientific advances, evaluate their societal impacts (pros vs side effects), and express well-structured B1 opinions!

Select active role to view checklist & starters:

Role A: Tech Innovator & Futurist

Advocates for progress, highlights efficiency, medical breakthroughs, and AI benefits.

  • "In my view, artificial intelligence results in remarkable medical breakthroughs."
  • "Renewable energy tech is essential for solving the global climate crisis."
  • "Automation leads to far higher workplace efficiency."

Role B: Skeptical Analyst & Bioethicist

Examines side effects, ethical dilemmas, privacy risks, and tech addiction.

  • "On the other hand, gene editing creates serious ethical dilemmas."
  • "Smartphones are a double-edged sword that harms attention spans."
  • "Data collection results in severe privacy risks for everyday users."

Role C: Audio Host & Media Journalist 🎵

Guides the interview, checks B1 grammar/vocab, manages time, and synthesizes conclusions.

  • "Welcome to Future Soundwaves 🎵! How will this breakthrough affect our daily lives?"
  • "Great cause-and-effect phrase! Let's weigh the pros and cons."
  • "Let's build our summary statement using the Science View Builder!"
