Co-Living Forum Roles

Flatmate Forum

"The Global Housemate Exchange"

You are flatmates from different countries sharing an apartment. Your goal is to discuss cultural customs, express pet peeves respectfully, rephrase requests politely, and establish a harmonious set of house rules for everyone!

Select active role to view checklist & starters:

Role A: Cultural Ambassador & Senior Housemate

Explains local customs, states personal living preferences, and shares past experiences.

  • "In my culture, we always take off shoes at the front door."
  • "I prefer having quiet hours starting from 10:00 PM."
  • "While I was living with previous roommates, we had already..."

Role B: International Exchange Guest

Complains about annoying habits politely, states likes/dislikes, and uses varied request formalities.

  • "My biggest pet peeve is leaving dirty dishes in the sink."
  • "Would you mind keeping the volume down after midnight?"
  • "In my country, we usually eat dinner much later, whereas..."

Role C: Communication Mediator & Cultural Coach

Monitors request formality, checks target B1 grammar (tenses & connectors), and logs house agreements.

  • "Great use of 'Would you mind...' for a polite request!"
  • "Notice how using 'whereas' highlighted the cultural difference well."
  • "Let's write down this house rule in our Harmony Charter!"
