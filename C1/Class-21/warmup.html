Stage 1 • Reporting Verbs & Cultural Framework

Reporting Cultural Beliefs & Comparing Views

When sharing superstitions from other cultures, we use reporting verbs (claim, report, state, maintain, believe) and comparative belief expressions ("In contrast to my culture...", "While locals state that...").

1. Reporting Verbs (Indirect Information)
  • • It is reported that...
  • • Locals claim that...
  • • Historians state that...
  • • Cultural guides maintain that...

"It is reported that eating 12 grapes at midnight brings good luck in Spain."

2. Reported Speech Shifts
  • • Direct: "We avoid the number four."
  • • Reported: They said they avoided the number four.
  • • Passive Report: The number four is believed to cause bad luck.

"They stated that writing living names in red ink brought misfortune."

3. Comparing Personal Beliefs
  • • Compared to my culture, where...
  • • While they believe X, I personally feel...
  • • In contrast to local customs,...
  • • I find it fascinating, though I don't believe...

"While locals claim the Nazar eye protects them, I personally view it as decor."
