Reporting Clauses with Indirect Objects

Learn how verbs like admit, agree, complain, confess, explain, announce take indirect objects + that clauses in the simple past.

Grammar Rule: Verbs followed by an indirect object + that

In reporting clauses, verbs such as admit, agree, announce, comment, complain, confess, disclose, explain, inform, and reveal are frequently followed by an indirect object (e.g. to his friend, with me, to the park staff). In this case, that should be retained for clarity.

Example 1 (agree + with + object):

"Several people agreed with me that logic is the best way to make decisions."

Example 2 (explain + to + object):

"Max explained to the teacher that a black cat never means bad luck in his country."
