Sentence Formula & Connectors

Supporting Opinion Text Structure

Master the 5-step formula for writing convincing B1 opinion texts: Stance Statement → Primary Support → Concrete Evidence → Cause/Effect → Conclusion.

STEP 1

Stance Statement

State your clear position on the best shopping method.

STEP 2

Primary Support

Provide your main reason (e.g. price, quality, variety).

STEP 3

Concrete Evidence

Give a specific real-world example or instance.

STEP 4

Cause / Effect

Explain the result of this purchasing strategy.

STEP 5

Conclusion

Reiterate your recommendation with final impact.

Stance & Primary Connectors
  • In my view, online marketplaces offer unmatched convenience.
  • First and foremost, buying bulk saves money per unit.
  • From my perspective, local markets provide better quality.
Evidence & Illustration
  • For instance, comparing seller ratings helps avoid bad items.
  • A clear case in point is buying certified refurbished laptops.
  • To illustrate, flea markets often have rare vintage denim.
Cause, Effect & Conclusion
  • This enables buyers to lower their monthly expenses.
  • As a result, shoppers acquire high quality goods for less.
  • On balance, direct buying is the most rewarding option.
