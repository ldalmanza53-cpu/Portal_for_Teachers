Writing Framework: The PREO Model

How to Write a Supporting Opinion Paragraph

A strong opinion text does not just state a belief—it supports it with clear reasons, concrete examples, and logical outcomes!

P

Point (Opinion)

State your clear position or advice directly.

"In my opinion, buying certified refurbished laptops is the smartest way to purchase tech."

R

Reason

Explain why you hold this opinion using logical justification.

"One main reason is that refurbished laptops undergo rigorous factory testing before being resold."

E

Example / Evidence

Provide concrete facts, personal experiences, or specific details.

"For instance, major tech brands offer a full one-year warranty on refurbished models."

O

Outcome (Result)

Summarize the final benefit or logical conclusion for the reader.

"As a result, smart consumers save up to 40% while still enjoying full peace of mind."

Transitional Toolbox

Supporting Opinion Connectors & Linkers

Introducing a Reason: "One key reason is that...", "The primary justification for this is...", "This is mainly because..."
Giving Evidence / Examples: "For instance, ...", "A clear example of this is...", "Such as when shoppers compare..."
Showing Result / Outcome: "As a result, ...", "Consequently, ...", "This demonstrates that...", "Therefore, consumers benefit from..."
