Grammar Reference Box: Double Comparative Structures

The + more / less + clause

"The more I listen to classical music, the more I appreciate it."

The + comparative adj + clause

"The more romantic a song is, the less my brother wants to listen to it."

The + comparative adv + clause

"The louder Mario plays his stereo, the more his neighbors complain."

Short Forms (no verbs)

"The sooner, the better!" • "The more, the merrier!" • "The bigger, the better!"
