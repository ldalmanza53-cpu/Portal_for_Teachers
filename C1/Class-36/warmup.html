Basic B1 vs. B1+ Upgraded Comparisons

Formula 1: Tendency
Subject + tend to / tends to + Base Verb

Expresses general habits or typical behavior without sounding overly rigid.

"As a rule, I tend to avoid caffeine after 4 PM."
Formula 2: Irritating Habits
Subject + is/are always + Verb-ing

Used to express mild annoyance or criticism regarding repeated actions.

"He is constantly interrupting people during presentations!"
Formula 3: Zero Conditional
If / When + Present Simple, Present Simple

States scientific facts, natural truths, or invariable cause-and-effect rules.

"When you heat ice, it melts into liquid water."
