Grammar Plus • Page T-144, Activity 16

Will & Would for Habits & General Truths

Learn when to use would vs. used to (Action vs. Stative Verbs) and present habitual will.

Grammar Rule Matrix: Used To vs. Would

Action Verbs (Past Habits)

Both used to and would can express repeated past actions or habits.

"Before he became a big star, Mark used to / would play music on the street for money."

CRITICAL RULE: Stative Verbs

Would CANNOT be used with stative verbs (be, have, like, live, love, mean, own). You must use used to.

✓ Correct: "I used to have a clarinet."
✗ Incorrect: "I would have a clarinet."
