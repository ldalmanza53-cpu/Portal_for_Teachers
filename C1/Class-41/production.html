3-Phase Change Collocation Matrix

Essential Collocations for Navigating Shift

Master natural verb + noun pairings used across three main stages of change!

Phase 1: Facing Change INITIAL STAGE
  • "face a challenge" To confront a difficult situation directly.
  • "step out of your comfort zone" To try new things that feel unfamiliar or challenging.
  • "embrace new opportunities" To welcome novel chances with enthusiasm.
  • "welcome a fresh start" To begin a new phase positively.
Phase 2: Stress & Support COPING STAGE
  • "deal with stress / handle pressure" To manage emotional tension effectively.
  • "seek support" To ask friends, family, or experts for assistance.
  • "build a strong support system" To create a network of reliable people.
  • "take things one day at a time" To focus on short-term progress without worrying about the distant future.
Phase 3: Adapting & Growing LONG-TERM STAGE
  • "adapt to change / adjust to a routine" To modify behavior to fit new circumstances.
  • "overcome obstacles" To successfully deal with problems blocking progress.
  • "bounce back from setbacks" To recover quickly after experiencing failure or trouble.
  • "make a smooth transition" To change from one state to another without friction.
