Grammar & Expression Matrix

Feelings vs. Behaviors Structure Guide

Learn to distinguish emotional states from actionable behavioral routines!

Feelings & Emotion Frames INTERNAL STATE
  • "I feel apprehensive / thrilled about..." "I feel apprehensive about moving to a new city alone."
  • "It gives me an immense sense of relief to..." "It gives me a sense of relief to turn off my work email on weekends."
  • "I find myself feeling overwhelmed when..." "I find myself feeling overwhelmed when notifications pile up."
Behavioral Pattern Frames ACTION / ROUTINE
  • "I tend to / make a point of..." "I make a point of meditating for 10 minutes every morning."
  • "Whenever stress triggers me, I react by..." "Whenever stress triggers me, I react by going for a long walk."
  • "I struggle to break the habit of..." "I struggle to break the habit of late-night snack cravings."
