Visual Collocation Patterns

Pattern 1: Verb + Noun

embrace + change

Direct action on the situation. No preposition needed.

Pattern 2: Verb + Prep + Noun

cope + with + pressure

Requires fixed prepositions like with or of.

Pattern 3: Fixed Idiomatic Phrase

take things in stride

Fixed multi-word expression describing calm behavior.
