Stage 2 • Analytical Language

Analyzing the Effects of Science & Technology

To analyze scientific developments at a B1 level, link cause and effect using clear connecting structures:

1. Cause & Result Connectors
  • • As a result of [advancement], ...
  • • This leads to / results in...
  • • Consequently,...

"As a result of solar advances, carbon emissions are decreasing."

2. Weighing Pros & Cons
  • • While it offers benefits, it also...
  • • On the one hand..., but on the other hand...
  • • One major advantage/drawback is...

"While AI boosts efficiency, it also raises ethical concerns."

3. Personal Conviction
  • • In my view, science should focus on...
  • • I strongly believe that technology must...
  • • From my perspective,...

"I strongly believe that gene editing requires international regulations."
