B1 Grammar Guide

Essential Structures for Discussing Success

Master these 3 core B1 sentence formulas to share opinions, advice, and routines effectively!

1. Expressing Strong Opinions

Opinion Starters

State your personal stance with confidence during summit debates.

"In my opinion, hard work beats talent."

"I strongly believe that mindset matters most."

"From my perspective, mentors are key."

2. Conditionals & Advice

If / Unless Structures

Give recommendations and state cause-and-effect rules.

"If you want to succeed, you should set clear goals."

"Unless you practice daily, you won't improve."

3. Discussing Habits & Trends

Tend to / Make a habit of

Describe typical routines and behaviors of high achievers.

"Successful people tend to wake up early."

"They make a habit of reading every night."
