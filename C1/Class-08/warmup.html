Stage 2 • Language Focus ✒️

How to Express a Personal Belief

Expressing a personal belief is stronger than simply stating a basic opinion ("I think..."). It shows conviction, values, and strong personal principles.

1. High Conviction Beliefs
  • • I strongly believe that...
  • • It is my firm belief that...
  • • I am convinced that...

"I am convinced that sustainable vintage fashion is better for our planet."

2. Personal Perspective Beliefs
  • • From my perspective,...
  • • It seems to me that...
  • • In my view,...

"From my perspective, fast fashion hurts worker rights."

3. Principle / Feeling Beliefs
  • • I personally feel that...
  • • I hold the view that...
  • • My belief is that...

"I personally feel that comfort should always come before trends."

Model B1 Writing Example:

"Over the past twenty years, fashion has shifted dramatically toward comfortable clothing. I am convinced that 90s baggy jeans made a comeback because younger generations value individuality and sustainability over rigid dress codes. It is my firm belief that old fashion trends will continue to return every twenty years."
