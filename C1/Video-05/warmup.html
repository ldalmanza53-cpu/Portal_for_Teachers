Grammar & Sentence Workshop

The Production Matrix Formulas

Master the core language structures used to describe how movies are created: Process Passive Voice, Relative Clauses of Role, and Sequence Connectors.

1

Process Passive Voice

Used when the action or stage is more important than who does it.

Subject + is/are + Past Participle

Singular: "The screenplay is written in advance."

Plural: "Sound effects are added in post-production."

2

Role Relative Clauses

Used to define specific crew responsibilities and production phases.

Person + is the specialist WHO...
Phase + is the stage WHERE...

Crew Role: "The cinematographer is the expert who controls lighting."

Phase: "Editing is the phase where raw footage is cut."

3

Sequence Connectors

Used to order process events clearly from development to release.

Prior to shooting... / Once + clause... / Subsequently...

Time Link: "Prior to filming, storyboards are sketched."

Next Step: "Once scenes are shot, color grading begins."
