Grammar & Pipeline Matrix

Passive Voice in Production & Process Sequencers

Master B1 grammar structures to explain how movies and TV shows are created step by step!

1. Passive Voice in Film Production
Present Passive (is / are + Past Participle):

"The script is written before actors are cast."

Past & Present Perfect Passive:

"Scenes were filmed on location after permits had been granted."

2. Process Sequencing Connectors
First / Once / Prior to:

"Once the storyboard is finalized, shooting begins."

Subsequently / While / Finally:

"Subsequently, special effects are added by VFX artists."

3. Technical Pipeline Terminology
On-Set & Studio Operations:

"Principal photography is conducted on sound stages."

Distribution & Release:

"The film is distributed to theaters and streaming apps."
