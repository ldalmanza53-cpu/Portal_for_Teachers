Stage 2: Stance Toolkit

How to Express Attitude at B1

Master three levels of attitude stance plus logical connectors to sound articulate.

1. Positive & Enthusiastic

Love / Prefer
"I'm a massive fan of..."
"I tend to prefer..."
"What really excites me is..."
"I find it captivating when..."

2. Neutral & Mixed

Undecided
"I'm on the fence about..."
"It seems to me that..."
"To be honest, it depends..."
"I can see both sides of..."

3. Negative & Irritated

Fed Up
"I'm fed up with..."
"What really annoys me is..."
"I can't stand..."
"I find it frustrating when..."
