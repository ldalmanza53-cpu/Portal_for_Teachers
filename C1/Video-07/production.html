Grammar & Societal Shift Matrix

Past vs. Present Contrasts & Modals of Social Progress

Master essential B1 grammar structures to express changing gender roles and advocate for workplace equity!

1. Past vs. Present Shifts
Used to / Whereas / Nowadays:

"Women used to be expected to manage homes, whereas nowadays..."

Where X was once... Y is now...:

"Where male breadwinning was once standard, dual careers are now norm."

2. Contrastive & Comparative Connectors
In contrast to / Compared to / While:

"In contrast to traditional norms, modern fathers share caregiving."

On the one hand... On the other hand:

"While career access has grown, domestic labor remains unequal."

3. Modals of Social Progress
Should / Ought to / Must:

"Corporations must dismantle the glass ceiling through fair hiring."

Is expected to / Needs to:

"Domestic responsibilities should be distributed equally at home."
