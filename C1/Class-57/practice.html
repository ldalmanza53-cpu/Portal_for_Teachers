Greetings, jobseekers!

Do job interviews make you nervous? I used to feel that way, too, but not anymore. My advice is to be prepared for the interview.

Point (1): Starting the advice list

"...be prepared for the interview. (1) do some research on the company you will interview with..."

...so you can talk about the company in an informed way during the interview. Learn about its business goals, products and services, and financial health.

Point (2): Sequencing the next step

"(2) anticipate the questions the interviewer may ask you – and think of good answers!"

Point (3): Adding an additional tip

"(3) it's a good idea to jot down your own questions about job responsibilities..."

At this point, you may feel totally ready for your interview.

Point (4): Introducing a contrasting warning/step

"(4) there's one more step you should take: role-play an interview with a trusted friend..."

That will increase your ability to communicate with self-assurance.

Point (5): Summarizing/concluding the letter

"(5) it may feel like all this preparation is a lot of work, but it'll be worth it..."

Grammar Plus • Page T-146 Section 7

Overview of Passives

Master modal passives in short answers and understand when to use `get` vs. `be` in passive sentences.

1. Modals in Short Answers

The passive voice with a modal can be used in short answers. Do not repeat the entire verb phrase.

A: Why wasn't that author awarded the Nobel Prize for literature?
B: I don't know, but he should have been.
(Full form: He should have been awarded the Nobel Prize for literature.)

2. 'Get' vs. 'Be' Passive

The verb get can act as an auxiliary for passive. It is less formal and indicates a change or process (meaning become). Be indicates an unchanging state or dynamic action.

Get: Larry and Natalie got married in 2006. (Their wedding occurred in 2006)
Be: Larry and Natalie were married in 2006 when they went to Greece. (They were already married)

3. Common 'Get' Expressions

The verb get is commonly used in reflexive and passive state transitions:

get acquainted get arrested get dressed get excited get married get scared
