Passive & Expression Structural Matrix

Upgrade basic active opinions into objective, formal B1+ academic stances using passive constructions and reporting expressions.

1

Passive Trait Formula

[Subject/Trait] + is/are + [V3 Past Participle] + [for/in/by + Object]

Puts emphasis on the quality or action rather than the person doing it.

"Empathy is valued above technical jargon in team discussions."

2

Passive Reporting Expressions

It is widely [believed / said / regarded] that + [Clause]

Establishes objective consensus in persuasive communication essays.

"It is widely believed that clarity prevents workplace errors."

3

Agentless Stance Markers

[Quality] + is expected / required to be + [Adjective/V3]

Creates professional norms without specifying individual actors.

"Constructive feedback is expected to be delivered with tact."

🚀A2 Active Opinions vs B1+ Passive Stance Upgrades

Compare informal active statements with objective passive transformations.

ACTIVE VOICE (A2) Direct Active

"Good leaders listen to people carefully in meetings."

PASSIVE UPGRADE (B1+) Trait + Passive Auxiliary

"Active listening is considered to be an indispensable trait in effective leadership."

ACTIVE VOICE (A2) Direct Active

"Managers must give feedback quietly and nicely."

PASSIVE UPGRADE (B1+) Passive Modal + Adverbial Tact

"Constructive feedback is best delivered in private with tact and respect."

ACTIVE VOICE (A2) Direct Active

"People often ignore body language in virtual calls."

PASSIVE UPGRADE (B1+) Passive Frequency Adverb

"Non-verbal cues are frequently overlooked during online video conferences."

ACTIVE VOICE (A2) Direct Active

"Everyone thinks emotional intelligence helps teamwork."

PASSIVE UPGRADE (B1+) It is widely believed that...

"It is widely believed that emotional intelligence drives successful team dynamics."
