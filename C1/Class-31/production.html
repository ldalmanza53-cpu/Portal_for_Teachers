Grammar Blueprint

Double Comparatives Formula

Express cause-and-effect relationships where one change directly causes another change!

Clause 1 (Cause / Condition)

THE + [COMPARATIVE] + Subject + Verb,

e.g., "The faster the music plays,"
Clause 2 (Effect / Result)

THE + [COMPARATIVE] + Subject + Verb.

e.g., "the faster my heart beats."
1. Short Adjectives (-er): loud ➔ the louder
fast ➔ the faster
2. Long Adjectives (more): energetic ➔ the more energetic
emotional ➔ the more emotional
3. Irregulars & Quantifiers: good ➔ the better
much/many ➔ the more
