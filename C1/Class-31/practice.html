Grammar Box • Page T-45

Double Comparatives

Use two comparatives, each preceded by the, to show how one quality or amount is linked to another. The first comparative expresses a condition for the second comparative.

Formula: The + [Comparative], the + [Comparative]

Condition (First Comparative)

The more you like to dance,

The more I hear a pop song,

The louder the music gets,

Result (Second Comparative)

the more you appreciate Latin music.

the less I feel like buying it.

the sooner I feel like leaving.
