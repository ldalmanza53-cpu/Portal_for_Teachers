"Style Spectrum Youth Magazine Editorial Room"

You are youth magazine editors crafting the cover story on modern fashion. Evaluate personal expression vs sustainability, challenge peer pressure, and negotiate a balanced manifesto using B1 opinion phrases.

Select your editor persona to view starter phrases:

Role A: The Expressive Stylist

Focuses on self-expression, confidence, personal branding, and creative trends.

  • "Clothing is a visual tool to express oneself..."
  • "Being a true trendsetter boosts confidence."
  • "Your personal style tells the world who you are."

Role B: The Eco-Conscious Practicalist

Focuses on sustainability, fast fashion critiques, durability, and minimal spending.

  • "We must criticize fast fashion pollution..."
  • "I prefer thrifting and sustainable items."
  • "Avoid impulse buys and focus on quality."

Role C: The Social Observer & Moderator

Focuses on peer pressure, dress codes, cultural norms, and finding consensus.

  • "How can youth handle peer pressure on campus?"
  • "Do dress codes restrict individual freedom?"
  • "Let's summarize a balanced story for our cover..."
