Section 2 • Review of Verb Patterns

The Four Core B1 Verb Patterns

Pattern a

verb + infinitive

When I choose clothes, I tend to think of comfort first.

Verbs: tend, refuse, seem, try, like, hate
Pattern b

verb + object + infinitive

Celebrities inspire me to change the way I look.

Verbs: inspire, allow, convince, require, compel, encourage
Pattern c

verb + gerund

I hate choosing my outfits in the morning.

Verbs: hate, enjoy, don't mind, can't help
Pattern d

verb + object + prep + gerund

High prices rarely prevent me from buying quality clothes.

Verbs: discourage...from, advise...against, prevent...from
