B1 Opinion Phrases

  • In my opinion, fast fashion is bad for the planet.
  • From my perspective, clothes show personal identity.
  • I reckon that buying vintage is more stylish.
  • As far as I'm concerned, comfort comes first.

Balancing & Contrasting Points

  • I see your point, but designer items are overpriced.
  • On the one hand... on the other hand...
  • Having said that, quality clothes last longer.
  • That's true, but we should also consider...

Key Fashion Vocabulary (B1 Level)

• Fast fashion: Cheap, trend-based clothes
• Sustainable: Eco-friendly production
• Self-expression: Showing personality
• Overpriced: Costs more than it's worth
• Vintage / Second-hand: Pre-owned apparel
• Trendsetter: Person who starts styles
• Dress code: Rules for required clothing
• Durable: Long-lasting quality material
