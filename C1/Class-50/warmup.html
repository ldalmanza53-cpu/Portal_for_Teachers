Classification Sentence Architecture

The Classification Essay Formula Matrix

Transform basic A2 descriptions like "Lions are big cats that eat meat" into B1+ academic classification statements: "Animals can be grouped into distinct categories based on their dietary criteria, with carnivores being distinguished by specialized physical adaptations for predation."

1

Thesis Statement Formula

[Kingdom/Group] + [can be classified into / divided into] + [X main categories] + [based on / according to] + [Principle Criterion]

Sets up the academic structure by stating the categories and overarching classification principle.

✔️ "Vertebrates can be divided into five distinct classes based on thermal regulation and skin structures."

2

Category Division Formula

[The first category / One key group] + [falls under / comprises] + [Category Name] + [which is characterized by / defined as] + [Key Feature]

Introduces a specific subgroup while providing defining biological traits.

✔️ "The primary category comprises mammals, which are characterized by endothermic regulation and mammary glands."

3

Distinguishing Feature Contrast Formula

[Category A] + [is distinguished from / differs from] + [Category B] + [by virtue of / in terms of] + [Contrasting Criteria]

Highlights key biological differences between two related groups.

✔️ "Reptiles are distinguished from amphibians by virtue of their waterproof keratin scales."
