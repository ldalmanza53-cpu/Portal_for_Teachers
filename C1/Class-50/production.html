Academic Writing Guide

Classification Essay Formula & Connectors

Learn how to organize an academic essay that divides a broad topic into clear, logical categories!

1

Classification Essay Structure

3-Part Academic Essay Formula
📌 Introduction & Thesis:

State the broad subject and state the Principle of Classification (e.g., "Animals in this biome can be classified into three distinct categories based on their feeding habits.")

🧩 Body Paragraphs (Categories 1, 2, 3):

Detail each group's distinguishing features and provide specific animal examples (e.g., "The first category consists of herbivores, which possess specialized teeth...")

🎯 Conclusion:

Summarize the main categories and explain the broader biological or ecological significance of these distinctions.

2

Classification Transitions

Connecting Phrases & Categorization Verbs
📂 Expressing Division:

"...can be divided into..." / "...fall under the category of..." / "...are classified according to..."

🔍 Describing Features:

"A defining characteristic of..." / "What distinguishes this group is..." / "Members of this class possess..."

⚖️ Contrasting Categories:

"In contrast to cold-blooded reptiles..." / "Unlike terrestrial species, aquatic animals..."
