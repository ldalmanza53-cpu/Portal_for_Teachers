Section A: Basic vs. B1+ Comparative Transformations

Shopping & Finance
A2 Baseline Sentence

"Older people like cash, but young people pay with phones."

B1+ Upgraded Generational Contrast

"While older generations used to prefer physical cash, younger cohorts are rapidly getting used to contactless digital payments."

Workplace Culture
A2 Baseline Sentence

"Boomers worked in one job for 40 years."

B1+ Upgraded Generational Contrast

"Unlike Baby Boomers, who used to stick to a single lifelong career, Millennials incline towards dynamic career switches."

Wellness & Health
A2 Baseline Sentence

"Young people go to gym and eat salad now."

B1+ Upgraded Generational Contrast

"Whereas previous eras viewed health reactively, Gen Z tends to prioritize proactive holistic wellness."

Section B: Visual Grammar Formulas

Master these three core formula patterns to speak and write with natural B1+ fluency.

Formula 1: Past Habit vs Present Trend
[Subj] + used to + [Base Verb]
whereas
[Subj] + tends to / inclines towards + [Verb-ing/Noun]

Used to contrast finished past routines with active modern habits.

Formula 2: Process of Adapting
[Subj] + is/are getting used to + [Verb-ing / Noun]
or
[Subj] + has/have adapted to + [Noun]

Expresses the dynamic journey of getting comfortable with lifestyle changes.

Formula 3: Generational Markers
Unlike [Gen A], [Gen B]...
or
Compared to older cohorts, [Gen Z]...

Elevates contrast transitions seamlessly without repeating basic words like "but".
