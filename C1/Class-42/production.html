Grammar Engine: Validate (B1) ➡️ Upgrade (B1+)

Generational Contrast Transformer Matrix

Validate baseline habit structures, then upgrade to nuanced contrast phrases!

Step 1: Baseline B1 Validation CORE B1
  • used to + verb (Past Habit) "Older generations used to stay in one company for 30 years."
  • compared to + noun (Direct Comparison) "Compared to my parents, I spend much more time online."
  • tend to + verb (General Truth) "Younger people tend to prioritize flexible working hours."
  • make an effort to + verb (Intentional Behavior) "Millennials make an effort to reduce plastic waste."
Step 2: B1+ Upgraded Complement UPGRADED B1+
  • ..., whereas / while ... (Clause Contrast) "Boomers valued career stability, whereas Gen Z seeks autonomy."
  • In contrast to older cohorts, ... "In contrast to older cohorts, modern youth favor second-hand thrifting."
  • There is a growing shift toward ... "There is a growing shift toward sober curious social gatherings."
  • Unlike previous generations, modern youth tend to ... "Unlike previous generations, Gen Z makes mental health a priority."
