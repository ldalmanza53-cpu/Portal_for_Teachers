Heroism Language & Grammar Matrix

Narratives, Moral Choices & Action Expressions

Combine past continuous story contexts with second conditional moral decisions and action verbs!

1. Narrative Context (Past Continuous + Simple)
"Subject + WAS/WERE -ING + when + Past Simple"
  • • "She was walking home when she noticed smoke coming from the window."
  • • "While everyone was waiting silently, a stranger stepped forward to help."
2. Moral Decisions (Second Conditional)
"If + Subject + Past Simple, Subject + WOULD + Verb"
  • • "If I saw someone being bullied, I would speak up immediately."
  • • "If we witnessed an accident, we would call emergency services right away."
3. Heroic Action Phrasal Verbs & Idioms
step up | stand up for | go above & beyond | lend a hand
  • • step up / take action: Take responsibility in a crisis.
  • • stand up for: Protect or defend someone being mistreated.
  • • go above and beyond: Do far more than what is required.
