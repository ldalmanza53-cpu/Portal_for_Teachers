Formula 1 Grammar Focus

Relative Clause Attribution

[Person] + who/whose + [heroic action]

Use relative clauses to precisely define everyday heroes without repetitive sentences.

"A quiet hero is someone who steps up when others hesitate."
Formula 2 Civic Stance

Moral & Civic Modals

[Subject] + should / must / ought to + [verb]

Express ethical duties, necessity, and recommended bystander responses.

"Citizens should act with integrity when witnessing injustice."
Formula 3 Impact Link

Action & Impact Links

[Action] + which / leading to + [societal effect]

Connect personal acts of kindness to broader societal transformation.

"Small daily gestures, which build trust, strengthen entire communities."
