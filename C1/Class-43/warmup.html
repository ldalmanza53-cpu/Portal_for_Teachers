🚀 Sentence Upgrade Transformations (A2 → B1+)

⚖️ Complex Comparatives
  • far more [adj] than "Online prices are far more reasonable than physical stores."
  • not nearly as [adj] as "Thrifting is not nearly as pricey as buying brand new."
🔀 Contrast Connectors
  • whereas / while "In-store shopping offers instant touch, whereas online shopping yields deeper discounts."
  • on the other hand "Online delivery is slow. On the other hand, price comparison is effortless."
💰 Value & Price Idioms
  • a total steal "Getting a designer coat for $20 was a total steal."
  • bang for your buck "Clearance sales give you maximum bang for your buck."
