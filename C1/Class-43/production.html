Language Engine: B1 Bargain Structures & Experience Connectors

Bargain Collocations & Comparative Connectors Matrix

Master precise shopping collocations and comparative connectors to contrast retail experiences!

Bargain Collocations ESSENTIAL B1
  • steep discount / clearance sale "The outlet mall offered a steep discount during the end-of-season clearance sale."
  • good value for money / get bang for your buck "Buying store-brand groceries gives you much better value for money."
  • impulse buy / price tag "I checked the price tag to avoid making an unplanned impulse buy."
  • price-match / flash deal "The electronics shop agreed to price-match the online flash deal."
Comparative Experience Connectors CONTRAST B1+
  • far cheaper than / a fraction of the price "Thrift clothes are a fraction of the price of brand-new retail items."
  • nowhere near as expensive as ... "Outlet stores are nowhere near as expensive as flagship luxury boutiques."
  • In terms of shopping atmosphere / On the flip side "Online deals save time. In terms of store atmosphere, physical browsing is nicer."
  • When compared with [X], [Y] offers ... "When compared with shopping malls, flea markets require more patience."
