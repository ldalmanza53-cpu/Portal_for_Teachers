CEFR B1 Grammar Blueprint

Origins & Grammar Matrix

Master passive voice of belief, historical origin connectors, and modals of speculation through blueprints and interactive puzzle reconstruction.

Rule 1 Passive Voice of Belief

Used when expressing widespread cultural beliefs without specifying an individual speaker.

Structure A:
It + is + believed/thought/said + that + [clause]
"It is believed that breaking a mirror brings seven years of bad luck."
Structure B:
[Subject] + is + thought/said + to + [verb]
"Knocking on wood is said to ward off evil spirits."
Rule 2 Cause & Origin Markers

Connect modern superstitions to their ancient historical roots and historical contexts.

stems from: [historical reason]
is rooted in: [ancient custom]
dates back to: [time period / era]
"The taboo around the number 13 dates back to ancient Norse mythology and medieval religious traditions."
Rule 3 Modals of Speculation

Expressing logical theories about how traditions began when exact origins are uncertain.

might have started when: [past event]
could originate from: [theory]
may bring: [hypothetical outcome]
"Throwing salt over the shoulder might have started because salt was extremely expensive in antiquity."
