Grammar & Origin Matrix

Explaining Causes, Origins & Possibilities

Master B1 grammar structures to explain how superstitions began and why people still practice them!

1. Cause & Origin Structures
Is rooted in / Stems from:

"Spilling salt is rooted in ancient beliefs about salt's value."

Was originally intended to:

"Throwing salt over the left shoulder was intended to ward off spirits."

2. Modals of Possibility / Assumption
Might / Could have originated:

"The fear of ladders might have originated in ancient Egypt."

Could be explained by:

"This ritual could be explained by people's desire for luck."

3. Beliefs & Psychological Function
Is believed to attract / Bring:

"A four-leaf clover is believed to attract good fortune."

Serves to reduce anxiety:

"Pre-game rituals serve to give athletes confidence."
