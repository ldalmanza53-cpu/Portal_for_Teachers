Grammar Focus

Success Formulas: Habits, General Truths & Stories

Master these 3 target structures to express industry facts and routines fluently!

1. General Truths (Zero Conditional)

If + Present Simple, Present Simple

Use this to state universal facts and rules in the music industry.

"If an indie artist interacts with fans daily, fan loyalty grows rapidly."
2. Habits & Routines

Tend to + Verb / Frequency Adverbs

Use this to describe typical routines and behaviors of top performers.

"Iconic songwriters tend to write lyrics late at night when inspiration strikes."
3. Success Stories

Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Contrast past struggles with completed milestones up to now.

"She started in a small garage, but she has released three gold albums so far."
