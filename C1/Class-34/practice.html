Bruno Mars

Hawaii, USA

Bruno Mars, born Peter Gene Bayot Hernández Jr., grew up in a very musical family in Hawaii. At age four, he would play with his family's band five days a week. When he was only 17, he moved to L.A. to write and produce music. At concerts, you'll see this multi-talented musician sing and play the piano or the guitar with ease.

Key Fact: Played in family band at age 4 ("would play"). Moved to L.A. at 17.
AD

Adele

London, UK

From a very young age, Adele would perform for her mother, impersonating her favorite singers. She went to a performing arts academy in her teens, and, while there, a friend posted some of her songs online. Two years later, recording companies started noticing her. Despite her fame, Adele will still sometimes suffer from stage fright before a performance.

Key Fact: Performed for mother as a child ("would perform"). Discovered online at academy.
RI

Rihanna

Barbados

As a girl in Barbados, Robyn Rihanna Fenty would sell clothes at a street stall. She loved to sing with neighbors and friends. True talent will always be noticed. In 2003, a visiting music producer discovered her. Today, superstar Rihanna will often reinvent her look with startling new hairstyles and clothes.

Key Fact: Sold clothes at street stall ("would sell"). Discovered in 2003 by producer.

Will and Would for Habits and General Truths

Master past narratives with would and present behavior or truths with will!

Grammar Rules: Will & Would Patterns

Past Habits WOULD

Use would to express habitual actions in the past. It is more formal than used to and requires a past time reference.

"From a very young age, Adele would perform for her mother."

Present Habits WILL

Use will to express personal habits or characteristic behavior in the present.

"Today, superstar Rihanna will often reinvent her look."

General Truths WILL

Use will to express facts that are generally true or universal predictions.

"True talent will always be noticed."
