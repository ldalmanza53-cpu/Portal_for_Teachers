Accuracy & Context Matrix

Upgrade simplistic statements into nuanced, balanced B1 discussion points using modals of obligation, conditionals for context, and evaluative stance markers.

1

Modals of Obligation Formula

[Group / Speaker] + [must / ought to / should / are expected to] + maintain + [Language Standard]

Expresses social or professional rules of language usage.

"Students are expected to maintain grammatical accuracy in academic writing."

2

Contextual Conditional Formula

[Language Feature] + is acceptable + [provided that / unless / depending on] + [Context / Audience]

Defines precise conditions under which informal register is appropriate.

"Casual slang is acceptable provided that the audience is close friends."

3

Evaluative Stance Formula

While [Feature A] + is valuable, + [Feature B] + ought to take priority + in [Context]

Balances accuracy against fluency or descriptive evolution against prescriptive rules.

"While perfection is ideal, clarity over perfection ought to take priority in live speech."

🚀A2 Simplistic Statements vs B1+ Evaluative Upgrades

Observe how simple opinions are elevated into mature CEFR B1 discussion arguments.

A2 SIMPLISTIC SENTENCE Direct Opinion

"Bad grammar is wrong and people must stop doing it."

CEFR B1+ EVALUATIVE UPGRADE Obligation & Context

"Communicators are expected to observe grammatical accuracy in formal contexts, though clarity ought to remain the main goal."

A2 SIMPLISTIC SENTENCE Direct Opinion

"Slang is okay because everyone speaks like that."

CEFR B1+ EVALUATIVE UPGRADE Conditional Appropriateness

"Casual slang is acceptable provided that the situation is informal, but formal register should be used with clients."

A2 SIMPLISTIC SENTENCE Direct Opinion

"Don't worry about mistakes if people understand you."

CEFR B1+ EVALUATIVE UPGRADE Balanced Stance Marker

"While standard English ensures precision, prioritizing clarity over perfection allows learners to build conversational fluency."

A2 SIMPLISTIC SENTENCE Direct Opinion

"Old grammar rules never change."

CEFR B1+ EVALUATIVE UPGRADE Language Evolution Perspective

"Although prescriptive rules preserve stability, descriptive usage shows that language evolution must be acknowledged."
