Language Correctness & Register Vault
Precision
The quality of being accurate and exact in word choice and grammar.
Register
The degree of formality in language appropriate for a specific context.
Subject-Verb Agreement
Ensuring singular subjects match singular verbs, and plurals match plurals.
Collocation
A natural combination of words that regularly occur together (e.g. 'make a decision').
Ambiguity
When language is open to more than one interpretation or creates confusion.
Proofreading
Carefully checking written text to find and correct grammar and spelling errors.
