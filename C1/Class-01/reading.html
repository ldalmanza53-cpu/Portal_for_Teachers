1. LESSON PLAN: A1 - A2 (BEGINNER / PRE-INTERMEDIATE)

Communicative Objective: Distinguish, isolate, and accurately pronounce key English phoneme contrasts (/s/ vs /ʃ/, /p/ vs /b/, /ɪ/ vs /iː/) through mouth gymnastics and minimal pair drills.
Cultural & Pedagogical: Nursery rhymes and tongue twisters. Task-Based Learning (TBL), Communicative Language Teaching (CLT), and TPR mouth gestures.

2. LESSON PLAN: B1 - C1 (INTERMEDIATE / ADVANCED)

Communicative Objective: Master connected speech mechanics (catenation/linking, intrusive /w/ & /j/, elision, schwa reduction) and expressive intonation contours.
Cultural & Pedagogical: Oratorical styles in public speaking, fast broadcasts. Dogme ELT, Speech Shadowing, and Intonation Theater.
