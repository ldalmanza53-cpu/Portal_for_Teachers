Context & Roles

Podcast Setup

Context: "Youth Voice International Podcast"

Welcome to today's episode on "Human Connections & Friendship". You are broadcasting live to international teenage listeners to explore what makes a true friend and resolve modern social dilemmas.

Select role tab to view responsibilities:

Role A: Podcast Host / Interviewer

  • Introduce the episode topic and welcome your guest speaker.
  • Ask outcome question prompts and follow-up with "Why do you think that?"
  • Keep track of time and manage the role swap at 5:00.

Role B: Guest Speaker

  • Express personal opinions and define friendship clearly.
  • Use B1 sentence starters (e.g. "In my view...", "A true friend is...").
  • Share real-life examples and stories from your experience.

Role C: Observer / Feedback Note-taker

  • Listen actively without interrupting during the interview.
  • Jot down key arguments, strong vocabulary, and main definitions in the Notepad.
  • Give 1 minute of constructive feedback during the wrap-up.
