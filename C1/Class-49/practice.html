Grammar Reference (T-71)

Whenever & Wherever vs. When & Where

Learn when to use universal adverbs (whenever / wherever) versus specific time/place adverbs (when / where).

1

Whenever & Wherever = "At any time" / "In any place"

Used for repeated habits, universal truths, or general conditions.

• Whenever experts talk about language, Alex is mentioned.
(At any time experts talk...)
• Wherever there are elephants painting, people are fascinated.
(In any place where elephants paint...)
⚡ Note: You can swap whenever/wherever with when/where if the meaning is general ("at any time/place").
2

When & Where = MUST be used for Specific Events

You CANNOT use whenever/wherever if referring to a single, specific time or place!

✔ When Brad Pitt worked with Bart in Legends of the Fall, he was impressed.
✖ (NOT: Whenever Brad Pitt worked... because it refers to one specific film project)
✔ There were a lot of people where the elephants were painting today.
✖ (NOT: wherever... because "today" specifies a unique location & time)
⚠️ Rule Check: Specific event = Only when/where. Universal habit = whenever/wherever.
