Time & Location Language Engine
SPECIFIC Time & Location Expressions:

Use when referring to concrete, exact historical events or precise geographical points:
• "At 9:00 AM yesterday at the local veterinary clinic..."
• "In July 2019 in Yellowstone National Park..."

UNDETERMINED Time & Location Expressions:

Use when referring to general, indefinite, or future conditions:
• "Somewhere in Africa at some point in the future..."
• "Ever / Never in a remote natural habitat..."

Animal Welfare & Roles Vocabulary Flashcards
Natural Habitat

Captivity

Species Preservation

Therapy Animal
A trained animal providing affection and comfort to people in medical facilities.
Domesticate
To adapt wild animals for human companionship or agricultural work.
Biodiversity
The variety of plant and animal life in a particular habitat or planet.
