Grammar Architecture & Formula Breakdown

The Time, Location & Opinion Matrix

Elevate basic A2 statements like "Dogs help people" into authoritative B1+ opinions: "In recent years, therapy dogs have provided emotional support to patients across urban hospitals."

1

Undetermined Time & Indefinite Location

[nowadays / in recent years] + [Subject] + [PRESENT PERFECT] + [somewhere / across habitats]

Used for ongoing trends, general roles, or unstated specific dates/places.

✔️ "Nowadays, bees have stabilized crops across global farms."

2

Specific Time & Precise Location

[in 2022 / last summer] + [Subject] + [PAST SIMPLE] + [at Sanctuary / inside Facility]

Used to ground your opinion in concrete historical evidence or specific case studies.

✔️ "In 2021, rescue dogs found survivors at the collapse site in Miami."

3

Nuanced Opinion & Role Stance

[In my view / I firmly contend] + [Animal Role] + [serve as / play a vital role in]

Frame your stance clearly using formal opinion markers combined with role vocabulary.

✔️ "From my point of view, keystone species serve as crucial ecological buffers."
