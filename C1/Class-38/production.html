Grammar Reference Engine

Perspective Prefixes Matrix

Attach prefixes to root words to instantly transform your opinion or perspective!

dis- Opposite / Negative

Examples: disagree, dissatisfaction, disrespect, disconnect

"I strongly disagree with this opinion."

un- Not / Unexpected

Examples: unpredictable, unusual, unrealistic, unaware

"The whole outcome was completely unpredictable."

mis- Wrong / Incorrect

Examples: misjudge, misunderstanding, miscalculate, misinform

"I misjudged the situation at first."

ir- / im- / in- Not / Illegal

Examples: irresponsible, inappropriate, impossible, illegal

"It is irresponsible to ignore safety rules."

over- Too Much / Excess

Examples: overrate, overdo, overestimate, overreact

"Many tourist spots are completely overrated."

re- Again / Re-evaluate

Examples: reconsider, re-evaluate, rethink, reconnect

"This event made me reconsider my stance."

non- Absence Of

Examples: non-essential, non-verbal, non-stop, non-binding

"We spent money on non-essential items."

Narrative Anchors Past Simple/Cont.

Phrases: "Once when I...", "In my experience...", "Last year..."

"I remember a time when I was traveling..."

3-Step Personal Experience Story Formula

Step 1: Set the Scene

"A couple of years ago, when I was..."

Establish the time, place, and background using Past Continuous ("I was studying when...").

Step 2: The Turning Point / Prefix Event

"Suddenly, an unpredictable event occurred..."

Describe the specific incident or misunderstanding using your target prefix word.

Step 3: The Lesson & Perspective Stance

"Looking back, I realized why I disagree..."

Connect your experience back to your stance on the main topic.
