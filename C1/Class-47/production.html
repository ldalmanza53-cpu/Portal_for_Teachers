Modals of Obligation & Recommendation

Policy Rules & Requirements
✔ Mandatory Action (Must / Have to):

"Brands must clearly disclose sponsored posts." / "Companies have to label native advertisements."

💡 Strong Recommendation (Should / Ought to):

"Advertisers should respect consumer privacy." / "Influencers ought to flag gifted products."

✖ Prohibition (Must not / Shouldn't):

"Companies must not write fake 5-star reviews." / "Marketers shouldn't manipulate young children."

2

Ethical Opinion Connectors

Watchdog Argumentation Frames
⚖️ Ethical Evaluation:

"From an ethical standpoint, this practice..." / "I consider this strategy highly deceptive because..."

🔍 Nuanced Balance:

"While it builds creative excitement, it crosses the line when..." / "Even if it generates organic buzz..."

📜 Proposed Regulation:

"To protect consumer trust, our ruling requires that..." / "We strongly advise enforcing penalties if..."
