B1 vs. B1+ Ethical Language Matrix

Elevate your ethical critiques! Transform simple opinions into structured, persuasive arguments using modals of obligation, conditional consequences, and ethical stance starters.

1

Modals of Obligation & Regulation

[Subject] + should / must / ought to / is allowed to + [Base Verb]

Use strong modals to express legal boundaries or moral responsibilities in marketing.

✔️ "Influencers must disclose paid sponsorships in every post."

2

Conditionals for Consequences

IF + [Present Simple], THEN + [Present/Future Result]

Demonstrate cause and effect between undercover tactics and consumer trust.

✔️ "If brands hide their identity, consumers lose trust."

3

Ethical Stance & Contrast

From an ethical perspective, while [X], [Y misleads buyers]

Balance practical business effectiveness against moral principles.

✔️ "While stealth marketing is effective, it misleads unsuspecting buyers."
