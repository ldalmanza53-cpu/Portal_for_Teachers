Target Vocabulary Summary (B1 Level)

Key Adjectives (Personality Traits)

  • Supportive: Giving help, sympathy, and encouragement.
  • Trustworthy: Honest and able to keep secrets.
  • Loyal: Remaining faithful to friends through bad times.
  • Thoughtful: Showing care for other people's feelings.
  • Distant: Cool, reserved, or emotionally unapproachable.

Key Phrasal Verbs (Relationship Actions)

  • Get along (with): Have a harmonious relationship.
  • Hit it off: Instantly become good friends upon meeting.
  • Drift apart: Slowly become less close over time.
  • Count on: Rely on someone for help or support.
  • Stick up for: Defend or support someone when criticized.
  • Fall out (with): Argue and stop being friendly.
  • Make up (with): Reconcile and become friendly after a fight.
