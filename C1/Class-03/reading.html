FORMATO PLANEADOR SMARTZONE PRESENCIAL INGLÉS

1. LESSON PLAN: A1 - A2 (CINEMA'S DAY: MOVIE TRAILER MAGIC & SNACK STAND STORIES)

Communicative Objective: Describe basic film genres, main characters, simple movie plots using present/past tenses, and successfully order cinema tickets and snacks using functional transactional phrases ("I'd like two tickets for...", "How much is a large popcorn?").

2. LESSON PLAN: B1 - C1 (CINEMA'S DAY: DIRECTORS' CUT & FILM REVIEW CRITIQUE)

Communicative Objective: Analyze complex cinematic themes, evaluate director choices (cinematography, character arcs, plot twists), deliver persuasive film critiques, and negotiate movie pitch ideas/alternative endings using advanced descriptive vocabulary and conditional structures ("Had the director chosen...").
