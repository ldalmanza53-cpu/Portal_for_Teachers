Personality & Trait Grammar Matrix

Transform basic personality descriptions (*He is shy...*) into sophisticated CEFR B1+ character evaluations using behavioral tendency verbs and contrast structures.

Blueprint 1: Behavioral Tendencies
• Subject + TENDS TO + Infinitive Verb
• Subject + HAS A HABIT OF + Gerund (-ing)
• Subject + IS INCLINED TO + Infinitive Verb

*Example:* "She is inclined to listen carefully before offering advice."

Blueprint 2: Character Contrast
• Person A + Verb..., WHEREAS + Person B + Verb...
• WHILE + Person A + Verb..., Person B + Verb...
• Person A + Verb... IN CONTRAST TO + Person B...

*Example:* "He prefers solitary research, whereas his partner excels in client meetings."

Blueprint 3: Deductions & Character Clauses
• Someone WHO + tends to + Verb...
• Subject + MUST BE / APPEARS TO BE + Adjective
• Subject + SEEMS TO THRIVE IN + Noun Phrase

*Example:* "She appears to thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environments."

A2 Basic Descriptions vs. B1+ Character Transformations

A2 Basic Description B1+ Enriched Character Form
"He is quiet and doesn't talk much in meetings."
"He is a reserved colleague who tends to observe discussions before making deliberate contributions."
A2 Basic Description B1+ Enriched Character Form
"She likes talking to people and he likes working alone."
"She is a charismatic speaker whereas her co-worker is inclined to work independently."
