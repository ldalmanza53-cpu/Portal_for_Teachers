Section 17 • Grammar Plus

Gerund vs Infinitive Meaning Differences

forget

+ Infinitive: Didn't actually do it.
Marcello forgot to meet his friend.

+ Gerund: Action done earlier in past.
Marcello never forgot meeting the actor.

mean

+ Infinitive: Intend to do.
I meant to visit Sheila.

+ Gerund: Involve or necessitate.
Inviting Emile means inviting Eva too.

regret

+ Infinitive: Polite bad news (inform/say).
We regret to inform you...

+ Gerund: Be sorry for past action.
Donna regretted missing the wedding.

Be + Adjective + Infinitive (+ to verb)

be amazed, be determined, be happy, be lucky, be ashamed, be eager, be hesitant, be ready

Be + Adj/Verb + Preposition + Gerund (+ verb-ing)

be bored with, be convinced of, be good at, be used to, be worried about, apologize for, complain about, think of
