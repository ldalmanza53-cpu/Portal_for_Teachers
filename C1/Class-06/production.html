Context & Advisor Roles

Campus Task Force

"Campus Connection Task Force: Newcomer Survival Guide"

You are student advisors creating an essential guide for new students arriving on campus. Your mission is to evaluate strategies for meeting new people and guide newcomers using precise Gerund (-ing) and Infinitive (to + verb) constructions.

Select your advisor role to view starter phrases:

Role A: Activity Advocate

Focuses on recommending social activities & habits using Gerunds (-ing).

  • "I strongly recommend joining a campus club..."
  • "Consider taking up a new hobby or sport..."
  • "How about attending orientation workshops?"
  • "Avoid staying isolated in your dorm room."

Role B: Experience Analyzer

Focuses on past attempts, decisions, & advice using Infinitives (to + verb).

  • "I decided to join the language exchange club."
  • "It's essential to offer help to classmates."
  • "Try not to feel nervous when breaking the ice."
  • "Remember to ask open-ended questions!"

Role C: Language & Strategy Auditor

Monitors accuracy, asks follow-up outcome questions, and logs key recommendations.

  • Checks if speakers correctly use Gerunds or Infinitives.
  • Asks: "Did you mean 'stop to talk' or 'stop talking'?"
  • Guides the team through the 20 Prompts and logs key strategies into the Scratchpad.

Verbs + Gerund (-ing) Action/Habit

Suggesting, enjoying, avoiding, or recommending activities.

Verbs + Infinitive (to + verb) Goal/Intention

Decisions, plans, hopes, promises, and direct intentions.

Verbs + Both (Meaning Shift) Key Difference

Meaning changes depending on whether Gerund or Infinitive is used!
