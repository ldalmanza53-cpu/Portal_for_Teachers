Grammar Rule: Subject-Verb Inversion

1. Past Unreal Conditionals (Had)

Replace If by inverting the subject and the auxiliary had.

If they had been unable to work together...

Had they been unable to work together...

2. Negative Past Unreal (Had + not)

Negative forms are NOT contracted. Not is separated from had.

If this company hadn't created the app...

Had this company not created the app...

3. Present/Future Real Conditionals (Should)

Replace If with Should to sound more formal. Does NOT express obligation.

If you want to buy natural products...

Should you want to buy natural products...
