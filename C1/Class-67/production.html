Language & Toolkit Matrix

Expressing Goals, Reasons & Hypothetical Conditionals

Master connectors of goal/reason and conditional structures for hypothetical situations!

1. Connectors of Goal & Purpose
IN ORDER TO / SO AS TO + [Verb] | WITH THE AIM OF + [Verb-ing]
  • • "Founders innovate in order to solve pressing global problems."
  • • "They launched the app with the aim of reducing food waste."
2. Connectors of Reason & Cause
BECAUSE OF / DUE TO + [Noun Phrase] | SINCE + [Clause]
  • • "The startup pivoted because of unexpected market shifts."
  • • "They secured funding due to their innovative business model."
3. Hypothetical Conditionals (2nd & 3rd)
IF + [Past Simple], WOULD + [Verb] | IF + [Had + V3], WOULD HAVE + [V3]
  • • "If I launched a tech startup, I would focus on AI ethics."
  • • "If they hadn't taken the risk, they wouldn't have disrupted the industry."
