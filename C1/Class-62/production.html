Biography Grammar & Writing Engine

Chronology, Tenses & 4-Part Bio Framework

Master how to structure a professional B1 biography from early life to enduring legacy!

1. Past Simple vs. Present Perfect
Completed Past Event vs. Ongoing Legacy
  • • Past Simple: "He was born in 1918 and won the Nobel Prize."
  • • Present Perfect: "Her books have inspired readers since 1960."
2. Time Connectors
In [Year] / Later / After / Since then
  • • "After graduating from university, she moved..."
  • • "A few years later, he made his breakthrough."
3. Relative Clauses in Bio
Person + WHO / WHOSE | Invention + WHICH
  • • "...a pioneer who transformed medicine."
  • • "...an author whose novels won accolades."
4. 4-Part Bio Structure
Early Life → Milestones → Obstacles → Legacy
  • • Para 1: Childhood & Influences
  • • Para 2: Breakthroughs & Accolades
  • • Para 3: Overcoming Obstacles
  • • Para 4: Lasting Impact
