Adverb Clauses of Condition Engine
Validation (Baseline "If" Clauses)
If + Present Simple, Will / Would + Base Verb

Example: "I will take the job if the salary is high."

Complement (Upgraded Adverb Clauses of Condition)
provided that | as long as | unless | on condition that | assuming that | in case

Example: "I would accept the position provided that remote flexibility is guaranteed."
Example: "Unless the corporate culture respects work-life balance, I would refrain from applying."

Condition Connectors & Job Preferences Vault
Provided that
On the condition that something happens or is guaranteed (e.g. 'provided that health insurance is included').
As long as
During the time that, or provided that a specific condition continues to be met.
Unless
Except on the condition that (equivalent to 'if not').
On condition that
A formal agreement that a specific requirement must be satisfied.
Assuming that
Taking for granted that a particular circumstance is true or will occur.
In case
As a precaution against a possible future occurrence or eventuality.
Autonomy
The freedom and independence to make decisions regarding one's work.
Remuneration
Financial payment or compensation received for work or services rendered.
Work-Life Balance
A healthy equilibrium between professional responsibilities and personal life.
Flexibility
The ability to adapt working hours or locations according to personal needs.
Professional Fulfillment
A feeling of deep satisfaction gained from meaningful career achievements.
Career Progression
The process of advancing to higher roles, salaries, or responsibilities over time.
