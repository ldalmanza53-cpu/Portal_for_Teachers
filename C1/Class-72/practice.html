Grammar Plus • T-147

Adverb Clauses of Condition

Learn advanced condition connectors (*in the event that*, *just in case*, *whether or not*, *even if*, *if only*) and practice matching and sentence completion.

In the event (that) vs. (Just) in case

Both introduce a condition upon which another situation depends. In the event (that) is more formal.

• In the event that a replacement cannot be found, you'll take extra responsibilities.
• Call this number just in case the copy machine breaks down.

Whether or not / Whether... or not

Introduces a condition that does not change or alter the outcome.

• Whether or not it involves travel, I'm taking this job.
• Whether it involves travel or not, I'm taking this job.

Even if (with 'still')

Introduces a condition which, even if true, doesn't affect the result. Frequently paired with still.

• I'm (still) going to call in sick tomorrow even if I'm not actually sick.

If only (Wish / Regret)

Introduces a condition that the speaker strongly wishes to be true (often unreal past or present).

• If only I had known about that job opening, I would have applied immediately.
