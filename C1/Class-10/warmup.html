Stage 2 • Grammar & Vocab Bank

Language Toolkit: Comparing First Impressions

When comparing first impressions vs. reality, or comparing two different presentations, use these B1 target structures:

1. Perception Verbs
  • • At first glance, it looks like...
  • • It seems / appears to be...
  • • My initial impression was that...

"At first glance, this chart looks like a simple graph, but it shows key revenue growth."

2. Comparative Contrast Markers
  • • Compared to [A], [B] is much more...
  • • Whereas [A] feels calm, [B] seems...
  • • On the one hand..., but on the other hand...

"Compared to long text slides, infographics are far easier to process."

3. Initial vs. Reality Shifts

Use past vs. present contrast to show how your view changed:

  • • Initially, I thought..., but now I realize...
  • • Although it sounded like..., it turned out to be...

"Initially, I thought the song sounded dark, but the lyrics are hopeful."
