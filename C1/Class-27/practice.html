Sentence Adverbs & Conjunctive Adverbs

Express speaker attitudes toward entire clauses and logically connect sequential ideas in B1 writing.

Sentence Adverbs

To express the speaker's attitude about the entire sentence, sentence adverbs are located at the beginning and set off by a comma:

• Predictably, the hero of the movie won the heart of the girl.

• People were amazed that... → Amazingly, the movie sold 26 million tickets.

• Nobody was surprised that... → Not surprisingly, the sequel was popular.

Conjunctive Adverbs

Conjunctive adverbs link a sentence with a preceding idea, showing cause/effect, time, or contrast:

accordingly • consequently • hence • indeed • meanwhile • otherwise • thus

Example: "The coming attractions ended and the movie began; accordingly, the audience fell silent."
