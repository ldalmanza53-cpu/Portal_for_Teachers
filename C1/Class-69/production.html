Inverted Conditionals Language Matrix

Validation Baseline (If) vs. Complement Upgrade (Inversion)

Master subject-verb inversion by omitting 'if' and placing the auxiliary verb before the subject!

1. Past Hypotheticals / Regrets
Standard 'If' Baseline:

"If we had known about the crisis, we would have acted."

Inverted Complement Upgrade:

"Had we known about the crisis, we would have acted."

2. Future Hypotheticals / Shifts
Standard 'If' Baseline:

"If the company were to expand, costs would rise."

Inverted Complement Upgrade:

"Were the company to expand, costs would rise."

3. Formal Contingencies
Standard 'If' Baseline:

"If you require assistance, contact our advisor."

Inverted Complement Upgrade:

"Should you require assistance, contact our advisor."
