Stage 1 • Structural Grammar & Frameworks

How to Categorize Information & Discuss Effects

When analyzing technology, unorganized facts confuse your audience. Good communicators group facts into clear categories (e.g., Health, Environment, Society, Ethics) and balance positive effects with negative consequences.

1. Categorization Phrases
  • • This falls under the category of...
  • • We can classify this as a...
  • • In terms of [Category],...
  • • This belongs to the field of...

"In terms of public health, medical robots reduce surgical recovery time."

2. Expressing Positive Effects
  • • A major positive effect is...
  • • It brings significant benefits to...
  • • This leads to an improvement in...
  • • It plays a crucial role in...

"A major positive effect of clean energy is the reduction of air pollution."

3. Negative Consequences
  • • A serious negative consequence is...
  • • It poses a severe risk regarding...
  • • One potential drawback includes...
  • • This results in harm to...

"One potential drawback includes increased screen addiction among teens."
