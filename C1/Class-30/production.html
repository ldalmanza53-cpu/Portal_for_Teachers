Welcome to the StreamHub Viewer Panel! As a TV critic and reviewer, your job is to discuss current television trends, express your preferences clearly, and give compelling recommendations to fellow viewers.

✅ Validate (What You Already Know):

Basic preference phrases ("I like...", "I prefer X to Y") and fundamental reason connectors ("because", "exciting / boring").

Elevated B1/B1+ phrases ("I'm totally hooked on", "not my cup of tea") and TV features ("binge-worthy", "slow-burn", "overrated").

B1 Phrasebook: Basic (Validated) vs Upgraded (Complemented)

Expressing High Interest

Basic (Validated): "I like [Show Name] very much."
🚀 Upgraded (Complemented):

• "I'm completely hooked on..."

• "I'm really into [Genre] because..."

• "I find [Show] absolutely captivating."

Expressing Dislike & Nuance

Basic (Validated): "I don't like it. It is boring."
🚀 Upgraded (Complemented):

• "It's not really my cup of tea."

• "I find it a bit overrated despite the hype."

• "I can't stand predictable storylines."

Comparing Preferences

Basic (Validated): "I prefer comedies than dramas." (Grammar Error!)
🚀 Upgraded (Complemented):

• "I prefer comedies TO dramas." (Correct preposition)

• "I tend to favor documentaries over reality TV."

• "I'd much rather watch a sitcom than a horror film."

TV Feature Adjectives

Basic (Validated): "It is exciting / good / funny."
🚀 Upgraded (Complemented):

• Binge-worthy: So good you watch all episodes at once.

• Slow-burn: Plot develops slowly but pays off.

• Mind-bending: Confusing, mystery plot twists.

• Thought-provoking: Makes you think deeply about real life.
