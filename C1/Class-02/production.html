Context & Editorial Roles

Youth Voice Pitch

Setting: "Youth Voice Magazine"

You are standardizing the lead article for next month's issue: "Do Friends Need Common Ground to Stay Close?" Your team has 10 minutes to debate opinions and pitch a unified thesis statement to the Editor-in-Chief.

Select team role to view duties:

Role A: Lead Debater

  • Ask outcome question prompts and challenge lazy assumptions.
  • Provide real personal examples (e.g. hobbies vs. deep values).
  • Offer counter-arguments (e.g. "What about opposing personalities?").

Role B: Thesis Architect

  • Group the group's ideas into main claims and secondary factors.
  • Operate the Interactive Thesis Builder tool during Phase 3.
  • Ensure the thesis is specific, clear, and takes a firm stance.

Role C: Magazine Editor / Presenter

  • Refine grammar, B1 vocabulary usage, and sentence structure.
  • Complete the Group Pitch Notepad & Exporter entries.
  • Deliver the 30-second final thesis pitch to the class during Phase 4.
