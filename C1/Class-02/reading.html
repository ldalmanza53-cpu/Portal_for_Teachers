1. LESSON PLAN: A1 - A2 (VOX POP & THE OPINION EXPRESS)

Communicative Objective: Express personal preferences, agreement/disagreement, and basic reasons regarding everyday topics using opinion starters ("In my opinion...", "I prefer... because...") in street interviews.

2. LESSON PLAN: B1 - C1 (DEBATERS' DEN & THE OXBRIDGE ROUNDTABLE)

Communicative Objective: Construct persuasive arguments (Claim, Evidence, Reasoning), defend viewpoints, counter opposing arguments diplomatically, and employ active discourse markers (hedging, concession) during parliamentary debates.
