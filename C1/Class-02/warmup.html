Core Learning Concept

What is a Thesis Statement?

A thesis statement is the single most important sentence in an essay. It clearly tells the reader your main argument (claim) and reasons.

The B1 Thesis Statement Formula

1. Counter-clause (Optional)

"Although friends can have different hobbies,"

2. Core Claim / Opinion

"they must share core moral values"

3. Linking Words

"in order to..." / "because..."

4. Supporting Reasons

"build trust and resolve conflicts easily."

Combined Strong Thesis Statement:

"Although friends can have different hobbies, they must share core moral values in order to build trust and resolve conflicts easily."

Too Broad / Vague

"Friendship is good and people like friends."

Why it fails: It gives no clear opinion or specific reasons to discuss in body paragraphs.

Just a Fact (No Claim)

"Many friends play video games together."

Why it fails: This is a simple fact, not an argument. There is nothing to prove or debate.

Strong B1 Thesis

"True friends need shared core values because common beliefs prevent misunderstandings."

Why it succeeds: Clear stance + direct reason that can be developed in an essay!

Final Output

Your Friendship Essay Plan

Here is how your generated thesis statement structures a full B1 essay outline:

Paragraph 1: Introduction

• Hook: Friendship is one of the most valuable relationships in human life.

• Background Sentence: People often debate whether friends need to be similar or if opposites attract.

Thesis Statement:

"Although friends can have different hobbies, they must share core moral values and honesty in order to maintain trust and resolve disagreements smoothly."

Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1 (Counter-Argument / Hobbies)

• Topic Sentence: On one hand, friends do not always need to enjoy the same hobbies or activities.

• Example/Explanation: For instance, one friend may love sports while another prefers reading, allowing them to learn new things from each other.

Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2 (Core Claim & Values)

• Topic Sentence: However, sharing core moral values is essential for a long-lasting bond.

• Example/Explanation: When friends agree on honesty and respect, they can resolve arguments easily and trust each other in tough times.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion

• Restated Thesis: In conclusion, while shared interests make hanging out fun, shared principles keep friendships alive.

• Final Thought: Therefore, true friendship is built on respect rather than identical hobbies.
