Character Qualities & Opinion Matrix

Outlining Qualities & Expressing Changemaker Opinions

Combine precise B1+ character descriptors with strong personal opinion frames!

1. Outlining Role Model Qualities (Character Descriptors)
selfless | empathetic | resilient | principled | accountable | altruistic | visionary | humble
  • • Selfless / Altruistic: Consistently putting others' needs before personal gain.
  • • Resilient: Bouncing back from severe setbacks with determination.
  • • Principled / Accountable: Staying true to moral values and taking responsibility.
  • • Empathetic / Humble: Understanding others' feelings while remaining modest.
2. Expressing Opinions on Changemakers
"In my view, a true role model is..." | "What impresses me most about people who make a difference is..."
  • • "In my view, a genuine role model is someone who leads through actions rather than words."
  • • "What impresses me most about changemakers is their unwavering ability to remain resilient during crisis."
  • • "I strongly maintain that people who make a difference prioritize community welfare over popularity."
  • • "If more leaders possessed empathy, our society would address inequality more effectively."
