Grammar Focus (T-93)

Superlative Compound Adjectives Rules

Learn how superlative forms are built, when to hyphenate, and how they behave before nouns vs. after verbs.

1

The Most / The Least Rule

Formed by adding the most or the least before the compound adjective.

⚠️ NEVER place a hyphen after most or least!
  • • the most easily impressed
  • • the most easygoing
  • • the least narrow-minded
2

Short First-Word Form (-est / best)

When the first word is a short 1 or 2-syllable adjective/adverb, use its superlative form + hyphen + participle.

✏️ Keep the hyphen between components!
  • • hard → the hardest-working
  • • good → the best-looking
  • • well → the best-trained
3

Predicate Position (After Verb 'Be')

Superlatives can occur after the verb be without a noun following. Hyphenation rules still apply.

💬 Conversational or formal position!
  • • "Of all the men I know, he's the hardest working."
  • • "She is one of the most warm-hearted."
