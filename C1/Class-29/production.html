Studio Roles

ROLE A

The Creative Showrunner

Story Lead & World-Builder

You focus on character relationships, dramatic plot hooks, sci-fi/mystery settings, and Season 1 cliffhangers.

🎯 Useful B1 Vocab: Protagonist, conflict, plot twist, setting, cliffhanger, backstory.
💬 Key Line: "The main storyline revolves around a character who discovers..."
ROLE B

The Streaming Executive

Audience & Popularity Lead

You care about target demographic, social media viral potential (TikTok/Insta), budget, and international appeal.

🎯 Useful B1 Vocab: Target audience, viral hit, binge-worthy, global appeal, marketing tagline.
💬 Key Line: "To ensure high ratings, this show appeals directly to young adults because..."
ROLE C

The Music & Culture Critic

Soundtrack & Vibe Lead

You focus on musical atmosphere, theme songs, acoustic tone, and adapting themes from popular songs or books.

🎯 Useful B1 Vocab: Soundtrack, ambient music, emotional resonance, theme song, mood, aesthetic.
💬 Key Line: "The soundtrack features an energetic indie-pop soundtrack that sets the emotional mood during..."

B1 Pitch Phrasebook Cheat Sheet

Expressing Pitch Ideas

  • "Our TV show concept is inspired by..."
  • "The storyline focuses primarily on..."
  • "What sets our show apart is the unique blend of..."

Describing Soundtrack & Mood

  • "The soundtrack creates an intense atmosphere during..."
  • "We want to use acoustic indie music to emphasize..."
  • "The theme song sets a dramatic tone right from..."

Explaining Popularity & Appeal

  • "This show will go viral on TikTok because..."
  • "It appeals strongly to viewers who enjoyed..."
  • "Viewers won't be able to stop binge-watching due to..."

Polite Pitch Feedback & Votes

  • "I really like the cliffhanger idea, but perhaps we could..."
  • "From an executive perspective, this show definitely deserves a..."
  • "I agree that the soundtrack matches the plot, however..."
