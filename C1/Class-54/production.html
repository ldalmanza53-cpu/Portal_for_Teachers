Noun Clause Opinion Language Engine
Formula A: Direct Object 'That-Clause' Opinions
I [believe / maintain / notice] + THAT + [Subject + Verb...]

Example: "I believe that marine biologists face tough field conditions."

Formula B: Subject 'What-Clause' Opinions
WHAT + [Subject + Verb...] + IS THAT + [Clause]

Example: "What makes park ranger jobs appealing is that they preserve wild nature."

Formula C: 'The Fact That' Fact-Opinion Clause
THE FACT THAT + [Clause] + [proves / shows / means...]

Example: "The fact that renewable tech is growing fast proves its career value."
