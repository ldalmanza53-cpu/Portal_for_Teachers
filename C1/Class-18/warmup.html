Stage 1 • Language Framework

How to Discuss "Is Technology Good or Bad?"

Instead of saying just "technology is good" or "technology is bad", B1 speakers present balanced views using contrast connectors and clear argument categories.

1. Expressing Positive Sides (Pros)
  • • A major advantage of tech is that...
  • • It brings huge benefits to...
  • • Technology makes it easier to...
  • • It plays a positive role in...

"A major advantage of tech is that it allows instant communication worldwide."

2. Expressing Negative Sides (Cons)
  • • On the negative side,...
  • • A significant drawback is that...
  • • Technology can lead to...
  • • One major concern is...

"On the negative side, excessive screen time can cause health problems."

3. Contrast & Balanced Conclusions
  • • On the one hand..., but on the other hand...
  • • While tech offers benefits, it also...
  • • However, we must consider...
  • • Overall, it depends on how we use it.

"While tech offers benefits, we must be careful about digital distraction."
