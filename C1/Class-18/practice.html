Grammar Plus • Page 15

-ing Clauses & Agent Alignment

Master how to align sentence agents correctly to avoid dangling modifiers, and practice expressions followed by -ing clauses.

Rule 1: Agent Alignment

When an -ing clause begins a sentence, the agent (the person or thing doing the action) must be the subject of the main clause that follows.

Incorrect (Dangling Modifier):

"Trying hard to fix my computer, the dog kept staring at me."

(Sounds like the dog was trying to fix the computer!)
Correct Agent Subject:

"Trying hard to fix my computer, I noticed the dog staring at me."

(I am fixing the computer, and I am the main clause subject!)
Rule 2: Expressions + -ing Clauses

Use the gerund (-ing form) immediately following these common time and experience expressions:

have a good time
have an easy time
have fun
spend time
have a hard time
have difficulty
have problems
waste time

Discussion • Page 17

Is Technology Good or Bad?

Read public opinions from South Korea and Paraguay, reflect on critical tech purchasing questions, and construct your own B1 response.

Kwang-hyun Lee

Busan, South Korea

"When you are ready to invest in a new technology, there are a few questions you should ask yourself. First, is the new item cheaper than what it replaces? Does it save space? Will it make your life easier? Is it energy efficient? Will you be able to recycle it or at least dispose of it properly? If not, don't buy it."

Key Focus: Practical & Environmental Criteria

Focuses on cost, space efficiency, convenience, energy rating, and disposal/recyclability.

Inés Candia

Asunción, Paraguay

"How we think about technology is flawed. We are overeager to have the newest gadget. We are too impressed by speed, noise, compactness, and general flashiness. We need a more mature and cautious way of thinking about technology. New is not always better."

Key Focus: Cautious Consumer Mindset

Warns against being seduced by "flashiness", speed, and impulse gadget purchases.
