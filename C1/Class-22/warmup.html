Stage 2 • Facts vs Opinions

Sharing Opinions on Fascinating Facts

In English, an Objective Fact can be proven true or false, while a Personal Opinion expresses feelings or beliefs (e.g., "I find it surprising that...", "What strikes me most is...").

Classification Challenge Statement 1 of 4

"Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood."

B1 Sentence Starters for Reacting to Facts

Expressing Surprise

"What surprises me most about this fact is..."

"I was amazed to discover that..."

Expressing Interest

"I find it fascinating that..."

"It's really interesting how..."

Expressing Skepticism

"To be honest, I'm a bit skeptical about..."

"It sounds hard to believe, but..."

Model B1 Speech Response:

"Today, I'd like to address the news about AI tutors in classrooms. According to recent reports, technology can personalize learning. On the one hand, AI provides quick answers, but on the other hand, human teachers provide emotional support.
