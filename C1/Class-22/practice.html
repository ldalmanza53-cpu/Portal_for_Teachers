Grammar • Activity 11 • Pages T-32 & T-33

Reporting Clauses in the Passive

Master general claims using It + passive verb + (that)... (e.g., It is said that..., It was reported that...).

Grammar Structure: It + Passive Reporting Verb + (that)...

To report a general claim, rumor, or opinion without naming a specific person, use it + a passive form of a reporting verb. The agent is omitted and understood to be "people in general" or "experts."

Simple Present:

"It is said that..."
"It is claimed that..."

Simple Past:

"It was explained that..."
"It was once thought that..."

Present Perfect:

"It's been confirmed that..."
"It has been reported that..."
