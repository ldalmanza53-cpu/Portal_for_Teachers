Summit Context & Interactive Roles

Global Youth Ambassador

"Global Youth Ambassador Summit"

You are candidates preparing for the international networking lounge. Your objective is to practice making instant rapport, projecting confidence through non-verbal cues, and navigating small talk with global delegates.

Select your active role to view starter guidelines:

Role A: The Networking Candidate

Presents key first impression strategies and shares personal networking experiences.

  • "To make a memorable impression, you should maintain open body posture..."
  • "In my experience, asking open-ended questions helps build quick rapport."
  • "I always make sure to repeat their name naturally during the greeting."

Role B: The Executive Evaluator

Challenges strategies, presents realistic networking challenges, and tests candidate adaptability.

  • "How would you handle a delegate who appears shy or uncommunicative?"
  • "What if there is a sudden cultural misunderstanding during greetings?"
  • "Could you demonstrate how you would politely exit a dying conversation?"

Role C: The Communication & Language Coach

Monitors B1 grammar (modals, gerunds/infinitives), tracks non-verbal cues, and keeps timing on track.

  • "Notice how candidate A used 'It's essential to...' with strong intonation."
  • "Remember to use gerunds for advice: 'Practicing active listening...'"
  • "Great body posture! Now let's move to the next phase on the timer."
