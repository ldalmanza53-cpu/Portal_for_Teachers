Global Streaming Summit

What Makes a TV Show a Worldwide Hit?

Welcome to the Global Entertainment Panel! You and your partner(s) are media experts participating in a 10-minute live podcast discussion. Your mission is to discuss personal TV viewing preferences, analyze why certain TV shows go viral, and agree on recommendations for international audiences.

10-Minute Activity Timeline

Min 0:00 - 1:00

1 Warm-Up & Role Sync

Greet panel members, introduce your assigned role personality, and set up your microphone.

Min 1:00 - 8:00

2 Core Panel Discussion

Draw cards from the Prompt Deck. Express preferences, explain viral popularity, and debate show quality (aim for 5-8 prompts).

Min 8:00 - 10:00

3 Final Pitch & Consensus

Each panelist pitches ONE ultimate show recommendation. Agree on a final winner before time expires!

Detailed Panel Roles

ROLE A

The Passionate Binge-Watcher

Focus: Emotional intensity, cliffhangers, sci-fi/thrillers

You live for dramatic plot twists and watching whole seasons in one sitting. You care deeply about character connections and mysterious storylines.

🎯 B1 Vocabulary: Binge-watch, addicting, cliffhanger, plot twist, season finale.
💬 Signature Line: "I couldn't stop watching! The cliffhanger at the end of episode 3 completely blew my mind."
ROLE B

The Culture & Media Critic

Focus: Social media trends, production budgets, cultural impact

You analyze why shows go viral on TikTok or Instagram. You pay attention to soundtrack quality, famous actors, and critical acclaim.

🎯 B1 Vocabulary: Viral trend, overrated, soundtrack, production budget, performance.
💬 Signature Line: "From a critical standpoint, what makes it popular is how it trends on social media."
ROLE C

The Casual Curator

Focus: Relaxation, light comedies, reality TV, mood watching

You watch TV to unwind after work/school. You prefer short, comforting, easy-to-follow episodes or guilty-pleasure reality shows.

🎯 B1 Vocabulary: Easy-to-watch, lighthearted, feel-good, unwind, broad appeal.
💬 Signature Line: "After a long day, I just want something comforting that makes me laugh without heavy drama."

B1 Panel Language Cheat Sheet

Expressing Personal Likes & Dislikes

  • "I'm really keen on..." (e.g., sci-fi shows with strong female leads)
  • "I tend to avoid..." (e.g., reality shows because they feel scripted)
  • "What draws me to a show is..." (e.g., unexpected plot twists)

Explaining Viral Popularity

  • "The main reason for its popularity is..." (e.g., social media hype)
  • "It spread rapidly on TikTok because..." (e.g., iconic soundtrack)
  • "It appeals to a global audience due to..." (e.g., relatable characters)

Agreeing & Disagreeing Politely

  • "I completely agree with you on..." (e.g., the soundtrack being amazing)
  • "That's a valid point, but from my perspective..."
  • "I see what you mean, though I felt that..." (e.g., the ending was rushed)

Pitching & Recommending

  • "If I could recommend only one series..."
  • "It is definitely worth watching because..."
  • "You shouldn't miss it if you enjoy..."
