You are co-hosting a youth culture episode titled "Decoding Connections: Modern Encounters & Music Bonds". Your goal is to debate ways of meeting people, use polite disagreement phrases, and share a story about an unexpected meeting 🎵.

Select a podcast role to inspect duties:

Role A: The Modernist / Spontaneous Connector

  • Advocates for social apps, music festivals, speed networking, and sudden public encounters.
  • Believes technology accelerates genuine bonds and makes people more approachable.
  • Shares a spontaneous unexpected meeting story from a concert or event.

Role B: The Traditionalist / Selective Friend

  • Advocates for traditional introductions, hobby clubs, workshops, and mutual friends.
  • Believes in slow, cautious relationship building; values safety and shared background.
  • Uses polite disagreement to challenge Role A's reliance on fast digital connections.

Role C: Podcast Host & Story Navigator

  • Asks outcome question prompts from the Prompts Hub to guide the debate.
  • Enforces the 3-step Polite Disagreement formula and monitors vocabulary usage.
  • Guides the "Unexpected Meeting 🎵 Story" outline and logs group summary notes.
