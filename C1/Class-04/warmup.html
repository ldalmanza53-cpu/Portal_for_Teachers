Stage 2 • Functional Language

Mastering Polite Disagreement

In English conversation, saying "You're wrong!" sounds rude. We use softeners and acknowledgments before stating our opinion.

1. Acknowledge Their Point

"I see your point..."
"That's a valid argument..."
"I understand what you mean..."

2. Soften the Pivot

"...however,"
"...but on the other hand,"
"...at the same time,"

3. State Your Opinion

"...I'm not so sure about that."
"...it might be better to..."
"...don't you think that...?"

Stage 4 • Storytelling & Narrative

🎵 Discuss an Unexpected Meeting

Have you ever bumped into someone in an unusual place? Practice telling anecdotal stories using Past Continuous (background) and Past Simple (unexpected event).

Grammar Formula: "I was [doing action in past continuous] when suddenly I [bumped into / ran into someone in past simple]!"

Model Anecdote Example:

"Last year, I was waiting at a busy airport when I suddenly ran into my old primary school teacher. I hadn't seen her in ten years! Surprisingly, we realized we were sitting next to each other on the same flight to London."

B1 Key Vocabulary & Functional Expressions Toolkit

Polite Disagreement Phrases:
  • I see what you mean, but...
  • That's a valid point, however...
  • I understand your view, but on the other hand...
  • I'm not so sure about that because...
Phrasal Verbs for Encounters:
  • Bump into / Run into: Meet by chance.
  • Hit it off: Become friends instantly.
  • Keep in touch: Maintain contact over time.
  • Catch up with: Talk after not seeing each other.
