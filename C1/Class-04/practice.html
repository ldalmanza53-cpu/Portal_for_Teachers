Grammar Bank: Gerund and Infinitive Constructions

Master which verbs require a Gerund (-ing), an Infinitive (to + verb), or either form!

Followed by Gerund

Verb + -ing

Verbs like appreciate, consider, enjoy, give up, keep, put off, suggest.

"They're considering starting a team!"

Followed by Infinitive

to + verb

Verbs like ask, decide, expect, intend, need, refuse, seem, tend.

"I never expected to meet nice people!"

Dual (Either Form)

Either OK

Verbs like begin, bother, continue, hate, prefer, start.

"I started taking / to take a cooking class."

Passive Voice

being / to be

Pattern: subject + verb + being / to be + past participle.

"I kept being invited to yoga class."

Monday, March 18

I've never been a really popular guy. I'm the type of person who tends one or two good friends rather than lots of acquaintances. Well, when I moved away from my hometown after getting a job in Denver, I really needed some new friends. Because I'm shy, I considered my personality to become more outgoing. But that was very difficult for me. Just the same, I didn't give up . I decided a new image for myself. I bought myself some new shoes, new clothes, and I even got a new haircut and started contact lenses. However, it didn't seem anything. I was beginning to regret my hometown when, all of a sudden, I got a call from my old friend Jim. He was planning here pretty soon. He wanted some advice about finding an apartment in the city, and I suggested an apartment with me. Well, he agreed! I really enjoy someone to spend time with, and together we've made a lot of new friends.
