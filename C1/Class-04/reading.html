1. LESSON PLAN: A1 - A2 (CULTURAL DAY: PASSPORT TO FESTIVALS & GLOBAL TRADITIONS)

Communicative Objective: Describe basic global festivals, traditional foods, clothing, and cultural celebrations using present simple tense, basic descriptive adjectives, and simple comparative phrases ("In my country, we wear... while in India they wear...") during interactive passport fair exchanges.

2. LESSON PLAN: B1 - C1 (CULTURAL DAY: CROSS-CULTURAL DIPLOMACY & GLOBAL HERITAGE FORUM)

Communicative Objective: Analyze intangible cultural heritage preservation, debate the ethics of cultural repatriation, negotiate cross-cultural business etiquette protocols, and deliver persuasive diplomatic presentations using advanced register and conditional structures ("Had we acted earlier...").
