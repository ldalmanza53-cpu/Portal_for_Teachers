Language & Register Engine

Casual Texting vs. Standard Rules & Modals of Propriety

Compare casual texting shorthand with formal rules and express recommendations!

1. Casual Texting Shorthand
Abbreviations, lower-case, missing commas
  • • "u comin to d mtg brb"
  • • "idk tbh sounds good"
  • • Dropped periods and question marks
2. Standard English Rules
Subject + Verb + Full Punctuation
  • • "Are you coming to the meeting?"
  • • "I do not know, to be honest."
  • • Capital letters & correct spelling
3. Modals of Propriety / Rules
SHOULD / OUGHT TO / MUST
  • • One should double-check autocorrect.
  • • Senders ought to avoid all caps.
  • • Employees must refrain from late texts.
4. Punctuation Tone Nuance
Period = Cold/Serious; Emoji = Friendly
  • • "Fine." (Can sound passive-aggressive)
  • • "Fine! 😊" (Warm & friendly)
  • • "NO WAY" (Interpreted as shouting)
