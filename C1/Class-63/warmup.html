Compound Adjective Structural Matrix

Upgrade basic A2 descriptions into rich, hyphenated B1+ compound adjective structures to convey impact with conciseness and style.

1

Noun/Adj + Present Participle

[Noun/Adj] + "-" + [Verb-ing]

Expresses an active effect or ongoing action that influences people or society.

"Dr. Sabin developed a life-saving vaccine that eradicated polio across continents."

2

Adverb/Noun + Past Participle

[Adv/Noun] + "-" + [Verb-ed/en]

Describes reputation, global recognition, or deeply rooted personal motivation.

"Marie Curie became a world-renowned scientist respected by peers globally."

3

Adjective + Noun + -ed

[Adjective] + "-" + [Noun-ed]

Formed by adding '-ed' to a noun to describe personal attributes, heart, or mind.

"Mandela was a strong-willed leader who remained steadfast under pressure."

💡
Essential B1 Hyphenation Rule:

Always use a hyphen when the compound adjective appears BEFORE the noun it modifies (e.g., "a world-renowned scientist"). If it appears AFTER a verb, the hyphen is usually omitted (e.g., "the scientist is world renowned").

🚀A2 Simplistic Descriptions vs B1+ Compound Upgrades

Observe how simple A2 facts upgrade into polished, cohesive B1+ profile descriptions using compound adjectives.

A2 SIMPLISTIC FORM Repetitive Clauses

"Nelson Mandela was famous everywhere. He had very strong determination."

CEFR B1+ COMPOUND UPGRADE Past Participle + Adj+Noun-ed

"Nelson Mandela was a world-renowned leader whose strong-willed resistance transformed a nation."

A2 SIMPLISTIC FORM Repetitive Clauses

"Marie Curie did research that changed lives and made people think deeply."

CEFR B1+ COMPOUND UPGRADE Present Participle Compounds

"Marie Curie conducted life-changing research and produced thought-provoking scientific breakthroughs."

A2 SIMPLISTIC FORM Repetitive Clauses

"Malala is very smart and speaks softly, but she has a big heart."

CEFR B1+ COMPOUND UPGRADE Character Compound Profile

"Malala is a quick-witted and warm-hearted activist who remains soft-spoken yet immensely powerful."

A2 SIMPLISTIC FORM Repetitive Clauses

"Steve Jobs thought about the future and broke sales records with phones."

CEFR B1+ COMPOUND UPGRADE Compound Adjectives + Nouns

"Steve Jobs was a forward-thinking innovator who introduced record-breaking consumer technologies."
