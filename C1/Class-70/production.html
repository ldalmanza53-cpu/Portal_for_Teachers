Work Values & Preferences Language Matrix

Expressing Preferences, Comparatives & Ideal Job Visions

Master intermediate structures to express work values and contrast career choices!

1. Expressing Preferences & Values
Would Rather + Base Verb... Than:

"I would rather work remotely than commute to an office every day."

Prefer [Gerund] To [Gerund]:

"I prefer leading independent projects to managing large bureaucratic teams."

2. Comparatives & Contrasts
Far More Rewarding Than / Whereas:

"A supportive team culture is far more rewarding than a corporate bonus."

As Opposed To / Compared To:

"I value creative flexibility as opposed to strict managerial control."

3. Ideal Role Conditionals & Vision
In My Ideal Position, I Would...:

"In my ideal position, I would flourish if given autonomy over my schedule."

My Dream Job Would Entail...:

"My dream job would entail collaborating with global, purpose-driven leaders."
