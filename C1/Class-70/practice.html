Unit 10 • T-101

Adverb Clauses of Condition

Conditional sentences do not necessarily use 'if'. Use these specialized connectors to specify distinct conditional relationships.

Provided / On the condition (that)

Introduces a strict condition on which another situation depends.

I would take almost any job provided that there were opportunities to learn.
Whether or not

Introduces a condition that does not influence or change the result.

I would quit a dishonest job, whether or not it were high-paying.
Assuming (that)

Introduces an assumption or prerequisite upon which another condition relies.

I wouldn't mind an office, assuming that I had creative freedom.
Supposing (that)

Introduces an imagined or hypothetical situation that could influence a choice.

Supposing I had the choice, I'd prefer to work with a group.
