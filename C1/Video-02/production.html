Fashion Language & Passive Voice Matrix

Describing Dress Codes & Contextual Trends

Master intermediate B1 structures to describe clothing functions, passive trends, and comparative contexts!

1. Style & Function Vocabulary
Tailored / Formal:

"Tailored garments create a structured and authoritative silhouette."

Customary / Traditional:

"Customary robes are handcrafted using heritage embroidery techniques."

2. Passive Voice for Customs
Is / Are + Past Participle:

"Formal dress codes are strictly enforced during court proceedings."

Was / Were Designed to:

"Heavy wool cloaks were originally designed to protect against arctic winds."

3. Contrasting Context Frames
While [Context A]..., [Context B]...:

"While corporate wear signals professionalism, streetwear prioritizes individuality."

In contrast to fast fashion...:

"In contrast to disposable fast fashion, vintage clothing promotes circular sustainability."
