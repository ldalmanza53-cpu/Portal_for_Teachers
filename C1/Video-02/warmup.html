📐 B1+ Target Grammar Matrix & Formula Blueprint

Master the three syntactic pillars required to describe fashion roles fluently in English.

Pillar 1 • Cultural Norms

Passive Voice Structures

[Subject] + [is/are/was] + [past participle] + [to verb]
  • is worn to show: "A suit is worn to show authority."
  • are expected to dress: "Lawyers are expected to dress formally."
  • was designed for: "Streetwear was designed for freedom."
Pillar 2 • Purpose & Function

Function Connectors

[Clothing] + [serves as / acts as] + [a symbol / means of]
  • serves as: "Clothing serves as non-verbal communication."
  • acts as a symbol of: "The robe acts as a symbol of unity."
  • in order to reflect: "...in order to reflect modesty."
Pillar 3 • Context Contrast

Context Contrast Markers

[While / Whereas] in Context A, Context B requires...
  • while in formal settings: "While suits rule finance, tech adopts casual."
  • in contrast to: "In contrast to fast fashion, eco-style lasts."
  • whereas traditional attire: "Modern wear is simple whereas traditional attire conveys history."
