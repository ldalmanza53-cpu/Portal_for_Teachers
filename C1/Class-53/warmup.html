Method & Benefit Grammar Matrix

To speak at CEFR B1 level, move beyond simple sentences. Use connectors like "by + gerund" to describe methods and "allows us to..." to explain health benefits.

1

Describing Methods

By + [Verb-ing] ...
Through + [Noun / Gerund] ...

Shows how an action is performed to achieve a natural connection.

"By planting local seeds, we support bees."

2

Expressing Health Benefits

allows / enables us to + [Verb]
fosters a sense of + [Noun]

Explains physical, mental, or emotional improvements clearly.

"Forest walks allow us to reduce stress."

3

Personal Recommendations

I strongly advise [Verb-ing] ...
It is essential to incorporate ...

Provides persuasive advice on outdoor lifestyle changes.

"I strongly advise spending time outside daily."

🚀A2 Basic vs B1+ Upgraded Structures

Compare basic simple sentences with fluent B1 structures using our method & benefit matrix.

BASIC (CEFR A2) Simple phrasing

"I walk in the park. I feel good and less stressed."

UPGRADED (CEFR B1) Method + Benefit

"By taking regular walks in the park, we can unwind and effectively lower stress levels."

BASIC (CEFR A2) Simple phrasing

"Gardening is nice. It helps people see plants grow."

UPGRADED (CEFR B1) Method + Benefit

"Through urban micro-gardening, city residents foster a deeper bond with plant lifecycles."

BASIC (CEFR A2) Simple phrasing

"I put my smartphone away outside. My brain rests."

UPGRADED (CEFR B1) Method + Benefit

"By turning off smartphones during hikes, outdoor lovers restore mental clarity and focus."

BASIC (CEFR A2) Simple phrasing

"You should go camping because nature is healthy."

UPGRADED (CEFR B1) Method + Benefit

"Engaging in wilderness trekking enables us to build physical endurance while connecting with nature."
