Language Framework

Nature Habit Structures & Benefit Connectors

Master how to describe your outdoor routines and explain the health benefits of nature!

1

Gerunds & Habit Structures

Describing Outdoor Activities
🌿 Gerund as Subject:

"Walking in the forest reduces mental fatigue after a long workday."

⚡ Make a point of / Frequency:

"I make a point of spending thirty minutes outdoors every single morning."

🌊 Tend to engage in:

"On weekends, my family tends to go hiking in nearby state parks."

2

Benefit Connectors & Expressions

Explaining Why Nature Helps
💡 Allows us to / Enables us to:

"Being outdoors allows us to disconnect from digital screens and stress."

🗣️ Helps us relax by / Promotes:

"Forest bathing helps us relax by engaging all five senses in natural greenery."

🌟 Leads to / Contributes to:

"Regular contact with nature contributes to better sleep and reduced anxiety."
