ROLE B

The Cautious Traditionalist

Primary Stance: Prioritizes mental wellbeing, financial safety, proven traditions, human connection, and caution against fast trends.

🎯 Target Upgraded Phrases:

"I hold serious reservations about...", "It is a misconception that...", "I contend that this trend poses unseen risks..."

ROLE C

The Pragmatic Mediator

Primary Stance: Balances advantages against risks, seeks compromise, and formulates pragmatic middle-ground solutions.

🎯 Target Upgraded Phrases:

"While I recognize the merits of X, I must emphasize...", "Taking everything into consideration, a balanced approach..."

Step 2 — Complement & Upgrade Engine

Basic B1 ➡️ Upgraded B1+ Phrase Transformer

Compare basic B1 statements with precise, nuanced B1+ complements!

1. Expressing Strong Belief UPGRADE
BASIC B1:

"I think remote work is much better for everyone."

UPGRADED B1+:

"I am firmly convinced that remote work significantly enhances schedule flexibility."

2. Personal Perspective Stance UPGRADE
BASIC B1:

"In my opinion, living in a small flat is hard."

UPGRADED B1+:

"From my perspective, micro-living requires spatial discipline and mental adjustments."

3. Expressing Polite Doubt / Caution UPGRADE
BASIC B1:

"I don't agree with deleting all social media accounts."

UPGRADED B1+:

"I hold serious reservations about total digital disconnection, as it may cause isolation."

4. Conceding & Balancing UPGRADE
BASIC B1:

"I agree vegan food is healthy, but it is expensive."

UPGRADED B1+:

"While I recognize the health advantages of plant-based diets, I contend that costs remain high."
