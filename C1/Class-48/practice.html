Subjunctive Rule Breakdown & Reference
B1 Grammar Guide
Common Subjunctive Verbs (Followed by 'that' + base verb):
advise beg require stipulate ask prefer specify vote
1. Active Subjunctive

Subject + Subjunctive Verb + that + Subject + Base Verb

He advised that his students be on time.
Store policy stipulates that associates report by 8:30.
2. Negative Subjunctive

Formed with not + Base Verb (No 'don't' / 'doesn't')

The contract required that he not receive a bonus that year.
I prefer that you not call so late.
3. Passive Subjunctive

Formed with be + Past Participle

Sponsors asked that their product be featured in the movie.
They preferred that it not be endorsed.
⚠️ Key Distinction (Subjunctive vs Indicative): Facts or general statements use indicative tenses (e.g. It's clear that sales have increased or I learned that she was a marketer). Urging/desires/rules trigger the subjunctive (e.g. She advised that pressure be applied).

Shopping Terms Glossary

Impulse buy: An unplanned purchase made on a sudden whim.
Buyer's remorse: Regret or guilt felt after making a purchase.
Shopping spree: A short period of intense or excessive shopping.
Credit limit: The maximum amount of money a bank lets you borrow on a credit card.

Discussion Questions

Q1. What are some other characteristics of a compulsive shopper?
💡 Ideas: Hiding receipts, feeling an emotional high while buying, buying multiples of the same item, buying things they can't afford.
Q2. What other problems do compulsive shoppers face?
💡 Ideas: Severe financial debt, arguments with family/spouse, lack of storage space, emotional stress.
Q3. What would you do to help a compulsive shopper?
💡 Ideas: Recommend counseling, suggest that they destroy credit cards, propose that a family member manage their finances.
