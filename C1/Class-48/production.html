Subjunctive Opinion Engine
Subjunctive Structure for Marketing Opinions:
It is [Essential / Crucial / Vital] + that + [Subject] + [BASE VERB] I [Recommend / Suggest / Insist] + that + [Subject] + [BASE VERB]

*Note: The verb following 'that' stays in its base form regardless of person (e.g., that a company be honest, not is; that he consider, not considers).
