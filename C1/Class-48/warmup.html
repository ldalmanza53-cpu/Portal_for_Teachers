The Subjunctive & Opinion Matrix

Master the mechanics of the English Subjunctive Mood! Used after adjectives of urgency or verbs of demand, the subjunctive always takes the bare base form of the verb (e.g., be, disclose, not track, audit) regardless of the subject pronoun.

1

Impersonal Expressions of Urgency

IT IS [essential / imperative / crucial / vital] + THAT + [Subject] + [BASE VERB]

Notice that the verb does not take '-s' or past tense '-ed', and uses 'be' instead of 'is/are'.

✔️ "It is essential that a company be transparent."

2

Verbs of Proposal & Mandate

[Subject] + [propose / recommend / insist / demand] + THAT + [Subject] + [BASE VERB]

Express strong policy recommendations or ethical mandates directly to organizations.

✔️ "I propose that an influencer disclose all paid gifts."

3

Negative Subjunctive Construction

IT IS [imperative / vital] + THAT + [Subject] + NOT + [BASE VERB]

In the negative subjunctive, simply insert 'NOT' directly before the base verb (do NOT use 'does not' or 'do not').

✔️ "It is imperative that targeted ads not exploit children."
