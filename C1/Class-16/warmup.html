Stage 2 • Narrative Tenses Focus

Combining Tenses for Tech Stories

Good tech trouble stories use three past tenses together:

1. Past Continuous (Background)

Sets the scene for what you were doing when the problem occurred.

"I was writing my final assignment..."

2. Past Simple (Main Event)

Describes the sudden tech interruption or main action that happened.

"...when my laptop suddenly crashed!"

3. Past Perfect (Prior Action)

Refers to something completed or forgotten before the crash.

"...because I hadn't saved my changes."
