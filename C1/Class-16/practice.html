Stan, 36

Remote Work Password Panic

"Working from home one Friday, I opened my company network log-in page. Unfortunately, I'd forgotten the password my boss had given me, and he had taken the day off. Being too embarrassed to call him at home, I had to go to work after all to access the network."

"Working from home..."

Function: Background action happening at the same time as opening the log-in page (Simultaneous action).

"Being too embarrassed..."

Function: Reason / Explanation ("Because I was too embarrassed to call him...").

Peter, 18

Dropped Hiking Calls

"I had dinner plans with my parents, but I was still out hiking when I was supposed to meet them at the restaurant. I kept trying to call them, but the calls were dropped before I could even say hello. Having gotten several calls from me, my parents became really worried. They were relieved when I finally got through an hour later."

"Having gotten several calls..."

Function: Action happening before another action (Sequence: First they got calls, then they became worried).

Tech Problem: Signal 1 -ing clause (Having)
👩‍💼

Vera, 24

Laptop Presentation Crash

"I had a great presentation ready for the business class I'm taking. Being a perfectionist, I had prepared it in detail on my laptop. The next day in class, my laptop crashed opening the file. Trying to stay calm, I gave the presentation as best I could from memory."

"Being a perfectionist..."

Function: Reason / Explanation ("Because I am a perfectionist...").

"...opening the file"

Function: Action during another action ("while it was opening the file").

"Trying to stay calm..."

Function: Action at the same time as giving the presentation.

Class 16 • Page 31 Grammar Box

Grammar: -ing clauses

To express two actions performed by the same person or thing in a single sentence, we can include an -ing clause containing an -ing participle.

1

Simultaneous Actions

Same time or during another action

The two actions happen at the same time or one action happens during another action.

• The next day in class, my laptop crashed opening the file.

(crashed while/when it was opening the file)

• Trying to stay calm, I gave the presentation from memory.

• She is at her desk typing a paper.

2

Sequence of Actions

Having + Past Participle

When one action happens before another action, use having + past participle.

• Having gotten several calls from me, my parents became really worried.

(My parents got several calls from me, and THEN they became really worried.)

3

Reasons & Explanations

Being / -ing for "Because"

Reasons and explanations for actions can also be expressed with -ing / being clauses.

• Being too embarrassed to call him at home, I had to go to work after all.

(Because I was too embarrassed to call him at home, I had to go to work.)
