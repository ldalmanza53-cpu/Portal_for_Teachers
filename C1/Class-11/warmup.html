Stage 2 • 📖 Reading & Practical Guide

How to Overcome a Bad First Impression

First impressions happen in under 7 seconds! If you were late, dressed inappropriately, or seemed distracted, don't panic. Read these 4 proven steps to repair your image:

1

Acknowledge & Apologize Gracefully

Don't make endless excuses. Own up quickly with sincerity: "I apologize for being late today; I appreciate your patience." Keep it concise and professional.

2

Demonstrate Consistency Over Time

Words are cheap; actions change minds. If you appeared disorganized, be hyper-prepared for every following meeting to prove it was an isolated incident.

3

Adjust Body Language & Listening

Maintain warm eye contact, smile genuinely, and practice active listening. Nodding and asking follow-up questions signals respect and interest.

4

Shift Focus to the Other Person

People remember how you make them feel. Ask about their work or passions: "I'd love to hear your thoughts on this project."
