Context & Action Roles

Image Lab Studio

"The Image & Reputation Consulting Lab"

You are image and communication consultants advising clients on personal branding. Your objective is to express polite, constructive opinions on clothing and appearance, identify why people misjudge initial impressions, and devise actionable recovery plans to turn around negative first encounters!

Select active role to view actionable checklist:

Role A: Style & Perception Consultant

Evaluates clothing, dress codes, body language, and initial impression risks.

  • "In my opinion, dressing smart-casual makes you look approachable."
  • "At first glance, a sloppy outfit might cause clients to misjudge you."
  • "Pay attention to body language and posture during introductions."

Role B: Reputation Recovery Strategist 📖

Formulates concrete steps to overcome awkward introductions and make amends.

  • "To turn around a bad first impression, remain sincere and apologetic."
  • "The best way to make amends is through consistent reliability."
  • "Send a polite follow-up note to rebuild your professional reputation."

Role C: Communication & Language Auditor

Monitors polite feedback phrases, checks target B1 vocabulary, and enforces session timing.

  • "Great use of 'make amends' to express a recovery step!"
  • "Notice how adding 'In my view' made your style opinion polite."
  • "Phase time reached! Let's present the recovery strategy."
