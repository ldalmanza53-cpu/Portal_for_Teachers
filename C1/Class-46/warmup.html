B1 vs. B1+ Expression Matrix & Formula Guides

Elevate your conversational fluency! Moving from basic phrases to B1+ nuanced expressions makes your marketing critiques sound professional, persuasive, and polite.

1

Polite & Softened Disagreement

[Softener] + [Acknowledge Point] + BUT + [Counter-argument]

Never say "You are wrong!" in business meetings. Soften your critique first to maintain rapport.

✔️ "I see your point, but pop-up ads can irritate customers."

2

Strong & Idiomatic Agreement

[Strong Intensifier] + [Target Marketing Aspect]

Replace basic "I agree" with rich phrases that emphasize shared vision and enthusiasm.

✔️ "I couldn't agree more with your strategy on eco-branding."

3

Constructive Advice Structures

If I were in your shoes, I'd + [Base Verb]

Use hypothetical second conditional or softened questions to propose campaign changes.

✔️ "If I were in your position, I'd focus on short video ads."
