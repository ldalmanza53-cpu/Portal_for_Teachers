Grammar & Expressions Matrix

Agreeing, Disagreeing & Giving/Asking for Advice

Master these functional B1 expressions to lead professional marketing discussions!

1

Expressing Agreement & Disagreement

Stance Expressions
✔ Strong Agreement:

"I completely agree that..." / "That's a very valid point." / "I couldn't agree more with that approach."

⚡ Partial Agreement:

"I see your point, but..." / "I agree up to a point, however..." / "That makes sense, although..."

✖ Polite Disagreement:

"I'm afraid I disagree because..." / "I'm not entirely convinced that..." / "I take a different view on that."

2

Asking For & Giving Advice

Consultant Recommendations
❓ Asking for Advice:

"What would you advise this brand to do?" / "How do you suggest they handle...?" / "What's your best advice for...?"

💡 Direct Advice:

"If I were advising them, I would..." / "My top recommendation is to..." / "They ought to focus on..."

🌱 Polite / Indirect Suggestion:

"It might be wise to..." / "Have they considered...?" / "I'd strongly suggest testing..."
