Verbs and Expressions in the Subjunctive

Subjunctive Trigger Verbs

demand insist propose recommend request suggest

"Some ads seem to demand that the customer buy the product."

It is [Adjective] that... Expressions

it is crucial it is essential it is imperative it is important it is vital it is critical

"I believe it is essential that an ad be clever and witty."

Golden Rule of Subjunctive Verbs

The subjunctive clause ALWAYS uses the BASE FORM of the verb for all subjects (I, you, he, she, it, we, they):

  • Drop the 3rd person singular '-s' (e.g., the customer buys $\rightarrow$ the customer buy)
  • Use 'be' instead of 'is/are/am' (e.g., advertisements are truthful $\rightarrow$ advertisements be truthful)
  • Use 'not + base verb' for negative subjunctive (e.g., I suggest that he not buy fake items)
