Worker Qualities & Grammar Blueprint Matrix

Transform basic workplace descriptions into sophisticated B1+ professional statements. Learn relative clauses of attribution, modal verbs of necessity, and comparative stance connectors.

Blueprint A: Relative Clauses of Attribution
• A standout worker is someone WHO + [Verb]...
• An employee WHOSE [Skill] + is strong...
• A quality THAT + differentiates leaders...

*Example:* "A reliable worker is someone who takes full accountability for their results."

Blueprint B: Modals of Necessity & Capability
• Subject + MUST / OUGHT TO + [Base Verb]...
• Subject + IS EXPECTED TO + [Base Verb]...
• Subject + CAN EFFECTIVELY + [Base Verb]...

*Example:* "Every team member ought to cultivate emotional intelligence to resolve workplace conflicts."

Blueprint C: Evaluative Stance Connectors
• FAR MORE vital / crucial THAN...
• WHAT SETS a great worker APART IS...
• NOT ONLY [Quality] BUT ALSO [Quality]...

*Example:* "What sets a standout worker apart is not only technical skill but also adaptability under pressure."
