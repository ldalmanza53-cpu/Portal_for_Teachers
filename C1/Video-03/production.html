Tech Grammar & Innovation Matrix

Describing Technological Mechanisms & Recent Breakthroughs

Master B1 grammar structures to explain how technologies function and how recent advances impact society!

1. Passive Voice for Tech Functions
Is / Are + Past Participle:

"Data is processed by neural networks in milliseconds."

Are programmed / Designed to:

"Autonomous drones are programmed to deliver medical packages."

2. Present Perfect for Breakthroughs
Has / Have + Past Participle:

"Generative AI has transformed content creation globally."

Have emerged / Reduced:

"Reusable rockets have reduced orbital launch costs by 70%."

3. Purpose & Societal Impact
In order to / So as to:

"Grid batteries are deployed in order to stabilize renewable power."

With the goal of:

"Biotech labs innovate with the goal of curing genetic disorders."
