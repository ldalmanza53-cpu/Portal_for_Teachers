Formula A Recent Trends

Present Perfect in Tech

Subject + [has / have] + Past Participle + [Time frame / Context]

Used to describe recent developments with present relevance.

"Engineers have created microgrids to stabilize renewable energy distribution."
Formula B Technical Process

Passive Voice in Tech

Tech System + [is / are / has been] + Past Participle + [by / for...]

Focuses on the technology or action rather than the agent.

"Autonomous drones are operated by central artificial intelligence algorithms."
Formula C Societal Impact

Cause & Effect Connectors

[Advancement] + [Connector] + [Societal / Economic Result]

Links technological capabilities directly to real-world outcomes.

"High-speed 5G networks expand bandwidth, enabling users to stream medical data in real time."
