Interview Conduct Language Matrix

Modals of Advice, Gerund Rules & Professional Framing

Master key B1 grammar structures to articulate job interview guidelines and professional standards!

1. Modals of Advice & Obligation
Advice & Best Practice (should / ought to):

"Candidates should research the firm."

"Applicants ought to prepare questions."

Strict Rules & Obligation (must / required to):

"Applicants must arrive 10 minutes early."

2. Gerunds After Avoid & Refrain
Avoid / Refrain from + Verb-ING:

"Avoid criticizing former employers."

"Refrain from checking your phone."

Negative Conduct Framing:

"Candidates must avoid using filler words..."

3. Professional Response Framing
Positive Framing & Weakness Nuance:

"To address a weakness, one can frame it as..."

STAR Method Structure:

"Detail the Situation, Task, Action, and Result..."
