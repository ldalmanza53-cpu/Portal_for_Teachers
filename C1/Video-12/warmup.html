Grammar & Syntax Blueprints

B1 Interview Language Matrix

Master Modals of Advice/Obligation, First & Second Conditionals for job scenarios, and Formal Action Verbs to articulate your skills convincingly.

1 Modals of Advice & Obligation
Subject + [should / must / ought to / had better / are expected to] + Verb
  • Should / Shouldn't: "Candidates should research company culture."
  • Had better: "You had better arrive 15 minutes early."
  • Are expected to: "Applicants are expected to maintain eye contact."
2 Conditionals in Interviews
1st: If + Present Simple, Will + Base Verb
2nd: If + Past Simple, Would + Base Verb
  • 1st Conditional: "If they ask about past mistakes, I will explain my learning process."
  • 2nd Conditional: "If I were hired, I would focus on streamlining workflow."
3 Action Verbs & Power Collocations
Action Verb + Key Outcome / Deliverable
  • Spearhead: "I spearheaded a project that reduced operational costs."
  • Demonstrate proficiency: "I demonstrate proficiency in data analysis."
