Polite Self-Correction Formula

[Original Statement] + "or rather," + [Corrected B1 Term] + "in order to ensure accuracy."

Smoothly fixes mistakes in live speech or writing without losing momentum.

"We discussed about the problem—or rather, we discussed the problem in order to ensure accuracy."

2

Contrastive Replacement Formula

[Incorrect Form] + "is often confused with" + [Correct Form] + "and should be replaced by it."

Explains grammatical distinctions clearly and provides standard alternatives.

"'Their' is often confused with 'there' and should be replaced by it in location contexts."

3

Precision Stance Formula

"From a grammatical standpoint, it is worth noting that" + [Precise Linguistic Rule].

Establishes a thoughtful, authoritative academic or professional tone.

"From a grammatical standpoint, it is worth noting that state verbs rarely take continuous forms."

🚀A2 Simplistic Sentences vs B1+ Precision Upgrades

Examine how common errors and basic expressions are upgraded into accurate, refined B1 phrasing.

A2 SIMPLISTIC / ERRONEOUS Direct Flaw

"I am agreeing with you because your point is good."

CEFR B1+ PRECISION UPGRADE Stative Verb Precision

"From a grammatical standpoint, I agree with your statement rather than using the continuous form."

A2 SIMPLISTIC / ERRONEOUS Direct Flaw

"People thinks that grammar is not important today."

CEFR B1+ PRECISION UPGRADE Contrastive Correction

"Contrary to popular belief, singular verb forms after 'people' should be replaced by plural agreement."

A2 SIMPLISTIC / ERRONEOUS Direct Flaw

"He explained me the rule yesterday in class."

CEFR B1+ PRECISION UPGRADE Prepositional Refinement

"He explained the rule to me—precisely speaking, 'explain' requires the preposition 'to' in order to ensure accuracy."

A2 SIMPLISTIC / ERRONEOUS Direct Flaw

"It is very bad when you make spelling mistakes."

CEFR B1+ PRECISION UPGRADE Nuanced Observation

"It is worth noting that minor typographical errors are often confused with structural grammar flaws."
