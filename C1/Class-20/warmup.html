Stage 1 • Writing Skill Focus

✒️ How to Restate a Thesis Statement

In essay writing, your conclusion must restate your main thesis in the introduction. However, you must not repeat the exact same words. You should rephrase your thesis using fresh vocabulary and structure!

1. Use Synonyms (Word Swap)

Replace key words with equivalent B1 terms.

• brings bad luck $\rightarrow$ invites misfortune

• cultural customs $\rightarrow$ traditional beliefs

2. Change Sentence Structure

Flip the order of clauses or reasons.

• Original: Because X happens, Y occurs.

• Restated: Y occurs as a consequence of X.

3. Shift Voice or Grammar

Switch between Active and Passive voice or verb forms.

• Active: Many cultures practice superstitions...

• Passive: Superstitions are practiced across cultures...

Stage 2 • Quality Evaluation

Is it a Good or Bad Thesis Restatement?

Read the Original Thesis about a cultural superstition. Then evaluate the proposed Conclusion Sentence. Is it a GOOD restatement (uses new words/structure) or a BAD restatement (repeats word-for-word or changes the meaning)?

Item 1 of 4 Original Thesis:

"In Spanish culture, eating twelve grapes at midnight on New Year's Eve is believed to bring good fortune for every month of the coming year."

Proposed Conclusion Restatement:

"Ultimately, consuming twelve grapes during the midnight chimes on New Year's Eve is thought to invite prosperity throughout all twelve upcoming months in Spain."
