High-Density Consonant Cluster Mechanics

Eliminate Schwa Insertion (/sətə/ → /str/)
/str/ Street, Strategy

Alveolar /s/ transitions to unvoiced stop /t/ with retroflex /r/ co-articulation.

⚠️ Avoid schwa: /sətəreɪ/ ❌
/spl/ Splash, Splendid

Unvoiced /s/ opens to bilabial plosive /p/ popping into lateral /l/.

⚠️ Avoid dropping /p/: /slæʃ/ ❌
/ðs/ Months, Clothes

Interdental /ð/ retracts rapidly behind incisors to unvoiced /s/.

⚠️ Avoid dropping /ð/: /mʌns/ ❌
