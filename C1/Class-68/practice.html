Writing • Section 5A (T-99)

Anatomy of a Formal Letter

Learn structural components and formal language conventions for official correspondence.

Key Formal Writing Principles

  • No Irrelevant Details: Keep information strictly focused on the official purpose.
  • Avoid Contractions: Use full forms (e.g., "I am writing" instead of "I'm writing").
  • Formal Punctuation: Place a colon (:) after the formal greeting (e.g., Dear Mr. Hayes:).

Part 4

I am writing to request more information concerning your study abroad programs. Your programs sound extremely interesting, and I hope to participate in one of them next year. Your Study Abroad in Paris program sounds particularly fascinating.

I would like to sign up for the Paris program beginning in June. I'm still trying to decide whether to choose the homestay option or the dormitory option. Would it be possible to send me further information about those two choices in order to help me make a decision?

I realize that all the spaces in your Paris program may already be filled. In that case, my second choice would be the Study Abroad in Toulouse program. My third choice would be your Study Abroad in Strasbourg program.

Thank you very much for your help. I look forward to receiving the information.
