Work & Letter Grammar Matrix

Transform basic A2 messages into polished B1+ formal business correspondence. Study structural blueprints and reconstruct scrambled formal letters.

Blueprint A: Salutation & Purpose
[Dear Mr./Ms. Surname / Dear Hiring Manager,] + I am writing to [inquire about / apply for / express interest in] + [Topic/Position]

Establishes immediate professional tone and clearly states your purpose.

Blueprint B: Polite Indirect Request
I would be grateful if you could + [Base Verb] + regarding / with reference to + [Topic]

Replaces direct demands ("Tell me about...") with respectful professional indirect phrasing.

Blueprint C: Experience & Connector
With reference to my background in [Field], + [Key Skill]. + Furthermore, + [Additional Value]

Connects work experience and qualifications using formal transition linkers.

A2 Informal vs. B1+ Formal Professional Transformations

A2 Basic Email B1+ Formal Letter
"Hi, I want to know about the job working hours and salary."
"Dear Hiring Manager, I am writing to inquire about the flexible working options and salary structure regarding this position."
A2 Basic Email B1+ Formal Letter
"Please send me information about training soon."
"I would be grateful if you could provide details concerning opportunities for professional development."
A2 Basic Email B1+ Formal Letter
"I worked before in a team and did many tasks."
"With reference to my qualifications, my core responsibilities included fostering a collaborative environment."
A2 Basic Email B1+ Formal Letter
"Bye, write back soon!"
"I look forward to hearing from you. Yours sincerely, Alex Mercer."
