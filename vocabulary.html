Infinitive Phrases Dummy 'It' Subject

It is considered unbecoming to criticize colleagues publicly.

Uses dummy "It" + be + adjective + to-infinitive to evaluate behavior objectively without naming a personal pronoun subject.

Gerund Phrases Action as Subject

Minding one's P's and Q's demonstrates refined social tact.

The gerund phrase (V-ing + object) acts as a singular noun phrase subject taking a singular verb (e.g., demonstrates, is, requires).

Unbecoming

Definition (B2/C1)

Not fitting or appropriate for a person's status or reputation; improper or unseemly.

Contextual Example & Syntax

It is deemed unbecoming of a team leader to make patronizing remarks during public debriefs.

⚡ Syntax Tip: Commonly paired with dummy 'It' ("It is unbecoming of [someone] to [verb]").
