Narrative Tenses & Passive Voice Masterclass

Essential B1 structure rules for telling accidental history and personal turning points.

1

Historical Passive Voice

Focuses on the invention/discovery object rather than who made it.

Formula: Subject + was / were + Past Participle (V3)
  • Accidental Discovery: Penicillin was discovered by Alexander Fleming in 1928.
  • Unintended Product: Post-it Notes were created when a weak adhesive was made.
  • Sudden Shatter: Safety glass was invented after a flask was dropped.
2

Narrative Tense Sequence

Combines background actions, prior events, and sudden interruptions.

Background: Past Continuous (was/were + -ing)
Interruption: Past Simple (suddenly / when)
Prior Cause: Past Perfect (had + V3)
  • Interrupted Activity: Percy Spencer was testing a radar tube when the candy bar in his pocket melted.
  • Prior Condition: Fleming realized mold had grown because he had left the window open.
3

Third Conditional Reflection

Speculates on how history or personal lives would be different without the accident.

Formula: If + had (not) V3, ... would / could have V3
  • Historical Impact: If Fleming hadn't left his petri dishes open, antibiotics wouldn't have been discovered then.
  • Personal Turning Point: If I hadn't taken a wrong turn, I would never have found my dream studio.
