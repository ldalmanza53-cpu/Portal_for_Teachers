B1 Diverse Tech Perspectives Language Guide

1. Expressing Technological Innovations & Benefits

Use clear B1 verbs and noun phrases to highlight advances:

  • [Technology] offers rapid improvements in efficiency by...
  • Proponents argue that [innovation] significantly increases accessibility...
  • This breakthrough enables specialists to diagnose conditions faster...

2. Highlighting Ethical Risks & Drawbacks

Express caution politely without exaggerating:

  • We must also consider the potential privacy risks regarding user data...
  • Ethical auditors warn about algorithmic bias and discrimination...
  • There are growing concerns about job displacement and lack of human oversight...

3. Concession Connectors & Global Perspective Invites

Balance both viewpoints and actively invite global community inputs:

  • While [technology] brings societal progress, we must also address privacy risks...
  • On one hand, it boosts productivity, but on the other hand, regulation is required...
  • How is this technology viewed in your local community?
  • We'd love to hear how your region approaches automated systems!
