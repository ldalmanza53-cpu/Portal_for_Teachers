B1 Recommendation Formulas

Target Structures & Verb Patterns

1. I recommend / suggest + GERUND (-ing): "I recommend visiting local food markets early in the morning."
2. It's best to / Make sure to + BASE VERB: "It's best to rent a bicycle to explore quiet neighborhoods."
3. How about + GERUND (-ing): "How about taking a local cooking class with a resident?"
4. You ought to / If I were you, I would + BASE VERB: "You ought to ask local shopkeepers for food tips."
Opinion & Contrast Connectors

Authentic Local Exploration Connectors

1. Rather than tourist traps: "Rather than tourist traps, try eating at family neighborhood diners."
2. To get a real feel for: "To get a real feel for local traditions, stay in a homestay."
3. In my view / From my perspective: "In my view, taking public buses gives you a better view of daily life."
4. Instead of + GERUND: "Instead of staying in luxury hotels, choose small eco-lodges."
