Grammar Focus • Page T-147 Unit 12B

The 4 Types of Conditional Relationships

Conditionals can describe how situations in the past affect events in the past, present, or future.

1. True Events in the Past

Use past tense in both clauses for past routines/facts:

✔️ "When I was younger, if I didn't behave well, my parents were disappointed."

2. Hypothetical Past ➔ Past Result

Past perfect + would have + past participle:

✔️ "If I had been born with a good voice, I would have started my own band years ago."

3. Hypothetical Past ➔ Present Result

Past perfect + would + base verb:

✔️ "If I had studied harder in school, I would have a better job today."

4. Hypothetical Past ➔ Future Result

Past perfect + would + base verb / be + -ing:

✔️ "If she had booked her flight, she would be in Paris next week."
