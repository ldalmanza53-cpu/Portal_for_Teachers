The Mindful Exchange Context & Roles Guide

🎧 The Podcast & Wellness Context

Welcome to The Mindful Exchange! You are broadcast crew members and guest callers on an international peer counseling podcast. Your goal is to discuss real-life young adult concerns, weigh their positive and negative outcomes, and offer compassionate, actionable advice.

🎭 Student Roles (Groups of 2-3)

Role A: Storyteller

Presents a personal concern. Explains how it positively motivates them and negatively affects them.

Role B: Support Counselor

Listens actively, asks 1-2 clarifying questions, and provides structured advice using modals (*should, could, if I were you*).

Role C: Producer/Auditor

Monitors the 10-minute timer, verifies target language in the analyzer, and fills out the peer rubric.

⏱️ 10-Minute Activity Breakdown

  • Phase 1 (Min 0-3): Role A selects a prompt, drafts speech in analyzer, and presents concern & impacts.
  • Phase 2 (Min 3-7): Role B asks follow-up questions and shares 2-3 helpful advice recommendations.
  • Phase 3 (Min 7-10): Role C reviews language balance, completes rubric, and team shares brief feedback.
