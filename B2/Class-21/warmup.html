📖 Comparative Language Handbook

1. Signature Comparison Formulas

Formula A: What sets [City X] apart from [Group] is...
"What sets Venice apart from other European capitals is its complete car-free canal network."
Formula B: What makes [City X] truly unique is...
"What makes Reykjavik truly unique is its 100% geothermal volcanic energy grid."
Formula C: Unlike [City X]'s [Feature], [City Y] [Adaptation]...
"Unlike Tokyo's subterranean rail, La Paz transports citizens through the sky."

2. Characterization Structures

is characterized by...
Followed by a noun phrase or feature.
"Tokyo is characterized by extreme density."
is defined by...
Highlights a core defining identity.
"Singapore is defined by vertical forests."
