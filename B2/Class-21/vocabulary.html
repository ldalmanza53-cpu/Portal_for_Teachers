Megacity

noun
Definition

A very large city, typically one with a population of over ten million people, serving as a hub for regional or global activity.

B2/C1 Evaluative Sentence

"Tokyo is arguably by far the most seamlessly interconnected megacity in the world, setting an unmatched benchmark for urban transport."

B2 Usage Note: Don't confuse with 'metropolis'. A megacity explicitly refers to scale (10M+ population) and functional density.
