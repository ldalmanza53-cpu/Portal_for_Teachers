Grammar & Vocabulary Reference • Textbook Activity 14

Compound Family Terms

Compound family terms combine base family words with prefixes or suffixes to express extended generation or marriage connections.

great- (Prefix)

One generation above grandparents/uncles/aunts. Hyphenated: great-aunt, great-uncle.

grand- (Prefix)

Two generations apart. No hyphen: granddaughter, grandson, grandmother.

great-grand- (Prefix)

Three generations apart. Hyphenated prefix: great-grandmother, great-grandson.

-in-law (Suffix)

Relation through marriage. Hyphenated suffix: sister-in-law, father-in-law.
