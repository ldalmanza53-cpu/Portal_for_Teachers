Reading Strategy: Scanning

The 4 Golden Rules of Scanning for Specific Information

Scanning is searching rapidly for specific facts without reading every single word on the page.

1 Target Data Type

Know what you are looking for before reading: a clock time (21:00), a quantity (5 members), or a restriction (gluten-free).

2 Visual Anchors

Look for digits (0-9), capital city names (Madrid, Tokyo), bold key labels, and specific symbols like minutes or clock times.

3 Systematic Movement

Sweep your eyes down the page in a Z-pattern or column-by-column. Do not stop to digest full sentence meanings.

4 Context Verification

Once your eye anchors on the target number or time, read 2-3 surrounding words to confirm it answers the exact prompt.
