B1 Expression Formula Matrix

1. Annoyance Idioms

Express strong negative reactions to situations.

"It drives me crazy/up the wall when..."
"I can't stand it when..."
"It gets on my nerves when..."

2. Habitual Annoying Continuous

Subject + is/are always/constantly + verb-ing.

"They are constantly making loud noise."
"He is always leaving dishes in the sink."

3. Active Listener Validation

Acknowledge feelings constructively.

"I completely understand why that bothers you."
"That must be so frustrating!"

4. Diplomatic Solutions

Suggest practical mediation steps.

"Have you tried talking to them?"
"If I were in your position, I would..."

B1 Grammar Guardrails

Watch out for two common B1 mistakes: 1. Always include the pronoun 'it' in "I can't stand it when..." 2. Use the present continuous (-ing) with always/constantly to emphasize annoying habits rather than simple present!

"I can't stand when they interrupt." (MISSING 'IT')
"I can't stand IT when they are always interrupting." (CORRECT)
