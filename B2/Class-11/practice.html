Grammar Guardrails • Speculation Reference

B1 Modals of Speculation Rules

100% Certain (Positive):

must have + V3

"They must have forgotten their keys inside."

100% Certain (Negative):

can't / couldn't have + V3

"He can't have stolen the money; he wasn't there."

50% Possible:

might / could / may have + V3

"It might have been caused by a chemical leak."

Continuous & Passive:

must have been -ing / V3

"A machine must have been making that sound."
