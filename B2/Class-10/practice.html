"Have You Ever..." Past Incident Survey

Ask your partner these past incident questions. Frame answers using had to or should have.

Incident Question 1:

"Have you ever run into a problem at work or school that you tried to ignore?"

Example Answer: "Yes, I ignored a computer virus, but I ended up having to reformat my laptop."

Incident Question 2:

"Have you ever made a decision that aggravated a situation instead of solving it?"

Example Answer: "I should have double-checked the train times instead of rushing to the station."

Incident Question 3:

"Was there a time when you were supposed to meet someone but couldn't make it?"

Example Answer: "I was supposed to meet my study group at 5:00, but I had to work late."
