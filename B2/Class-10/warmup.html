Pillar 1

Describing Experiences

Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

Present Perfect (Unspecified Time / Life Experience)

have / has + Past Participle (V3)

"I have managed several cross-cultural projects."

Past Simple (Specific Time Marker)

Verb-ed / Irregular Past + (last year, in 2023, ago)

"Last month, I handled a major complaint in London."

Audit Rule: Start with Present Perfect to introduce experience, then shift to Past Simple for details!
Pillar 2

Mistakes & Obligations

Past Obligations & Missed Expectations

was / were supposed to (Unmet Plan)

"We were supposed to deliver the report on Friday, but we missed it."

had to (Forced Past Necessity)

"Because of the outage, I had to call every client individually."

should have + V3 (Past Regret / Critique)

"I should have checked the itinerary before leaving."

Audit Rule: Express responsibility without blame by using target past modals!
Pillar 3

Problems & Solutions

Root Causes & Successful Actions

due to / caused by (Root Cause)

"The initial delay was due to bad weather."

managed to + Infinitive (Overcoming Challenge)

"Despite the bug, we managed to fix the code."

succeeded in + Verb-ing (Achievement)

"We finally succeeded in negotiating a discount."

Audit Rule: Use managed to when effort was required to overcome difficulty!
