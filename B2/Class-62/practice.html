Grammar Plus • Page T-146 (Unit 9B)

Past Habitual with used to and would

Master past habits, repeated actions, and past states. Learn how to ask habitual questions with Did... use to...? and negative guesses with Didn't... use to...?

1. Asking Habitual Questions

Standard & Negative Question Patterns

To ask questions about a habitual action or situation in the past, use:
Did + subject + use to + base verb...?

✓ Did you use to listen to rock music when you were younger?

✓ Did you use to share a bedroom with your brother?

Use negative questions Didn't... use to...? to confirm a guess or surprise:

✓ Didn't you use to work at a grocery store after school?

✓ Didn't he use to play on the school soccer team?

CRITICAL RULE: NEVER use Would...? to form questions about past habits! (e.g. Would you listen to rock? is INCORRECT).

2. "Used to" vs. "Would" Restrictions

Stative States vs. Repeated Dynamic Actions

A. Stative Verbs (States / Conditions) ➜ ONLY "used to"

Verbs like live, be, have, like, own, know describe ongoing states.

✓ Lesley used to live in Brazil. (Lesley would live ❌)

B. Dynamic Action Verbs ➜ BOTH "used to" & "would"

Actions repeated regularly in the past (e.g. go biking, play, volunteer, ride).

✓ Serena used to go / would go mountain biking.
