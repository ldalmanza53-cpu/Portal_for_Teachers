Topic Sentence Masterclass

Learn why topic sentences are essential for structured writing and spoken answers.

The Gold Standard Formula
Topic Sentence = Specific Topic + Controlling Idea (Core Change)

❌ Rambling Opening (No Topic Sentence)

"Well, in the past I used to stay up until 2:00 AM on my phone and then I was really tired at work, but last year I bought an alarm clock and now I go to sleep early..."

Flaw: Plunges directly into minor details without telling the listener what the paragraph is actually about.

✅ Strong Topic Sentence Opening

"Establishing a strict evening routine has fundamentally improved my daily energy levels."

Strength: Instantly states the area of life (evening routine) and the core transformation (improved energy).
