✔️ Positive Completed
Subject + WILL HAVE + Past Participle (V3)

"By 6 PM, I will have finished the report."

❌ Negative Unfinished
Subject + WON'T HAVE + Past Participle (V3)

"She won't have saved enough money by next month."

❓ Question Inquiry
WILL + Subject + HAVE + Past Participle (V3)?

"Will you have graduated by the time you turn 25?"

💡 Crucial Time Marker Rules: "BY" vs "BY THE TIME"
1. BY + Specific Time / Noun Phrase

"I will have left by 5 PM." / "by next year."

Directly attach a noun, year, date, or time.

2. BY THE TIME + Present Simple Clause

"By the time you arrive (present simple), we will have eaten."

Do NOT use "will" in the time clause (Incorrect: "by the time you will arrive").
