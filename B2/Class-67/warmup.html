B1/B2 Dissatisfaction Formulas & Grammar Assembly

Master the 3 main grammatical structures native speakers use to express grievances formally or emphatically.

Structure 1: Emotional Impact

Subject + Passive/Reflexive Emotion

States how a company's failure affected you emotionally using passive verbs or participial adjectives.

I was/felt + [Adjective] + by/when + [Clause]

"I was completely misled by the fine print."

Structure 2: Cleft Focus

What Clause Emphasis

Isolates and highlights the single most egregious aspect of the consumer failure.

What left me feeling most + [Adj] + was + [Noun/Clause]

"What left me feeling most appalled was the clerk's attitude."

Structure 3: Evaluative 'It'

Objective Unacceptability

Frames the situation as objectively violating reasonable consumer standards.

It is completely + [unacceptable / appalled] + that + [Clause]

"It is completely unacceptable that the item was damaged."
