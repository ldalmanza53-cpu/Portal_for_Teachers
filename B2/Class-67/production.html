B1 Consumer Complaint Formula Matrix

1. Negative Feeling Starters

Express strong emotional reactions to faulty service.

"I am deeply disappointed with..."
"I feel extremely frustrated that..."
"I was completely appalled by..."
"I feel totally ripped off because..."

2. Problem Descriptions

Specify the exact commercial failure.

"...a defective item / damaged goods."
"...unexpected hidden charges."
"...a severely delayed delivery."
"...misleading product descriptions."

3. Polite Resolution Requests

Demand compensation or remedies firmly.

"I would appreciate a full refund."
"I insist on an immediate replacement."
"I expect proper compensation for..."

4. Specialist Validation

Apologize & offer solutions empathetically.

"I am terribly sorry to hear about..."
"I completely understand your frustration."
"Let me resolve this for you by offering..."

B1 Pragmatic & Grammar Guardrails

To construct an effective B1 complaint, always combine 3 key elements: 1. State your negative emotion (e.g., "I am deeply disappointed...") 2. State the specific problem (e.g., "...because the smartphone screen was cracked.") 3. Request a fair resolution (e.g., "I would appreciate a full refund.")

"I am angry. My order is bad." (VAGUE & LACKS FORMULA)
"I am extremely frustrated that my order arrived damaged, so I insist on a replacement." (COMPLETE B1)
