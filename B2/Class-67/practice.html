Unit T-85 • Section 11

Simple & Complex Indirect Questions

Master indirect question word order. Remember: indirect questions use statement word order (subject + verb) and remove auxiliary inversion or 'do/does/did'!

1. Simple Indirect Questions

Category A

Begin with expressions such as I wonder, I'd like to know, or I can't understand followed by statement word order.

Direct Question: Why don't they have more buses at rush hour?
Simple Indirect Question: "I wonder why they don't have more buses at rush hour."

2. Complex Indirect Questions

Category B

Begin or end with clauses or phrases containing the verb be (e.g., My big concern is..., ...is beyond me, The thing I don't get is...).

Example 1 (Starts with clause + be): "My big concern is whether I'll be able to get into the class."
Example 2 (Ends with clause + be): "How anyone can afford them is beyond me."
