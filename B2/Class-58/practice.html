B1 Patterns for Speculating & Describing Behavior

Master the grammar structures used to describe people's tendencies, preferences for conformity, and willingness to change.

Speculating with Modals

Subject + is probably + [Adjective]
"Ed is probably amenable..."

Describing Tendencies

I imagine / I think + Subject + Verb
"I imagine he only confronts people if he really has to..."

Stating Opinions

In my opinion, someone who... tends to...
"Someone who makes waves tends to be unconventional."
