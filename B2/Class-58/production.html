Activity Context & B1 Sub-Skill Focus

Global Voices Magazine Editorial Desk

Welcome, journalists! Your team is publishing a special edition feature comparing cultural customs worldwide. Your goal is to write cohesive paragraphs using Contrasts (whereas, on the other hand, unlike), Exceptions (except for, unless, apart from), and crucial Supporting Statements (For instance, A clear example is, This is because) to back up your points with concrete evidence!

✒️ Core Writing Formula: Point $\rightarrow$ Contrast/Exception $\rightarrow$ Supporting Evidence

Step 1: Base Point & Contrast

State a typical local habit and contrast it with other nations using whereas or unlike.

"In my city, people commute by bike, whereas in major capitals they take metro trains."
Step 2: Add Exception Rule

Highlight unique conditions or exceptions using except for or unless.

"...except for during heavy winter rainstorms when everyone drives."
Step 3: Insert Supporting Statement

Provide specific evidence, facts, or local examples using For instance or This is because.

"For instance, city survey data shows over 60% of residents cycle to work daily."

💡 CEFR B1 Grammar & Supporting Statement Toolkit

Master the three essential dimensions of B1 cultural feature writing: expressing contrasts, stating local exceptions, and providing concrete supporting evidence!

🔄 1. Contrast Connectors

Highlight Differences
whereas / while

In my country dinner is at 9 PM, whereas in Northern Europe people eat at 6 PM.

unlike / in contrast to

Unlike fast-paced capitals, shopkeepers here take afternoon breaks.

on the other hand / however

Buses can be delayed. On the other hand, fares are extremely affordable.

⚠️ 2. Exception Markers

Rule Outliers
except for / apart from

Shops are closed on Sundays except for local bakeries.

unless / except when

People rarely dress formally unless attending a wedding.

with the exception of

Uniforms are required, with the exception of festival Fridays.

✒️ 3. Supporting Statements

Evidence & Examples
For instance, / For example,

For instance, local families prepare multi-course meals during holiday reunions.

A clear example is...

A clear example is the traditional festival held every spring in central plaza.

This is because / In fact

This is because hospitality is considered a core cultural value across generations.
