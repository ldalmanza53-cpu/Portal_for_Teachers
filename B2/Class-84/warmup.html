Emotional & Resilience Vocabulary Matrix

Master key idioms and expressions describing psychological states during cultural adaptation.

Challenge State

Out of one's depth

In a situation that is beyond your capability or knowledge.

"When I entered the Seoul post office, I realized I was completely out of my depth."
High Stress

Overwhelmed

Completely overcome or overpowered by intense thoughts or feelings.

"She felt overwhelmed by the endless subway maps and unfamiliar street signs."
Confusion

Disoriented

Lacking direction; confused about time, place, or cultural surroundings.

"Stepping out into the bustling market, I felt completely disoriented."
Strength Trait

Resilient

Able to bounce back rapidly from adversity, mistakes, or hardships.

"Despite language barriers, Maya proved to be remarkably resilient."
Adaptability Idiom

Take it in stride

To accept setbacks calmly without becoming stressed or discouraged.

"When her bus took the wrong turn, she learned to take it in stride."
Growth Goal

Find one's footing

To establish confidence and stability in unfamiliar situations.

"By month two, Maya had finally found her footing in her neighborhood."

2. Narrative Tense & Mixed Conditional Transformation

Combine the two reality statements into a reflective Mixed Conditional (If + Past Perfect, ... would/could + base verb).

  1. Situation: Maya didn't learn basic Korean phrases before moving. She feels disoriented when reading street signs today.

    2. If Maya ________________________________________________________________________________

    👉 Answer: If Maya had learned basic Korean phrases before moving, she wouldn't feel disoriented when reading street signs today.

  2. Situation: I panicked during my postal challenge last month. I didn't take the advice in stride at the time.

    3. If I ___________________________________________________________________________________

    👉 Answer: If I hadn't panicked during my postal challenge, I would have taken the advice in stride.
