-ed vs -ing Adjectives

Feelings vs Situations

-ed = How a Person Feels overwhelmed, frustrated, embarrassed, confused, exhausted, relieved
-ing = What the Situation Is overwhelming, frustrating, embarrassing, confusing, exhausting, challenging
Time & Contrast Connectors

Linking Emotional Transitions

At first... but eventually...
Although it was exhausting...
Even though I felt anxious...
Gradually, I realized that...
Coping Action Verbs

Strategies & Actions

adapt to (new cultures)
overcome (language barriers)
cope with / deal with (stress)
ask for assistance / navigate
