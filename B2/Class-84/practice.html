Video Story Transcript: Jessica in Berlin

Scene 1: Arrival & Transit Challenges

"Hi, I'm Jessica from Los Angeles! When I first landed in Berlin, everything felt completely different. Coming from sunny Southern California, getting around on the public U-Bahn and S-Bahn train system was confusing at first, and understanding fast German spoken in markets was really tough!"

Scene 2: Weather & German Food Culture

"I had a hard time adjusting to the freezing winter weather in Berlin. In L.A. it's always warm! But I fell in love with local German bakeries and Currywurst. Trying traditional fresh breads every morning made me feel right at home."

Scene 3: Making Friends & Culture Integration

"At first I was worried about making friends because people seemed reserved. But once I joined a photography workshop and practice sessions at university, I realized Germans are incredibly warm and helpful. Berlin is now my second home!"

Grammar Focus • Difficulty & Preference Structures

Expressions for Adaptation Difficulties

Master the target structures used to express hardship, preferences, and adjustments.

Key Difficulty Structures

1. have a hard/difficult time -ing "I'd have a difficult time eating strange foods."
2. can't stand -ing / Noun "I can't stand being cold in winter."
3. find it tough / hard to Verb "I find it tough to understand fast German."
4. get used to -ing "It takes time to get used to taking the subway."
