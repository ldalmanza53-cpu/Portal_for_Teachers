Verbs Followed by Gerunds & Prepositional Expressions

Digital recreation of Textbook Reference Page VRB-3 / T-3

Unit 2 Grammar

1. Gerund Only

Use the gerund form (-ing) after these specific verbs:

enjoy going to parties where I don't know everyone.
avoid expressing my feelings and ideas in public.
don't mind giving up my time to help other people.

2. Preposition + Gerund

Expressions containing prepositions MUST take a gerund:

insist on making my own decisions.
always feel like going dancing!
am into going out to foreign restaurants.
am afraid of making people angry.

3. Gerund OR Infinitive

Either form is correct with little to no change in meaning:

can't stand being / to be in a messy room.
love taking / to take friends to cool new clubs.
hate getting up / to get up early for classes.
