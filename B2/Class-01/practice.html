Grammar: Verbs Followed by Gerunds & Agreement Expressions

Verbs + Gerunds
  • enjoy + -ing
    I enjoy going to parties.
  • avoid + -ing
    I avoid expressing my feelings.
  • don't mind + -ing
    I don't mind giving up time.
Prepositions + Gerunds
  • insist on + -ing
    I insist on making decisions.
  • feel like + -ing
    I always feel like dancing!
  • into / afraid of + -ing
    I'm into going out to restaurants.
Gerund or Infinitive
  • can't stand being / to be
  • love taking / to take
  • hate getting / to get
  • Both forms are correct with these verbs!

Target Agreement Structures

Showing Agreement (Same traits)
• So do I. / I do, too. (Affirmative verbs)
• So am I. / I am, too. (Be verb)
• Neither do I. / I don't either. (Negative verbs)
• I'm the same way.
Showing Disagreement (Different traits)
• I'm not like that.
• Really? I don't.
• I'd say I'm more...
• That's not true for me.
