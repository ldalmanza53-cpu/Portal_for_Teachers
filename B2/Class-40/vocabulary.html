Pragmatic Function

Used to introduce a memorable or dramatic personal anecdote by signaling to listeners that an engaging story is beginning.

Contextual Example

I'll never forget the time I accidentally CC'd the entire executive board on an email rant about the office coffee machine.

B2 Pragmatic Discourse Rule

Sets up high expectations for narrative climax; follow immediately with past continuous or simple past setting the scene.
