Full Interview Audio Transcript

Interviewer: Welcome back to 'Career Disasters'! Today we are speaking with Dan Carville, who has had some of the most embarrassing jobs on record. Dan, welcome!

Dan Carville: Thanks! It all started when I got my first job as a department store salesperson. I was supposed to sell luxury mattresses. But the showroom was so quiet and comfortable that I climbed into a display bed on the showroom floor and fell fast asleep! My manager found me snoring in front of customers two hours later and fired me on the spot!

Interviewer: Oh no! What happened after that?

Dan Carville: Well, next I tried working as a house painter. What happened was, my supervisor gave me an address to paint a white house yellow. The thing you have to know is, I mixed up the street numbers! I painted house 424 instead of 422 while the owners were on vacation! When they came home and saw their house painted bright yellow, they were furious. So later on, I was let go again.

Interviewer: Unbelievable! And what was your third job?

Dan Carville: I became a taxi driver. But I didn't know the city streets at all! I kept getting lost, taking passengers to the wrong places, and turning 10-minute trips into hour-long journeys. And in the end, the company fired me after a passenger had to give ME directions to the airport! Looking back, I didn't take any of those jobs seriously at all – I treated them like funny misadventures!
