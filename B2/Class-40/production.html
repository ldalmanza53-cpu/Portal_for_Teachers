B1 Narrative Expression Quick Reference

🌅 Setting & Hook
  • Believe it or not... — Used to introduce a surprising fact. "Believe it or not, I once met a celebrity on a night bus!"
  • A few years back... / It all started when... — Establishes time and setting.
⚡ Shock & Twist
  • Out of the blue... / All of a sudden... — Introduces an unexpected action.
  • To make matters worse... / On top of that... — Adds an extra complication.
  • To my surprise... / As luck would have it... — Highlights luck or unexpected events.
  • I couldn't believe my eyes! — Expresses extreme shock or disbelief.
🎯 Resolution & Lesson
  • Long story short... — Summarizes the outcome quickly.
  • Looking back... — Expresses a reflection on past events.
  • Moral of the story... — Concludes with a lesson learned.
