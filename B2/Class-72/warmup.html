Net Societal Outcome Framework

Evaluating decisions by net human welfare and suffering reduction

1. Consequentialist Standard: An action or policy is morally justified if its real-world consequences produce a greater net balance of well-being over harm compared to available alternatives.

2. Aggregate Human Welfare: Decision-makers evaluate the total sum of suffering prevented and positive flourishing created across all affected sentient individuals.

3. Measurable Metrics: In policy and ethical analysis, outcomes are calculated through metrics like Quality-Adjusted Life Years (QALYs), harm mitigation factors, and long-term societal growth indices.
