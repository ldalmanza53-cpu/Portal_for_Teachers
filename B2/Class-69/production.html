Role A

Customer / Client with a Problem

Expresses service or product issues politely using indirect question starters (e.g., "Could you tell me why...", "I was wondering if..."). Avoids blunt direct questions.

Key Target Syntax:

"Could you tell me why the screen is flickering?"

"I was wondering if you could check my order."

Goal: Obtain help politely without triggering customer friction.

Direct vs. Indirect Question Matrix

1. Tech Issues

Direct: "Why is the screen flickering?"

"Could you tell me why the screen is flickering?"

2. Delivery Status

Direct: "Where is my package?"

"I was wondering where my package is."

3. Technician Arrival

Direct: "When will the repairman arrive?"

"Do you know when the repairman will arrive?"

4. Yes/No Inquiry

Direct: "Is my account unlocked?"

"I'd like to know if my account is unlocked."

The B1 Golden Rule: Statement Word Order

When attaching an indirect question starter, change the clause into Statement Word Order (Subject + Verb). Remove question inversion and auxiliary do/does/did!

❌ Incorrect: "Could you tell me why is the screen flickering?"
✓ Correct: "Could you tell me why the screen is flickering?"

Support Agent Resolution Cheat Sheet

1. Active Empathy & Validation

"I completely understand how frustrating this delay must be..."

"Thank you for bringing this issue to our attention."

2. Clarifying Indirect Inquiries

"Could you tell me what order ID is listed on your invoice?"

"I was wondering if you could confirm your current shipping address."

3. Structured Diplomatic Solutions

"I will dispatch a technician immediately to inspect the line."

"I have initiated a full refund, which will appear in 3-5 business days."
