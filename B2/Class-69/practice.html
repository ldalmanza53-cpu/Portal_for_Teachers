Statement Beginning Clause

When the matrix clause is a statement, the overall sentence is a statement and ends with a period (.). Notice subject comes BEFORE verb!

  • I'm curious about why he didn't complain to the landlord.
  • I'm not sure who is responsible for repairing the roads.
  • The big question is if / whether we can get the city officials to listen.
?

Question Beginning Clause

When the matrix clause is in question word order (auxiliary verb first), the sentence is a question and ends with a question mark (?).

  • Do you have any idea if I need a visa to visit China?
  • Could you tell me where I can go to pay my parking ticket?
  • Don't you wonder how a place with poor service stays in business?

T-85 Unit 18

Buyer Beware! Smart Advice for Smart Shoppers

Explore essential rules to avoid consumer problems and practice sympathizing expressions.

1. Reputable Company

Buy from a reputable company with established customer support and valid reviews.

2. Check Guarantees

Make sure there's a written warranty or guarantee protecting against defects.

3. Examine Purchases

Examine your purchases thoroughly before paying or leaving the shop floor.

4. Comparison Shop

Do some comparison shopping online and in stores before committing.

5. Return Policy

Find out about the return policy, especially for items on clearance or sale.

6. Care Instructions

Find out how the item should be cared for (e.g. dry clean only, hand wash).
