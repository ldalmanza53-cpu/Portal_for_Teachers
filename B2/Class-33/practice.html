Reported Speech Tense Backshifting & Transformations

When reporting what someone said in the past, move the verbs one step back into the past (Backshifting). Pay close attention to pronouns, question structures (if / whether), and imperatives (told / warned + to-infinitive).

1. Direct to Reported Tense Shifts

Backshifting
Simple Present → Simple Past

"I work hard." → He said he worked hard.

Present Continuous → Past Continuous

"I am leaving." → She said she was leaving.

Simple Past / Present Perfect → Past Perfect

"I bought a car." → He stated he had bought a car.

Will / Can → Would / Could

"I will help." → She promised she would help.

2. Reported Questions (If/Whether)

Question Order

Change question word order to statement word order (Subject + Verb). Do NOT use auxiliary do / did in reported questions.

Yes/No Questions (Use if / whether):

"Are you hungry?" → She asked if I was hungry.

Wh- Questions (Keep Wh- word + statement order):

"Where do you live?" → He asked where I lived.

3. Commands & Requests (To-Infinitive)

Imperatives

Report commands using told / warned / asked + object + (not) to + verb.

Positive Command:

"Please close the door." → He asked me to close the door.

Negative Command:

"Don't touch that!" → The guard warned us not to touch that.

4. General Truths Exception

No Shift

If the statement describes a universal truth, a fact that remains true, or a permanent habit, tense backshifting is optional.

Permanent Truth Example:

"Water boils at 100°C." → The teacher explained that water boils at 100°C.
