B1 English Reference: Reported Speech & Public Etiquette

1. Essential B1 Reporting Verbs

  • complained that: "A passenger complained that someone was blaring loud music."
  • explained that: "The attendant explained that passengers needed to stand on the right."
  • asked him to: "She asked him to move his luggage from the aisle."
  • pointed out that: "The guard pointed out that recycling bins were nearby."
  • insisted that: "He insisted that he had been waiting in line first."
  • reminded us to: "The announcement reminded us to mind personal space."

2. Public Decorum & Etiquette Vocabulary

Queue-jumping: Cutting ahead of waiting people in line.
Speakerphone calls: Playing phone audio loudly in public spaces.
Littering: Leaving trash or wrappers on park grounds or streets.
Blocking walkways: Obstricting doorways or aisles with bags or strollers.

3. Tense Shift Quick Reference

  • Present Simple ("I stand here") ➔ Past Simple ("She said she stood there")
  • Present Continuous ("He is playing music") ➔ Past Continuous ("She complained he was playing music")
  • Imperative ("Move your bag!") ➔ Asked/Told + object + to-infinitive ("She asked him to move his bag")
