Reporting Verbs That-Clauses

Pattern: Verb + (that) + Subject + Past Verb
complain, boast, admit, insist, claim, assert

Use nuanced reporting verbs instead of generic said. For example: "He complained that the ringtone was too loud."

Reported Speech in Action Tense Backshifting

Direct: "I am closing a huge deal right now!"
Reported: He boasted that he was closing a huge deal at that moment.

Backshift present continuous to past continuous and update time expressions (right now → at that moment).
