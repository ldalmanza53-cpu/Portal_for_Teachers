📖 Public Echo Reference Handbook

1. Public Behavior & Etiquette Terminology

  • Spatial Awareness: Being conscious of how much physical space one occupies relative to others.
  • Loudspeaker Etiquette: Using headphones instead of playing audio on public speakers.
  • Oblivious: Unaware or unconcerned about how one's actions impact surrounding people.
  • Commandeering Seats: Taking up extra public seats with bags or personal belongings.

2. Tense Backshifting Rules in Indirect Speech

Present Simple → Past Simple
Present Continuous → Past Continuous
Present Perfect → Past Perfect
Will / Can → Would / Could

3. Time & Place Deictic Shifts

here → there
now → then / at that time
today → that day
tomorrow → the next day
yesterday → the day before
this → that

4. Reporting Verb Syntactic Patterns

• Verb + that + Clause: complain, explain, retort, admit, claim

• Verb + Object + to + V1: urge, warn, advise, order, encourage

• Verb + Preposition + -ing: insist on, apologize for, accuse [person] of
