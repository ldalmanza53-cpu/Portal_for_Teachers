B1 Restorative Language Matrix

1. Damage Descriptors

Specify physical damage precisely.

"The screen is cracked..."
"The door is severely dented..."
"The jacket is stained / ruined..."
"The lens was shattered / broken..."

2. Value & Emotional Impact

Describe emotional or monetary worth.

"This item has high sentimental value."
"It is irreplaceable to my family."
"I feel deeply upset / stressed out..."
"It holds priceless memories."

3. Conditionals & Advice

Propose fair restorative options.

"If I were in your place, I would..."
"Could you pay for the repair?"
"How about splitting the cost?"
"I ought to replace it with..."

4. Sincere Apologies & Offer

Take accountability without excuses.

"I am terribly sorry for my carelessness."
"I take full responsibility for..."
"I'm willing to cover the entire repair."

Restorative Mediation Formula

Combine 3 essential elements to reach a diplomatic agreement: 1. Describe damage & value (e.g., "My borrowed laptop screen is cracked, and it holds my work files.") 2. Express feelings (e.g., "I am deeply upset and stressed out.") 3. Propose a conditional/modal solution (e.g., "If you paid half of the repair cost, I would appreciate it.")

The Property Mediation Bureau Briefing

🏠 Personal Property Resolution Desk

Welcome to "The Property Mediation Bureau", a restorative desk where friends, roommates, and colleagues resolve accidental property damage conflicts diplomatically without ruining personal relationships.

🎭 Interactive Student Roles

Role A: Property Owner

Describes what was damaged, explains sentimental/monetary value, and demands fair repair/replacement.

Role B: Accidental Damager

Apologizes sincerely without making defensive excuses and proposes concrete repair/cost-sharing options.

Role C: Bureau Mediator

Guides negotiation using 2nd conditionals ('If I were you...') and advice modals, tracking rubric scores.

⏱️ 10-Minute Roadmap

  • Min 0-3 (Phase 1): Describe damaged possession, specify emotional/monetary worth, state impact.
  • Min 3-7 (Phase 2): Propose repair, replacement, or cost-splitting using 2nd conditionals and modals.
  • Min 7-10 (Phase 3): Finalize agreement, complete peer audit rubric, and export summary report.
