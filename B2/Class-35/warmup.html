📖 CivilityLab De-escalation & Vocabulary Handbook

1. Key Vocabulary: Describing Incivility

  • Brazen: Bold and shameless behavior without regard for social rules or others' feelings.
  • Patronizing / Condescending: Speaking or acting as if the other person is inferior or stupid.
  • Talk Over / Cut Off: Interrupting mid-sentence to dominate speaking time.
  • Commandeer: Unfairly taking over shared physical space or resources.
  • Brush Off: Dismissing or ignoring someone curtly or rudely.
  • Disingenuous: Insincere, hypocritical, or pretending to be candid while withholding truth.

2. Public Space Playbook (Low Stakes, High Unpredictability)

• Broken Record Technique: Repeat your neutral boundary sentence verbatim without emotional escalation (*"Excuse me, the line starts behind us."*).

• Environmental Shift: Remove yourself from the immediate vicinity of volatile individuals whenever feasible.

• Authority Delegation: Rely on staff, security, or conductors to enforce rules in public venues.

3. Professional Space Playbook (High Stakes, Ongoing Relationships)

• Interruption Reset: *"Hold that thought, Marcus—I'm almost finished with this point, and then I'd love your input."*

• Reclaiming Credit Diplomatically: *"Thanks for bringing that point up, Marcus. To build on the analysis I pulled together over the weekend..."*

• Addressing Patterns One-on-One: *"When you use that tone in meetings, it feels dismissive. Moving forward, let's keep our feedback focused on objective data."*
