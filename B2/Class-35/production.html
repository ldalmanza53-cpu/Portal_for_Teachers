B1 Grammar Reference: Public Civility & Softening Expressions

1. Softening Phrases & Polite Requests

"Excuse me, but..."
"Excuse me, but speakerphone calls are restricted in this train car."
"Would you mind -ing...?"
"Would you mind lowering the volume on your phone?"
"I'd really appreciate it if..."
"I'd really appreciate it if you could use headphones."
"I'm sorry to bother you, but..."
"I'm sorry to bother you, but the queue starts over there."

2. Advice Modals & Diplomatic Guidance

  • you should politely...: "You should politely inform line-cutters where the line begins."
  • it's best to...: "It's best to keep public conversations quiet during peak hours."
  • you ought to / had better: "You ought to throw away your fast-food wrappers before leaving."

3. Conditionals & Peaceful Resolution Alternatives

"If someone cuts line..."
"If someone cuts line, stay calm and address them with a smile."
"Instead of getting angry..."
"Instead of getting angry, request assistance from staff."
