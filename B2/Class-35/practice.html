Reported Speech with Modals & Reporting Rules

When reporting what someone said in the past, modal verbs shift to their past forms. Learn how modal verbs change, master the distinction between Say vs. Tell, and understand when tense backshifting is omitted for Recent Statements.

Modal Tense Shift Matrix

Past Backshift

Say vs. Tell Rule Breakdown

Object Pronoun Rule
TELL + Object

Requires a personal object pronoun (me, him, her, us, them) right after.

"She told me to practice every day."
SAY + (that) Clause

No direct personal object pronoun required directly after.

"She said (that) I should practice every day."

Recent Statements Exception

No Tense Backshift

If a statement was made very recently or the information remains true right now, you do not need to backshift the tense!

Immediate Report Example:

Direct: "I want to go out for dinner tonight."

Reported: "She said she wants to go out for dinner tonight." (No shift to 'wanted')

Reporting Verbs Usage Quick-Guide

claimed that / explained that

Followed by a clause to report assertions or explanations.

wanted to know / wondered

Used for indirect questions and inquiries (if / wh- words).

encouraged me to / advised me to

Verb + Object pronoun + infinitive for positive recommendations.

warned me not to / promised to

Negative warnings (not to do) or commitments (to do).
