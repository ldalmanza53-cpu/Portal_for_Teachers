Confront Head-On

Definition & Nuance (B2/C1)

To address a rude, aggressive, or hostile behavior directly and assertively without hesitation or evasion.

Reaction Syntax Example

Rather than ignoring the insult, she chose to confront the aggressive customer head-on.

⚡ B2 Grammar Pitfall: 'Confront' is a transitive verb; do not insert prepositions like 'with' when referring to the person directly (Say: confront someone, NOT confront with someone).
