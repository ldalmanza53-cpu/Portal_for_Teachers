B1 Storytelling Expressions Reference Sheet

🌅 Hooks & Openings
  • It all started when... — Introduces the main initiating event.
  • Believe it or not... — Prepares the listener for a surprising fact.
  • Picture this... — Invites the listener to visualize the setting.
  • As usual... — Establishes the background routine before disruption.
⚡ Sequence & Dramatic Twists
  • Out of the blue... / All of a sudden... — Signals an unexpected disruption.
  • To make matters worse... — Introduces an added obstacle or complication.
  • To my utter surprise... — Expresses dramatic shock or astonishment.
  • As luck would have it... — Signals a sudden stroke of good or bad luck.
🎯 Resolutions & Outcomes
  • Long story short... — Summarizes the climax outcome briefly.
  • In the end... / Ultimately... — Concludes the narrative arc.
  • Looking back... — Reflects on the experience from the present moment.
  • Moral of the story... — Shares the takeaway lesson learned.
