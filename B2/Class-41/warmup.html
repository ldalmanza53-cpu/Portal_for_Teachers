Narrative Mechanics & Expressions Handbook

1. Inverted Pyramid vs STAR Arc

Inverted Pyramid (News/Events) Starts immediately with the lead climax (Who/What/When), then adds supporting details and minor context.
STAR Arc (Personal Stories) Progresses chronologically: Situation, Task, Action, and Result with reflective key takeaways.

2. Narrative Multi-Past Tense Matrix

  • Past Simple (*steered*): Completed sequential past events.
  • Past Continuous (*was sailing*): Background actions in progress when interrupted.
  • Past Perfect Simple (*had departed*): Actions completed prior to another past moment.
  • Past Perfect Continuous (*had been searching*): Ongoing duration prior to a past point.

3. Time Adverbs & Syntactic Positions

Fronting Placed at clause start (*Out of the blue,...*). Sets scene mood immediately.
Mid-Position Placed before main verb or after auxiliary (*had incessantly trained*). Smooth flow.
End-Position Placed at clause end (*...at long last*). Delivers final climactic focus.

4. Storytelling Discourse Connectors

*Out of the blue* (Sudden shock), *To make matters worse* (Escalation), *In retrospect* (Hindsight reflection), *As it turned out* (Surprise reveal).
