/prɪˈdɪk.ə.mənt/
Definition

A difficult, unpleasant, or embarrassing situation from which it is hard to extricate oneself.

Contextual Example

When I realized I had already sent the unedited draft to the client, I found myself in a sticky predicament.

B2 Narrative Tense Rule

Notice how 'had already sent' (Past Perfect) occurred prior to the moment of realization ('realized' in Simple Past).
