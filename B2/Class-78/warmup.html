Ongoing vs. Accomplished Future Actions

Understand how to frame what will be happening (Future Continuous) versus what will be finished (Future Perfect) at a given point in time.

Ongoing Action Future Continuous

will be + [Verb]-ing

Describes an action that will be in progress at a specific time in the future.

Formula: Subject + will be + Verb-ing

"This time next year, I will be studying in Barcelona."

"At 8 PM tonight, she will be presenting her project."

💡 Key Time Signals:

This time tomorrow, at 5 o'clock next Friday, while you are resting, this time next month...

Accomplished Action Future Perfect

will have + [Past Participle]

Describes an action that will be completed/accomplished BEFORE a specific point in the future.

Formula: Subject + will have + Past Participle

"By 2030, we will have launched our new business."

"Before the deadline, they will have completed all tests."

💡 Key Time Signals:

By the time..., before next year, by December, in five years' time...
