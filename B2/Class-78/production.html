Grammar Focus #1

Future Continuous

Ongoing actions in progress in the future.

Formula: Subject + will be + Verb(-ing)

B1 Context Examples:

  • "This time next year, I will be working in London."
  • "In 2030, she will be leading her own tech startup."
Grammar Focus #2

Future Perfect

Completed milestones before a future time.

Formula: Subject + will have + Past Participle

B1 Context Examples:

  • "By 2030, I will have graduated from university."
  • "By next June, they will have launched their new product."
Coaching Expressions

Encouragement Phrases

Boosting peer motivation during roleplay.

Target B1 Key Phrases:
You've got this! Keep up the momentum! Well on your way!

Coach Practice Prompts:

  • "That sounds inspiring! You're well on your way!"
  • "I have no doubt you will have achieved that goal!"
