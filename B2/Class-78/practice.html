    Grammar Section 2 • Page T-97

    Future Perfect vs. Future Perfect Continuous Matrix

    Master the contrast between completed achievements and ongoing duration at a future point in time.

    COMPLETED ACTION

    Future Perfect

    will have + past participle

    Use to emphasize that something will be completed or achieved by a particular point in the future.

    "By this time next year, your self-confidence will have increased."
    ONGOING DURATION

    Future Perfect Continuous

    will have been + -ing (present participle)

    Use to emphasize the duration of an activity in progress at a particular point in the future.

    "By the end of next year, you will have been studying for 12 months."
