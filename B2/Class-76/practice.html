Grammar Explorer: Wishes and Regrets

Expressing dissatisfaction about past and present situations using comparatives and intensifiers.

1. Comparative Words

Use (not) enough, more, less, fewer, and better to quantify what you wish was different.

  • • fewer + countable nouns (e.g., fewer clothes / assignments)
  • • less + uncountable nouns (e.g., less money / coffee)
  • • more + countable or uncountable nouns (e.g., more time)
  • • better for adverbial/adjectival improvement (e.g., swim better / a better cook)
2. Intensifiers

Use really, very, and so to modify adjectives and express the intensity of a regret.

  • • "I got really angry last night." → "If only I hadn't gotten so angry last night."
  • • "I don't understand math very well." → "I wish I understood math better."
  • • "I felt really sleepy." → "I wish I hadn't felt so sleepy."
