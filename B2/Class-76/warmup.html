Backwards Tense Shift Rule

Expressing Wishes & Regrets Across Time

When expressing wishes or regrets, we step into a hypothetical world by shifting the verb **one tense backwards** from reality.

1. Present Wish

Unreal Present State

Formula:

wish / if only + Past Simple

"I don't own a car." ➔ "I wish I had a car."

2. Past Regret

Unreal Past Event

Formula:

wish / if only + Past Perfect (had + V3)

"I didn't study." ➔ "If only I had studied!"

3. Future Capability

Desired Opportunity

Formula:

wish / if only + could + base verb

"I can't attend tomorrow." ➔ "I wish I could attend."

4. Annoyance / Habit

Behavioral Complaint

Formula:

wish / if only + would + base verb

"He taps his pen continuously." ➔ "I wish he would stop!"

⚠️ B1 Grammar Trap: The Self-Desire Rule

Never use "I wish I would..." for your own desires or states!

Incorrect:

"I wish I would have a bigger apartment."

"I wish I would speak fluent Italian."

Correct:

"I wish I had a bigger apartment." (State ➔ Past Simple)

"I wish I could speak fluent Italian." (Ability ➔ could)
