Reference Sidebar

4 Pillars of American Core Values

Key cultural concepts highlighted in the documentary clip.

👤 1. Individualism & Self-Reliance

Emphasis on personal rights, individual decision-making, and taking personal responsibility rather than relying heavily on extended family or group consensus.

🎯 2. Direct Communication Style

Low-context communication where explicit language, clear "yes" or "no", and direct feedback are favored over indirect hints or saving face.

⏰ 3. Time as a Commodity

Monochronic time view ("Time is Money"). Schedules, strict deadlines, and promptness for meetings are prioritized as signs of respect.

🤝 4. Informality & Egalitarianism

Informal social etiquette, casual greetings, using first names with bosses or teachers, and an preference for equal status over rigid hierarchies.

Syntax Pillar 1

Contrast Connectors

Highlighting differences between two cultures cleanly.

whereas • while • unlike • compared to • in contrast to

"Whereas Americans favor direct feedback, we prefer..."

"Unlike in the US, schedules are more flexible here."

Syntax Pillar 2

Behavior Modifiers

Avoiding rigid stereotypes by hedging general observations.

tend to • as a rule • generally speaking • it is common to

"In my hometown, people tend to drop by unannounced."

"As a rule, elders are consulted first."

Syntax Pillar 3

Value & Etiquette Vocabulary

Target terms for expressing cultural priorities.

punctuality • directness • individualism • hospitality • informality

"Hospitality is considered our highest social priority."

"We balance individualism with strong community ties."
