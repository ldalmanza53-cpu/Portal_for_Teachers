Reference Guide

Grammar & Livability Vocabulary Handbook

1. Relative Clauses (where vs which / that)

where + subject + verb: Used for places when referring to an action happening inside that location.
Example: "This is the neighborhood where I live."

which / that + verb: Used when the relative pronoun acts directly as the subject or object of the clause.
Example: "I bought an apartment which has a huge balcony." (Not 'where has')

2. Second Conditional & Wish Structures

Second Conditional (Hypothetical): If + Past Simple, ... would + Bare Verb
Example: "If I had enough money, I would buy a penthouse."

Wish / If Only (Unreal Present Desires): Subject + wish(es) + Past Simple
Example: "I wish my apartment had an elevator." (Not 'has')

3. Target Livability Vocabulary

Walkability: Pedestrian convenience to amenities on foot.
Serene: Calm, peaceful, free from urban noise.
Amenities: Facilities like gyms, pools, and gardens.
Commute: The regular travel trip to work or school.
High-rise: Tall multi-story residential building.
Vibrant: Full of energy, life, culture, and activity.
Gated Community: Secure residential neighborhood with entrance gates.
Low Crime Rate: High safety with minimal reported criminal incidents.
