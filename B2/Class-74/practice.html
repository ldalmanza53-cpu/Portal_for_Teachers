Grammar Masterclass • Page T-93 Section 11

Wishes and Regrets Rules

Master how to express present/future desires versus past regrets using wish and if only.

Present / Future Wishes

Use wish + simple past / past continuous / could / would + verb:

✔️ "I wish I had enough nerve to ask for a promotion."

✔️ "I wish I could find the time to do volunteer work."

✔️ "If only my boss would consider promoting me!"

Regrets About the Past

Use wish + past perfect OR if + past perfect + could/would have + past participle:

✔️ "I wish I had taken a few Spanish classes."

✔️ "I wish I hadn't given my parents such a hard time."

✔️ "If I had stuck with something, I could have gotten good at it."
