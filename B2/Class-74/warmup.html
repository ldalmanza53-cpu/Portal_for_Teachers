The Tense Shift Golden Rule

Hypothetical Distance in English Grammar

1. Present Unreal Wishes: To express a desire to change a current reality, shift back 1 step into the past: Wish + Past Simple. (e.g. I don't have time → I wish I had time).

2. Past Regrets: To express regret over an unchangeable past event, shift back 2 steps into past perfection: Wish + Past Perfect (had + V3). (e.g. I stayed up late → I wish I had not stayed up late).

3. The "Would vs. Could" Trap:

  • Use COULD for your own personal ability or permission (I wish I could fly). Never say I wish I would... for yourself!
  • Use WOULD to express annoyance or desire for someone/something else to change behavior (I wish my neighbor would turn down the music).
