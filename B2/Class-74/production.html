🎤 Role A The Guest

Reflection Guest / Storyteller

Selects scenarios, shares genuine or creative personal turning points, and voices past regrets and present wishes clearly.

Key Tasks:

  • Use I wish I had + past participle for regrets.
  • Use I wish I were/had... for present desires.
  • Use If only... for intense feelings.
🎧 Role B The Host

Turning Point Coach

Guides the conversation, asks probing questions, and prompts the guest to expand on their wishes and regrets.

Key Questions to Ask:

  • "If you could turn back time, what would you change?"
  • "What do you wish you had done differently?"
  • "What do you wish would happen next?"

Retrospective Auditor

Audit Checklist:

  • Are any present tense errors flagged?

Past Regrets

wish / if only + Past Perfect

Used to express regret about something in the past that cannot be changed.

Subject + wish + subject + HAD + V3

If only + subject + HAD NOT + V3

• "I wish I had studied harder for the exam."

• "If only I hadn't missed the bus!"

Present Wishes

wish + Past Simple / Were

Used for situations in the present that you want to be different.

Subject + wish + subject + PAST SIMPLE

Subject + wish + subject + WERE

• "I wish I lived closer to my friends."

• "I wish I were more confident speaking English."

Future & Ability

wish + Would / Could

Used to express desired future changes or abilities/possibilities.

Subject + wish + subject + WOULD + V1

Subject + wish + subject + COULD + V1

• "I wish it would stop raining tomorrow."

• "I wish I could play the guitar."
