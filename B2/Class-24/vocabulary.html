Reduction Rule

Time clauses with (right) before, (right) after, and while can be reduced to -ing participle phrases ONLY if both clauses share the exact same subject!

Wind down

phrasal verb
Definition

To gradually relax or calm down after a period of intense activity, work, or stress.

Context & Reduced Time Clause

"While winding down before bed, I avoid looking at digital screens to improve my sleep quality."

Grammar Pitfall: 'While winding down...' is reduced from 'While I am winding down...'. The subject of the main clause ('I') MUST match the implicit subject of 'winding down'.

Forbidden Taboo Words (Do NOT Say)

Try using these reduced time clause sentence stems while explaining:

While + [verb-ing]...

"While feeling exhausted after work, a person usually does this to regain energy..."

Right after + [verb-ing]...

"Right after drinking a strong espresso, someone might suddenly do this..."

Before + [verb-ing]...

"Before going to sleep for the night, people perform this routine..."
