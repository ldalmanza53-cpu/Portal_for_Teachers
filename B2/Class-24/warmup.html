Daily Routine & Habit Grammar Rules

B1-B2 Core Rules for Adverbs, Prepositions, and Verb Tenses.

1. Frequency Adverbs Placement (*always, usually, rarely, never*)

  • BEFORE main verbs: I always drink water before my morning coffee.
  • AFTER the verb 'to be': He is rarely late for his morning shift.
  • AFTER auxiliaries/modals: You should always hydrate after waking up.

2. Prepositions of Time (*AT, IN, ON*)

  • AT + Precise Times / Moments: at 6:30 AM, at noon, at night, at bedtime.
  • IN + Parts of Day / Months / Seasons: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening.
  • ON + Specific Days / Dates: on Mondays, on weekdays, on weekends.

3. Present Simple vs Continuous

Use Present Simple for permanent habits and daily routines ("I wake up at 6 AM"). Avoid using Present Continuous for regular habits (❌ "I am waking up at 6 AM every day").
