Daily Routine & Habit Grammar Guide

Essential rules for B1 level routines, frequency adverbs & sequence connectors

Frequency Adverbs Placement

Frequency adverbs tell us how often something happens:

Rule 1: Before Main Verbs

Subject + Frequency Adverb + Main Verb
e.g., "I usually drink coffee in the morning."

Rule 2: After Verb "To Be"

Subject + Verb To Be + Frequency Adverb
e.g., "He is always focused during focus blocks."

Sequence Connectors & Time Expressions

Order of Events:

First, I wake up at 7:00. Then, I exercise for 20 minutes. After that, I take a quick shower. Finally, I start my workday.

Time Expressions:

on weekdays, every morning, during lunch break, twice a week, before going to sleep

Action Verbs Bank (Simple Present)

wake up, shower, drink, eat, commute, start, review, focus, organize, stretch, meditate, cook, unwind, disconnect, sleep
