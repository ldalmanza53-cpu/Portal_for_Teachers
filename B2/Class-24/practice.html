Grammar Concept

Mastering Reduced Time Clauses

Time clauses show when actions occur. You can make your English sound more concise and natural by reducing time clauses!

REDUCIBLE TIME CLAUSES (R)

A time clause can be reduced ONLY IF these two conditions are met:

  • The connector is before, after, or while.
  • The subject in both clauses is the exact same person or thing.

Formula:

[Connector] + [Subject] + [Verb] ➔ [Connector] + [Verb-ing]

Example:

Before I go to sleep, I like to read.

Before going to sleep, I like to read.

CANNOT BE REDUCED (N)

A time clause CANNOT be reduced if:

  • It uses other connectors: once, every time, till, as, the first/next/last time, as soon as, whenever, until, ever since.
  • The subjects in the two clauses are different!

Why it fails:

1. Different subjects: "Before the baby goes to sleep, his mother reads to him." (Two different subjects! Cannot reduce).

2. Non-reducible connector: "As soon as I get up, I drink water." ('As soon as' cannot be reduced).
