💡 CEFR B1 Grammar Toolkit: Contrasts & Exceptions

Master these essential B1 connectors to compare traditions, express sharp contrasts, and highlight cultural exceptions effortlessly.

🔄 1. Contrast Connectors (Highlighting Differences)

7 Key Connectors
whereas / while Direct Contrast

In my country dinner is late at 9 PM, whereas in Northern Europe people eat at 6 PM.

on the other hand / however Sentence Transition

Public transport is crowded. On the other hand, it is extremely cheap and punctual.

unlike / in contrast to / compared to Noun Comparison

Unlike most Western countries, bowing is common when greeting elderly people.

⚠️ 2. Exception Structures (Rule Outliers)

6 Key Structures
except for / apart from Noun Exclusion

Shops are open every day except for national holiday Sundays.

with the exception of / other than Formal Exclusion

Most students wear uniforms, with the exception of high school seniors on Fridays.

unless / except when Condition Exclusion

People rarely arrive on time for casual parties unless it is an official business meeting.
