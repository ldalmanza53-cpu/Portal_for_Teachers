Grammar Reference • Verbs of Belief & Explanations

CEFR B1 Verbs of Belief Framework

Certain Verbs (High Certainty):

be certain, be positive, be sure, know for a fact

"I know for a fact that hair strands contain no pain receptors."

Not Certain Verbs (Speculation):

assume, bet, doubt, figure, guess, have a hunch, suppose, suspect

"I assume giraffes developed long necks to eat high leaves."

Explanation Clauses:

because, to + verb, the reason why...

"Yeah, I bet that's the reason why they avoid the 13th floor."
