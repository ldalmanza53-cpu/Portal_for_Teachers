Sub-Skill 1

Scanning for Specific Details

Extracting facts without reading word-for-word

What is Scanning?

Scanning is moving your eyes quickly across a text looking for specific anchors like numbers (45,000), timestamps (03:15 AM), financial amounts ($18.4M), or proper nouns (Zurich).

Key Data Anchors to Scan For:
  • Numbers & Percentages: 400%, 85 knots, 350%
  • Timestamps & Dates: 23:59 PM, October 4, 14:22 UTC
  • Measurements: 6,200 meters, 85,000 feet, 14.5 km
Pillar 2

Degrees of Certainty Modals

Deducting Present vs. Past Anomalies

100% Positive Deduction

must be / must have + V3

"The drone must have suffered an encrypted signal breach."

50-70% Possible Hypothesis

might / may / could / could have + V3

"The temperature spike might have been caused by geothermal vents."

100% Negative Deduction

can't be / couldn't have + V3

"The satellite can't have drifted without automated engine firing."

Pillar 3

Personal Reaction Starters

Expressing Stance & Feelings on Dossier Findings

I was genuinely shocked to read...

"I was genuinely shocked to read that $18.4 million was moved in seconds."

What strikes me most is...

"What strikes me most is that all 128 doors unlocked simultaneously."

I find it hard to believe that... / It concerns me that...

"I find it hard to believe that a glacier moved 45 meters in 24 hours."

Pillar 4

Tri-Part Response Structure

Combining Fact + Certainty + Reaction

1. Scanned Fact:

"At 03:15 AM, $18.4M was transferred in Zurich..."

2
2. Certainty Modal Deduction:

"...so the suspect must have used high-speed automated algorithms."

3
3. Personal Reaction:

"What strikes me most is how negligible the $1.20 fee was."
