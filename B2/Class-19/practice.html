Connecting Contrasting Ideas

In English, we connect two opposite or surprising facts using different types of transition words. Choosing the right word depends on whether you follow it with a clause (Subject + Verb), a noun phrase, or start a new sentence.

1

Clause Connectors

+ Clause (S + V)
although even though

Used to connect two clauses. Always followed immediately by a Subject + Verb.

Although I love living here, it is expensive.

2

Prepositional

+ Noun / -ing
despite in spite of

Functions like a preposition. Followed by a Noun, Noun Phrase, or Verb-ing.

Despite the high rent, I decided to stay.

3

Sentence Transitions

+ Comma (Sentence)
however nevertheless on the other hand

Connects two full sentences. Starts a new sentence, followed by a comma ( , ).

Rent is high. However, the location is perfect.
