Common Learner Error Note (B2 Level Rules)

Avoid confusing despite and although! Prepositions like despite or in spite of take a noun phrase or gerund (-ing). Conjunctions like although or even though require a full clause (subject + verb).

despite

/dɪˈspaɪt/

Definition & Structure Base Form Analysis

Without being affected by; in spite of.

Grammar Rule / Variations:

Followed by a Noun or Gerund (-ing verb). Never followed directly by subject + verb.

Common Collocations:

despite the rain, despite having, despite all efforts

Example Sentences:
  • They arrived on time despite the heavy traffic.
  • Despite feeling exhausted, she finished the project.
