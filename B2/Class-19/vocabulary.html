Noun

Sovereignty

The supreme power or authority of a self-governing state or city-state to govern itself independently.

Context Example:

Although the island nation achieved legal sovereignty, it remains economically dependent on neighboring regional powers.

Grammar Rule: Although introduces a subject + verb clause (the island nation achieved...). Do NOT use despite here unless followed by a noun phrase or gerund.

Contrast Connectors Quick Reference

1 Prepositions (Followed by Noun Phrase / Gerund -ing)

despite / in spite of

Rule: NEVER put a full subject + finite verb immediately after these! Always use a noun phrase, pronoun, or gerund (-ing).

"Despite high population density, public transit runs efficiently."

2 Subordinating Conjunctions (Followed by Full Clause)

although / even though / though

Rule: MUST be followed by a clause containing a Subject + Verb.

"Although the city grew rapidly, green spaces were preserved."

3 Conjunctive Adverbs (Sentence Transitions)

however / nevertheless / on the other hand

Rule: Used to connect two independent ideas or sentences. Typically followed or set off by a comma.

"The region sought autonomy; however, economic ties remained strong."
