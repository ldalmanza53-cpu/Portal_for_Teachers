Contrast Linking, Intensity Modifiers & OSASCOMP Guide

Intensity & Comparative Modifiers

Use modifiers to express degree when contrasting locations:

Slight Difference

slightly less / a bit more

"Vienna is slightly quieter."

Significant Difference

significantly / considerably

"Rio is significantly warmer."

Extreme Contrast

far more / exceptionally

"Rio is far more vibrant."

Adjective Order Sequence (OSASCOMP)

When joining multiple descriptive adjectives before a noun:

1. Opinionlively
2. Sizespacious
3. Ageancient
4. Shapecurved
5. Colorgolden
6. OriginAustrian
7. Materialwooden

Contrast Connector Rules

whereas / while Direct clause comparison: "Rio is energetic, whereas Vienna is tranquil."
in contrast to / unlike Prepositional contrast: "In contrast to Tokyo, Dublin has smaller streets."
although / despite Concession clauses: "Although Amsterdam is rainy, its lifestyle is great."
