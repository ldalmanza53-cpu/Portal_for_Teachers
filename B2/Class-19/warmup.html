📖 Comparative Language Handbook

1. Modifiers of Degree

Use modifiers to show how large or small the difference between two places is:

  • Large Difference: substantially, significantly, considerably, far
  • Small Difference: slightly, marginally, a bit

2. Contrast Connectors

  • Subordinating: whereas, while (Clause + , + whereas + Clause)
  • Transitions: In contrast, On the other hand (Sentence 1. In contrast, Sentence 2.)
  • Prepositions: despite, in spite of (Despite + Noun Phrase / -ing, Clause)
