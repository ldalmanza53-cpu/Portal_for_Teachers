Melatonin Suppression

Definition (B2/C1)

The reduction or delay in the secretion of melatonin—a hormone regulating sleep-wake cycles—caused by blue-light exposure.

Example with Reason / Condition Clause

Considering that late-night tablet usage causes melatonin suppression, sleep onset is significantly delayed.

⚡ Grammar Tip: Considering that is followed by a clause (subject + verb), whereas considering can take a direct noun phrase.
