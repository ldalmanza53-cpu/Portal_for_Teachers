/ˈspaɪ.wer/
Definition

Software that enables a user to obtain covert information about another's computer activities.

Passive Voice Context

"Malicious spyware is currently being installed on unprotected laptops through unverified email links."

B2 Passive Syntax Pitfall

When the hacker (agent) is unknown or obvious, passive is preferred ('is being installed'). Avoid active forms when the actor cannot be identified.
