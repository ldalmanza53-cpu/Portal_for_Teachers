Passive Voice & Review Reference Matrix

1. Passive Voice Tense Formulas

Present Simple Passive Subject + am/is/are + V3 (Past Participle)

E.g., "Provocative titles are prioritized by algorithms."

Present Continuous Passive Subject + am/is/are + being + V3

E.g., "User data is currently being monetized."

Past Simple Passive Subject + was/were + V3

E.g., "The viral meme was created in 2023."

Present Perfect Passive Subject + has/have + been + V3

E.g., "Millions of videos have been uploaded."

Passive Modals Subject + modal (must/should/can) + be + V3

E.g., "Community rules should be enforced strictly."

2. Agent Omission Rules

Omit the agent (the by... phrase) when the actor is unknown, obvious, or generalized (e.g., "by people", "by users"). Focus on the action and object!

3. The 5-Part Tech Review Architecture

1. Headline & Hook: Punchy title summarizing the verdict.
2. Overview: What the tool/trend is and who uses it.
3. Feature Analysis: Concrete examination of capabilities.
4. Pros & Cons: Contrast strengths with operational drawbacks.
5. Verdict & Score: Final score (e.g., 4/5) and target fit.
