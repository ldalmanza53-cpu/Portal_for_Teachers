B1 Opinion & Trend Grammar Cheat-Sheet

1. Expressing Stances & Personal Opinions

Use these sentence openers at B1 level to express personal views smoothly:

  • In my opinion, [trend] is [adjective]...
  • From my perspective, [trend] helps users to...
  • I strongly believe that [trend] has a huge effect on...
  • As far as I'm concerned, [trend] should be regulated...
  • I'm convinced that [trend] will change the future of...

2. Giving Justifications & Causes

Connect your opinion with a strong reason or cause:

Clause Connectors:

...because + [subject + verb]

...since + [subject + verb]

Noun Connectors:

...due to + [noun phrase]

...owing to + [noun phrase]

3. Soft Disagreement & Contrast

Politely disagree or add contrast without sounding aggressive:

  • I see your point, but [trend] can also lead to...
  • I'm not so sure about that because...
  • While I agree that [trend] is popular, I still think...
