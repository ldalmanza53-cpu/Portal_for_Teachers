Metropolis

Urban Exploration Term

Reveal IPA, Definition & Grammar
/mɪˈtrɒpəlɪs/
B2/C1 Definition

A very large, densely populated, and influential city.

Grammar in Context Non-defining

Tokyo, which is a bustling metropolis, attracts millions of travelers every year.

⚠️ Relative Clause Warning

Never use 'that' instead of 'which' in non-defining relative clauses (clauses surrounded by commas).
