Welcome to The Global City Explorer Studio

Master CEFR B1 target syntax for describing world cities using relative clauses! Learn the difference between Defining Relative Clauses (Sydney is a city that has a famous opera house) and Non-Defining Relative Clauses (Barcelona, which is located in Spain, is famous for its architecture). Pay close attention to relative pronouns (who, which, where, when, that) and essential comma placement!
