B1 Opinion Starters

Express professional perspectives naturally instead of simply repeating "I think".

Formula: Opinion Phrase + (that) + Main Clause
  • Formal Conviction: In my opinion, pedestrian streets revitalize local commerce.
  • Professional Point of View: From my perspective, reliable public transit is the top priority.
  • Strong Personal Belief: As far as I'm concerned, suburban sprawl damages ecosystems.
  • Firm Stance: I firmly believe that green roofs reduce the urban heat island effect.
2

Comparatives & Contrast

Compare cities, neighborhoods, and infrastructure solutions with precision.

Modifiers: far / significantly / much + comparative
Equatives: as + adjective + as / not nearly as...
Contrast: whereas / while / compared to
  • Emphasized Superiority: Light rail is far more efficient than widening highways.
  • Negative Comparison: Suburban cars are not nearly as sustainable as electric buses.
  • Direct Contrast Clause: Modern cities invest in parks, whereas older cities prioritized parking.
