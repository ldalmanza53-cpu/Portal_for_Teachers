📖 ESL Grammar Handbook

1. OSASCOMP Adjective Order Rules

Order Category Examples
1. OOpinionpicturesque, vibrant, charming, chaotic
2. SSizecompact, sprawling, huge, tiny
3. AAgemedieval, modern, ancient, historic
4. COrigin / ColorEuropean, Asian, Greek, Italian
5. TType / Purposecoastal, financial, maritime, industrial

2. Modifiers of Degree

Large Difference
far, significantly, substantially, considerably
"Zurich is far more expensive than Naples."
Small Difference
slightly, marginally, a bit
"Vienna is slightly cleaner than Munich."
Equal / Negligible
just as, almost as
"Lyon is just as charming as Lille."

3. Contrast Connectors Taxonomy

Subordinating Conjunctions (whereas, while, although)
Connects two clauses inside one sentence. [Clause 1], whereas [Clause 2].
Transition Adverbs (however, in contrast, on the other hand)
Starts a new sentence followed by a comma. [Sentence 1]. In contrast, [Sentence 2].
Prepositional Phrases (despite, in spite of, unlike)
Followed directly by a Noun Phrase or Gerund (-ing). Never use "despite of"!
