Coastal resort

Category: Compound Term

/ˈkoʊstəl rɪˈzɔːrt/

Definition: A seaside town built primarily around holiday tourism.

Nuance: Implies high seasonal tourism surges vs off-season local quietness.

Example Sentence

Although developers built picturesque large modern concrete hotels, tourists still prefer charming small old wooden cottages.

Modifier Sequence Quality (picturesque) → Size (large) → Age (modern) → Material (concrete)
Contrast Connector 'Although' introduces an adverb clause (+ subject + verb).
B2 Common Pitfall: Never use 'Despite' directly before a subject + verb clause (e.g. Avoid 'Despite developers built...').
