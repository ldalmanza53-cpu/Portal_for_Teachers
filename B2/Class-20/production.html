2. Connecting Ideas (+ Addition & Cause)

furthermore / moreover / in addition Adding key features: "Medellín has a warm climate; furthermore, its metro is very clean."
consequently / therefore Showing results: "Bogotá has extensive bus lanes; consequently, commuting is faster."

3. Contrasting Ideas & Concession

whereas / while Direct comparison: "Medellín is sunny, whereas Bogotá is significantly cooler."
however / on the other hand Introducing contrast: "Tokyo is fast; on the other hand, Kyoto is serene."
although / despite Expressing concession: "Although London is expensive, its culture is unmatched."
