Order of Adjectives Before Nouns

When using multiple adjectives to describe a noun (e.g. shape, color, material), they follow a strict natural order in English. Memorize the 8-stage sequence below!

Textbook Adjective Sequence Matrix

Quality → Size → Shape → Age → Color → Type → Material → Noun
Quality Size Shape Age Color Type Material Noun
quaint little winding - - - - streets
picturesque - - old brightly colored resort - hotels
- small - - - Thai wooden fishing boats

Adverb Clauses

+ Clause (S + V)
although even though

Followed immediately by a Subject + Verb.

Although I love big cities, I prefer small towns.

Sentence Transitions

+ Full Sentence
however nevertheless on the other hand

Starts a new sentence followed by a comma ( , ).

I love big cities. However, housing is expensive.

Prepositions

+ Noun / -ing
despite in spite of

Followed by a Noun Phrase or Gerund.

Despite the high costs, I stay here.
