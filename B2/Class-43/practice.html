Section 2: Grammar Focus (T-55)

Advanced Passive Matrix

Learn how emphasis shifts from the doer (agent) to the object (receiver) of the action.

B1 Passive Formula:

Object + Auxiliary (Be) + Past Participle (+ by Agent)

Present Continuous Passive

For ongoing or currently changing actions.

am / is / are + BEING + Past Participle

"More employees are being allowed to work remotely."

Present Perfect Passive

For recently completed or connected actions.

has / have + BEEN + Past Participle

"Music fans have been given unlimited access."

Future Passive

For predictions or upcoming events.

will be / is going to be + Past Participle

"Store purchases will be made using mobile devices."
