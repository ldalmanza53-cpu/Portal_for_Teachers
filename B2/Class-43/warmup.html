Passive Voice Formula Matrix & Digital Vocab

1. Passive Voice Tense Formula Matrix

Present Simple Passive

[am / is / are + V3]

"Memes are shared across social feeds daily."

Present Continuous Passive

[am / is / are + BEING + V3]

"A new trend is being replicated by millions."

Past Simple Passive

[was / were + V3]

"User data was leaked during the outage."

Present Perfect Passive

[has / have + BEEN + V3]

"Record revenue has been generated this quarter."

Passive Modals & Infinitives

[modal + BE + V3] / [to BE + V3]

"Copyrighted audio must be muted." / "This video deserves to be seen."

2. Why Use Passive Voice in Tech & Digital Media?

In digital culture, passive voice is frequently used to shift focus onto systemic results, automated processes, or when the agent (e.g., algorithm, unknown hackers, or millions of anonymous viewers) is irrelevant or unknown:

  • Systemic Automation: "Your video has been processed." (Focus on video, not the server)
  • Anonymity & Security: "Accounts were compromised." (Focus on impacted accounts)
  • Viral Outcomes: "Over 10M views were accumulated." (Focus on statistical milestone)

3. Key Internet Culture Vocabulary

Shadowban: Stealth blocking of a user's content from feeds.
Retention Rate: Percentage of viewers watching a clip to completion.
Algorithm: Automated rule-set delivering content to users.
Monetization: Earning revenue from digital content views.
