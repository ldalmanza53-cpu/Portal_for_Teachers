B1 Passive Voice Grammar Reference

📱 Present Simple Passive (is / are + Past Participle)

Used for general facts, routines, and ongoing digital processes.

Example: "Short clips are watched by millions every day."

🤖 Present Continuous Passive (is / are being + Past Participle)

Used for trends happening right now at this moment.

Example: "New AI voice models are being generated currently."

📜 Past Simple Passive (was / were + Past Participle)

Used for finished past actions or internet history milestones.

Example: "The first video was uploaded to YouTube in 2005."

⚡ Present Perfect Passive (has / have been + Past Participle)

Used for past actions with a strong present impact.

Example: "Traditional broadcasting has been transformed by social algorithms."
