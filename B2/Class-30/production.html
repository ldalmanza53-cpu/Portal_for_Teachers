B1 Outlining & Transition Guide

1. Hierarchical Outline Hierarchy

Outlines organize complex thoughts into levels using indentations and numbers/letters:

Title: Strategic Client Communication
I. Main Point / Speech Heading
A. Supporting Detail 1 (Evidence / Example)
B. Supporting Detail 2 (Evidence / Example)
II. Conclusion / Summary Statement

2. Key B1 Transition Markers

Sequence & Order First of all, To begin with, Secondly, Next...
Examples & Details For instance, For example, Specifically...
Adding Information Furthermore, In addition, Also...
Summary & Ending In summary, To conclude, Overall...
