The PREP Formula Explained

PREP is a powerful micro-structuring tool used in spontaneous speaking, business pitches, and persuasive discussions.

P — Point State your main idea directly in 1 crisp sentence.
R — Reason Explain the core 'why' or rationale driving your point.
E — Example Provide concrete metrics, data, or an illustrative case study.
P — Point Restated Reiterate your takeaway and connect to the actionable next step.

Standard Alphanumeric Outlining Rules

Hierarchical structure ensures complex topics remain logically grouped and easy for listeners or readers to follow.

I. Roman Numerals: Primary Heading / Thesis
A. Capital Letters: Major Supporting Points
1. Arabic Numbers: Specific Sub-points & Data
a. Lowercase Letters: Micro details or examples

Softening & Epistemic Markers

Reduces perceived hostility or dogmatism.

    "Playing devil's advocate..." "From my perspective..." "It seems to me that..."

Alignment & Validation Markers

Establishes rapport and empathy before pivoting.

    "Help me understand where..." "I hear what you're saying..." "Do you want advice or space to vent?"
