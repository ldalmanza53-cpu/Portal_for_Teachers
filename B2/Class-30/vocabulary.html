Pleasantry

Definition (B2/C1)

An inconsequential remark made as a polite greeting or part of light social conversation.

Contextual Example & Syntax

Exchanging a brief pleasantry about the venue is standard procedure before delving into formal discussion.

⚡ Usage Tip: Most commonly used in the plural form ("exchanging pleasantries"). Collocates with verbs like exchange, trade, or mutter.
