Project Time Capsule: Documenting Past Human Habits

Welcome, Memory Archivists! The year is 2050, and the Global Memory Archival Bureau is collecting official records of how humans used to live, work, play, and communicate in past decades. Your mission is to write vivid archival records contrasting past repeated habits (would, used to, didn't use to) with modern digital life (now, these days, nowadays).

📐 B1 Archival Formula: Past Repeated Actions / States $\rightarrow$ Present Contrast

1. Used To (States & Habits)

Use used to for both past repeated actions AND past states (feelings, living locations, beliefs).

"We used to live in a quiet neighborhood..."
2. Would (Repeated Actions Only)

Use would ONLY for repeated past actions or nostalgic routines (NOT for state verbs like be, live, know, have).

"Every summer, we would play outdoors until sunset..."
3. Didn't Use To (Negatives)

Use didn't use to (note: no 'd' in use) to highlight past habits that were absent.

"Children didn't use to have handheld smartphone screens..."
4. Present Contrast

Contrast past habits with modern reality using now, these days, or but nowadays.

"...but these days, everyone streams games instantly."

📜 CEFR B1 Past Habits Language & Structure Toolkit

Master the four pillars of explaining past habits: expressing general past habits & states with used to, describing nostalgic repeated actions with would, expressing absent habits with didn't use to, and contrasting with present reality!

🔄 1. Used To + Infinitive

States & Habits
used to + verb (Action)

My grandmother used to write handwritten letters to her relatives every month.

used to + state verb (State)

I used to be very shy when I was in primary school.

didn't use to + verb (Negative)

People didn't use to carry portable digital devices everywhere.

🌅 2. Would + Infinitive

Repeated Actions Only
would + action verb

Every weekend, my father would bake fresh bread for the whole family.

Nostalgic Routine

During summer holidays, we would gather around the radio in the evening.

⚠️ CRITICAL B1 RULE

NEVER use 'would' for past states!
❌ "I would live in London" $\rightarrow$ ✅ "I used to live in London."

⚖️ 3. Present Contrast

Now vs Then
but now / these days

We used to watch VHS tapes, but these days we stream high-definition movies.

but nowadays / today

I didn't use to like vegetables, but nowadays I eat salads every lunch.

no longer / don't anymore

My brother would play board games every night, but he no longer plays them.
