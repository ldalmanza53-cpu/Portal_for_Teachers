REASON CLAUSES Change of Circumstance

Now that = "because now"

Introduces a new reality or changed circumstance that explains the action in the main clause.
Ex: "Now that I work early, I go to bed at 9 PM."

CONDITION CLAUSES Prerequisites & Alternatives

Whether or not | Provided / Providing (that)

Whether or not: Dual position (before or after condition).
Provided that: Condition that must be met for main clause to be true.
