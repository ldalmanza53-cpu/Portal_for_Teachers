Clauses Stating Reasons & Conditions

In English B1, we use specific connectors to explain why things happen (reasons) or under what circumstances they happen (conditions). Click any card below to hear audio examples and review syntax rules!

Clause Order & Comma Rules

1. Condition Clause First (Comma Required)
[ Connector + Clause ] , [ Main Clause ]

Example: "Provided that I get all my work done, my weekend will be free."

2. Main Clause First (No Comma)
[ Main Clause ] [ Connector + Clause ]

Example: "She goes jogging every morning whether or not it's bad weather."
