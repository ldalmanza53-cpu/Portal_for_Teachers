Clauses Stating Reasons & Conditions

In English B1, we use special connectors to express why things happen (reasons) or under what circumstances they happen (conditions). Click on any connector below to see its structure, rules, and audio examples!

Clause Order & Position Rules

Condition First (Comma Required)
[ Connector + Clause ] , [ Main Clause ]

Example: "Provided that I get all my work done, my weekend will be free."

Main Clause First (No Comma)
[ Main Clause ] [ Connector + Clause ]

Example: "She goes jogging every morning whether or not it's bad weather."
