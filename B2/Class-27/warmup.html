📖 Grammar Handbook: Habits, Reasons & Conditions

1. Expressing Habits & Tendencies

  • Present Simple + Frequency Adverbs: I usually drink water...
  • Tend to + Base Verb: He tends to stay up late.
  • Whenever (Trigger Habit): Whenever I feel anxious, I walk.

2. Expressing Reasons & Purpose

  • In order to + Base Verb: I stretch in order to prevent injury.
  • So that + Subject + Modal + Verb: I prepare clothes so that I can save time.
  • Due to / Because of + Noun Phrase: Delayed due to bad weather.

3. Setting Conditions

  • Unless (= If not): I walk unless it rains. (No double negative!)
  • As long as / Provided that: You can join as long as you finish.
