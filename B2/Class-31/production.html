B1 English Expressions for Social Situations

1. Greetings, Introductions & Compliments

  • Formal Introduction: "May I introduce you to [Name]? They are our senior manager."
  • Small-talk Opener: "It's a wonderful evening, isn't it? Have you tried the refreshments?"
  • Giving Compliments: "That was an excellent presentation. I really enjoyed your insights!"
  • Responding to Compliments: "That's very kind of you to say, thank you so much."

2. Invitations, Reactions & Polite Disagreements

  • Inviting: "Would you care to join us for a cup of coffee tomorrow morning?"
  • Accepting: "I'd delighted to! Thank you so much for the invitation."
  • Declining Politely: "I'd love to, but unfortunately I have a prior engagement."
  • Soft Disagreement: "I see your point; however, I believe another perspective is..."

3. Apologies, Interruptions & Graceful Exits

  • Polite Apology: "Please accept my sincere apologies for arriving a bit late."
  • Interrupting Gracefully: "Forgive me for interrupting, but I wanted to quickly mention..."
  • Excusing Yourself: "If you'll excuse me for just a moment, I need to make a quick call."
  • Taking Leave: "It was an absolute pleasure meeting you, but I must return to my colleagues."
