Social Pragmatics Reference Guide

Target expressions, conversation categories, softeners, and impression frames

social butterfly

An outgoing person who moves easily between different social groups.

wallflower

A shy person who stays on the sidelines during social gatherings.

conversation monopolizer

Someone who dominates discussions without letting others contribute.

comes across as / strikes me as

Expressions used to describe initial impressions tactfully without absolute judgments.

Key Softening Categories

Negative Questions "I wonder if we might..." / "Couldn't we consider..."
Epistemic Hedges "From my perspective..." / "It seems to me..."
Objective Framing "Playing devil's advocate for a moment..."
Alignment & Empathy "I hear what you're saying, and..."
