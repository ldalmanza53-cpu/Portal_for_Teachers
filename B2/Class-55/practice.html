Non-defining Relative Clauses as Sentence Modifiers

In English, we can use a relative clause beginning with , which to modify or make a comment about an entire previous sentence or situation, rather than just a single noun.

Verb Patterns & Rules

Some common verbs used in sentence modifiers are: surprise, depress, encourage, suggest (that), contribute to, and result in.

CRITICAL RULE: Verbs describing emotion (e.g., depress, encourage, surprise) MUST be followed by an object pronoun (me, him, her, us, them).
✔️ which encourages me to study harder.
❌ which encourages to study (Missing object)

Target Examples

My teacher praised my English today,

which encourages me to study harder.

Peter took up golf,

which suggests that hobbies are good for health.

My husband tried to repair a leak with foil,

which has resulted in a ruined carpet.
