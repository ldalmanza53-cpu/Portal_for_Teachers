Operational Context: The Moral Compass Bureau

Welcome to The Moral Compass Bureau! You are delegates serving on an international ethics tribunal. Your mission is to evaluate challenging modern dilemmas and state what course of action you would take if you were placed in these hypothetical situations.

Grammar Focus Objective: Express hypothetical choices and imaginary consequences using the Present Unreal Conditional (Second Conditional): If + Past Simple, S + would / could / might + base verb.

Tribunal Team Roles (Groups of 2 or 3)

ROLE A

Case Presenter (Strategist)

Presents the assigned dilemma and formulates a hypothetical solution using 2nd conditionals.

  • Selects an outcome scenario from the directory.
  • States their decision using "If I were in this situation, I would..."
  • Provides 2 moral reasons for their hypothetical choice.
Key Phrase: "If this happened to me, I would..."
ROLE B

Defense Advocate (Cross-Examiner)

Tests the Presenter's decision by raising hypothetical counter-arguments and unexpected consequences.

  • Challenges assumptions with 2nd conditional questions.
  • Asks "What would you do if [new twist]...?"
  • Evaluates if the Presenter's choice is morally sound.
Key Phrase: "If someone offered you money, would you..."
ROLE C

Tribunal Chair & Auditor

Manages the 10-minute workshop, checks grammar accuracy, and completes the peer rubric.

  • Controls the 10-minute master timer.
  • Audits 2nd conditional grammar accuracy (If + Past Simple).
  • Submits peer rubric scores and generates the final report.
Key Focus: Audit "If + Past Simple + Would"

Second Conditional Grammar Blueprint (CEFR B1)

Use the Present Unreal Conditional to discuss imaginary situations and hypothetical choices in the present or future.

Condition Clause (If Clause)
If + Subject + Past Simple Verb
e.g., "If I found $1,000 in a bag..." / "If I were you..."
Result Clause (Main Clause)
Subject + would / could / might + Base Verb
e.g., "...I would return it to the police immediately."
B1 GUARDRAIL WARNING
❌ If I would see a thief, I would call police. ✅ If I saw a thief, I would call the police. Never use 'would' inside the 'If' clause! Use Past Simple in the 'If' clause.
