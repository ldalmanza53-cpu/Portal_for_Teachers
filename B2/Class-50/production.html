B1 Grammar Reference: Obligation & Qualities

1. Modals of Obligation & Necessity:

• must be + adjective: "A Game Director must be imaginative."

• is required to have + noun: "An art lead is required to have strong vision."

• needs to possess + skill: "Designers need to possess problem-solving skills."

• should be skilled in + -ing/noun: "They should be skilled in teamwork."

2. Creative Professional Adjectives:

• Mindset: imaginative, adaptable, resilient, visionary, meticulous, curious

• Collaborative: articulate, empathetic, open-minded, diplomatic, communicative
