B1 Expressing Irritation Formula Matrix

1. Irritation Keyphrases

Express strong negative reactions to habits.

"It drives me crazy when..."
"I can't stand it when..."

2. Annoying Continuous Habits

Subject + is/are always/constantly + verb-ing.

"They are always playing loud music at 2 AM."

3. Empathetic Validation

Validate feelings actively as a listener.

"I totally get where you're coming from! That must be so frustrating."

4. Diplomatic Solutions

Offer constructive advice gently.

"Have you tried talking to them?"
"If I were you, I would..."

Critical B1 Guardrail: 'It' in "Can't Stand It When"

In B1 English, when using "I can't stand..." before a clause starting with "when" or "that", remember to include the object pronoun 'it'!

"I can't stand when people chew loudly." (MISSING 'IT')
"I can't stand IT when people chew loudly." (CORRECT!)
