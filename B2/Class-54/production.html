B1 Grammar Reference: Expressing Invention Impact

1. Describing Life Before (Past Habit):

• Before [invention], people used to + verb: "Before the printing press, people used to copy manuscripts by hand."

2. Expressing Change & Impact:

• transformed the way we + verb: "It transformed the way we read and share knowledge."

• allowed / enabled people to + verb: "Refrigeration allowed people to store fresh food for days."

• has made it much easier to + verb: "Smartphones have made it much easier to stay connected."

3. Impact Adjectives:

• revolutionary, life-changing, indispensable, practical, transformative, efficient
