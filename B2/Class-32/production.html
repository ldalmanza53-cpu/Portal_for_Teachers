B1 English Reference: Reported Speech & Topic Summaries

1. Essential B1 Reporting Verbs

  • Mentioned that: "They mentioned that the merger was approved."
  • Explained how: "She explained how the software worked."
  • Discussed whether: "We discussed whether we should hire new staff."
  • Claimed that: "He claimed that his new vehicle was completely silent."
  • Asked if / whether: "They asked if the shuttle service was complimentary."
  • Was talking about: "She was talking about her trip to Madrid."
  • Pointed out that: "The manager pointed out that deadlines were approaching."

2. Standard B1 Tense Shift Guide

Direct: Present Simple ("I work here.")
Reported: Past Simple ("She said she worked there.")
Direct: Present Continuous ("We are leaving.")
Reported: Past Continuous ("They said they were leaving.")
Direct: Present Perfect ("I have finished.")
Reported: Past Perfect ("He said he had finished.")
Direct: Future with Will ("I will call you.")
Reported: Would ("She promised she would call.")

3. Summarizing Conversation Topics

  • "They were having a conversation regarding..."
  • "The main topic of discussion was..."
  • "According to overheard sources..."
  • "They expressed concern over..."
