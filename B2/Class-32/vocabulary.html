Tense Backshifting Reporting Verbs

Direct: "I'm leaving tomorrow."
Reported: She mentioned that she was leaving the following day.

Backshift tenses when reporting past statements (Present Continuous → Past Continuous; tomorrow → the following day). Use verbs like confided, admitted, alleged, divulged.

Questions & Commands Statement Order

Direct: "Did you hear that? Don't tell anyone!"
Reported: He asked if I had heard that and urged me not to tell anyone.

Reported questions use statement word order without auxiliary inversion (if / whether / wh- word). Commands use reporting verb + object + (not) to-infinitive.

Verb

Eavesdrop

Definition (B2/C1)

Secretly listen to a private conversation without the speakers' consent or knowledge.

Reported Speech Context

He overheard them in the corridor and later reported that they had been discussing company cutbacks.

⚡ B2 Grammar Pitfall: Requires the preposition on (e.g., "eavesdrop on someone").
