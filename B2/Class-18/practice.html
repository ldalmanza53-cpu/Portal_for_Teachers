Welcome, Urban Analysts! Today you will learn how to organize complex city descriptions using precise adjective sequences and express contrast using concession linkers.

Learning Objectives (CEFR B1)

Order of Modifiers

Master modifier order: Determiner + Quality + Size + Age + Type + Noun + Descriptive Phrase (e.g., "a picturesque little resort town with year-round outdoor activities").

Concession & Contrast Linkers

Use contrast transitions accurately: however, although, even though, despite, in spite of, on the other hand, nevertheless.

Compound Town Vocabulary

Identify and define 10 town types: border town, coastal town, college town, industrial town, mountain town, port town, resort town, rural town, suburban town, tourist town.

Urban Comparison & Evaluation

Compare city versus town benefits and draft professional city profile reports.

City Search: Contrast & Concession Gap-Fill

Complete the two city descriptions below by selecting the correct concession sentence (a-f) for gaps (1) to (6).

Sentences Options (a-f):
a. However, housing costs are high. A nice apartment is about $2,500 per month.
b. So, even though our streets are safe, the evenings can be dull.
c. But be careful - in spite of all the late-night activity, the crime rate is high.
d. On the other hand, it can sometimes be difficult to find a job.
e. Although it's fast, clean, and cheap, it's pretty crowded during rush hour. Nevertheless, it's becoming one of the most popular ways for people to get to work.
f. Despite the attractive location, the prices of houses are surprisingly reasonable.
1. Exciting Large City

This exciting large city with bustling streets is a great place to live. Most evenings you can choose from a movie, a concert, or even a museum. (1) There are lots of jobs here, and the average salary is about $4,000 per month. (2) Our efficient new subway system can get you anywhere you want to go. (3)

2. Picturesque Resort Town

This is a picturesque little resort town with year-round outdoor activities. There's something to do in all four seasons. But there's not much action here at night. (4) There are many quaint little stores and boutiques in this beautiful town. (5) Apartments are affordable, too. You can get a great place for about $1,000 a month, and average monthly salaries are about $2,500. (6)
