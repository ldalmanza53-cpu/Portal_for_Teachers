📖 Grammatical Reference Guide

1. Modifiers of Degree

Large Difference
significantly, far, considerably, substantially, much
"Tokyo is far more expensive than Hanoi."
Small Difference
slightly, marginally, a bit, a little
"Prague is slightly colder than Vienna."
Negligible / Equal
just as, almost as, nearly as
"Valencia is just as charming as Seville."

2. OSASCOMP Adjective Order

Order Category Examples
1. OOpinionpicturesque, vibrant, charming, chaotic
2. SSizecompact, sprawling, huge, tiny
3. AAgemedieval, modern, ancient, 19th-century
4. SShape / Stylecircular, narrow, high-rise
5. COrigin / ColorEuropean, Asian, Greek, Italian
6. M/PMaterial / Purpose / Typecoastal, financial, historic, industrial

3. Contrast Connectors Taxonomy

Subordinating Conjunctions (whereas, while, although)
Grammar: [Clause 1], whereas [Clause 2].
Transition Adverbs (however, in contrast, on the other hand)
Grammar: [Sentence 1]. In contrast, [Sentence 2].
Prepositional Phrases (despite, in spite of, unlike)
Grammar: Despite [Noun Phrase / Verb-ing], [Main Clause].
