PeakPulse B1 Language Vault

Key frequency, sequence, and bio-wellness structures

1. High-Impact Frequency Adverbs

Position these adverbs before main verbs (or after 'be') to describe consistency:

consistently / without fail

"I consistently get 10 minutes of morning sunlight without fail."

systematically / usually

"She systematically takes a 5-minute break every hour."

2. Sequence & Time Connectors

Use these connectors to transition logically between routine phases:

First of all, ... / As soon as ...

"First of all, drink 500ml of water. As soon as you wake up, avoid checking emails."

During peak focus hours, ... / Prior to going to bed, ...

"During peak focus hours, work in 25-minute blocks. Prior to going to bed, dim room lights."

3. Mind & Body Vocabulary

Mental Clarity & Focus:

deep focus, cognitive fatigue, brain power, alertness, afternoon slump, mindfulness

Physical Awakening & Recovery:

hydration, circadian rhythm, light stretching, digital detox, sleep hygiene, recovery
