Forbidden Taboo Words (Do NOT Say)

Try using these reduced sentence frames in your explanation:

While + -ing:

"While feeling extremely sleepy in the morning, a person might experience this..."

Right after + -ing:

"Right after waking up suddenly, you feel confused and sluggish..."

Before + -ing:

"Before having my morning coffee, I suffer from this state..."

Unreducible Exception (Full Clause Required):

"Ever since I was a kid, I've had trouble getting up early."
