🧬 Science & Strategy Knowledge Base

Neurobiology and physiology mechanisms explaining why these specific protocols unlock peak brain and body performance.

☕ Adenosine Pathway

Caffeine Timing & 90m Delay

Sleepiness is driven by adenosine binding to neural receptors. Delaying coffee 60-90 minutes post-waking allows residual adenosine to clear naturally, preventing afternoon energy crashes.

🌅 Circadian Entrainment

Cortisol Awakening Response (CAR)

Viewing 10-15 minutes of direct morning sunlight stimulates retinal ganglion cells, triggering a healthy cortisol peak, boosting dopamine, and setting your sleep timer for 14 hours later.

🏃 Metabolic Health

Post-Prandial Glucose Regulation

A short 10-15 minute walk immediately after meals activates GLUT4 glucose transporters in skeletal muscle independently of insulin, attenuating blood sugar spikes and fatigue.

🧠 Neural Rhythms

Ultradian Cognitive Work Cycles

Human brain wave activity naturally shifts between peak alertness and fatigue in ~90-minute ultradian cycles. Pushing past 90 minutes without rest triggers cognitive decay.

🧘 Parasympathetic Shift

Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR)

10-20 minutes of NSDR or Yoga Nidra guided relaxation restores striatal dopamine levels, calms the autonomic nervous system, and accelerates neuroplastic learning retention.

🌙 Sleep Architecture

Melatonin Ramp & Thermal Drop

Melatonin secretion requires darkness and a ~1°C drop in core body temperature. Blue spectrum overhead lighting tricks the brain into suppressing melatonin by up to 50%.
