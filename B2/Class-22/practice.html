Key Target Vocabulary Spotlight

Morning person

Someone who feels energetic and works best early in the day.

Late riser

A person who prefers sleeping late and waking up late.

Grouch

A bad-tempered, easily annoyed person (especially right after waking).

Power napper

Someone who takes short daytime naps (5-20 mins) to refresh.

Night owl

A person who prefers staying up late and working at night.

Spurt of energy

A sudden brief increase or burst of physical/mental power.

Grammar Focus 2A

Reduced Time Clauses

Time clauses show when actions happen. If the subject is the same in both clauses of the sentence, time clauses with (right) before, (right) after, and while can be reduced to an -ing phrase!

REDUCIBLE TIME CLAUSES

Allowed with before, after, while (Subject must be identical):

Full Clause:

After I finish my workout, I head to the office.

Reduced Clause:

After finishing my workout, I head to the office.

Full Clause:

While I take my lunch break, I sneak a nap.

Reduced Clause:

While taking my lunch break, I sneak a nap.

Full Clause:

I'm not approachable right after I wake up.

Reduced Clause:

I'm not approachable right after waking up!

NON-REDUCIBLE TIME CLAUSES

Connectors like ever since, as soon as, until, whenever, from the moment usually CANNOT be reduced:

Ever since

Ever since I was a kid, I've had trouble getting up early.

As soon as

As soon as I get up in the morning, I race off to the gym.

Until

Until I've had my coffee, I'm such a grouch.

Whenever

Whenever you have to work with numbers, plan to do it around noon.
