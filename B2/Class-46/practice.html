Grammar Focus

Negative & Tag Questions for Giving Opinions

In English, we use negative questions or tag questions to soft-offer an opinion and politely invite someone to react or agree with us.

Negative Questions

Start with a negative auxiliary verb to open an opinion.

  • Isn't it weird how...
  • Doesn't it seem like...
  • Wouldn't it be great if...
  • Shouldn't the government...
Tag Questions

State an opinion first, then attach a mini-question tag at the end.

  • Apps are cool, aren't they?
  • It makes kids aggressive, doesn't it?
  • They should stop ads, shouldn't they?
"Don't You Think" Phrase

Can be placed at the beginning or at the end of an opinion.

  • Don't you think there are too many websites...?
  • It's actually dangerous, don't you think?
