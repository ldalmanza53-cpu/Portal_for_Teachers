B1 Opinion & Reaction Grammar Guide

1. Expressing Stances & Personal Opinions

At B1 level, use diverse sentence openers instead of repetitive phrases:

  • In my view, [topic] is essential because...
  • From my perspective, [topic] impacts students by...
  • I'd argue that [topic] should be reconsidered...
  • As far as I'm concerned, [topic] offers clear advantages...
  • I strongly believe that [topic] requires strict guidelines...

2. Inviting Reactions from Partners

Keep the debate flowing by explicitly inviting your co-hosts to respond:

  • What's your take on this issue?
  • Don't you think that [topic] creates unnecessary problems?
  • Where do you stand on enforcing this rule?
  • How do you feel about this proposal?

3. Politeness & Counter-Reactions

Acknowledge your partner's view before introducing a polite counter-argument:

  • I see your point, but we must also consider...
  • I'm not so sure about that because...
  • While I agree that [point], I still think that...
