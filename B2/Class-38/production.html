B1 Timeline Audit Performance Self-Assessment

B1 Time References & Events Reference Guide

1. Describing Past Events & Milestones

Use specific past time anchors to show finished retrospectives:

  • ago: Calculated back from present ("launched three months ago").
  • since: Starting point in time ("in operation since 2022").
  • prior to: Before a specific event ("prior to the venue setup").

2. Describing Current Live Operations

Express ongoing activities and simultaneous operational tasks:

  • currently / at present: Ongoing status ("engineers are currently testing sound").
  • while: Two continuous actions happening together ("while staff clean, technicians test lights").
  • during: Time period noun ("during the audit phase").

3. Describing Future Schedules & Target Milestones

Establish deadlines and sequential future milestones:

  • as soon as: Immediate sequence ("as soon as doors open").
  • by next week / by the time: Target completion deadline ("by next week").
  • subsequently / in two months: Follow-up timeline ("subsequently, we publish the report").
