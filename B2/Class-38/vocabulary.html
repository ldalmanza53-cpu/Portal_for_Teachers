Flip Card
/rɪˈseʃ.ən/
Definition

A period of temporary economic decline during which trade and industrial activity are reduced.

Aspectual Context Example

The country has been suffering from a severe recession for over six months, although analysts predicted last quarter that recovery had already begun.

B2 Aspectual Pitfall

Use Present Perfect Continuous (has been suffering) to emphasize the ongoing duration, but switch to Simple Past (predicted) when mentioning specific finished timeframes (last quarter).
