Narrative Tenses & Reference Handbook

1. Core Narrative Tenses

Past Simple

Completed actions in chronological sequence.

"She stepped into the courtroom."

Past Continuous

Ongoing background actions interrupted by main event.

"Crews were working while traffic remained frozen."

Past Perfect Simple

Completed action prior to another past point.

"She had not suspected the coding error."

Past Perfect Continuous

Continuous duration up to a specific past landmark.

"She had been reading for two hours when..."

2. Inverted Narrative Structures

When initiating a sentence with restrictive time adverbs, auxiliary verb subject inversion is required:

Negative Adverb (Hardly/No sooner) + HAD + Subject + Past Participle + CONNECTIVE (when/than) + Past Simple
  • Hardly / Barely / Scarcely pair with WHEN.
  • No sooner pairs with THAN.

3. Pacing & Perspective Markers

Out of the blue: Sudden turning point or interruption.
Prior to that: Establishes an earlier time horizon.
In the interim: Temporary gap action between events.
In retrospect: Reflective glance back from present.
