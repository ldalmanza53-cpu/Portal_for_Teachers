Sleep Science & Idiom Handbook

Quick Reference for Sleep Biology and Key English Expressions.

1. Biological Sleep Drivers

  • Circadian Rhythm: Your body's internal 24-hour clock regulated by light exposure entering the eyes.
  • Adenosine (Sleep Pressure): Chemical that builds up in the brain every hour you are awake. Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors temporarily.
  • Melatonin: The "hormone of darkness" released by the pineal gland to signal the body it is time to wind down. Blue light suppresses melatonin production.

2. Top Sleep Idioms Cheat Sheet

  • Hit the sack: Go to bed. ("I'm exhausted, I'm going to hit the sack.")
  • Toss and turn: Move restlessly in bed without sleeping.
  • Out like a light: Fall asleep instantly.
  • Pull an all-nighter: Stay awake all night to study or work.
  • Bright-eyed and bushy-tailed: Wide awake and full of energy.
