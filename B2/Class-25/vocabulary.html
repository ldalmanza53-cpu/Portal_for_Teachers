be fast asleep

idiomatic phrase
Definition

Sleeping deeply and soundly; completely unconscious in sleep.

Context & Reason/Condition Clause

"Given that he had worked a 12-hour shift, he was fast asleep within minutes of hitting the pillow."

Grammar Pitfall: 'Given that' introduces an established fact as a reason clause. It must be followed by a full clause with a subject and verb.
