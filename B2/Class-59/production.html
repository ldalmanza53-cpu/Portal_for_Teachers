Global Workplace Synergy Consultancy

Welcome, behavioral consultants! International companies and remote co-living hubs hire us to analyze team work habits and personality tendencies. Your goal is to write cohesive behavioral profiles using Typical Behaviors (tends to, has a habit of, as a rule, is inclined to), Contrasts (whereas, while, unlike, on the other hand), and Exceptions (except for, apart from, unless, with the exception of).

📐 Core Behavioral Profile Formula: Typical Habit $\rightarrow$ Contrast $\rightarrow$ Exception Rule

Step 1: Typical Behavior

State a colleague's general workplace tendency using tends to, has a habit of, or as a rule.

"Sarah tends to check emails early in the morning..."
Step 2: Team Contrast

Contrast her habit with teammates using whereas, unlike, or on the other hand.

"...whereas her teammate Alex prefers late night coding sessions..."
Step 3: Exception Rule

Identify specific outliers or rare conditions using except for, unless, or apart from.

"...except for during product launch weeks when everyone works sync."

💡 CEFR B1 Behavioral Language & Structure Toolkit

Master the three core target pillars for analyzing workplace habits: describing typical tendencies, building clear team contrasts, and identifying exceptions!

🔄 1. Describing Typical Behaviors

Habits & Patterns
tends to + verb

Our design lead tends to work in focused 2-hour bursts without checking chats.

has a habit of + gerund

He has a habit of sending voice notes instead of typing lengthy Slack messages.

as a rule / is inclined to

As a rule, our engineering team is inclined to avoid meetings on Fridays.

⚖️ 2. Team Contrasts

Opposing Habits
whereas / while

Elena prefers turn-taking discussions, whereas Marcus speaks spontaneously.

unlike / in contrast to

Unlike extroverted team members, quiet researchers prepare written notes before calls.

on the other hand / however

Some love video calls. On the other hand, remote developers often prefer text updates.

⚠️ 3. Behavioral Exceptions

Rule Outliers
except for / apart from

The team works asynchronously, except for weekly emergency status syncs.

unless / except when

Nobody messages past 6 PM unless a critical server outage occurs.

with the exception of

All co-living residents share kitchen duties, with the exception of night-shift nurses.
