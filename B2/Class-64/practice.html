📐 Relative Clauses vs. Noun Clauses

Master the structural difference between describing PEOPLE/THINGS (Relative Clauses) vs. SITUATIONS/WHEN (Noun Clauses).

👤 Structure 1: Relative Clauses

A Relative Clause modifies a specific subject or object noun using pronouns like who / that.

Something (that) bugs me is people who take up two seats.
• that bugs me = relative clause describing "something"
• who take up two seats = relative clause describing "people"
⏰ Structure 2: Noun Clauses

Some sentences use a relative clause combined with a Noun Clause starting with a question word like when.

The thing (that) I hate is when kids ride scooters on the sidewalk.
• when kids ride scooters... = noun clause acting as predicate noun describing the scenario!
