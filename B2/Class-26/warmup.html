📖 Oneironautics & Dream Science Handbook

1. Why Do We Dream?

Modern neuroscience shows that dreams during REM sleep process intense waking emotions, consolidate memory, and stress-test subconscious anxieties.

2. Decoding Symbolism

A dream symbol rarely has a universal meaning; its true interpretation depends on the dominant emotion felt during the dream and where that emotion occurs in waking life.

3. Key Analysis Questions

  • What was the primary emotion (e.g., terror, embarrassment, freedom)?
  • Where in waking life do you currently feel that same emotion?
  • What situation or decision feels unresolved right now?
