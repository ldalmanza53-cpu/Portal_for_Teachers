REASON CLAUSES Cause & Consequence

Given that, Seeing that, Now that, Considering that

Used to state established facts or newly changed realities behind a mental state or action.

CONDITION CLAUSES Prerequisites & Precautions

Provided that, As long as, Unless, In case, On condition that

Used to state strict prerequisites, warnings, or precautionary measures.

Lucid Dream

Noun /ˈluː.sɪd driːm/
DEFINITION

A dream during which the sleeper is aware that they are dreaming and can often exert control over dream events.

EXAMPLE (REASON/CONDITION CLAUSE)

"Given that she realized it was a lucid dream, she was able to alter the nightmare's outcome."

⚠️
B2 Grammar Note: "Given that" is followed by a subject + verb clause, not just a plain noun.
