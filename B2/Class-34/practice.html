Grammar Structure Breakdown & Patterns

1. Verb + (that) Clause

Used for reporting statements and explanations.

"He claimed that he had lost his wallet."

2. Verb + Object + (not) To-Infinitive

Used for commands, advice, warnings, and requests.

"She advised me to stay calm."

3. Verb + If / Wh- Clause

Used for indirect reported questions and inquiries.

"He wanted to know where I was going."
