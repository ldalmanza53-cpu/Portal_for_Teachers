B1 Speaking & Grammar Rubric Checklist:

B1 Grammar Reference: Reported Speech Rules

1. Tense Backshifting Rules (Direct ➔ Reported)

Present Simple ➔ Past Simple
"I work here." ➔ She said she worked there.
Present Continuous ➔ Past Continuous
"We are fixing roads." ➔ He said they were fixing roads.
Will ➔ Would
"I will finish tomorrow." ➔ She said she would finish the next day.
Can ➔ Could
"We can help you." ➔ They said they could help us.

2. Reported Questions & Commands

  • Yes/No Questions (asked if / asked whether): "Do you plan to increase taxes?" ➔ The reporter asked if/whether they planned to increase taxes.
  • Wh- Questions (keep question word + statement order): "Where is the chief?" ➔ He asked where the chief was.
  • Commands & Requests (ordered/asked/told + object + to-infinitive): "Clear the room!" ➔ The officer ordered them to clear the room.

3. Time & Place Expressions Reference Table

now ➔ then
today ➔ that day
tomorrow ➔ the next day
here ➔ there
