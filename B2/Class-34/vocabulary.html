Verb Patterns Infinitives vs. Clauses
Verb + Object + To-Infinitive:
advised me to, warned me not to, encouraged me to, urged me to
Verb + That-Clause:
claimed that, explained that, insisted that, promised that

Select the reporting verb structure based on whether you are advising an action or making a statement.

Questions & Commands Statement Word Order
Indirect Questions:
wanted to know if / whether, wondered where / why...
Reported Imperatives:
warned me not to..., promised to..., requested that...

Indirect questions follow regular statement word order (Subject + Verb) without auxiliary inversion (do/does/did).

Claimed that

Pattern & Meaning (B2)

Asserted something as true without presenting immediate definitive proof.

Reported Speech Syntax

She claimed that her car was being repaired at the garage.

⚡ B2 Grammar Pitfall: Claimed that + clause takes a subject and backshifted verb, NOT an object pronoun (Do not say claimed me that).
