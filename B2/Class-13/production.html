1. Opinion Certainty Scale

Stance Conviction
High Certainty 90 – 100%

Unshakeable conviction & absolute stance

  • "I am absolutely convinced that..."
  • "Without a doubt, the council must..."
  • "I firmly maintain that..."
Moderate Certainty 60 – 80%

Strong belief with balanced openness

  • "It seems to me that we should..."
  • "I strongly believe that governments..."
  • "In my view, this measure ought to..."
Tentative Certainty 30 – 50%

Cautious hypothesis & initial impression

  • "I suspect that this policy might..."
  • "I'm not entirely sure, but..."
  • "As far as I can tell, companies could..."
