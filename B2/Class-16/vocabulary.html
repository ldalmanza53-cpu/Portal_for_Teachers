Sprawling

Urban Description & Mind Mapping

Click to view IPA, Meaning & Context
/ˈsprɔː.lɪŋ/
B2/C1 Definition

Spreading over a large area in an untidy or irregular way.

Example Sentence

The sprawling city requires a detailed mind map to categorize its diverse suburbs and transit hubs.

⚠️ Learner Usage Note

Don't confuse 'sprawling' with 'crawling'; 'sprawling' refers to geographic expansion.
