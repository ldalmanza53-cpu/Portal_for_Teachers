Reference Guide

Classification Strategy Reference Handbook

The 5 Core Travel Drivers

🏛️ 1. Historical & Culture

Monuments, ancient architecture, world heritage sites, art museums, and local traditions.

🍜 2. Gastronomy & Culinary

Local specialties, street markets, famous dining districts, regional drinks, and food tours.

🌿 3. Atmosphere & Lifestyle

Safety, walkability, welcoming locals, neighborhood charm, parks, and overall city pace.

🎭 4. Entertainment & Events

Live music, theater shows, vibrant nightlife, shopping districts, and seasonal festivals.

🚆 5. Convenience & Accessibility

Reliable transit/subways, English signage, easy airport links, and proximity to day trips.

The 4-Step Writing Strategy

  1. Raw Brainstorm Dump: Write down all thoughts without filtering or worrying about order.
  2. Bucket Categorization: Sort items into the 5 core travel drivers to prevent jumping randomly between topics.
  3. Prioritization: Select the top 1-2 most persuasive points per bucket and eliminate weak details.
  4. Structured Outline & Draft: Connect categories using B1-B2 transitional linkers (*From a culinary perspective...*, *In terms of accessibility...*).
