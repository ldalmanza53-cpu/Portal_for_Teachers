Part 5B

Cuzco Paragraph Analyzer

Click on the underlined phrases in the paragraph that correspond to the ideas from the Cuzco mind map.

Cuzco has something for everyone. It's one of the oldest cities in the Americas, and it was once the capital of the Inca empire. Today, Cuzco is Peru's tourist capital because of its unique mix of history and culture. People who are interested in architecture will love the nearby Inca ruins of Machu Picchu and the palace of Inca Roca. Cuzco has many places to stay, which range from first-class hotels to cozy inns. There are also many cafés and restaurants where you can eat delicious local dishes or international cuisine. Also, Cuzco has great markets where you can shop for local arts and crafts. When you visit Cuzco, you should try to experience all it has to offer.

B1 Relative Clause Blueprint

  • People who... "People who are interested in architecture..."
  • Which range from... "...places to stay, which range from first-class hotels..."
  • Where you can... "...restaurants where you can eat...", "...markets where you can shop..."
