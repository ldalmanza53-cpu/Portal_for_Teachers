Past Obligations & Regrets Grammar Matrix

Master the exact structures needed to explain past expectations, forced actions, and regrets.

1. Past Expectation

was / were supposed to

Used for past plans, rules, or obligations that did not happen as intended.

"We were supposed to print 50 copies."

2. Past Necessity / Force

had to / didn't have to

Used for actions forced by circumstances, or actions that were unnecessary.

"Because of rain, we had to move indoors."

3. Past Regret / Critique

should have + V3

Used to evaluate a past mistake and state the ideal action that was missed.

"I should have verified the reservation."

Essential Incident & Breakdown Vocabulary:

Oversight: An unintentional failure to notice something. Miscommunication: Failure to express ideas clearly. Bottleneck: A point of congestion in a system. Lapse in judgment: A temporary failure to act sensibly. Protocol: The official procedure or system of rules.
