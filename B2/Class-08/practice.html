Welcome to The Life Lessons Bureau

Everyone makes mistakes—from impulse shopping to breaking dorm rules or missing important deadlines. As B1 Life Lessons Specialists, your job today is to analyze real-life regrets, reflect on past obligations, and give constructive advice using past modals and phrasal modals.

Regrets & Recommendations: should have / shouldn't have + V3
Rules & Expectations: was/wasn't supposed to + V1
Necessity & Freedom: had to / didn't have to / needed to
