B1 Writing Skill Focus

Why and When Do We Start a New Paragraph?

A paragraph is a group of sentences focused on **one main idea**. Starting a new paragraph gives your reader a breath and shows that your thoughts are moving to a new point!

💡

The TiPToP Rule (When to Split)

Start a new paragraph whenever one of these changes occurs:

Ti

Time changes

Moving to a different period or stage (e.g., *In childhood... Later in life...*)

P

Place changes

Shifting to a different location or setting.

To

Topic or Aspect changes

Discussing a new characteristic (e.g., *Curiosity vs. Risk-taking*).

P

Person / Viewpoint changes

Introducing another perspective or contrasting opinion.

🏗️

B1 Paragraph Anatomy

Every strong paragraph follows a clear 3-part layout:

1. Topic Sentence + Transition Starter

The opening line. Uses a transition word to state the main idea of this paragraph.

2. Supporting Details & Explanation

2–3 sentences that explain *why* or *how* this characteristic works.

3. Example or Micro-Conclusion

A real-world example or short summary sentence linking back to creativity.

🌟 B1 Model Answer

Creativity is one of the most vital qualities in modern society. Creative people not only produce art and music, but they also find innovative solutions to complex daily problems.

To start with, one key characteristic of creative people is intense curiosity. They are naturally open-minded and always ask questions about how things work around them. This endless desire to learn allows them to generate original ideas and make connections that others might miss.

In addition to curiosity, another crucial trait is strong persistence. Creative individuals are not afraid of failure; instead, they view mistakes as valuable opportunities to brainstorm new approaches. They remain persistent even when facing difficult obstacles.

To sum up, creativity is not just a talent you are born with, but a mindset built on curiosity and hard work. By staying open-minded and willing to take risks, anyone can unlock their full creative potential.
