B1 Grammar Guide: Beginning New Paragraphs

When starting a new paragraph, use strong transition openers to signpost the transition from one sub-topic to another.

Paragraph Openers Formula
  • • Adding a Point: "Another key characteristic is..."
  • • Changing Sub-topics: "Turning to the topic of..."
  • • Adding Evidence: "Furthermore, creative thinkers..."
  • • Addressing Obstacles: "Moving on to how they handle failure..."
  • • Concluding Idea: "Ultimately, what sets them apart is..."
