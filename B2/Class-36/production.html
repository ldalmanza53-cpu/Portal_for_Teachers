B1 Newsroom Grammar Reference Guide

1. Passive Voice in Journalistic Writing

Use passive voice to emphasize the event or discovery rather than who performed it.

"was announced by..."
"The zero-carbon grid was announced yesterday by lead researchers."
"were discovered in..."
"Ancient stone towers were discovered in the deep Mariana Trench."

2. Past Continuous for Simultaneous Background Actions

  • While [Subject] were -ing...: "While scientists were testing the chamber, energy production set a new record."
  • As [Subject] were -ing...: "As delegates were assembling in Tokyo, UN ambassadors opened the summit."

3. Reporting Verbs for Formal News Statements

"confirmed that..." / "stated that..."
"Lead oceanographers confirmed that high-resolution sonar revealed symmetrical structures."
"reported that..." / "explained how..."
"City officials reported that over two million residents will receive clean power."
