Flip for Context & Past Time Contrast
/ˈwɪsəlˌbloʊər/
Definition

A person who informs on a person or organization engaged in illicit activity.

Past Time Syntax Contrast

Present Perfect: An anonymous whistleblower has leaked classified documents. (Unspecified past time / ongoing news impact)

Simple Past: The whistleblower spoke to federal investigators yesterday morning. (Specific past time marker)

B2 Grammar Pitfall

Do not use specific past time adverbs (yesterday, in 2021) with Present Perfect.

Forbidden Taboo Words

Use these structures when describing news concepts to enforce accurate B2 syntax:

Present Perfect Reporting: "According to recent reports, journalists have uncovered evidence that..."
Simple Past Specification: "When the story broke last week, officials refused to comment."
Indefinite Past (Present Result): "Media outlets have long argued that transparency is essential."
Retraction/Correction Narrative: "Although the paper has retracted the claim, damage occurred yesterday."
