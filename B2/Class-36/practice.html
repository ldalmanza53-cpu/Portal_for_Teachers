Language Focus • Gerunds, Infinitives & Softeners

Etiquette Language & Softening Expressions

Master key B1 English grammar structures used to discuss social etiquette: Gerunds as Subjects ("Talking loudly is..."), It's + Adjective + Infinitive ("It's rude to..."), and Polite Softening Phrases.

Pattern A: Gerund as Subject

Verb + -ING + Verb

[ Verb-ing ] + is + [ Adjective / Impolite ]

• Interrupting someone is considered impolite in many countries.

• Talking loudly on a cell phone bothers other people.

• Making small talk helps break the ice with strangers.

Pattern B: It's + Adj + Infinitive

It's + Adj + to + Verb

It's + [ impolite / rude / acceptable ] + to + [ Verb ]

• It's rude to videotape someone without asking permission.

• It's acceptable to ask for help with homework.

• It's weird to sit next to someone when empty seats are available.
