Context: The Global Youth Ethics Advisory Council

You are serving as an ethical advisor for the Global Youth Ethics Council. Everyday citizens, students, and employees submit real-life moral dilemmas involving personal relationships, academic honesty, digital privacy, commercial choices, and community duty. Your mission is to analyze these dilemmas, evaluate them from a value perspective, debate solutions, and deliver an ethical verdict.

Council Delegate Roles (Groups of 2-3)
Role A
Dilemma Presenter

Presents the dilemma clearly, takes an explicit ethical stance, and justifies their choice using core value vocabulary.

  • Explains who is involved and what is at stake.
  • Uses 2nd conditionals: "If I were in this position, I would..."
  • Identifies key values: honesty, loyalty, fairness.
Role B
Value Cross-Examiner

Challenges assumptions, explores alternate consequences, and probes with hypothetical counter-questions.

  • Asks probing counter-questions: "What if...?"
  • Points out conflicting values: "Doesn't that compromise privacy?"
  • Explores consequences: "If everyone did that, what would happen?"
Role C
Council Chair & Auditor

Ensures balanced participation, tracks target B1 grammar usage, and synthesizes the final council verdict.

  • Enforces time limits across debate phases.
  • Tracks target B1 vocabulary and grammar structures.
  • Delivers the final verdict summary and fills the rubric.
