Consequentialist & Outcome-Based Ethics

Evaluating decisions strictly by net human flourishing and suffering reduction

1. The Principle of Utility: Actions are morally right in proportion as they tend to promote happiness, and wrong as they tend to produce the reverse of happiness. Intention matters less than observable net real-world impact.

2. Aggregation across Stakeholders: Utility calculates the total welfare of all affected sentient beings. No single individual's suffering or comfort carries privileged weight over another's.

3. Quality-Adjusted Life-Years (QALYs): In public health and policy, outcome analysis uses standardized metrics like QALYs to balance longevity, severity of suffering, and probability of recovery.
