📖 B1 Grammar Cheat Sheet

1. Past Habits & States: "Used to"

Use used to + verb (base form) to talk about past habits or long-term situations that are no longer true today.

Example: People used to write physical letters, but now we send instant messages.

2. Modern Transformation: Present Perfect

Use has / have + Past Participle to emphasize past actions with strong present results.

Example: Smartphones have transformed how humans work and socialize.

3. Cause & Effect Connectors

Link your ideas together logically with B1 cause-and-effect transitions.

  • As a result: High-speed internet expanded. As a result, remote work became normal.
  • Led to: Constant connectivity has led to higher stress levels.
  • Due to: Due to AI developments, automation is growing fast.
