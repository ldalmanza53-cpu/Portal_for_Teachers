B1 Vocabulary Bank

Essential Tech & Lifestyle Terms

Revolutionize (v.)

To completely change the way something is done.

"High-speed internet has revolutionized education."

Obsolete (adj.)

No longer used because something newer exists.

"Printed encyclopedias have become nearly obsolete."

Indispensable (adj.)

Too important or useful to be without.

"GPS apps have become indispensable for drivers."

Automate (v.)

To make a process operate using technology with minimal human control.

"AI tools have automated basic customer support."

Interconnected (adj.)

Connected with each other across global networks.

"The world has become far more interconnected."

Target B1 Grammar Formulas: Contrasting Past & Present

Master these three core sentence structures during your studio talk show.

1. Used to (Past Habit) Past

Subject + used to + Verb (base)

"People used to buy printed street maps before GPS apps."

2. Present Perfect (Recent Change) Present

Subject + has/have + Participle

"Online shopping has transformed how families buy goods."

3. Noun Clause / Opinion Frame Opinion

Don't you agree that + Clause?

"Don't you agree that AI has simplified research?"
