Grammar Focus • Passive Voice for Inventions

Inventions & Passive Structure Explorer

Inventions are often described using the passive voice (Past Passive & Present Passive) because the action and object are more important than the inventor.

Simple Past Passive

Subject + was / were + Past Participle

"Indoor plumbing was brought inside homes in the late 19th century."

Present Passive Continuous

Subject + is / are being + Past Participle

"Barcodes are being scanned in millions of supermarkets every minute."

Present Perfect Passive

Subject + has / have been + Past Participle

"Personal checks have been replaced by credit cards and digital apps."

Interactive Passive Sentence Builder

Shipping containers were invented to simplify daily human labor.
