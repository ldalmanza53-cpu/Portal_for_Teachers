📖 StoryCraft Narrative & Journalism Handbook

1. The Inverted Pyramid (News & Formal Event Reporting)

  • The Lead (5 Ws): Who, what, where, when, and why placed right in the opening sentence.
  • Key Supporting Details: Eyewitness accounts, official statements, and chronological developments.
  • Background Context: Historical details, related precedents, and ongoing implications.

2. The STAR Narrative Arc (Personal Stories & Anecdotes)

• Situation & Setting: Establish time, location, and initial mood (*"I was sitting in the station lobby..."*).

• Conflict / Inciting Incident: Introduce the unexpected disruption (*"All of a sudden, the power failed..."*).

• Action: Explain the steps taken to overcome the hurdle (*"I teamed up with two strangers..."*).

• Resolution & Reflection: Share the ultimate outcome and personal takeaway (*"In retrospect, it taught me resilience..."*).

3. Narrative Tenses Matrix

• Past Continuous: Ongoing background actions (*"Rain was pouring down..."*).

• Past Simple: Sequential completed events (*"...when the alarm sounded."*).

• Past Perfect Simple: Action completed prior to a past point (*"I realized I had left my wallet..."*).

• Past Perfect Continuous: Ongoing duration leading up to a past event (*"We had been waiting for two hours..."*).

4. Pacing & Drama Transitions

Out of the blue (Sudden event)
To make matters worse (Complication)
In the blink of an eye (Rapid speed)
In retrospect (Looking back)
