B1 Narrative Grammar Cheat Sheet

1. Past Simple (Main Actions)

Used for completed single events that happened sequentially in the story.

Example: "We arrived at the station and bought our tickets."

2. Past Continuous (Background & Interrupted Actions)

Used for continuous actions in progress when a sudden event happened.

Example: "While I was walking home, it started to rain."

3. Past Perfect (Earlier Past)

Used for an action that occurred BEFORE another past action.

Example: "When we arrived, the bus had already left."

4. Narrative Linkers & Connectors

  • While / When: Connect background action with single incident.
  • Suddenly / Unexpectedly: Introduce turning point or surprise climax.
  • After that / As soon as: Sequence consecutive events.
  • Eventually / In the end: Conclude narrative resolution.
