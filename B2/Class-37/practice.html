Grammar Spotlight • Section 2A

Present Perfect vs. Simple Past

Learn how journalists switch between reporting recent headlines without specific times (Present Perfect) and reporting specific past details (Simple Past).

Present Perfect

Have / Has + Past Participle

📌 Use to report a recent event without giving a specific time reference:

  • • "I've locked my keys in the car."
  • • "Maggie has created dozens of paintings."
  • • "The police have been called to a surfing contest."

Key Time Clues: so far, recently, already, ever, never, lately, just

Simple Past

Verb-ed / Irregular Past

📌 Use to report a completed event with a specific time reference:

  • • "After a day at the mall, the woman went out to her car."
  • • "I started teaching her to paint last year."
  • • "Last night at 1:00 A.M., the alarm went off."

Key Time Clues: yesterday, last year, at 1:00 A.M., in 2022, 5 minutes ago
