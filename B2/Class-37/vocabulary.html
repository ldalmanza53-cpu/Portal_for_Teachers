Epidemic

Click to reveal definition & aspectual syntax rules

Flip for Context & Aspectual Contrast
/ˌep.əˈdem.ɪk/
Definition

A widespread occurrence of an infectious disease in a community at a particular time.

Aspectual Syntax Contrast

Continuous Aspect: Epidemiologists have been monitoring the viral epidemic for months. (Ongoing duration)

Simple Aspect: Health officials have identified three major hot zones during this epidemic. (Completed count / result)

B2 Aspectual Pitfall

Use Present Perfect Continuous for uninterrupted activities, but Present Perfect Simple when stating completed quantities or specific numerical results.
