B1 Tech Review Writing Formula Guide

1. Product Title & Opinion Starters

Begin by naming the IT tool and expressing your perspective.

Example: "In my opinion, NovaAI Assistant 3.0 is a groundbreaking tool..."

2. Key Features & Usability Adjectives

Highlight positive user experience, speed, and design.

Example: "It features a user-friendly UI and lightning-fast response speed."

3. Contrast, Drawbacks & Privacy

Balance your review by addressing limitations or privacy risks.

Example: "While it offers great features, on the downside, it is slightly overpriced."

4. Star Rating & Final Recommendation

Conclude with target audience advice and overall rating.

Example: "I would highly recommend this app to students. Overall verdict: 4 out of 5 stars."
