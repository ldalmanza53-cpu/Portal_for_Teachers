/ˈek.oʊ ˌtʃeɪm.bɚ/
Definition

An environment where a person only encounters information or opinions that reflect and reinforce their own.

Review & Discourse Context

"Social media algorithms often trap users in an echo chamber, severely limiting exposure to diverse perspectives."

B2 Register & Usage Pitfall

'In an echo chamber' is the standard prepositional collocation. Avoid confusing with 'filter bubble', which refers specifically to automated content curation.
