Essay Architecture

The 3-Step Essay Conclusion Formula & Trap Inspector

A conclusion should never merely copy the thesis statement or introduce brand-new arguments. Learn the 3-step formula and inspect common B1/B2 writing traps side-by-side.

1

Restate Thesis (Inverted)

Reaffirm your main argument using fresh vocabulary and inverted sentence structures. Avoid copying word-for-word from the introduction.

Key Starters: "Ultimately,", "Taking everything into account,", "In the final analysis,"
2

Synthesize Main Points

Merge your body paragraph arguments into a single cohesive summary. Connect the hard realities with the positive growth.

Linkers: "While... nevertheless", "Although initial struggles occur, ", "Despite language barriers, "
3

Memorable Final Thought

Leave the reader with a broader philosophical takeaway, future outlook, or call to action regarding international mobility.

Key Focus: Broader impact, personal transformation, or cultural wisdom.

Identified Traps: Cliché starter ("In a nutshell"), informal tone, lacks synthesis.
Write repair here: ____________________________________________________________________________________
