Part 1: The Transition

Concluding Connectors

Signal to the reader that you are summarizing your final thoughts.

Target B1 Connectors:
In conclusion To sum up Overall All in all On balance

Example:

"To sum up, moving to a new country brings both unexpected obstacles and tremendous joy."

Part 2: The Balance

Hardships vs. Joys Summary

Contrast the struggles (culture shock) with the advantages (growth).

Contrast Markers:
Although... While... Even though... On one hand... on the other

Example:

"Although language barriers create initial anxiety, the personal independence gained is life-changing."

Part 3: Final Advice

Recommendation / Thought

Give a final piece of advice or recommendation on how to adjust.

Advice Modals & Phrases:
Therefore, it is essential to... Expats should... It is worth...

Example:

"Therefore, new expats should stay patient and open-minded to fully enjoy their journey."
