Essential B1/B2 English Grammar

Expressing Unreal Present Situations

We use I wish + Past Simple / Could / Were to talk about things in the present that we want to be different, but are impossible or untrue right now.

Reality ➔ Wish Transformation Matrix

Notice how the verb tense moves one step back into the past, and the polarity flips!

⚡ Core Rule: Change Positive ↔ Negative
Category / Target Present Reality (Fact) Wish Expression (Imaginary) Listen
Possession / Having "I don't have a car." "I wish I had a car."
Ability / Can "I can't speak French." "I wish I could speak French."
State / Being (Subjunctive) "I am not rich." "I wish I were rich." (or "was")
Obligation / Habit "I have to work today." "I wish I didn't have to work today."
Weather / Environment "It is raining." "I wish it weren't raining."
1

The Polarity Switch Rule

If the reality is negative ("I don't live in Japan"), your wish is positive ("I wish I lived in Japan"). If reality is positive, the wish is negative.

2

The Subjunctive "Were"

In formal/B2 written English, use were for all pronouns: "I wish I were...", "I wish he were...". In everyday informal conversation, "was" is also heard.

3

Ability with "Could"

When wishing for present abilities, change can't ➔ could: "I can't play piano" ➔ "I wish I could play piano." Never use "can" after wish!
