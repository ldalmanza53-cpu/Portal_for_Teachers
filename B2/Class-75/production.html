Welcome to The Reality Innovation Lab

You are delegates at a global ideation forum. Your task is to propose hypothetical realities you wish were true today—such as instant skill acquisition, zero pollution, or universal peace—and explore how society would change if these wishes came true.

10 Minutes Total 3 Studio Roles CEFR B1 Grammar Focus
🌟 Role A The Wisher

Reality Visionary

Presents hypothetical realities they wish existed using 'I wish + past simple/were/could' and describes how daily life would change.

Key Formulas:

  • "I wish organic food were free..."
  • "I wish everyone could speak all languages..."
  • "If only cities had no traffic!"
🔍 Role B The Evaluator

Impact Analyst

Probes deeper into the hypothetical wish using second conditional questions to examine potential benefits and challenges.

Key Questions to Ask:

  • "If this wish were true, how would people react?"
  • "What would happen to existing jobs?"
  • "Do you think society would become safer?"
📝 Role C The Timekeeper

Innovation Auditor

Monitors the 10-minute session timer, tracks target wish grammar, flags guardrail errors (like using present tenses after wish), and completes the peer rubric.

Audit Checklist:

  • Did Wisher use past simple/were/could?
  • Did Analyst use 2nd conditional ('would')?
  • Flagged any 'wish + is/have/can' errors?

Present Wish

wish + Past Simple

To express a wish about a present situation that you want to be different.

Subject + wish + Past Simple

"I wish cities had no air pollution."

State / Being

wish + Were

In formal B1 speech/writing, use 'were' for all subjects (I/he/she/it).

Subject + wish + WERE

"I wish fresh food were free for everyone."

Ability / Freedom

wish + Could

To express a desire for an ability or possibility that is currently impossible.

Subject + wish + COULD + V1

"I wish I could teleport to work instantly."

Strong Emotion

If only...

Adds dramatic emphasis to present or hypothetical wishes.

If only + Past / Were / Could

"If only clean energy were unlimited!"
