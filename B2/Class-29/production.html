SocialSphere Agency Context Briefing

Mission & B1 Learning Objectives

Welcome to SocialSphere Event Agency! You are preparing the guest strategy for an international networking gala. Your mission is to analyze guest archetypes and build clear B1 relative clause profile descriptions.

🎯 Target B1 Grammar Formula:
[Archetype] + [is a person / guest] + [WHO / THAT / WHICH / WHERE] + [Verb Phrase]
  • Who / That: For describing people ("is someone who loves starting stories...")
  • Which / That: For describing habits or styles ("has a style which puts people at ease...")
  • Where: For describing preferred event spots ("prefers quiet areas where deep talks happen...")
  • Whose: For describing possessions/qualities ("a guest whose ideas inspire others...")

📖 B1 Grammar Cheat Sheet & Social Vocabulary

Defining Relative Clauses & Networking Phrases

Defining Relative Clauses Guide

WHO / THAT (People)

"A Networker is a person who connects people easily."

WHICH / THAT (Things/Styles)

"They have a communication style which engages everyone."

WHERE (Places/Locations)

"They prefer the lounge area where they can chat comfortably."

WHOSE (Possession)

"An Active Listener is a guest whose focus is on others."
