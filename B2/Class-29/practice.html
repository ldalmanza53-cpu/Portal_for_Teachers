Grammar Masterclass • Unit T-37 Exercise 2

Infinitive and Gerund Phrases for Social Behavior

Learn how to comment on behavior using two interchangeable B1 structures: starting with It + be + Adjective or starting with a Gerund Phrase (-ing) as the subject.

Grammar Rule Matrix & Sentence Equivalence

Pattern A: It's + [adjective/noun] + [infinitive]

Used to comment on general social behavior. Can include "is considered" or "for + person".

  • • It's rude to ignore your partner.
  • • It's a good idea to try out different topics.
  • • It's considered impolite to interrupt people.
  • • It's customary for the complimenter to say nice things.
Pattern B: [Gerund Phrase (-ing)] + is + [adjective/noun]

Restates the sentence by turning the action verb into a gerund subject at the beginning.

  • • Ignoring your partner is rude.
  • • Trying out different topics is a good idea.
  • • Interrupting people is considered impolite.
  • • Saying nice things about others is customary for the complimenter.
