Infinitive Phrases Dummy 'It' + Infinitive

It is + [Adjective] + to + [Verb] ...

Used to state social norms or evaluate behavior objectively (e.g., "It is considered churlish to decline...").

Gerund Phrases Gerund as Subject

[Gerund Phrase] + [Singular Verb] ...

Used when the action itself is the topic of discussion (e.g., "Addressing peers condescendingly disrupts teamwork").

Decorum

Definition (B2/C1)

Behavior in keeping with good taste, propriety, and social etiquette.

Contextual Example & Syntax

Observing proper decorum in formal diplomatic meetings is essential for maintaining mutual respect.

⚡ Grammar Tip: When a gerund phrase acts as the subject, it always takes a singular verb ("Observing proper decorum is...").
