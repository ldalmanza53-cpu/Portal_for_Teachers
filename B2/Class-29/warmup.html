Social Reference Handbook

Core archetypes, descriptive adjectives, and impression frames

Positive Traits

  • Approachable: Warm and easy to talk to.
  • Charismatic: Magnetically charming.
  • Gregarious: Fond of company and crowds.
  • Articulate: Expressive and fluent.

Neutral / Situational

  • Reserved: Quiet by choice.
  • Outspoken: Direct and candid.
  • Aloof: Distant or detached.
  • Introspective: Reflective & thoughtful.

Challenging Traits

  • Obnoxious: Unpleasant and loud.
  • Pretentious: Exaggerating importance.
  • Condescending: Patronizing superiority.
  • Overbearing: Domineering behavior.

"He has a habit of checking his watch whenever someone else speaks."
