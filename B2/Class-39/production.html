B1 Podcast Teaser Performance Self-Assessment

B1 Adverbs of Time Reference Guide

1. Initial Actions & Beginning Sequences

Set up the opening situation before main events unfold:

  • initially / first: At the starting point ("Initially, the museum seemed empty.").
  • previously / beforehand: Before a specific point in the story ("Beforehand, they had checked the door locks.").
  • early on: In the beginning stage ("Early on, we noticed odd footprints.").

2. Plot Twists & Parallel Actions

Create suspense or describe simultaneous events:

  • suddenly / unexpectedly: Rapid surprise change ("Suddenly, the lights flickered out.").
  • meanwhile: Action happening at the exact same time elsewhere ("Meanwhile, police approached the gate.").
  • simultaneously / instantly: Exactly together or right away ("Instantly, the alarm sounded.").

3. Climax Progression & Final Resolutions

Show how the story concludes or resolves:

  • afterwards / later on: Next stage in time ("Afterwards, they examined the strange key.").
  • eventually: After some delay or effort ("Eventually, they unlocked the hidden door.").
  • in the end / ultimately: The final result ("In the end, the secret was revealed.").
