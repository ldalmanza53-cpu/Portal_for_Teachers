Adverbs with Simple Past & Past Perfect

Master narrative time ordering! Use Past Perfect (had + past participle) for prior events or states before a past moment, and Simple Past for simultaneous or subsequent actions.

1. Prior Time / State

Past Perfect

Target Time Adverbs:

Up until then Before that Until that time

"Up until then, I had never had anything stolen."

"Before that, she had lived with her parents."

⚡ Key Rule: Events/states that happened BEFORE a reference point in the past require Past Perfect (had + V3).

2. Same Time

Simple Past

Target Time Adverbs:

When As soon as The moment

"As soon as I moved in, I threw a party."

"The moment it happened, I called the police."

⚡ Key Rule: Actions that happened at the same time or immediately after use Simple Past.

3. Later Time

Simple Past

Target Time Adverbs:

Afterwards Later The next day

"Afterwards, I practiced driving every day."

"The next day, he spilled coffee on my laptop."

⚡ Key Rule: Events that occurred later in time continue the narrative using Simple Past.
