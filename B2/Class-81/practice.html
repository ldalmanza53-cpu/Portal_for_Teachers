Reading • Page T-103 Section 14

Get Yourself LOST (by Arthur Frommer)

Discover the benefits of exploring a new city on foot without a rigid itinerary.

Travelers to a new city are often encouraged to take a bus tour. The thinking is that they can later return to the places that captivated them... but that's nonsense! What you see from the inside of a fast-moving bus is sanitized and unreal, utterly removed from the authentic sights, sounds, and smells outside.

The best way to experience any destination is by foot, without an itinerary, wandering where your spirit leads you. Even in the largest cities, the savvy traveler plunges into the very center of town and walks down the nearest street, experiencing the actual life of the people.

Here are a few ways to enhance your travels:

  • Know before you go: Learn about history and culture before departing.
  • Move around like a local: Use local subways, trains, and buses.
  • Check bulletin boards: Find free lectures, concerts, and workshops.
  • Take a walking tour: Book inexpensive foot tours conducted by dynamic residents.
