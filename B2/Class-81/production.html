The Travel Advisory & Experience Desk

Welcome travelers and advisors! In this 10-minute interactive workshop, your squad will analyze travel experiences and share travel tips using Mixed Conditionals. Learn to connect Past Causes → Present Results (e.g., If I had booked earlier, I wouldn't be standing in line now) and Present Traits → Past Outcomes (e.g., If I spoke Italian, I would have understood the announcement yesterday).

Pattern 1: Past Cause → Present Result

If + Past Perfect, ... would + Base Verb (Now)

Connects an unreal or modified past action to a current present result.

Formula: If + had / hadn't + V3, ... would / wouldn't + base verb (now/today)

Travel Examples:

"If I had booked the express train earlier, I wouldn't be stuck in traffic right now."

"If we had packed rain jackets, we would feel dry today."

Pattern 2: Present Trait → Past Outcome

If + Past Simple, ... would have + Past Participle (V3)

Connects a general present trait or state to an unreal past event.

Formula: If + Past Simple (were / spoke / knew), ... would / wouldn't have + V3

Travel Examples:

"If I spoke fluent Spanish, I would have understood the hostel receptionist yesterday."

"If she were more adventurous, she would have joined the mountain trek last week."
