Noun Clause Formulas & Syntactic Positions

A noun clause is a dependent clause that performs the function of a noun in a sentence.

Type 1: Subject Position [Clause] + Verb + Comp

Subject Noun Clause

"What I appreciate about my family is that everyone supports each other."
  • • Starter: What I love... / How my family handles... / Whether my relatives visit...
  • • Function: Acts as the subject before the main verb.
Type 2: Object Position Subj + Verb + [Clause]

Object Noun Clause

"I don't know whether my step-siblings will attend the extended family reunion."
  • • Starter: I wonder whether... / She explained that... / He asked how...
  • • Function: Follows transitive verbs like know, explain, wonder, remember.
Type 3: Subject Complement Subj + Be + [Clause]

Predicate Noun Clause

"The reason why we are close-knit is that we communicate daily."
  • • Starter: The main reason is that... / My favorite memory is how...
  • • Function: Completes the meaning after linking verbs (e.g., is, was, becomes).
