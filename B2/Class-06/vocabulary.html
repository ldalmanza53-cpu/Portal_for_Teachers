Grammar Phrase

The downside of

Click to flip card

Definition

The negative aspect or disadvantage of a situation.

Correct Example

The downside of sharing a bedroom is that it's hard to have any privacy.

Common Mistake

Don't say "The downside for". Always use the preposition "of".
