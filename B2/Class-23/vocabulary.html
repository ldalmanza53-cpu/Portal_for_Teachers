Recuperation

noun
Definition

The process of recovering health, energy, or strength after illness, mental exhaustion, or prolonged stress.

Context & Reduced Clause

"While undergoing a period of recuperation, professionals should establish clear mental boundaries."

Writing / Grammar Tip: Topic sentences about recuperation should state a main benefit without listing every single resting activity in the first sentence.
