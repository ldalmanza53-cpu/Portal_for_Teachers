Topic Sentence Mastery Guide

How to construct effective topic sentences at the B1 level

The Topic Sentence Formula

An effective topic sentence states the main idea of a paragraph. It must contain two essential components:

1. The Topic

The general subject you are writing about (e.g., "Exam anxiety," "Physical exercise," "Digital boundaries").

2. The Controlling Idea

Your specific angle or opinion on the topic (e.g., "can be managed through three simple steps," "is crucial for preventing burnout").

Weak vs. Strong Examples

❌ Too Vague / Weak:

"Stress is bad for students."

Problem: Lacks a clear controlling idea or specific direction.

✅ Strong B1 Topic Sentence:

"Managing daily student stress requires combining mental reframing with consistent sleep hygiene."

Strength: Clear Topic ("Managing daily student stress") + Clear Controlling Idea ("requires combining mental reframing with consistent sleep hygiene").

Key B1 Stress Vocabulary

Mindset Terms:

reframing, mindfulness, overthinking, cognitive overload, perspective, focus

Physical & Boundary Terms:

somatic relief, sleep hygiene, boundaries, burnout, micro-tasks, resilience
