Lateral Thinking Strategy #1

Deconstruct Objects into Physical Traits

Functional fixedness happens when we see an object solely by its original label (e.g., "binder clip"). Break down items into fundamental physical traits like spring tension, high friction, conductivity, or absorbency to uncover $0 ingenious everyday hacks!
