Unit 11 Grammar: Non-defining Relative Clauses as Sentence Modifiers

You can use non-defining relative clauses starting with comma + which to make a comment about an entire preceding sentence or action rather than just a single noun!

1. , which means...

Explains a result or logical conclusion.
e.g., I have three cats, which means there's usually a lot of cat fur on my clothes.

2. , which is why...

Explains a cause or reason for an action.
e.g., My roommate is a slob, which is why I want to get my own apartment.

3. , which is great / strange...

Expresses an evaluation or emotional reaction.
e.g., She knew how to fix it, which is great because it saved my jacket.
