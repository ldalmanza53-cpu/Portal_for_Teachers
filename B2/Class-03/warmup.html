Personal Trait & Growth Sentence Traps

Explore why certain topic sentences fail and how to calibrate them to "Just Right".

Trap #1: Too Vague / Broad

Lacks Specificity

Uses sweeping generalizations like "Change is good" or "I grew a lot". Gives the reader no specific direction.

❌ "Moving to a new city was an interesting experience that changed my life."
Why it fails: What specifically changed? What trait did you develop?
Trap #2: Too Narrow / Pure Fact

Pure Plot Fact

States a single factual event with zero insight, leaving nowhere for the rest of the paragraph to develop.

❌ "I unpacked three boxes on August 12th in my new apartment."
Why it fails: Pure factual event; offers no personal reflection or thesis.
Goldilocks: Just Right

Balanced Thesis

Pairs a clear specific event with a distinct personal growth outcome or controlling perspective.

✅ "Relocating alone forced me to replace daily dependence on family with proactive self-reliance."
Why it succeeds: Clear topic (relocating alone) + clear insight (building self-reliance).
