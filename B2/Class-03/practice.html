Grammar Plus Reference • Textbook Page 7

Verbs Followed by Gerunds vs. Infinitives

Review target verb patterns before completing the interactive exercises.

Followed by Gerund (-ing)
deny discuss finish mention practice quit resist suggest
• They discussed going exotic.
• She suggested taking a break.
Followed by Infinitive (to + verb)
arrange claim decide demand deserve expect pretend refuse volunteer
• He volunteered to help at hospital.
• We arranged to meet at 7:00.
Gerund Expressions & Meaning Change
• busy / trouble / good time: + -ing
"She's busy cooking right now."
• Stop + to-infinitive: paused to do something new.
• Stop + gerund: ended that activity.
