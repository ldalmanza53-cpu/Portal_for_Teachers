ROLE B Featured Alumni Candidate

Candidate Responsibilities

  • Rule #1: Open your answer immediately with a bold Topic Sentence (e.g., "Over the past year, I have fundamentally transformed my approach to leadership.").
  • Follow up with specific contrast details: Describe how you used to be vs. how you are nowadays.
  • Incorporate target adjectives like proactive, adaptable, meticulous, or composed.
"Your goal: Hook the reader in Sentence #1 without rambling!"
B1/B2 Grammar Standard

The Topic Sentence Master Formula

A high-impact response must state the specific area of change and the controlling outcome in sentence #1.

Specific Area of Life + Core Personality Trait Transformation = Winning Topic Sentence
Weak / Rambling Opening

"Well, I guess I used to get really angry when plans changed in my old job, and then last month something happened and I tried to fix it..."

❌ Missing clear controlling idea in sentence 1; unfocused story start.

Strong Topic Sentence Opening

"Over the past year, I have become significantly more adaptable when dealing with unexpected project shifts."

✔ Clear topic, specific trait, immediate controlling idea for the editor!
