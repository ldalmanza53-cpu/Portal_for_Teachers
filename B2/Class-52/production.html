B1 Grammar Guide: Non-Defining Relative Clauses

Non-defining relative clauses give extra, non-essential information about a noun. If you remove the clause, the sentence still makes complete sense.

Grammar Rules & Commas
  • • Comma Rule: ALWAYS enclose non-defining clauses with commas: Maya Lin, who studied at Yale, designed the memorial.
  • • No "That": You CANNOT use that in non-defining clauses! Always use who, whose, or which.
  • • Punctuation ending: If the clause comes at the end of a sentence, end with a period: They visited the memorial, which was built in 1982.
Pronoun Usage Guide
  • • , who ... , → Used for people (e.g., Steve Jobs, who co-founded Apple, ...)
  • • , whose ... , → Used for possession (e.g., Frida Kahlo, whose paintings are famous, ...)
  • • , which ... , → Used for things or entire projects (e.g., The iPhone, which was released in 2007, ...)
