Unit 8A Grammar: Reduced Relative Clauses

Relative clauses can be reduced by dropping the relative pronoun (who, which, that) and the verb be, or by changing active present verbs to an -ing participle.

1. Drop Relative Pronoun + "be"

Full: Curtis Jackson, who is better known as 50 Cent...
Reduced: Curtis Jackson, better known as 50 Cent...

2. Active Present Verbs -> -ing

Full: The photographer who lives upstairs...
Reduced: The photographer living upstairs...

3. Non-Defining Clauses & Commas

Non-defining clauses require commas around the reduced clause!
e.g., Einstein, thought to be a genius, struggled in school.
