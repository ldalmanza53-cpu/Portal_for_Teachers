B1 Grammar Target

Describing Creative Minds with Extra Information

A **non-defining relative clause** gives extra, non-essential information about a person, idea, or possession. If you remove this extra clause, the sentence still makes complete sense!

who

For People

Adds extra facts about a person

Maya Angelou, who wrote inspiring poetry, was a famous author.
whose

For Possession

Shows belonging (their mind, their work)

Artists, whose minds are curious, see beauty everywhere.
which

For Things & Concepts

Adds extra facts about objects or ideas

Daydreaming, which fuels imagination, helps solve problems.
⚡

The Golden Rules of Non-Defining Relative Clauses

  • • ALWAYS wrap the extra clause with two commas , extra information ,
  • • NEVER use "that" in non-defining clauses! (e.g., ❌ Leonardo, that was an inventor...)

🌟 B1 Exemplar Essay

The Habits of Creative Minds

Creativity is essential for human progress. Creative thinkers help society solve complex problems and view the world through fresh perspectives.

To start with, Leonardo da Vinci, who was both a master painter and a visionary engineer, kept thousands of detailed notebook pages. Similarly, modern innovators, whose minds are constantly searching for connections, rarely settle for obvious answers.

Furthermore, habits like daily brainstorming, which allows original ideas to surface naturally, play a vital role in breakthrough thinking. Creative individuals remain persistent even when facing initial failure.
