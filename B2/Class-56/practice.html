B1 Grammar Focus

Reduced Relative Clauses for Qualities

In B1 English, we shorten relative clauses modifying nouns by omitting the relative pronoun (who/that) and the verb be, or by using present/past participle phrases (-ing / -ed).

1

Adjective Reduction

"An actor who is expressive..."
$\rightarrow$ "An expressive actor..."

2

Active (-ing) Reduction

"An actor who needs to perform stunts..."
$\rightarrow$ "An actor needing to perform stunts..."

3

Passive (-ed) Reduction

"Someone who was trained in drama school..."
$\rightarrow$ "Someone trained in drama school..."
