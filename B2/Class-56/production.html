💡 CEFR B1 Grammar & Career Vocabulary Cheat Sheet

Use these target modal verbs to express necessity, obligation, recommendation, and ability when analyzing careers and discussing job requirements.

Target B1 Modal Verbs for Job Requirements

must / have to / has to Strong Obligation

An actor must memorize all their lines before entering the set.

need to / needs to Necessity

A flight attendant needs to stay calm during severe weather turbulence.

should / ought to Advice / Recommendation

Candidates should practice vocal warming exercises to manage audition nerves.

can / should be able to Ability & Skill

An experienced event manager should be able to solve unexpected budget issues quickly.
